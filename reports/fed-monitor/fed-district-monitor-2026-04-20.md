The current publication cycle is dominated by administrative distribution rather than new thematic white papers, suggesting a "wait-and-see" posture from the regional banks. We should maintain our current positioning but remain hyper-vigilant for any specific policy pivots emanating from the Board of Governors and New York Fed.
