Source excerpt

In an April post, using millions of homeowners' insurance contracts matched with property-level exposure and disaster risk, we showed that although households are insured, they are still "on the hook” because deductibles and coverage limits leave homeowners responsible for part of a loss. Because insurers cannot perfectly observe how well homeowners maintain or protect their properties (a problem known as “moral hazard”), contract terms such as deductibles can play an important role in balancing risk sharing and incentives.
