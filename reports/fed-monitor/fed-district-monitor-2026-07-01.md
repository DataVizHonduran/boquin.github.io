1. **[NY] Liquidity Fades as Treasuries Age**: This research highlights a stark liquidity concentration, where less than 4% of the $30 trillion Treasury market (on-the-run securities) drives the bulk of trading activity. This suggests significant structural fragility in the off-the-run market, which could exacerbate volatility in long-term yields and complicate the Fed's quantitative tightening (QT) efforts.

2. **[RIC] How Much Does the US Economy Rely on Fossil Fuels?**: The findings indicate that aggregate fossil fuel use in gross output has remained relatively constant despite the prevailing energy transition narrative. This suggests that the shift to renewables is currently additive rather than substitutive, implying that the real economy remains more exposed to carbon-price shocks and energy commodity volatility than policy assumptions suggest.

**Synthesis:** The recent research highlights a persistent gap between headline narratives and structural realities, specifically regarding the fragility of Treasury market plumbing and the slow pace of the energy transition. Together, these insights suggest a higher-than-anticipated risk of volatility in both the funding markets and the real economy.
