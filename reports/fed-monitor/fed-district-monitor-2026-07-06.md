4. **[STL] Chicago Publications:** This paper analyzes the divergence between regional labor market tightness and national aggregates. The findings suggest that "pockets of overheating" in specific sectors are driving core services inflation, implying that the Fed may ignore overall unemployment rises if these specific sectors remain tight.

5. **[STL] Philadelphia Publications:** This research explores the transmission mechanism of current monetary policy on small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs). It indicates a growing "credit crunch" at the local level, which serves as a leading indicator for a broader economic slowdown in the coming two quarters.

**Synthesis:** The collective research suggests a regime where structural productivity gains are fighting against localized credit fragility and a potentially higher neutral rate. We should pivot our strategy to favor high-productivity equities while hedging against a tightening credit impulse in the SME sector.
