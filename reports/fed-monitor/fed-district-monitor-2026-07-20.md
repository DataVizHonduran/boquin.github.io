1. **[NY] Nonbank Subsidiaries and the Hidden Fragility of Internal Capital Markets Reallocation**
This research highlights how bank holding companies use nonbank subsidiaries to bypass traditional regulatory capital constraints, creating "hidden" fragilities. For our outlook, this suggests that systemic risk may be understated in official Basel III reporting, as internal capital reallocation can mask liquidity gaps during stress events.

**Synthesis:** The current research cycle indicates a pivot toward examining "regulatory arbitrage" within the banking sector's organizational structures. We should monitor for potential policy tightening aimed at nonbank subsidiaries, as this could trigger a sudden repricing of risk for diversified financial holding companies.
