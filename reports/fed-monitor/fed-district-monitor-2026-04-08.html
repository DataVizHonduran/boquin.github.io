The research argues that depositor panics are symptoms rather than causes, with weak fundamentals almost always preceding the collapse. Synthesis: The current research suggests a shift toward managing structural risks, specifically regarding the technicalities of liquidity provision and the long-term erosion of labor supply. We should remain cautious of "hidden" solvency issues in the banking sector while pricing in a tighter labor market driven by wealth-effect retirements.
