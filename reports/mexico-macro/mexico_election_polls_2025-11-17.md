**Analysis:** MORENA maintains strong plurality support for 2027 mid-terms with 45-46% Chamber preferences. If allied with PVEM and PT (as in 2024 election), coalition could potentially secure another supermajority in Chamber of Deputies. High favorability (78%) provides buffer against economic headwinds.

### Traditional Opposition (PAN, PRI, PRD)
- **PAN:** 7% Chamber preference (tied for 3rd place with PRI)

- **Combined:** ~15% (down from historical dominance pre-2018)

- **Trajectory:** ↓ Continued decline; **first time in modern history neither PAN nor PRI holds 2nd place**

**Analysis:** Traditional opposition collapsed to historic lows. PAN and PRI combined support (15%) less than MORENA alone by 3:1 margin. Negative party images (60%+ unfavorable) suggest structural, not cyclical, weakness. Coalition efforts (e.g., "Fuerza y Corazón por México" in 2024) failed to reverse trend.

### Movimiento Ciudadano (MC)
- **Current Polling:** 10% Chamber preference (2nd place)
- **Position:** First time MC displaces PAN/PRI from runner-up position
- **Trajectory:** ↑ Emerging as principal opposition force

**Analysis:** MC's rise to 10% and 2nd-place finish marks significant realignment of Mexican opposition. Positioned as centrist alternative to both MORENA and traditional parties. Likely benefiting from PAN/PRI collapse and urban/educated voter migration.
