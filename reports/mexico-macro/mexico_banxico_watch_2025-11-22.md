## Executive Summary

Banxico cut rates by 25 bps to 7.25% on November 6 in a 4-1 split vote, with Deputy Governor Jonathan Heath dissenting for the fourth consecutive meeting. Governor Rodríguez Ceja signaled further easing is "highly probable" but the Board softened forward guidance language, removing previous text about "assessing further adjustments." Heath's hawkish dissent emphasizes premature easing risks de-anchoring inflation from the 3% target, while the majority prioritizes economic slack. Inflation moderated to 3.63% (headline) in early October but core remains sticky at 4.24%, creating a dovish-hawkish divide within the Board.
