## Executive Summary

Institutional investor sentiment on Mexico remains cautiously optimistic despite near-term headwinds, with key players like BlackRock maintaining overweight positions citing nearshoring structural trends and early-cycle rate cut opportunities. Ashmore views Mexico as significantly undervalued (trading two standard deviations below average) due to constitutional reform concerns, but expects President Sheinbaum to take a market-sensitive approach. Currency positioning is polarized: UBS forecasts 4-5% peso appreciation to 18.50-19.50 by end-2025, while Bank of America recommends shorting MXN citing overvaluation. Emerging market fund flows into Mexico remain positive with record FDI (>$3B H1 2025), though portfolio investment fell in late 2024. The consensus centers on Mexico's nearshoring advantage and fiscal discipline, balanced against political uncertainty from constitutional changes and US trade policy risks under potential Trump tariffs.
