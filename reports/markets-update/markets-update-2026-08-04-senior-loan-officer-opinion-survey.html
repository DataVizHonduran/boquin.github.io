(i) Growth: The return to 0.0% tightening for large firms suggests a supportive environment for capital expenditure (CapEx) and business investment. The removal of credit constraints typically acts as a pro-growth catalyst, potentially offsetting any lingering effects of previous rate hikes.

5. Policy Outlook

Forecast: Hold / Neutral Bias
