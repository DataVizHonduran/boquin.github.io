3. Macro Characterization

(i) Growth: The aggressive expansion of M2 is a strong leading indicator for nominal GDP growth. The consistent rise in the money stock suggests robust credit creation and increased spending capacity, pointing toward an expansionary phase of the business cycle.

(ii) Labor Market: While H.6 data is a monetary measure, the surge in M1 (liquid assets) typically correlates with higher corporate cash balances and consumer purchasing power, which generally supports sustained aggregate demand and prevents significant labor market softening.

(iii) Inflation: The data presents a bullish case for future inflation. With M2 at its 100th percentile, the "monetary overhang" is substantial. If the supply side of the economy cannot keep pace with this liquidity injection, we expect upward pressure on CPI in the coming quarters.

5. Policy Outlook

Forecast: Hawkish Pivot / Pause in Easing

The balance of risks has shifted decisively toward inflation. With M2 at a decade-high and accelerating, the Fed is likely to view current financial conditions as "too loose." We expect the Federal Reserve to halt any current easing cycle and potentially signal a rate hike in the next 3-6 months to drain excess liquidity and prevent an inflationary spiral. The risk of "over-shooting" on the growth side now outweighs the risk of a recessionary contraction.
