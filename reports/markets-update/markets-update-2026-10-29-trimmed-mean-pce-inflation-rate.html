3. Macro Characterization

(i) Growth: While PCE is a price measure, the stability of the 12-month trimmed mean near its 10-year median suggests an economy that is neither overheating nor collapsing, but rather operating at a sustainable, albeit slower, pace of expansion.

(iii) Inflation: Inflation is characterized by a "descending staircase" pattern. The long-term trend is downward, but the recent 1-month print of 2.84% indicates a risk of "bumpy" disinflation where monthly shocks threaten to derail the annual glide path.
