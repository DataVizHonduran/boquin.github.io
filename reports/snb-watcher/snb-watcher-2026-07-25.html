Thematic Analysis

1. CPI & Inflation Outlook (0–2% target)
Inflation remains comfortably within the target range, with June CPI printing at 0.5%. While this confirms price stability, the "flatlining" nature of inflation mentioned by Chairman Schlegel suggests a risk of undershooting the target or a lack of domestic inflationary pressure, reducing the incentive to keep rates at or above 0%.

2. CHF Strength & FX Intervention
Market sentiment indicates the CHF remains a primary concern, with EUR/CHF hovering around 0.9200. Financial news highlights that "intervention risk" continues to keep the Franc from appreciating further, confirming that the SNB is still actively using FX interventions as a primary tool to support the export sector.

3. Negative Rate Risk & Policy Space
The most critical development this month is the explicit mention of negative rates. Having reached 0% in March 2026, the SNB is now signaling that the "zero lower bound" is not a hard floor. Schlegel’s openness to negative rates suggests the board is preparing the markets for a return to the 2015–2022 regime if CHF appreciation threatens the economy.

4. Global Economy & Swiss Export Sector
Chairman Schlegel’s July 8 speech focused on the current economic situation, linking monetary policy to the resilience of the export sector. The SNB remains wary of global headwinds that could drive safe-haven flows into the CHF.

5. Quarterly Bulletin Themes
While the full Bulletin is a recurring publication, recent communications emphasize financial stability (Antoine Martin) and the resilience of the banking sector, specifically noting that UBS possesses sufficient capital to meet planned Swiss reforms.

6. Forward Guidance Evolution
Forward guidance has shifted from "maintaining stability at 0%" to "openness to negative territory." The SNB is moving from a posture of normalization (the 2024-2026 cutting cycle) back toward a posture of aggressive CHF deterrence.
