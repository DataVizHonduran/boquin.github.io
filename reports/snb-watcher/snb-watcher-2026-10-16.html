Inflation remains firmly within the mandate. Chairman Schlegel specifically addressed concerns regarding food price inflation, attributing the rise to "hot weather" rather than systemic inflationary pressure, suggesting the SNB views these as supply-side shocks rather than a reason to tighten.

2. CHF Strength & FX Intervention
The SNB continues to monitor CHF appreciation closely. Market reports (Societe Generale) suggest a "dialed down" intervention bias, but the SNB's decision to hold rates at 0% (the world's lowest) serves as a primary tool to discourage excessive safe-haven inflows.

3. Negative Rate Risk & Policy Space
With the policy rate currently at 0%, the SNB has reached the zero lower bound. While no immediate cut was signaled in September, the baseline for Chairman Schlegel remains pragmatic regarding the use of negative rates if CHF appreciation threatens the economy.

4. Global Economy & Swiss Export Sector

5. Quarterly Bulletin Themes

6. Forward Guidance Evolution
