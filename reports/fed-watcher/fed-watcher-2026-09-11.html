Executive Summary

The last 30 days have been characterized by a sharpening divide within the Federal Reserve regarding the necessity of interest rate hikes to combat persistent inflation. This internal tension coincides with reports of persistent inflation in August and 30-year Treasury yields hitting 19-year highs, increasing market speculation of a hawkish pivot.
