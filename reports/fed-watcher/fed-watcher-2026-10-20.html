Executive Summary

The last 30 days indicate a decisive shift toward a more hawkish posture within the FOMC. While the committee may skip a rate hike in October, there is strong consensus among several officials—including Kashkari, Waller, and Hammack—that further rate increases are necessary before the end of 2026 to combat persistent inflation. A new primary driver of inflation has emerged: the "AI buildout," which is now replacing tariffs as the main catalyst for core price pressures. While some officials remain focused on avoiding labor market pain, the prevailing sentiment is that restrictive policy must be maintained or tightened to prevent an "inflationary mindset" from taking hold.
