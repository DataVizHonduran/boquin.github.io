Executive Summary

The Federal Reserve has pivoted back to a tightening cycle, raising interest rates on September 16, 2026, for the first time in over three years to combat stubborn inflation. While the FOMC signaled the possibility of one more hike this year, the committee remains divided. Hawkish members, led by Lorie Logan and Neel Kashkari, are advocating for aggressive further increases (50bps+), citing persistent inflationary pressures. Conversely, Governor Waller has urged patience, suggesting the Fed "give disinflation a chance." Recent data showing cooler inflation has introduced some uncertainty, potentially providing a window to pause, though the prevailing momentum remains focused on ensuring a "timelier" retreat in inflation.
