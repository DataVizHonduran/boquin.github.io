Executive Summary

The past 30 days have seen a marked hawkish shift among several Federal Reserve officials, diverging from previous easing expectations. While the June FOMC minutes indicate a committee "united" on current communications, they reveal deep concerns regarding inflation risks stemming from AI, tariffs, and geopolitical tensions (specifically Iran). Notably, Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan, Minneapolis Fed President Neel Kashkari, and Governor Christopher Waller have explicitly signaled that interest rate hikes may be necessary if inflation remains elevated or core inflation stays "hot." This contrasts with New York Fed President John Williams, who suggests inflation has peaked and rates are "well positioned." The prevailing sentiment has shifted from "when to cut" to a cautious openness toward further tightening to ensure price stability.
