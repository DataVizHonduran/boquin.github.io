Executive Summary

The Federal Reserve has pivoted toward a tightening cycle, approving an interest rate hike on September 16, 2026, with signals that another hike is likely this year. Conversely, some officials emphasize flexibility in the pace of hikes. The labor market is generally viewed as "pretty good," reducing the immediate urgency for easing and providing the Fed room to combat price pressures.
