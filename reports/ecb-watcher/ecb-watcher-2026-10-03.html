Executive Summary

The past 30 days have seen a sharp pivot in the inflation narrative. Following a rate hike on September 10th (Deposit Facility Rate to 2.5%), new data released on October 2nd shows Eurozone inflation spiking to 3.8%, significantly exceeding expectations. Simultaneously, the Governing Council is managing structural transitions, including the resignation of Isabel Schnabel (moving to the IMF) and ongoing concerns regarding AI-driven financial instability. While Philip Lane continues to provide a data-dependent outlook, the sudden surge in inflation and geopolitical risks (specifically Iran) have shifted the immediate policy bias toward a hawkish posture.
