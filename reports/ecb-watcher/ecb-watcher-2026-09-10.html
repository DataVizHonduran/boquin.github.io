Executive Summary

The Governing Council (GC) has pivoted to a tightening bias, raising key interest rates by 25 basis points to 2.5% on September 10, 2026. This move was driven by a spike in inflation fueled by energy shocks and geopolitical tensions, specifically citing the "Iran war" as a primary catalyst for inflationary pressure. While the decision was widely anticipated, the internal debate remains nuanced. Isabel Schnabel has emerged as a leading voice for further hikes based on economic strength, while Joachim Nagel, though supporting the current hike, expressed caution regarding the trajectory beyond September. The overall tone has shifted from a focus on easing to a renewed commitment to quell energy-driven inflation.
