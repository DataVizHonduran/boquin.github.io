Executive Summary

The past 30 days have seen a decisive shift toward a more restrictive stance. On September 10, the ECB raised interest rates to 2.5%, citing upside inflation risks fueled by geopolitical tensions (specifically the Iran war). Recent data showing Eurozone inflation hitting 3.8% has intensified pressure for further hikes. While Chief Economist Philip Lane suggests that growth drags may limit the need for aggressive action, Hawkish members like Joachim Nagel are explicitly signaling the possibility of further hikes to tame inflation. A significant institutional shift occurred on September 24 with the announcement of Isabel Schnabel's resignation to join the IMF, removing one of the Executive Board's most persistent hawks.
