Executive Summary

The last 30 days have been marked by extreme volatility in both leadership and inflation expectations. The Governing Council held rates at 2.5% on October 29, but the narrative has shifted sharply toward further tightening. Eurozone inflation surged to 4% in October—double the ECB's target—triggering a wave of hawkish signals from both the Executive Board and National Central Bank (NCB) governors. Simultaneously, the ECB faces a leadership transition: President Christine Lagarde announced her resignation effective February 7, 2027, citing a desire for "freedom of speech." The nomination of Klaas Knot (DNB) by the Netherlands introduces a strong hawkish candidate into the succession race, further reinforcing the market's expectation of a December rate hike.

Thematic Analysis

1. Inflation Assessment
Inflation has experienced a significant resurgence, hitting 4% in October 2026. This is the highest level in three years and double the ECB's 2% target. The surge has fundamentally altered the GC's outlook, moving the conversation from "when to cut" to "how much to hike."

2. Growth Outlook
Growth concerns remain, but are currently secondary to inflation. Fabio Panetta noted that rising bond yields are acting as a restraint on the economy, suggesting that the restrictive stance is transmitting, though the inflation spike may necessitate further tightening despite growth risks.

3. Labor Markets & Wages
The ECB Wage Tracker (Nov 4) provides a hawkish signal, suggesting that wage growth in early 2027 will exceed 2026 levels. This indicates persistent upward pressure on services inflation and potential second-round effects.

4. Financial Conditions & Credit
The Bank Lending Survey (Oct 27) and SAFE survey (Oct 26) both confirm that lending conditions have tightened. This suggests that the "transmission" of previous hikes is still working through the system, even as new hikes are considered.

5. Balance Sheet (APP/PEPP rundown)
No specific updates on the pace of APP/PEPP rundown were provided in the current coverage period.

6. Forward Guidance Evolution
Guidance has shifted from a neutral hold to a "near certain" hike expectation for December. The resignation of Lagarde and the nomination of the hawkish Klaas Knot suggest a potential structural shift toward a more restrictive regime in 2027.
