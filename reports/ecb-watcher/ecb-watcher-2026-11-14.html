Thematic Analysis

1. Inflation Assessment
The ECB remains vigilant. The shift in tone from Philip Lane and Fabio Panetta suggests that inflation is not descending as quickly or sustainably as previously hoped. The focus has shifted toward "taming prices" through potentially higher borrowing costs.

2. Growth Outlook
Financial news reports (Bloomberg, Nov 13) indicate the Euro-Zone is showing "solid growth," which reduces the immediate fear of a recession and provides the ECB with the "policy space" to consider further rate hikes without triggering a severe contraction.

3. Labor Markets & Wages
This is the primary hawkish driver. The Nov 4 Wage Tracker is a critical data point, signaling that wage growth is accelerating into 2027. This raises the risk of second-round effects, which is a primary concern for the Bundesbank and the Executive Board.

4. Financial Conditions & Credit
