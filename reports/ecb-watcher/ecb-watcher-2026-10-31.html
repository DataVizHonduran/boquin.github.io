Executive Summary

The October 29 Governing Council meeting was dominated by two major developments: the decision to hold key interest rates at 2.5% and the surprise announcement that President Christine Lagarde will step down early on February 7, 2027. While rates remained steady, the policy tone has shifted toward a "hawkish hold." The ECB flagged a potential December hike as energy shocks pushed inflation to 3.8%. Internal tensions are evident, with hawks like Joachim Nagel and even traditionally dovish members like Fabio Panetta signaling that further hikes may be necessary to tame prices, despite concerns that rising bond yields are weighing on economic growth.
