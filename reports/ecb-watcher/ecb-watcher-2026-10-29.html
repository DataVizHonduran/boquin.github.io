Executive Summary

The October 29 meeting was dominated by two major developments: the ECB held key interest rates steady at 2.5%, and President Christine Lagarde announced her early resignation, effective February 7, 2027. While rates remained unchanged, the policy tone shifted toward a more hawkish bias due to a new "energy shock" threatening to reignite inflation. Market focus has shifted toward a potential December hike. The Governing Council is currently balancing these mounting inflation risks against stagnant growth and tightening bank lending conditions.
