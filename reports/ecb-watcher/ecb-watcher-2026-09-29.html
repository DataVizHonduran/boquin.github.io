Executive Summary

The past 30 days mark a decisive hawkish pivot for the ECB. On September 10, the Governing Council raised the deposit rate to 2.50%, citing an inflation jump fueled by geopolitical tensions (specifically the Iran war). President Lagarde and Chief Economist Philip Lane have both signaled that upside risks to inflation remain significant, with Lane explicitly leaving the door open for further hikes. A major institutional shift occurred on September 24 with the announcement of Isabel Schnabel's resignation to join the IMF, removing one of the Executive Board's most consistent hawks. Despite this, the overall tone remains restrictive, driven by energy price volatility and a modest uptick in negotiated wage growth.
