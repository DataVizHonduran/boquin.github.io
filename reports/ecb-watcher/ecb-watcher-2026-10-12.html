Executive Summary

The policy narrative has shifted sharply toward a hawkish tilt following a surprise surge in Eurozone inflation, which hit 3.8% on October 2nd. Market sentiment, reflected in recent polls, now anticipates a potential rate hike in December as inflation nearly doubles the 2% target. While the Governing Council remains officially data-dependent, Joachim Nagel has explicitly prioritized inflation over bond spread concerns. Simultaneously, a secondary theme has emerged regarding the systemic risks of AI, with Fabio Panetta and Christine Lagarde warning of potential market corrections and the risk of Europe being "cut off" from AI progress. The overall tone has transitioned from a focus on easing to a vigilant stance against a resurgence of price pressures.

2. Growth Outlook
Growth concerns remain, but are currently secondary to the inflation shock. The Eurogroup is weighing energy support to mitigate fiscal pressures and borrowing costs.
