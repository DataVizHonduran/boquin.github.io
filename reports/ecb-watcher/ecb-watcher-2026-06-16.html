Executive Summary

The Governing Council (GC) executed a significant policy pivot on June 11, 2026, raising interest rates by 25 basis points—the first hike since 2023. This decision was driven by an inflationary shock stemming from the war in Iran and escalating energy costs, leading the ECB to revise its inflation forecasts upward. While President Lagarde characterized the move as a "necessary decision," the internal dynamics have shifted; notably, traditionally dovish members like Fabio Panetta have signaled support for hikes to combat persistent inflation. Despite a recent US-Iran deal bringing oil prices closer to baseline, hawkish members, led by Joachim Nagel, remain vigilant, suggesting that inflation relief is not yet guaranteed and keeping the door open for further action in July.
