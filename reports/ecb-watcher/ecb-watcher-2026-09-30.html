Executive Summary

The past 30 days mark a significant hawkish pivot for the ECB. On September 10, the Governing Council raised interest rates by 25 bps to 2.5%, citing an inflation spike fueled by geopolitical tensions (specifically the Iran war). President Lagarde highlighted a precarious balance of upside inflation risks and downside growth risks. The hawkish shift is reinforced by Bundesbank President Joachim Nagel, who signaled that further hikes may be necessary. Simultaneously, the Executive Board faces a structural shift with the announced resignation of Isabel Schnabel to join the IMF. While Philip Lane continues to emphasize data-dependency, the overall trajectory has shifted from easing/pause to active tightening to combat renewed inflationary pressures.
