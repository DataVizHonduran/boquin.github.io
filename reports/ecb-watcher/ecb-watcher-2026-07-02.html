Executive Summary

The past 30 days mark a significant pivot in ECB policy, characterized by the first interest rate hike since 2023 (June 11). This "necessary decision" was driven by a surge in inflation resulting from the Iran war and escalating energy costs. While the ECB has returned to a tightening cycle, internal divergence is growing. Isabel Schnabel and Joachim Nagel emphasize the need for further hikes to secure the 2% target, while Philip Lane advocates for a "measured response," warning of delayed energy price effects. Christine Lagarde’s "Back to Basics" speech at Sintra signals a shift toward a more flexible, data-dependent playbook, explicitly stating that the ECB is not guided by a specific neutral rate estimate.
