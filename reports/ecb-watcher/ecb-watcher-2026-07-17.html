Executive Summary

The last 30 days have seen a marked shift toward hawkishness within the Governing Council, driven by renewed geopolitical instability in the Hormuz Strait and persistent energy price volatility. Executive Board member Isabel Schnabel has emerged as a leading voice for further tightening, explicitly questioning if "inflation is back" and signaling the need for additional rate hikes. While President Lagarde and Chief Economist Philip Lane maintain a data-dependent, "not boxed in" stance, the June 10-11 GC accounts reveal a concerning trend of rising inflation despite previous hikes. Conversely, Fabio Panetta continues to highlight a "fragile" economic outlook, though he has recently emphasized the necessity of keeping inflation expectations anchored. Parallel to monetary policy, the ECB has advanced its digital euro pilot, selecting 36 payment service providers.
