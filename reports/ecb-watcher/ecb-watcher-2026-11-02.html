Executive Summary

The last 30 days have been dominated by a historic leadership transition and a cautious monetary policy stance. On October 29, the ECB held key interest rates steady at 2.5%, though Governing Council (GC) discussions revealed a significant internal debate regarding the appropriateness of a rate hike. Simultaneously, President Christine Lagarde announced her early resignation, effective February 7, 2027, citing a desire for "freedom of speech" regarding world events. While the official hold suggests a pause, hawkish signals are intensifying; Joachim Nagel and Fabio Panetta both indicated that further hikes may be necessary to tame inflation. This creates a complex backdrop of policy uncertainty as the EU begins the succession process for the ECB presidency.
