Executive Summary

The ECB has pivoted back to a tightening cycle, raising the deposit rate to 2.5% on September 10th. This move was driven by an inflation spike fueled by energy costs and geopolitical tensions (specifically the Iran war), alongside upward revisions to inflation and growth forecasts. While President Lagarde maintains a balanced tone, highlighting both upside inflation risks and downside growth risks, the Governing Council is currently leaning hawkish. Isabel Schnabel and Joachim Nagel have been the primary drivers of this shift, with Nagel signaling that further hikes may be necessary. The ECB also continues to advance its digital agenda, announcing investments in tokenized securities.

Thematic Analysis

1. Inflation Assessment
Inflation is currently viewed as having significant upside risks. The September 10th rate hike was explicitly linked to an "inflation spike" and energy costs exacerbated by the Iran war. The ECB is monitoring "second-round effects" and the role of sectoral shocks.

2. Growth Outlook
The outlook is mixed. While the ECB raised growth forecasts on September 10th, President Lagarde explicitly noted "downside risks to growth," suggesting a precarious balance between fighting inflation and supporting the economy.

3. Labor Markets & Wages
The Wage Tracker (released 2026-09-16) shows negotiated wage growth at 2.7% for H1 2027, indicating a "modest uptick." This provides a hawkish catalyst, as wage growth remains a key driver of underlying inflation.

4. Financial Conditions & Credit
The ECB is focusing on the "new age of capital" and the impact of AI on productivity. There is an increasing focus on how AI adoption will shape future inflation and productivity.

5. Balance Sheet (APP/PEPP rundown)
No specific updates on the pace of APP/PEPP rundown were provided in the current coverage period.

6. Forward Guidance Evolution
Guidance has shifted from "easing" to "tightening." The September 10th decision signals that a December hike remains "on the table," moving the GC away from a pause/cut narrative toward a renewed hike cycle.
