1. Inflation Assessment
Inflation has hit 4%, the highest level in three years. This resurgence is the primary driver of current policy discussions, shifting the narrative from "when to cut" to "whether to hike."

2. Growth Outlook
Growth is under pressure. Fabio Panetta noted that rising bond yields are acting as a restraint on the economy, suggesting a tension between inflation fighting and growth support.

3. Labor Markets & Wages
The ECB Wage Tracker (2026-11-04) provides a hawkish signal, indicating that wage growth in the first three quarters of 2027 is projected to exceed the average for 2026, raising concerns about second-round effects.

4. Financial Conditions & Credit
