Executive Summary

The Governing Council is shifting toward a more hawkish posture ahead of the September 10 meeting. A combination of an energy shock (pushing inflation to 3.3%) and rising oil prices has reignited inflation concerns. Key figures, most notably Isabel Schnabel and Joachim Nagel, have explicitly signaled the need for further rate hikes to contain inflation risks. Market consensus and recent official rhetoric suggest a high probability of a rate increase in September, though Nagel suggests caution regarding further moves beyond this "insurance" hike. While the Chief Economist (Philip Lane) remains focused on structural drivers and defense spending, the immediate policy narrative has pivoted from easing to inflation containment.
