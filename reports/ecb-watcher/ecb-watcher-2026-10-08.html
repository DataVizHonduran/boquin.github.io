Executive Summary

The past 30 days mark a significant hawkish pivot for the ECB. On September 10, the Governing Council raised interest rates by 25 basis points to 2.50%, citing upside inflation risks fueled by geopolitical tensions (specifically the Iran war). While President Lagarde maintains a balanced tone regarding growth risks, the "hawkish" wing—led by Joachim Nagel—is openly signaling the possibility of further hikes to tame persistent inflation, which recently hit 3.8%. A notable institutional shift is the announcement that Isabel Schnabel will leave the Executive Board early for a role at the IMF. Philip Lane provides a slight counter-balance, suggesting that growth drags may limit the necessity for further aggressive action.
