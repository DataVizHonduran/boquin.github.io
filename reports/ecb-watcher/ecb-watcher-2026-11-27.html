Executive Summary

The last 30 days have been marked by a significant leadership transition and a sharp pivot toward a more hawkish monetary stance. The Eurozone is facing a resurgence of inflation, which hit 4% in early November, coupled with a sharp increase in consumer inflation expectations (October survey). This has triggered a shift in rhetoric: traditionally dovish members like Fabio Panetta are now signaling the need for further rate hikes to prevent inflation from taking root. Simultaneously, the EU has selected Pablo Hernández de Cos to succeed Christine Lagarde as ECB President. With Joachim Nagel explicitly calling for a December hike and the Governing Council emphasizing the need to "act in time," the policy trajectory has shifted from "easing/pause" to "active tightening."
