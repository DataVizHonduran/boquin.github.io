Executive Summary

The past 30 days mark a significant pivot in ECB policy. On September 10, the Governing Council raised the deposit rate by 25 basis points to 2.5%, citing an inflation spike driven by energy costs and geopolitical tensions (specifically the Iran war). While President Lagarde acknowledged downside risks to growth, the primary focus has shifted toward "longer-lasting" inflation.
