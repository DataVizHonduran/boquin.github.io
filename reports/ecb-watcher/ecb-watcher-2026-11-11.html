Executive Summary

The last 30 days have been dominated by a major leadership transition announcement: President Christine Lagarde will step down on February 7, 2027. This has triggered a political race for her successor, with the Netherlands nominating Klaas Knot and Spain nominating Pablo Hernández de Cos. On the monetary front, the tone has shifted noticeably hawkish. Joachim Nagel and Philip Lane have both signaled that a December rate hike is "obvious" or "prudent" if data remains unchanged. This hawkish tilt is supported by new ECB wage tracker data suggesting higher wage growth in early 2027 than in 2026, complicating the path toward the 2% target.
