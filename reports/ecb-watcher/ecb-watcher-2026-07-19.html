Executive Summary

The ECB is navigating a volatile environment characterized by renewed geopolitical instability in the Hormuz Strait and resurgent energy prices. The prevailing narrative has shifted from easing toward a "back to basics" approach, with President Lagarde emphasizing data-dependency over neutral rate estimates. Conversely, Fabio Panetta highlights a "fragile" growth outlook and expects inflation to linger around 3% until early 2027. Market expectations are pivoting, with recent polls suggesting a potential September hike if energy volatility persists. The Governing Council remains divided between those prioritizing the 2% target amidst supply shocks and those concerned with growth risks and political pressure.
