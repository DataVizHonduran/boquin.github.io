Executive Summary

The last 30 days have been marked by a significant shift in the Eurozone's monetary landscape. Thematic Analysis

1. Inflation Assessment
Inflation has surged to 4% (October 2026), reaching a three-year high and doubling the ECB's 2% target. This has fundamentally shifted the narrative from "when to cut" to "whether to hike."

2. Growth Outlook
Growth is under pressure. Fabio Panetta noted that rising bond yields are acting as a restraint on the economy, while the SAFE survey confirms tightening credit conditions for enterprises.

3. Labor Markets & Wages
The ECB Wage Tracker (Nov 4) provides a hawkish signal, suggesting that wage growth in the first three quarters of 2027 will exceed the 2026 average, raising fears of persistent domestic price pressures.

4. Financial Conditions & Credit
Credit is tightening. Both the Bank Lending Survey and the SAFE survey indicate that lending conditions have tightened, suggesting the transmission of previous hikes is fully operational.
