There is a renewed focus on the "stickiness" of inflation. The shift in Fabio Panetta's rhetoric and Joachim Nagel's insistence on further hikes suggest the GC believes the 2% target is not yet secured.

2. Growth Outlook
Growth is viewed as being under pressure from monetary tightening. Panetta noted that rising bond yields are acting as a restraint on the economy.

3. Labor Markets & Wages
This is currently the primary driver of hawkishness. The Nov 4 Wage Tracker indicates an accelerating trend in wage growth moving into 2027, raising fears of a wage-price spiral.
