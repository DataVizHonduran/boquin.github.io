Executive Summary

The ECB has entered a period of heightened vigilance due to external geopolitical shocks. At the July 23 meeting, the Governing Council held interest rates steady at 2.25%, but the accompanying rhetoric shifted toward a more hawkish posture. The primary driver is the "reignited Iran war," which has sparked significant inflation fears. While the ECB Wage Tracker suggests stable negotiated wage pressures (2.7% in Q1 2027), officials including Joachim Nagel and Fabio Panetta have emphasized the need to keep price expectations anchored. Markets are now pricing in the possibility of further rate hikes in late 2026. Additionally, political maneuvering is surfacing regarding the ECB presidency, with nominations for Klaas Knot and talks involving Joachim Nagel.
