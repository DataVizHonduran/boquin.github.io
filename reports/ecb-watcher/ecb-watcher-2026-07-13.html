Thematic Analysis

1. Inflation Assessment
The ECB is managing a dichotomy: recent data shows inflation falling more than expected, yet "mid-sized shocks" (specifically energy/Gulf supply) are creating persistent upside risks. Schnabel explicitly warns against "looking through" these shocks, suggesting they may have a lasting impact.

2. Growth Outlook
The outlook is described as "fragile" by Panetta. There is growing concern that high defense spending and potential aid cuts (noted in German budget approvals) could create political pressure on central bank independence and impact growth.

3. Labor Markets & Wages
While not the primary focus of recent speeches, the reliance on the Consumer Expectations Survey suggests the GC remains vigilant regarding wage-push inflation and second-round effects.

4. Financial Conditions & Credit
The ECB is refining its collateral framework, specifically integrating non-financial credit claim portfolios, to ensure stability as temporary measures phase out.

5. Balance Sheet (APP/PEPP rundown)

6. Forward Guidance Evolution
A significant shift is underway. Lagarde’s "Back to Basics" speech and Lane’s refusal to be "boxed in" indicate the ECB is abandoning prescriptive paths in favor of extreme data-dependency.
