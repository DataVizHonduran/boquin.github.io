Executive Summary

The last 30 days mark a significant hawkish pivot for the ECB. On September 10, the Governing Council raised interest rates by 25 bps to 2.5%, citing upside inflation risks fueled by geopolitical tensions (specifically the Iran war). Recent data has intensified this pressure, with Eurozone inflation hitting 3.8% (Oct 2), nearly double the 2% target. The discourse has shifted from "easing" to "fighting an inflation jump," with market polls now pricing in another hike for December.
