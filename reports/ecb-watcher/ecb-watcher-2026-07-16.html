Executive Summary

The past 30 days have seen a sharp pivot in the ECB's risk assessment, driven by renewed geopolitical instability in the Strait of Hormuz and subsequent oil price surges. While President Lagarde has emphasized "economic resilience" and a "balanced" risk profile, the hawkish wing—led by Isabel Schnabel—has explicitly signaled that more rate hikes may be necessary to counter energy-driven inflation. Conversely, Fabio Panetta has highlighted a "fragile" economic outlook and the risks of political pressure on central bank independence. The Governing Council is currently grappling with a conflict between fragile growth and a volatile inflation outlook, leading to increased market bets on further tightening.
