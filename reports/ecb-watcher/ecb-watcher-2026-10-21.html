Executive Summary

The last 30 days have been characterized by a resurgence of inflation concerns, with Eurozone inflation hitting 3.8% on October 2nd, significantly exceeding expectations. This has shifted the narrative from gradual easing toward the possibility of further tightening. President Lagarde has emphasized that higher yields from recent bond sell-offs will act as a transmission mechanism to slow both growth and inflation. While Chief Economist Philip Lane continues to focus on diagnostic challenges and structural shifts (AI), the hawkish wing, led by Joachim Nagel, has reaffirmed a strict focus on the 2% target over bond spread concerns. Market expectations and IMF projections now suggest at least one more 25 bps hike may be necessary in 2026 to anchor expectations.
