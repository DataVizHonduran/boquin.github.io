Executive Summary

The Governing Council (GC) pivoted to a tightening bias this month, delivering a 25 basis point interest rate hike on September 10, bringing the deposit facility rate to 2.50%. This move was driven by a spike in inflation and rising energy costs, with the ECB raising both inflation and growth forecasts. While President Lagarde emphasized a balance between upside inflation risks and downside growth risks, the hawkish wing—led by Isabel Schnabel and Joachim Nagel—successfully pushed for the hike, citing a "strong economy" and the need to contain persistent price pressures. Forward guidance remains open, with policymakers signaling that a further hike in December remains "on the table," contingent on energy price evolution and underlying inflation trends.
