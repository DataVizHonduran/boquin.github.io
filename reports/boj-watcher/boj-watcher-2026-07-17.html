Executive Summary

The past 30 days reveal a widening divergence within the BOJ Policy Board. A hawkish contingent, led by External Members Tamura and Takata, is pushing for an accelerated normalization pace, with Tamura explicitly identifying the neutral rate at approximately 2.0% and calls for hikes "every few months." Conversely, new member Toichiro Asada has established a dovish anchor, insisting on evidence of demand-driven inflation before supporting further hikes. Governor Ueda and Deputy Governor Himino have signaled increased vigilance regarding inflation overshooting the 2% target. While the BOJ remains prepared to accelerate hikes if risks mount, political pressure from PM Takaichi for policy restraint and market concerns over JGB yield spikes provide a counterweight to the hawkish momentum.

Thematic Analysis

1. Inflation Assessment (CPI, Core, Wages)
The BOJ is increasingly concerned with "overshoot" risks. Governor Ueda and Deputy Governor Himino both highlighted the possibility of inflation exceeding the 2% target. This is supported by rising household inflation expectations (reported July 16). While real wages have risen for five consecutive months, the pace of growth is slowing (July 7), creating a tension between the "wage-price spiral" hawks fear and the "demand-driven" evidence doves require.

2. Growth Outlook
Internal sources suggest the BOJ may raise its growth forecasts in upcoming reports (July 12), which would provide the macroeconomic justification for further rate hikes.

3. Yen / FX Considerations
While not the primary focus of recent speeches, the "high bar for hawkish repricing" suggests the market is cautious. Political pressure from PM Takaichi for "policy restraint" (June 21) indicates that the government remains sensitive to the economic impact of rapid tightening.

4. Financial Conditions & JGB Market
There is emerging concern regarding market stability. Former policymakers have warned that the BOJ may face pressure to ramp up bond-buying if JGB yields spike (July 16), suggesting that the "Accommodative Hike" philosophy of Deputy Governor Uchida remains relevant to prevent market dysfunction.

5. Balance Sheet (JGB purchase taper)
No new specific announcements on taper acceleration were made in the last 30 days; however, the baseline tapering process continues.

6. Forward Guidance Evolution
The guidance is shifting from "cautious normalization" to "conditional acceleration." The board is now explicitly discussing the "neutral rate" (Tamura's 2% estimate) and the possibility of hikes every few months, moving the goalposts further away from the current 0.75% rate.
