1. Inflation Assessment (CPI, Core, Wages)
Governor Ueda has shifted his language from "monitoring" to "anchoring." By stating underlying inflation is "quite close" to the 2% target and declaring a zero-tolerance policy for significant overshoots, the BOJ is signaling that the "virtuous cycle" of wages and prices is now viewed as established.

2. Growth Outlook
Consumer confidence showed signs of weakness (CCI dipped to 35.4 in Sept 2026), which may provide the "cautionary" counterbalance to the Governor's hawkish inflation rhetoric.

3. Yen / FX Considerations
The Yen remains under pressure. While not explicitly mentioned in recent speeches, the Governor's hawkish pivot on inflation is likely a response to the need to support the currency and prevent imported inflation.

4. Financial Conditions & JGB Market
The BOJ is now explicitly linking technological shifts (AI boom) to financial conditions, suggesting that productivity gains from AI could raise the neutral rate, providing more room for policy normalization without choking growth.

5. Balance Sheet (JGB purchase taper)

6. Forward Guidance Evolution
