Executive Summary

The Bank of Japan (BOJ) maintained the policy rate at 1% at the July 31 MPM, but the accompanying communications signal a shift toward a more aggressive tightening bias. Most critically, the July Summary of Opinions reveals that the Board actively debated accelerating the pace of rate hikes. While Governor Ueda remains cautious, the internal consensus is shifting toward a potential September hike to combat currency-driven inflation, marking a transition from "data-dependent" to "risk-preemptive" positioning.
