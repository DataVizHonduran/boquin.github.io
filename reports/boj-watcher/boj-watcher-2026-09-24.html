Executive Summary

The Bank of Japan (BOJ) has entered a new phase of monetary normalization, raising the policy rate by 25 basis points to 1.25% on September 18, 2026—a 31-year high. This move reflects mounting pressure from persistent inflation and a shift toward a more aggressive hiking cycle. While Governor Ueda continues to manage the pace, the BOJ has flagged further hikes as policy enters this "new phase," with market participants now eyeing quarterly increases.
