Executive Summary

The past 30 days mark a significant pivot in the Bank of Japan's normalization trajectory. On September 17-18, the BOJ raised the benchmark interest rate to 1.25%, a 31-year high, shifting toward a "preemptive" inflation fight. While Governor Ueda maintains a cautious tone regarding the outlook, the Board is increasingly divided on the pace of further tightening. The hawkish wing, led by Takata and reinforced by Masu, is pushing for a "nimble" approach to cap inflation at 2%, while the dovish wing remains concerned about market stability and external pressures.
