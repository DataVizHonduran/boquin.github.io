Thematic Analysis

1. Inflation Assessment (CPI, Core, Wages)
Inflation remains the primary driver of policy. Household inflation expectations are rising (July 16), providing the BOJ with the necessary "social" cover to hike. While real wages have risen for five consecutive months, the pace is slowing (July 7), which may provide some ammunition for the dovish wing (Asada) to argue against aggressive hikes.

2. Growth Outlook
The outlook is improving. Reports suggest the BOJ may raise its 2026 growth forecast (July 12), reducing the fear that tightening will trigger a recession.

3. Yen / FX Considerations
The Yen showed resilience and held gains following the July 7 rate hike. However, the market remains sensitive to the pace of normalization, with some analysts suggesting a "high bar" for further hawkish repricing.

4. Financial Conditions & JGB Market
There is emerging concern regarding JGB volatility. Former policymakers suggest the BOJ may face pressure to increase bond-buying if yields spike too rapidly (July 16), indicating that while the policy rate is rising, the BOJ is not yet ready to let the market fully dictate long-term yields.

5. Balance Sheet (JGB purchase taper)
Tapering continues, but the potential for "emergency" bond-buying to stabilize yields suggests a pragmatic, non-linear approach to the balance sheet reduction.

6. Forward Guidance Evolution
Guidance has shifted from "cautionary" to "active normalization." The mention of moving the policy rate closer to 2% by hawkish members suggests the "neutral rate" is now the primary target, rather than just exiting NIRP.
