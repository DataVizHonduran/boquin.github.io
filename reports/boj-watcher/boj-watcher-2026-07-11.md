### Thematic Analysis

**1. Inflation Assessment (CPI, Core, Wages)**
The BOJ is increasingly concerned about an "inflation overshoot." While real wages rose for a fifth straight month (as of July 7), the pace is slowing, suggesting that while the wage-price spiral is present, it may be losing momentum.

**2. Growth Outlook**
Reports from July 10 suggest the BOJ may raise its growth forecasts in the upcoming outlook, which typically provides the "economic cover" necessary to justify further rate hikes.

**3. Yen / FX Considerations**
Yen volatility was a primary driver for the June hike to 1%. However, recent geopolitical tensions (US-Iran attacks) have put renewed upward pressure on the USD, potentially complicating the BOJ's timing for the next move.

**4. Financial Conditions & JGB Market**
The move to 1% is the most significant shift in decades. The Board is balancing the need to fight inflation with the risk of market instability, a point emphasized by Deputy Governor Uchida.

**5. Balance Sheet (JGB purchase taper)**
Analysis indicates the BOJ intends to complete its tapering process by 2027.

**6. Forward Guidance Evolution**
Guidance has shifted from "cautionary" to "active tightening." The BOJ now explicitly flags the risk of inflation overshooting the 2% target as a trigger for accelerated hikes.
