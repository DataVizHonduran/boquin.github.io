Executive Summary

The Bank of Japan has entered a new phase of monetary normalization, raising the benchmark interest rate by 25bps to 1.25% on September 18, 2026—a 31-year high. This move was widely expected and driven by persistent inflation risks and a pivot toward a more "preemptive" fight against rising prices. The decision follows a month of aggressive signaling from board hawks (Takata, Masu) and a shift in Governor Ueda's tone toward timely action. While the hike was executed, Governor Ueda provided mixed signals regarding the immediate next move, suggesting a data-dependent approach despite the acceleration in the pace of tightening.
