Thematic Analysis

1. Inflation Assessment (CPI, Core, Wages)
Inflation is the primary driver of current hawkishness. Tokyo core inflation reached a 16-month high, and services inflation hit a 36-year peak. This suggests that the "virtuous cycle" of wages and prices is manifesting in the services sector, providing the BOJ with the necessary justification to move away from accommodative levels.

2. Growth Outlook
Mixed. While inflation is strong, some analysis suggests the economy remains "fragile" amid a new cycle, providing the primary ammunition for the dovish wing (Asada) to argue for caution.

3. Yen / FX Considerations
Market sentiment suggests that while rate hikes are backed by inflation data, they may not be sufficient to "rescue the yen" significantly, indicating that the BOJ is prioritizing domestic price stability over FX intervention via rates.

4. Financial Conditions & JGB Market
The focus is shifting toward the "Money and Capital Market Transformation." The BOJ is monitoring whether a paradigm shift in market function will occur as rates rise.

5. Balance Sheet (JGB purchase taper)
The BOJ is aggressively accelerating the exit from equity holdings, as evidenced by the tripling of the ETF sales pace in late October. This complements the interest rate normalization path.

6. Forward Guidance Evolution
Guidance is shifting from "whether" to hike to "how fast." The emergence of "flexible" and "faster-paced" language from Tamura and Takata suggests the board is debating the slope of the policy path.
