Executive Summary

The Bank of Japan (BOJ) has significantly accelerated its normalization timeline, raising the benchmark interest rate to 1.25% on September 18, 2026—the highest level in 31 years. This move signals a pivot toward a "preemptive" fight against inflation. The decision was preceded by aggressive signaling from board member Hajime Takata, who urged a "nimble" approach. While Governor Ueda maintains a cautious tone regarding the outlook, the board's action suggests a shift in the internal balance of power toward the hawkish wing, driven by persistent inflation risks and external pressures.
