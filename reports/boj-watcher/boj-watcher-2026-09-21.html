Executive Summary

The Bank of Japan has entered a new phase of monetary normalization, raising the benchmark interest rate by 25bps to 1.25% on September 18, 2026—the highest level in 31 years. This move was preceded by a surge in hawkish rhetoric from External Member Hajime Takata and Deputy Governor Uchida, signaling a "nimble" approach to combat persistent inflation. While Governor Ueda maintains a cautious tone regarding the outlook, the Board has explicitly flagged concerns over rising prices. The decision reflects a shift toward more aggressive tightening to cap the price trend at 2%, though the BOJ continues to provide mixed signals regarding the immediate timing of the next hike.
