### Thematic Analysis

**1. Inflation Assessment (CPI, Core, Wages)**
The BOJ is increasingly concerned about an "inflation overshoot." While real wages rose for a fifth straight month (as of July 7), the pace is slowing, suggesting that while the wage-price spiral is active, it may be losing momentum.

**2. Growth Outlook**
The BOJ continues to monitor the transition to demand-driven inflation. The current hike to 1% suggests the Board believes the economy can sustain higher rates without stifling growth.

**3. Yen / FX Considerations**
Yen volatility was a primary driver for the June 16 hike. The BOJ is using the policy rate as a tool to ward off "war inflation" and currency-induced price shocks.

**4. Financial Conditions & JGB Market**
The move to 1% is the most significant shift in decades. The Board is balancing the need for normalization with the risk of market instability, though the "Accommodative Hike" era is transitioning toward a more standard tightening cycle.

**5. Balance Sheet (JGB purchase taper)**
The BOJ has explicitly signaled that the current tapering of JGB purchases will be completed by 2027.

**6. Forward Guidance Evolution**
Guidance has shifted from "cautionary" to "proactive." The Board is now openly discussing the "neutral rate" (estimated at 2% by Tamura) and the possibility of hikes occurring every few months.
