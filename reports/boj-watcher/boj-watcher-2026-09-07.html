Executive Summary

The past 30 days have seen a marked shift toward a hawkish consensus, with Governor Ueda and Deputy Governor Uchida both signaling that a September rate hike is on the table. Market reaction has been immediate, with the Yen strengthening toward 155 and benchmark JGB yields hitting a 30-year high of 3%, creating a complex environment where the BOJ must balance normalization with financial stability.
