BOJ Watcher Report: June 10, 2026

Executive Summary

The last 30 days have seen a decisive shift in Governor Ueda’s rhetoric toward an "inflation-fighting mode," signaling a higher probability of a rate hike in June. This pivot is driven by concerns that temporary energy shocks could become persistent and a belief that rates must rise to contain inflation. However, the policy landscape has been abruptly complicated by the news on June 10 that Governor Ueda has been hospitalized and will miss the upcoming June MPM. While the hawkish chorus has grown—led by external members Takata, Tamura, and the newly joined Masu—the absence of the Governor leaves Deputy Governors Uchida and Himino to steer the meeting, potentially introducing a more pragmatic or cautious tone to the final decision.

3. Yen / FX Considerations
The Yen remains a critical variable. The BOJ has signaled scope for more hikes to support the currency, and external pressure (e.g., U.S. Treasury Secretary Bessent) has surfaced supporting a June hike.

4. Financial Conditions & JGB Market

6. Forward Guidance Evolution
