Executive Summary

The past 30 days have highlighted a widening divergence within the BOJ Policy Board. A hawkish wing, led by Naoki Tamura, is explicitly pushing for a faster trajectory toward a neutral rate of approximately 2%, suggesting hikes every few months. Conversely, new member Toichiro Asada has signaled a dovish requirement for "demand-driven inflation" before supporting further tightening. Governor Ueda remains the central pivot, acknowledging the risk of inflation exceeding the 2% target while managing external political pressure for restraint from PM Takaichi. Market data, including rising household inflation expectations and a May core CPI of 2.7%, provides the fundamental backdrop for this internal debate.
