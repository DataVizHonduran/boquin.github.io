Executive Summary

The past 30 days indicate a strengthening hawkish tilt within the Bank of Japan, driven by inflation breaking 2% in October and internal debates regarding the pace of normalization. Governor Ueda has acknowledged "upside risks" to inflation, while Deputy Governor Himino has signaled readiness to raise borrowing costs. However, a stark divide remains: the October Summary of Opinions reveals active debate over the need for faster rate hikes, contrasted by new data showing a significant drop in household outlays. While the "hawks" (Takata, Tamura) continue to push for a faster path to the neutral rate, the Governor remains cautious about the sustainability of the 2% inflation target.
