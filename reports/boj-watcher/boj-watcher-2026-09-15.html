Inflation remains the primary catalyst for tightening. Tokyo Core CPI rose 1.9% in August, maintaining pressure on the Board. There is emerging concern among policymakers regarding "non-linear" inflation spikes, suggesting a fear that price increases could accelerate faster than the BOJ's current projections.

2. Growth Outlook
While specific growth data was limited in the coverage period, reports indicate Japan's "economic comeback" is facing a critical test as the BOJ balances normalization with economic stability.

3. Yen / FX Considerations
The Yen has rallied (hitting 155 to the USD) as markets price in a September hike. The rate differential remains a dominant theme, but the prospect of higher JGB yields is beginning to incentivize institutional funds to remain in domestic assets.

4. Financial Conditions & JGB Market
The market is currently pricing in a move to 1.25%. There is growing consensus that the "era of ultralow rates and deflation is over," though the bond market is expected to face "aftershocks" as rates rise.

6. Forward Guidance Evolution
Guidance is evolving from "cautionary" to "nimble." The focus has shifted toward "policy normalization" and the necessity of reaching a neutral rate faster to prevent a wage-price spiral.
