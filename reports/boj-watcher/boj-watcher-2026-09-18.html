Executive Summary

The Bank of Japan (BOJ) has significantly accelerated its normalization path, raising the policy interest rate by 25bps to 1.25% on September 18, 2026—the highest level in 31 years. This move signals a pivot toward a "preemptive" fight against inflation. The decision was preceded by a surge in hawkish rhetoric from external members (Takata, Masu) and a shift in tone from Deputy Governor Uchida, who moved from his usual pragmatic caution to emphasizing "timely" hikes. While the hike was widely expected by markets, Governor Ueda's post-decision communication remains mixed, providing cautious signals regarding the immediate pace of future increases.
