Thematic Analysis

1. Inflation Assessment (CPI, Core, Wages)
Inflation remains the primary driver for tightening. Tokyo Core CPI rose 1.9% in August, sustaining the case for hikes. Board members are now expressing vigilance regarding "non-linear" inflation spikes, suggesting a fear that inflation could accelerate faster than the current trend.

2. Growth Outlook
While regional speeches (Fukui, Sapporo, Saitama) indicate ongoing monitoring, the overarching narrative has shifted from "supporting growth" to "capping price trends" at 2%.

3. Yen / FX Considerations
The Yen rallied to a 1-month high of 155 against the USD in early September, directly correlating with Hajime Takata's calls for aggressive hikes. Market participants are viewing the narrowing rate differential as a key catalyst for Yen strength.

4. Financial Conditions & JGB Market
The market is pricing in a 25bps hike to 1.25%. There is growing concern regarding "aftershocks" for households and the bond market as the era of ultra-low rates definitively ends.

5. Balance Sheet (JGB purchase taper)
Not explicitly detailed in recent speeches, but the focus has shifted heavily toward the policy rate (short-end) rather than the taper (long-end).

6. Forward Guidance Evolution
Guidance is evolving from "cautionary normalization" to "nimble tightening." The phrase "turning point" used by Takata suggests the BOJ may move away from the slow, telegraphed approach toward a more data-responsive, faster pace.
