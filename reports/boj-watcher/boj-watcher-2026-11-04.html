Executive Summary

The Bank of Japan (BOJ) maintained interest rates at the October 30 MPM, but the meeting marked a pivotal shift in narrative. For the first time since 2013, the BOJ officially acknowledged that the underlying inflation trend has aligned with its 2% target. While rates remained steady, Governor Ueda signaled that further hikes remain on the table, provided economic forecasts are met. Notably, Deputy Governor Himino has signaled a readiness to raise borrowing costs, suggesting a broadening of the hawkish consensus. The primary tension remains between the "normalization" camp (Takata, Tamura, Masu) and the pragmatic/dovish wing (Uchida, Asada), with the BOJ now operating from a position of confirmed inflation target achievement.
