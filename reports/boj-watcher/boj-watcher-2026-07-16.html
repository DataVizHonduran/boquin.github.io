Executive Summary

The past 30 days have been characterized by a widening divergence within the Policy Board. While the BOJ maintained its tightening guidance and signaled a readiness to accelerate hikes if inflation risks grow, a clear "hawk-dove" split has emerged. Naoki Tamura has emerged as a primary driver of hawkish sentiment, explicitly targeting a neutral rate of ~2% and advocating for hikes every few months. Conversely, new member Toichiro Asada has established a dovish anchor, requiring demand-driven inflation before supporting further hikes. Despite this internal friction, the overall policy trajectory remains upward, supported by rising household inflation expectations and a general consensus that inflation overshoot risks are mounting.
