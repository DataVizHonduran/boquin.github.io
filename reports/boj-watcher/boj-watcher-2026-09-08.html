Executive Summary

The past 30 days have seen a marked shift toward a more aggressive normalization narrative. Governor Ueda and Deputy Governor Uchida have both signaled that a September rate hike remains on the table, coinciding with July core inflation accelerating to 2.0%. While the Board remains cautious of market stability, the convergence of rising inflation data and explicit signals from the Governor and Deputy Governor suggests a high probability of a rate increase at the next MPM.
