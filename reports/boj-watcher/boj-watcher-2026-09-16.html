Executive Summary

The past 30 days have seen a decisive shift toward a more aggressive normalization path. Thematic Analysis

1. Inflation Assessment (CPI, Core, Wages)
Inflation remains the primary catalyst for tightening. Tokyo Core CPI rose 1.9% in August, sustaining the case for hikes. Member Masu explicitly linked the need for continued rate increases to the goal of capping the price trend at 2%.

2. Growth Outlook
The "era of ultralow rates and deflation" is widely viewed as over. While growth is continuing, the focus has shifted toward managing the "hardest BOJ test in a generation" regarding the transition to a higher-rate environment.

3. Yen / FX Considerations
The Yen has reacted positively to hawkish signals, hitting a 1-month high of 155 to the dollar on September 3rd. However, analysts note that rate differentials remain a dominant factor despite interventions.

4. Financial Conditions & JGB Market
Higher yields are expected to keep more institutional funds within Japan. There are concerns regarding "aftershocks" for households and the bond market as the BOJ tightens.

5. Balance Sheet (JGB purchase taper)
No specific new updates on the taper schedule were provided in the last 30 days of data, though the general trend of normalization continues.

6. Forward Guidance Evolution
Guidance is shifting from "cautionary" to "nimble." The market is now pricing in a 1.25% rate for September, with discussions moving toward the terminal rate (Goldman Sachs projecting 1.75%).
