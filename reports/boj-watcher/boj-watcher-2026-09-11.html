Executive Summary

The past 30 days have seen a marked shift toward a more aggressive normalization bias within the Bank of Japan (BOJ). Governor Ueda and Deputy Governor Uchida have both signaled that a September rate hike remains on the table, supported by July core inflation accelerating to 2.0% and elevated August wholesale prices. The hawkish wing, led by Hajime Takata and now explicitly joined by Kazuyuki Masu, is pushing for "nimble" and potentially "rapid" rate hikes to cap inflation at 2% and address yen volatility. Market expectations have shifted toward a September hike to 1.25%, with the Yen reacting positively, hitting a one-month high of 155 per dollar. The consensus is moving from "cautionary" to "proactive" as the Board weighs price risks against financial stability.
