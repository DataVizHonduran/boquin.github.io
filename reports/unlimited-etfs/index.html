The message is that BOJ normalization rhetoric is being faded; these funds believe yen weakness continues. The commodity book is being unwound, consistent with reduced conviction in the fiscal-expansion and Chinese demand recovery narratives. US equity exposure was also pared — S&P 500 down 7+ points in both HFGM and HFMF — though neither fund moved to underweight. HFMF, now the most aggressively short fund by gross exposure (79.5%), is almost entirely driven by the sterling bet; its underlying longs in EUR, S&P 500, gold, and Nikkei are all being reduced. HFND is the most defensive — nearly 50% in money market — with active directional bets in HY credit, EM equities, and a modest short duration tilt. HFEQ is unchanged in character: 153.5% net long, EM-heavy, with a new VIX long (5.6%) that hints at a modest tail hedge against the equity exposure.
