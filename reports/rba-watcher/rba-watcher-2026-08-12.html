Executive Summary

The RBA Board held the cash rate steady at 4.35% on August 11, 2026. Despite the hold, Governor Michele Bullock has shifted the communication tone toward a more hawkish posture, explicitly stating that further rate hikes are "quite possible" if inflation remains elevated. This represents a significant pivot from the early 2025 easing cycle. The primary driver is a persistent concern over inflation pressures, despite some recent data undershooting forecasts. The Board remains focused on the sustainable return of trimmed mean CPI to the 2-3% target band, with the Governor emphasizing that the path forward is entirely data-dependent and open to both directions.
