Executive Summary

The RBA has undergone a sharp hawkish pivot over the last 30 days. Governor Michele Bullock and Deputy Governor Andrew Hauser have both issued explicit warnings that inflation remains too high and that the labor market is too "hot." Governor Bullock has signaled that the Board views risks to the outlook as skewed to the upside and has explicitly suggested a September rate hike may be necessary to take "heat" out of the job market. This marks a significant departure from the early 2025 easing cycle, with the RBA now prioritizing the fight against sticky inflation over employment stability, even suggesting that unemployment rising to 4.5%–5.0% would be a desirable outcome to ease price pressures.
