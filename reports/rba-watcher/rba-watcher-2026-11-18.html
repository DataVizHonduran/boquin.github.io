Executive Summary

The RBA has pivoted back to a restrictive stance, delivering its fifth rate hike of 2026 on November 3 to bring the cash rate to 4.85%. This decision followed a "materially worse" inflation outlook and September CPI data (4.3% YoY; Trimmed Mean 3.7%) that exceeded forecasts. The November 3 minutes reveal a significant internal divide, with a split 6-3 vote in favor of the hike. Governor Bullock emphasized that "pain now" is necessary to prevent worse outcomes later, while a minority of the board warned of a "materially sharper downturn" if tightening continued. Despite the hike, market sentiment is shifting toward a pause as the board seeks "policy assurance" and manages a decline in public trust regarding inflation management.
