Executive Summary

The RBA has shifted to a decisively hawkish posture over the last 30 days. Following a rate hike to 4.60% in September, the Board's tone has intensified. Deputy Governor Andrew Hauser has explicitly warned that the inflation outlook is "materially worse," while the latest CPI print (October 28) showed inflation rising to 4.3% (Trimmed Mean at 3.7%), exceeding market expectations. This data, combined with internal concerns from Chris Kent regarding whether policy is sufficiently restrictive and a rising estimate of the neutral rate, has significantly increased the probability of a fifth rate hike in November. The Board is now focused on preventing a wage-price spiral and ensuring inflation returns to the 2-3% target sustainably.
