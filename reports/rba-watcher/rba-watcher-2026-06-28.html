Executive Summary

The RBA maintained the cash rate at the June 16 meeting, marking a critical pause following a sequence of three consecutive increases. Governor Bullock and the Board have signaled that the "pause" should not be interpreted as a pivot; rather, further rate hikes remain "on the cards" if underlying inflation does not return to the 2-3% target sustainably. The prevailing sentiment has shifted from the early 2025 easing cycle back toward a restrictive bias, with a heavy emphasis on data-dependency and the risks of premature easing.
