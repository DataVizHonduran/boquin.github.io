Executive Summary

The past 30 days mark a dramatic pivot in RBA policy. On September 29, the Board raised the cash rate to 4.60%, the highest level in 15 years, reversing the easing cycle that began in February 2025. This decision was driven by stubbornly high inflation (August data) and a labor market that Governor Bullock described as needing "heat" taken out of it. While the Governor noted no evidence of a "wage-price spiral," the Board has explicitly stated that further hikes are "not off the table." The shift is characterized by a return to a restrictive stance to ensure inflation returns to the 2-3% target band sustainably.
