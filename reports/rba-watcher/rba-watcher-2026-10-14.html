Executive Summary

The RBA has pivoted sharply back to a tightening cycle, raising the cash rate to 4.60% on September 29, 2026. This marks the fourth increase of the year, reversing the easing trend seen in early 2025. Governor Michele Bullock has adopted a decisively hawkish tone, citing "stubbornly high" inflation and the need to take "heat" out of the labor market. The Board has explicitly left the door open for further hikes, with the Governor suggesting that unemployment may need to rise to 4.5%–5.0% to ensure inflation returns to the 2-3% target. The primary driver is upside price risk and a perceived need for a higher neutral rate.
