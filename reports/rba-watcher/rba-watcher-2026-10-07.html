Executive Summary

The past 30 days mark a dramatic pivot in RBA policy. On September 29, the Board raised the cash rate to 4.60%, the fourth increase of 2026 and a 15-year high. This reverses the easing cycle that began in February 2025. The move was driven by stubbornly high August inflation and a perceived need to take "heat" out of the labor market. Governor Bullock and Deputy Governor Hauser have both signaled that further hikes remain "on the table," shifting the Board's collective stance from data-dependent neutrality to an aggressively hawkish posture to combat persistent inflationary pressures.
