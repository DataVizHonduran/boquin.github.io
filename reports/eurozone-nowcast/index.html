Eurozone GDP Nowcaster

Real-time estimate of Euro Area GDP growth (YoY%) — updated monthly as new data arrives

How the nowcast works

The Euro Area nowcaster tracks Eurostat’s quarterly GDP growth (chain-linked volumes, YoY%) — the flash estimate is published ~30 days after each quarter closes. This model bridges the lag using four OLS bridge equations fitted on quarterly data since 2006:

  • Activity bridge — S&P Global Euro Area Manufacturing PMI and Composite PMI (3-month moving averages), capturing real-time business-cycle momentum across manufacturing and services.
  • External bridge — US Industrial Production YoY and Brent crude oil YoY, reflecting global demand and the Eurozone’s sensitivity to energy prices.
  • Financial bridge — EUR/USD YoY and Euro Stoxx 50 YoY, incorporating real-time financial-market signals.
  • Industrial bridge — Eurozone Industrial Production Index YoY (Eurostat, seasonally adjusted), directly tracking the manufacturing and construction output channel.

Each bridge is validated with an expanding-window out-of-sample test (train from 2006, evaluate 2010–2025, excluding COVID quarters). Bridges are combined using inverse-RMSE ensemble weights. The current-quarter estimate (red star, plotted at today’s date) uses only months of data available so far — it updates each month as new data arrives. The 90% confidence interval comes from 1,000 residual bootstrap iterations.

Known model blind spots: (1) Energy price shocks (2022 Russia-Ukraine gas crisis) can break the historical relationship in the External bridge — the model will underpredict severity. (2) The sovereign debt crisis (2011-2013) introduced structural breaks in peripheral Euro Area countries not fully captured at the aggregate level. (3) OLS bridges cannot predict idiosyncratic country-level fiscal shocks (e.g., German industrial policy, Italian fiscal stress). COVID quarters (2020 Q2 – 2021 Q2) are excluded from estimation and validation.
