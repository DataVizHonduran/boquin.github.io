## Macro Strategy Commentary: Week of 2026-05-25

### 1. Dominant Macro Themes
The primary focus this week is the **AI Infrastructure Supercycle**, shifting from software "vibes" to hard physical assets (Gigawatt datacenters, orbital compute, and specialized accelerators). Parallel to this is a deep dive into **Credit Market Fragility**, specifically the "handoff" of downgraded assets and the opaque nature of Private Credit marks.

| Theme | Newsletters Flagging It | Consensus View |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AI Infrastructure** | SemiAnalysis, Newcomer, NotBoring, MacroCredit | Massive capex shift toward "physical" AI (power/chips/space). |
| **Credit/Private Debt** | JunkBondInvestor, DebtSerious, MacroCredit | High risk in "stranded" assets; narrative-driven pricing. |
| **Labor Market** | MacroMostly, EPB Research | Divergence: National data looks warm, but specific sectors are recessed. |

### 2. Divergences
*   **Labor Market Health:** *MacroMostly* views the labor market as "warming" and "upbeat," while *EPB Research* argues a "quiet" recession is already underway in specific, high-impact slices of the workforce.
*   **AI Valuation:** *Newcomer* and *NotBoring* are bullish on the "AI Supremacy" race and upcoming IPOs (SpaceX, OpenAI), whereas *MacroCreditThinking* warns of "Generative Financing" inflating a bubble.
*   **Gold/Debasement:** *Robin Brooks* suggests the "debasement trade" is in trouble following gold's price drop, contrasting with the general "risk-on" sentiment in AI.

### 3. Most Actionable Signals
*   **The "Handoff" Trade:** Watch the transition of downgraded credits; the event isn't the downgrade, but who buys the debt next (*JunkBondInvestor*).
*   **AI Hardware Inflection:** Focus on the "prefill phase" optimization (Nvidia Rubin CPX) and HBM bottlenecks as the new primary constraints for AI scaling (*SemiAnalysis*).
*   **Housing Divergence:** Look for regional disparities in US home prices; national indices are masking a real recession in half the country (*EPB Research*).

### 4. Under-covered Risks
*   **Geopolitical Energy Shifts:** The UAE-Saudi rift and its potential to embolden Iran regarding the closure of the Strait of Hormuz (*SovereignVibe*).
*   **The "Productivity Paradox":** The risk that AI gains are not translating into macro productivity, mirroring the "Model T" era where tech existed but efficiency lagged (*Technically*).
*   **Fed Independence:** Growing political attacks on the Fed following perceived policy errors (*ThematicMarkets*).

### 5. One-line Verdict
**Mixed:** Bullish on AI infrastructure and quant-trading alpha, but cautious on credit quality and sovereign stability.
