## Macro Strategy Review: Week of 2026-06-15

### 1. Dominant Macro Themes
The primary focus this week is the **convergence of AI infrastructure and capital markets**. We are seeing a shift from "AI software hype" to "AI physical reality," characterized by massive capital raises (SpaceX, OpenAI, Anthropic) and the emergence of new asset classes, specifically **GPU-backed lending** by giants like Apollo and Blackstone. Simultaneously, there is a strong focus on the **"Debasement Trade,"** with analysts debating whether the structural decline of the USD is a permanent tailwind for Emerging Markets or a trade that has peaked.

| Theme | Newsletters flagging it | Consensus view |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AI CapEx/Infrastructure** | Newcomer, DebtSerious, SemiAnalysis, Rupak Ghose | Massive scale-up; shift toward GPU-collateralized debt. |
| **The Debasement Trade** | SovereignVibe, Robin J Brooks, Thematic Markets | Bullish for EM, but questioning if the peak is in. |
| **Labor Market Resilience** | MacroMostly | US jobs remain surprisingly strong despite headwinds. |
| **Geopolitical Inflation** | EPB Research, Thematic Markets | Iran/China tensions are actively derailing housing/stability. |

### 2. Divergences
*   **The Debasement Trade:** *SovereignVibe* views structural forces as a long-term tailwind for EM, while *Robin J Brooks* questions if the trade is over, suggesting it was too dependent on Fed cut expectations.
*   **AI Scaling:** *SemiAnalysis* highlights the physical breakthroughs (Rubin CPX, Gigawatt datacenters), whereas *MacroCreditThinking* warns that the "race to build" may fail if LLM scaling laws hit a ceiling.
*   **US Economy:** *MacroMostly* reports a healthy labor market, but *EPB Research* highlights a stark divergence: record corporate margins paired with falling real wages for workers.

### 3. Most Actionable Signals
*   **Specific Trade:** Long **MSGS** (Madison Square Garden Sports) flagged as a "glaring discount" (*OpenInsights*).
*   **Credit Opportunity:** **Bath & Body Works ($BBWI)** long bonds (7.7% YTW) identified as potentially undervalued given FCF and leverage (*JunkBondInvestor*).
*   **Inflection Point:** The **SpaceX IPO** is flagged as a major catalyst for the broader tech ecosystem, potentially impacting S&P/Nasdaq valuations (*Rupak Ghose, Newcomer*).
*   **Data Focus:** Watch for continued "normalization" of Bitcoin valuations as it moves from a "belief" asset to a priced asset (*Thematic Markets*).

### 4. Under-covered Risks
*   **The "Ghost City" Contagion:** *ChinaTalk* highlights the $58bn Chinese-financed empty capital in Egypt, suggesting a hidden layer of sovereign risk in Belt and Road investments.
*   **Housing Bifurcation:** *EPB Research* notes that national indices are hiding a "real housing recession" where half the country is underwater, creating a localized systemic risk.
*   **Regulatory Capture:** *Newcomer* mentions the risk of AI labs (Anthropic) using regulatory pleas to stifle competition.

**Verdict: Mixed.** (Bullish on AI infrastructure and specific credit plays; Bearish/Cautious on US housing and geopolitical inflation).
