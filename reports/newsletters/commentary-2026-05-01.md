## Dominant Macro Themes
*   **The Iran-Oil Nexus:** The primary focus is the "Sanguine Strait Stoppage." Despite the largest oil supply shock in history and the closure of Hormuz, crude prices have remained unexpectedly low, suggesting a decoupling of physical scarcity and market pricing.
*   **AI Maturity & Friction:** The narrative is shifting from "AGI" to specific applications (Voice AI) and operational failures ("AI Dementia" or context rot). Legal warfare (Musk vs. OpenAI) is now a primary headwind for the sector.
*   **Industrial Reshoring:** A strong push toward "China-proofing" the US economy, focusing on the Quantum industrial base and the "Scarcity Supercycle."

## Divergences
*   **Compute Scarcity:** While the broader market fears a compute shortage, Bill Gurley (via Newcomer) explicitly questions the validity of this shortage.
*   **Labor Market Interpretation:** WeightyThoughts argues that US jobs numbers are being misread; the "slowdown" is likely a function of shrinking immigration rather than AI-driven displacement.

## Most Actionable Signals
*   **Sector Call:** **Voice AI.** High traction in enterprise applications (Deepgram, Wispr, Abridge) suggests a pivot point for investment.
*   **Inflection Point:** Watch **CENTCOM's** requests for Dark Eagle hypersonics; this is a leading indicator that the US-Iran ceasefire may be failing.
*   **Trade Signal:** Monitor the SpaceX IPO stakes; it remains a bellwether for high-capex "frontier" tech valuations.

## Under-covered Risks
*   **Context Rot:** The phenomenon of AI agents becoming "dumber" during long interactions (AI Dementia) could significantly limit the ROI of AI agents in complex enterprise workflows.
*   **India VC Liquidity:** A shift in the Indian VC landscape toward returning cash faster suggests a tightening of domestic liquidity or a change in exit expectations.

**One-line verdict:** **Mixed** — Bullish on Voice AI and strategic industrialization, but high-alert on geopolitical volatility and energy fragility.
