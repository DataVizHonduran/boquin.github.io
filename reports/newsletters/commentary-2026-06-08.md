## Macro Strategy Synthesis: Week of 2026-06-08

### 1. Dominant Macro Themes
The overarching narrative this week is the **collision of geopolitical volatility (Iran War) and stubborn inflation**, which has fundamentally shifted expectations for central bank policy. Markets are transitioning from anticipating an easing cycle to pricing in potential hikes. Simultaneously, a massive **AI capital expenditure super-cycle** continues to drive unprecedented funding rounds and infrastructure builds (Gigawatt datacenters), creating a stark divergence between "AI-driven growth" and "macro-driven instability."

### 2. Divergences
*   **The "Bad News" Paradox:** *Global Macro Method* argues that "bad news isn't good news" because inflation prevents the Fed from cutting during downturns. Conversely, *MacroMostly* highlights improving high-frequency labor data, suggesting the economy may be resilient enough to withstand higher rates.
*   **AI Scaling:** While *SemiAnalysis* and *Newcomer* focus on the physical expansion (Rubin CPX, Colossus 2), *MacroCreditThinking* questions if LLMs can actually scale linearly with more GPUs, hinting at a potential productivity plateau.
*   **Housing Outlook:** *EPB Research* sees a "quietly underway" recession fueled by the Iran war, while other macro views focus more on the broader inflationary pressure than specific sector collapses.
