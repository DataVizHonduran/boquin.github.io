## Macro Strategy Synthesis: Week of 2026-06-22

### 1. Dominant Macro Themes
The primary focus this week is the **"Warsh Regime"** at the Fed. Analysts are dissecting Chairman Warsh’s policy architecture, debating whether his "hawk" reputation translates to active hikes or a preference for passive market tightening. Parallel to this, the **AI Infrastructure Build-out** remains a massive theme, shifting from pure compute (GPUs) to the physical constraints of power, data centers, and the emerging "GPU-backed loan" credit market.

### 2. Divergences
*   **Fed Policy:** *Thematic Markets* warns that markets are underpricing potential hikes under Warsh, while *Robin Brooks* suggests Warsh will avoid active hikes, preferring the market to tighten organically.
*   **AI Scaling:** *SemiAnalysis* focuses on the massive scale of new gigawatt datacenters, whereas *MacroCreditThinking* (earlier) questioned if LLM scaling laws are hitting a ceiling.
*   **Labor Market:** *MacroMostly* reports a "mixed bag" of high-frequency data, while *EPB Research* highlights a stark divergence between record corporate margins and falling real wages.

### 3. Most Actionable Signals
*   **Credit Opportunity:** Zero-coupon notes for $ODD (Oddity Tech) trading at 69 are flagged as a potential value play vs. the equity's collapse.
*   **Sector Rotation:** Increased interest in **Open Source AI** startups as costs for proprietary models soar.
*   **Trade Idea:** "Con-Vex-Ity" flagged by *Global Macro Method* as the "Next Trade" following the FOMC outcome.
*   **Data Point:** Watch the "residual value" of lab-based real estate; if tenants fail to earn their cost of capital, the underlying bonds are at risk.

### 4. Under-covered Risks
*   **The "Shadow Fleet":** *Robin Brooks* highlights the surge in Russian shadow fleet activity in the Baltic as a critical security gap requiring a blockade.
*   **Grid Bottlenecks:** *ChinaTalk* points out that while the world focuses on GPUs, the real bottleneck is the lack of transformers, converters, and inverters for power transmission.
*   **The "Debasement Trade":** *SovereignVibe* suggests a structural tailwind for Emerging Markets as the dollar faces long-term debasement pressures.

**One-line verdict: Mixed; cautious on Fed policy and geopolitical spikes, but bullish on AI infrastructure "plumbing."**
