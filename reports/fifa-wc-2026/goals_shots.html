• Goals/90
    — goals scored, normalized to a 90-minute match (total goals ÷ minutes played × 90).
  • Shots/90
    — shots taken, normalized to a 90-minute match.
  • Bubble size
    — minutes played in the tournament so far — bigger bubble, more minutes.
  • Dotted lines
    — median Goals/90 and median Shots/90 across all qualifying players, splitting the chart into four quadrants.
  • Min. 1 goal filter
    — only players who have scored at least once are shown.
  • Color
    — confederation of the player's national team.
