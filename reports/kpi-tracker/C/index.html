Why these KPIs

Every Citigroup earnings release presents the identical 'Financial Results' summary table -- Total Revenues, Net Income, Diluted EPS, RoTCE, Efficiency Ratio and CET1 Capital Ratio -- as its standard quarterly disclosure, the metrics management and bank analysts use every quarter to track profitability, cost discipline and capital adequacy. Only 6 quarters of history are available: Citigroup files thousands of routine 8-Ks per year for structured note offerings, so EDGAR's 'recent filings' window for this issuer only reaches back to September 2025; two additional quarters (Q1-Q2 2025) were recovered from an older EDGAR submissions page, but Efficiency Ratio was not included in the summary table of those two releases.
