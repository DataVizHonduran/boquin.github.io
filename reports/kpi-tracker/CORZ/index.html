Why these KPIs

Core Scientific reports Total Revenue, Gross Profit and Adjusted EBITDA every quarter, and has consistently broken out its data-center-services revenue line item (labeled 'HPC hosting revenue' through 2024, renamed 'Colocation revenue' in 2025, now 'HDC revenue') as the company pivoted from bitcoin mining to AI high-density colocation. Capital Expenditures and Liquidity became standard bullets starting Q1 2025 (Liquidity) and Q2 2025 (CapEx) as the company began quantifying its colocation buildout; null for the two quarters before each was first disclosed.
