Why these KPIs

EQT's earnings releases headline total sales volume, capital expenditures, realized pricing and per-unit operating costs as top bullets every quarter, while the financial performance table centers on Adjusted EBITDA and Free Cash Flow attributable to EQT as its core non-GAAP profitability/cash measures. The attributable-to-EQT breakout for EBITDA/FCF began appearing once EQT's midstream JV noncontrolling interest structure was established (not yet disclosed as of Q3 2024, partially in Q4 2024).
