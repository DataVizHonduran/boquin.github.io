Why these KPIs

Every NVIDIA release headlines Total Revenue and Data Center Revenue in its opening bullets, with GAAP-to-non-GAAP gross margin and EPS reconciliation tables every quarter. NVIDIA retired its Gaming/Professional Visualization/Automotive segment breakout starting Q1 FY2027, replacing it with a consolidated 'Edge Computing' segment -- both are tracked here (mutually exclusive by period, null before/after the switch) since each was the disclosed non-datacenter metric of its era. Note: beginning Q1 FY2027 NVIDIA's non-GAAP figures no longer exclude stock-based compensation, so non-GAAP EPS/margin before vs. after Q1 FY2027 aren't computed on an identical basis.
