Why these KPIs

Every Conduent earnings release presents the identical 'Key Financial Results' table -- Revenue, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Diluted EPS and Adjusted Free Cash Flow -- plus a 'New Business Signings ACV' headline bullet every quarter, the metrics management uses to track its ongoing transformation and portfolio-rationalization strategy. Note: starting Q2 2026, Conduent's announced divestitures of its Transit and Tolling businesses moved those segments to discontinued operations, so Q2 2026 Revenue (and related metrics) is reported on a narrower continuing-operations basis than prior quarters, which mechanically explains the sequential revenue decline.
