The company&rsquo;s adjusted earnings per share for the first quarter were $1.13, compared to $1.02 per share for 2025. It also owns and operates independent power generation businesses. # # # CMS Energy will hold a webcast to discuss its 2026 first quarter results and provide a business and financial outlook on Tuesday, April 28 at 10:00 a.m. (EDT).
