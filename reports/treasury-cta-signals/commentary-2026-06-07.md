## CTA Treasury Positioning Analysis: 2026-06-07

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs are **uniformly short duration** across the curve (+). Crowding is most acute in the belly and front end, with the **5Y (+29.7)** and **2Y (+28.3)** seeing the highest positioning. This signals a high-conviction bet on rising yields, specifically targeting the "higher-for-longer" narrative rather than a long-end term premium play.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
The curve is steep (10Y-2Y at +42bps; 30Y-2Y at +92bps). CTA positioning aligns with this steepening bias; by being more aggressively short the 2Y/5Y than the 30Y (+17.3), CTAs are effectively betting on a bear-steepening or a shift in the front-end anchor.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
There is no directional divergence, but a significant **magnitude divergence**. 
*   **Fast Mode** is aggressively pushing shorts (Avg ~25).
*   **Slow Mode** is more conservative (Avg ~18).
This indicates a recent acceleration in the bear trend. The trend is currently "fast-led," suggesting momentum is increasing, but the gap between modes creates a vulnerability to sharp mean-reversion if a catalyst shifts.

### 4. Recent Exhaustion Signals
High-conviction signals in Fast Mode (41) are more than double those in Slow Mode (18). This "front-loading" of conviction in the fast window often precedes a local top in yields. While the trend remains intact, the disparity suggests the trade is becoming "crowded-fast," increasing the risk of a liquidity-driven squeeze if yields plateau.
