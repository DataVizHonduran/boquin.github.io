* **Concentration:** Crowding is most acute in the 5Y (+31.6 Fast) and 2Y (+29.1 Fast), signaling a strong trend-following bet on **rising yields**, particularly in the belly of the curve.
* **Signal:** The positive positioning suggests a momentum-driven push for higher yields.

* **Curve Shape:** The curve is steep (10Y-2Y: +46bps; 30Y-2Y: +99bps).
* **Positioning Alignment:** CTAs are most short-duration in the 2Y/5Y and least short-duration in the 30Y. This indicates a "bear-flattening" trend preference, where CTAs are aggressively betting against the front end relative to the long end.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences

* **Implication:** The trend is accelerating. The widening gap between fast and slow signals suggests a high-velocity momentum phase, but increases the risk of a "snap-back" if yields plateau.

* **High-Conviction Density:** 35/119 signals in Fast mode are high-conviction (score $\ge$ 60). 
* **Exhaustion Risk:** With Fast mode positioning pushing toward the +30 range in the 5Y, the trade is becoming crowded. Any catalyst for falling yields could trigger a rapid unwind of these short-duration positions.

### 5. Actionable Watch-list
* **Trigger:** A break below the 20-day moving average in 5Y yields.
* **Action:** Monitor for a "short-squeeze" in Treasury futures as Fast-mode CTAs are forced to flip from short-duration (+) to long-duration (-) rapidly.
