## CTA Positioning Analysis: Treasury Markets (2026-11-16)

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs remain structurally **Short Duration (+)** across the curve. Crowding is most acute in the **10Y (+58.8)** and **5Y (+53.7)** tenors within the Slow Mode. This indicates a systemic bet on rising yields, with the belly of the curve serving as the primary vehicle for the trend-following short.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
The curve is positively sloped (10Y-2Y: +24bps; 30Y-2Y: +57bps). The concentration of short positions in the 10Y/5Y relative to the 2Y suggests CTAs are betting on a "bear steepening" or a shift in the term premium, rather than a pure front-end policy rate hike play.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
A critical **bullish divergence** is emerging. 
*   **Slow Mode:** Remains heavily Short (+45 to +58).
*   **Fast Mode:** Positioning has collapsed significantly (+23 to +24), with recent high-conviction "Long" signals triggered across the 2Y, 5Y, and 30Y between Nov 3rd and Nov 10th.
*   **Implication:** Trend conviction for the bear move is evaporating. Fast-mode CTAs are already pivoting to long duration, while slow-mode CTAs are still "trapped" in shorts.

### 4. Exhaustion Signals
High-conviction exhaustion is evident. The Fast Mode signals for the 5Y (strength 71.3) and 2Y (strength 65.5) indicate a violent reversal in short-term momentum. With the 10Y signal strength dropping to 47.2, the "short" trade is no longer being defended by fast-money flows, signaling a potential **short-squeeze** as slow-mode CTAs are forced to cover.
