## Treasury CTA Positioning Analysis: 2026-06-22

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs are **universally short duration** across the curve. Crowding is most acute in the front-to-belly tenors:
*   **Primary Crowding:** 2Y (+29.2) and 5Y (+28.9) in Fast Mode.
*   **Signal:** This indicates a high-conviction bet on "higher-for-longer" or a hawkish policy shift. The long end (30Y: +11.4) is significantly less crowded, suggesting CTAs are less convinced of a structural term-premium surge compared to a front-end rate hike cycle.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
The curve is steep (10Y-2Y: +29bps; 30Y-2Y: +73bps). CTA positioning reinforces this steepening bias; by aggressively shorting the 2Y/5Y while remaining relatively light on the 30Y, trend-followers are effectively betting on a "bear steepener" or a regime where short-term rates remain elevated relative to long-term growth expectations.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
There is **strong alignment** between Fast (20/50/100) and Slow (50/100/200) modes. 
*   **Conviction:** Both modes are positive (+), meaning there is no trend conflict.
*   **Acceleration:** Fast mode is more aggressive than Slow mode across all tenors. This implies the trend is not just established but is currently accelerating, increasing the risk of a "blow-off top" in yields.
