## CTA Positioning Analysis: Treasury Markets (2026-07-13)

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs are currently **net short duration across the entire curve (+)**. Crowding is most acute in the **front-end (2Y/5Y)**, where positions are deepest (+23.6 to +27.0). This signals a strong conviction in a "higher-for-longer" regime or an expectation of continued upward pressure on short-term rates. The long end (30Y) is the least crowded, suggesting limited conviction in a steepening trade.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
With a positive 10Y-2Y (+38bps) and 30Y-2Y (+89bps) spread, the curve is no longer inverted. CTA positioning is aligned with this **bull-steepening/bear-steepening transition**, but the heavy shorting of the 2Y relative to the 30Y indicates that trend-followers are betting on the front-end remaining anchored high or rising faster than the long end.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
There is **high alignment** between Fast and Slow modes across all tenors. 
* **2Y/5Y:** Both modes are aggressively short.
* **30Y:** Slow mode (+10.5) is slightly more aggressive than Fast mode (+7.7).
This synchronicity implies **high trend conviction**. There are no significant "divergence warnings" that would suggest a trend reversal is imminent; the momentum is structurally entrenched.

### 4. Exhaustion Signals
Despite the strong trend, **high-conviction signals are waning**. 
* Fast Mode high-conviction signals dropped to 40.
* Slow Mode high-conviction signals are low at 18.
While the direction remains positive (short duration), the shrinking number of high-conviction signals suggests the trade is reaching a **plateau**. We are seeing "trend persistence" rather than "trend acceleration."

### 5. Actionable Watch-list
**Watch: 2Y Yield Breakout/Mean Reversion.**
Given the extreme crowding in the 2Y (+27.0), any sudden dip in 2Y yields could trigger a rapid "short-covering" cascade. Monitor for a break below the 20-day moving average as a trigger for a fast-mode unwind.
