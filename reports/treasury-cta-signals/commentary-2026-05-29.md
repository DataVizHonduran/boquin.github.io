## CTA Positioning Analysis: 2026-05-29

### 1. Duration Crowding
* **Direction:** CTAs are universally **short duration** across all tenors (all positions positive).
* **Crowding:** The 5Y (+32.3 Fast / +19.7 Slow) is the most crowded short-duration bet, followed closely by the 2Y.
* **Signal:** Strong trend-following conviction for **rising yields**. The concentration in the belly (5Y) suggests a bet on sustained upward pressure in medium-term rates.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
* **Curve State:** The curve is steep (10Y-2Y: +0.48bps; 30Y-2Y: +1.01bps).
* **Positioning Alignment:** CTAs are most aggressively short duration in the 2Y/5Y and least aggressive in the 30Y. This indicates a "bear-flattening" bias—betting that short-to-medium yields will rise faster than long-end yields.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
* **Conviction Gap:** Fast mode (+23 to +32) is significantly more aggressive than Slow mode (+14 to +19).
* **Implication:** The short-duration trend is accelerating. However, the lower conviction in Slow mode suggests the long-term trend is not yet fully locked in, leaving room for a sharp reversal if yields plateau.

### 4. Exhaustion Signals
* **High-Conviction Clusters:** 35 signals in Fast mode are $\ge 60$, compared to only 12 in Slow mode.
* **Risk:** The extreme positivity in Fast mode (especially 5Y/2Y) suggests the trade is becoming "over-crowded." We are entering a zone where any catalyst for falling yields could trigger a violent short-covering rally.

### 5. Actionable Watch-list
* **Trigger:** Watch for a break below the 20-day moving average in 5Y yields.
* **Action:** If 5Y yields drop, expect a rapid "unwinding" of the +32.3 Fast position, leading to a sharp, non-fundamental rally in the belly of the curve.
