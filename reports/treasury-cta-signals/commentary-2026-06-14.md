## CTA Positioning Analysis: Treasury Markets (2026-06-14)

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs are **uniformly short duration** across the curve. Crowding is most acute in the **belly (5Y: +30.9 / 2Y: +29.8)**, signaling a high-conviction bet on "higher for longer" or a delayed pivot. The 10Y and 30Y are less crowded but still firmly positive, suggesting a broad-based bearish trend rather than a specific curve-play.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
With the 10Y-2Y at +40bps and 30Y-2Y at +90bps, the curve has transitioned to a healthy steepening regime. CTA positioning aligns with this; the heavier shorting of the front-end (2Y/5Y) relative to the long-end (30Y) indicates that trend-followers are riding the **bear-steepening** momentum.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
*   **Alignment:** Both Fast and Slow modes are positive (+), confirming a strong, multi-timeframe bearish trend.
*   **Conviction Gap:** Fast mode signals are significantly more aggressive (e.g., 5Y: +30.9 vs. Slow: +23.4). This indicates that while the long-term trend is down, the **short-term acceleration** is intensifying, increasing the risk of a "blow-off" top in yields.

### 4. Exhaustion Signals
High-conviction signals are concentrated in the Fast mode (41 signals $\ge$ 60). While the trend is strong, the widening gap between Fast and Slow modes often precedes a **mean-reversion event**. The extreme positioning in the 2Y/5Y suggests the trade is becoming "over-crowded," making it vulnerable to a sharp rally (yields drop) if a catalyst triggers a fast-mode exit.

### 5. Actionable Watch-list
**Watch: 10Y Yield break below 4.35%.** 
A breach here would likely trigger a cascade of Fast-mode CTA exits (covering shorts), providing a technical tailwind for a rapid rally in duration regardless of fundamental data.
