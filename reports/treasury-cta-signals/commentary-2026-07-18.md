## CTA Positioning Analysis: Treasury Markets (2026-07-18)

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs are **universally short duration** across the curve. Crowding is most acute at the front end, with the **2Y (+26.2)** and **5Y (+24.7)** seeing the heaviest positioning. This signals a high-conviction bet on rising short-to-medium term yields, likely driven by hawkish macro expectations or persistent inflation prints.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
The curve is currently steep (30Y-2Y at ~93bps). CTA positioning mirrors this steepness: they are significantly more aggressive in the 2Y/5Y tenors than in the 30Y (+12.8). This suggests CTAs are not betting on a broad Treasury sell-off, but specifically targeting a **bear-flattening or bear-steepening** move centered on the front end.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
There is **zero divergence** between modes; both Fast and Slow modes are positive. This alignment indicates strong trend conviction. However, the Fast Mode is more aggressive and carries higher conviction (41 signals $\ge$ 60) than the Slow Mode (19 signals $\ge$ 60), suggesting the trend is currently accelerating.

### 4. Recent Exhaustion Signals
We are seeing signs of **short-term overcrowding**. The fact that Fast Mode conviction is more than double Slow Mode conviction often precedes a "blow-off" top in yields. When the fast-trend becomes this decoupled from the long-term average, the trade becomes vulnerable to a rapid "short squeeze" if a catalyst triggers a yield dip.
