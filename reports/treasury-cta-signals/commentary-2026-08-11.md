CTAs are currently **net short duration across the entire curve** (Positive sign convention). Crowding is most acute in the **belly (5Y)** and **long-end (10Y/30Y)**. 
*   **Signal:** The uniformity of the short bias (+24 to +29 across tenors) suggests a systemic "bear" trend. The high concentration in the 5Y (+27.3 Fast / +29.9 Slow) indicates a strong conviction that mid-curve yields will continue to rise.

*   **Risk:** When fast-mode conviction peaks while yields are at multi-month highs (10Y @ 4.65%), the trade becomes "overcrowded." We are seeing signs of a **momentum peak**; any catalyst for a rally could trigger a violent "short squeeze" as fast-mode signals flip rapidly.
