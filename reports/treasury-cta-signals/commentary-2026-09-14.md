## CTA Positioning Analysis: Treasury Markets (2026-09-14)

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs are aggressively **Short Duration (+)** across the entire curve. Crowding is most acute in the **10Y (+34.5 Fast / +45.5 Slow)** and **5Y** tenors. This indicates a high-conviction bet on rising yields, with the belly of the curve serving as the primary vehicle for the trend-following short.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
The curve is positively sloped (10Y-2Y: +39bps; 30Y-2Y: +81bps). CTAs are effectively "riding the steepening" by maintaining short positions. The fact that positioning is heaviest in the 10Y/30Y suggests CTAs are betting on a term-premium expansion or a "higher-for-longer" regime rather than a front-end liquidity squeeze.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
There is **strong alignment** between Fast and Slow modes, but a notable **magnitude gap**. 
*   **Slow Mode** is significantly more aggressive (e.g., 10Y: +45.5) than **Fast Mode** (+34.5).
*   **Implication:** The long-term trend remains firmly bullish on yields, but the Fast Mode decay suggests the immediate momentum is softening. We are seeing a "cooling off" period, though not yet a trend reversal.

### 4. Exhaustion Signals
High-conviction signals are declining. Fast Mode high-conviction signals (39) are roughly double Slow Mode (18), but both are low relative to total signals. The divergence between the aggressive Slow Mode and the retreating Fast Mode is a classic **exhaustion signal**. The trade is "over-extended," increasing the risk of a violent short-covering rally (yields falling) if a catalyst triggers a stop-loss cascade.

### 5. Actionable Watch-list (Next 5 Days)
**Watch: 10Y Yield break below 4.85%.** 
A breach of this level would likely trigger Fast Mode flips to neutral/long, forcing a rapid unwind of the crowded 10Y short positions and accelerating a rally in duration.
