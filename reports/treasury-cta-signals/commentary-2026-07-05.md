## CTA Positioning Analysis: Treasury Markets (2026-07-05)

### 1. Duration Crowding
Positioning is skewed heavily toward the **front-end (2Y/5Y)**. With Slow Mode readings at +24.1 (2Y) and +24.2 (5Y), CTAs are most aggressively betting on rising yields in the short-to-medium term. The 30Y is the least crowded tenor, suggesting a lack of conviction in long-end bear trends.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
The curve is positively sloped (10Y-2Y: +31bps; 30Y-2Y: +80bps). CTA positioning mirrors this steepening bias; the concentration of short duration in the 2Y/5Y relative to the 30Y indicates a "bear-flattening" trade that has evolved into a "bull-steepening" risk if the front end rallies.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
*   **Front-End Alignment:** Fast and Slow modes are in lockstep for the 2Y (+27.8 vs +24.1), signaling high trend conviction for rising short-term yields.
*   **Long-End Divergence:** A critical split has emerged in the 30Y. Slow Mode remains net short duration (+9.7), but Fast Mode has flipped to neutral (0.0). This suggests the long-end bear trend is decaying rapidly.

### 4. Exhaustion Signals
High-conviction signals are waning. Only 18 signals remain in Slow Mode $\ge 60$. More importantly, the **June 24th 30Y Long signal** (strength 53.5) indicates that fast-money trend followers are already pivoting toward duration. The 30Y's move to 0.0 in Fast Mode is a classic "exhaustion" signal for the short-duration trade.
