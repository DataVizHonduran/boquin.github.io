## CTA Positioning Analysis: Treasury Markets (2026-06-09)

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs are currently **net short duration across the entire curve** (Positive sign convention). Crowding is most acute in the **belly (5Y: +30.5 fast / +21.9 slow)** and the **front end (2Y: +29.3 fast / +19.1 slow)**. This signals a strong conviction bet on rising yields, likely driven by a "higher-for-longer" macro regime or persistent inflation expectations.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
The curve is positively sloped (10Y-2Y: +38bps; 30Y-2Y: +84bps). CTAs are positioning for a **bear-flattening or parallel shift upward**. The fact that the 2Y and 5Y are more heavily shorted than the 30Y (+16.9 fast) suggests CTAs are more aggressive on the short-to-medium end, betting that the front end has further to climb to catch up with long-term risk premiums.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
There is **strong alignment** between Fast and Slow modes; both are firmly positive. However, the **Fast Mode is significantly more aggressive** (e.g., 5Y: +30.5 vs +21.9). This indicates that while the long-term trend is bearish for bonds, the recent momentum has accelerated. The lack of divergence suggests high trend conviction and low immediate probability of a "trend flip."

### 4. Exhaustion Signals
High-conviction signals are elevated in Fast Mode (41 signals $\ge$ 60) compared to Slow Mode (18 signals $\ge$ 60). While the trend is strong, the **extreme concentration in the 2Y/5Y tenors** is entering "crowded" territory. We are seeing the early stages of a potential "short-squeeze" risk if a macro catalyst triggers a sudden yield drop, as Fast Mode triggers would flip rapidly.

### 5. Actionable Watch-list
**Watch the 5Y Yield (4.29%):** A break below the 20-day moving average would trigger a rapid unwind of the most crowded CTA position (+30.5). Monitor for a "cascade" of long-duration covering if 5Y yields drop $\sim$15-20bps.
