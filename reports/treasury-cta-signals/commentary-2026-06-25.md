## CTA Treasury Positioning Analysis: 2026-06-25

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs are **uniformly short duration** across the curve. Crowding is most acute at the front end, with the **2Y (+31.1)** and **5Y (+28.8)** seeing the heaviest positioning. This signals a high-conviction bet on "higher-for-longer" rates or a hawkish policy regime, with the market aggressively pricing in a floor for short-term yields.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
The curve is currently steep (10Y-2Y at +34bps). CTA positioning mirrors this steepness; they are significantly more aggressive in shorting the front end than the long end (30Y at +7.7). This suggests CTAs are trading a **bear steepening** regime, focusing their risk on the policy-sensitive part of the curve rather than long-term inflation premiums.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
There is **strong alignment** between Fast and Slow modes (both positive), indicating a robust, multi-timeframe trend. However, Fast Mode conviction (40 signals $\ge$ 60) significantly outweighs Slow Mode (18 signals $\ge$ 60). This implies the trend is **accelerating**, with short-term momentum driving the current positioning more than long-term structural shifts.

### 4. Recent Exhaustion Signals
The disparity between Fast and Slow conviction suggests the trade is becoming **overextended**. When fast-mode signals aggressively lead slow-mode signals in a one-sided trade, the risk of a "momentum snap" increases. The extreme crowding in the 2Y/5Y tenors makes the front end vulnerable to a rapid cover-rally if any dovish catalyst emerges.
