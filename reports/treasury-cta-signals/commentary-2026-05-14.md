* **Implication:** The trend is accelerating, but the "anchor" (slow mode) hasn't fully committed. This creates a **fragile trend**; a sharp yield spike could trigger rapid fast-mode liquidations before slow-mode signals even flip.

* **Signal:** The aggressive fast-mode build-up into the 2Y/5Y suggests the trade is becoming **overcrowded**. We are seeing "trend exhaustion" characteristics where the fast-money is stretched.
