## CTA Positioning Analysis: 2026-05-28

### 1. Duration Crowding
* **Direction:** CTAs are universally **SHORT duration** across the curve (all positive scores).
* **Concentration:** Crowding is most acute in the 5Y (+32.7 Fast / +19.4 Slow) and 2Y (+30.4 Fast / +16.5 Slow).
* **Signal:** High positive positioning suggests a strong consensus bet on **rising yields**. The belly (5Y) is the most crowded, increasing the risk of a violent short-squeeze if yields pivot lower.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
* **Curve Shape:** The curve is steep (10Y-2Y: +49bps; 30Y-2Y: +102bps).
* **Positioning Alignment:** CTAs are most short-duration in the front-end/belly and relatively less short in the 30Y. This suggests trend-followers are playing the "higher-for-longer" front-end narrative rather than betting on a long-end blowout.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
* **Conviction Gap:** Fast mode (+23.9 to +32.7) is significantly more aggressive than Slow mode (+14.5 to +19.4).
* **Implication:** The trend is accelerating. The widening gap between fast and slow signals indicates high momentum, but the low high-conviction count in Slow mode (12) suggests the long-term structural trend is not yet fully locked in.

### 4. Exhaustion Signals
* **Overextension:** Fast mode scores exceeding +30 in the 2Y and 5Y are approaching historical "crowded" thresholds. 
* **Risk:** With high-conviction fast signals (35) dominating, the trade is becoming "top-heavy." Any catalyst for falling yields will trigger rapid profit-taking (covering shorts), accelerating a rally in bond prices.

### 5. Actionable Watch-list
* **Trigger:** A break below the 20-day moving average in 5Y yields.
* **Expected Move:** Rapid unwinding of the +32.7 Fast position, leading to a sharp, non-fundamental rally in the belly of the curve.
