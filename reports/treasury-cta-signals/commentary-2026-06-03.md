* **Direction:** CTAs are universally **short duration** across all tenors (all positions positive).
* **Crowding:** The belly of the curve is the most crowded; the **5Y (+30.5)** and **2Y (+28.3)** show the strongest short-duration bias. 
* **Signal:** This indicates a high-conviction bet on **rising yields**, with the most aggressive positioning concentrated in the front-to-mid end.

### Yield Curve Context
* **Curve State:** The curve is steepening (10Y-2Y: +0.42bps; 30Y-2Y: +0.94bps).
* **Positioning Alignment:** CTAs are shorting the 2Y/5Y more aggressively than the 30Y (+20.1). This suggests they are riding a trend of rising short-term rates while remaining slightly less aggressive on the long-bond, consistent with a bear-steepening regime.

### Fast vs. Slow Divergences
* **Trend Acceleration:** Fast mode is significantly more positive than slow mode (e.g., 5Y: +30.5 vs +20.4).
* **Conviction:** The divergence indicates a recent acceleration in the short-duration trend. The "fast" trend is leading the "slow" trend higher, suggesting strong immediate momentum for rising yields.

### Exhaustion Signals
* **Conviction Levels:** High-conviction signals in Fast Mode (34) are nearly triple those in Slow Mode (13).
* **Risk:** While not yet at extreme exhaustion, the widening gap between fast and slow modes suggests the trade is becoming "crowded" in the short term. A failure for yields to break higher could trigger a rapid short-covering rally.
