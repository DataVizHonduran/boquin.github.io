## CTA Positioning Analysis: Treasury Markets (2026-06-19)

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs are currently **net short duration across the entire curve** (Positive sign convention). Crowding is most acute in the **front-end (2Y/5Y)**, where positions are deepest (+29.2 / +28.9 in Fast Mode). This signals a strong conviction bet on "higher for longer" or a delayed pivot, with the market aggressively fighting any rally in short-dated notes.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
The curve is positively sloped (10Y-2Y: +29bps; 30Y-2Y: +73bps). CTA positioning mirrors this steepening bias; the heaviest shorts are concentrated in the 2Y/5Y, while the 30Y is the least shorted (+11.4). This suggests CTAs are playing a **bear-steepening regime**, betting that front-end yields will remain sticky while long-end volatility limits aggressive shorting of the 30Y.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
There is **strong alignment** between Fast and Slow modes. Both are consistently positive across all tenors. 
* **Conviction:** High. When fast-trend signals (20/50/100) and slow-trend signals (50/100/200) synchronize in direction, the trend is considered "embedded."
* **Implication:** There is currently no "trend conflict," meaning a reversal would require a significant fundamental catalyst to trigger a mass exit.

### 4. Exhaustion Signals
While the trend is aligned, **high-conviction signals are decaying** in the Slow Mode (only 17 signals $\ge 60$ vs. 42 in Fast Mode). The front-end is heavily crowded, increasing the risk of a "short squeeze" if inflation data prints lower than expected. The gap between the 2Y (+29.2) and 30Y (+11.4) suggests the long-bond is the primary candidate for a trend reversal.
