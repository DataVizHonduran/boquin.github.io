## CTA Positioning Analysis: Treasury Markets (2026-11-02)

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs are **uniformly short duration** across the curve. Crowding is most acute in the **10Y tenor**, where both Fast (+42.2) and Slow (+74.2) modes show the highest positive readings. This signals a high-conviction bet on rising yields, with the belly of the curve acting as the primary engine for the trend-following short.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
The curve is positively sloped (10Y-2Y: +37bps; 30Y-2Y: +71bps). CTAs are aggressively riding this steepening bias. The fact that the 30Y is less "shorted" in Fast mode (+29.8) compared to the 10Y suggests a slight preference for shorting the belly over the ultra-long end, likely reflecting a view that the 10Y is more sensitive to the current macro catalyst.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
There is **strong alignment** between modes; both are firmly positive. However, the **Slow Mode is significantly more aggressive** (e.g., 10Y +74.2 vs Fast +42.2). This implies that while the long-term trend is deeply entrenched, the "Fast" signals are beginning to flatten or mean-revert. This divergence suggests a peak in trend conviction and a potential transition from "aggressive entry" to "position maintenance."

### 4. Exhaustion Signals
Exhaustion risk is elevated. With **37 high-conviction signals in Fast mode** and **18 in Slow mode**, the trade is heavily crowded. When Slow mode positioning reaches these extremes (+74.2 on 10Y) while Fast mode begins to lag, it often precedes a "short-squeeze" or a sharp rally in bonds if a catalyst triggers a trend reversal.

### 5. Actionable Watch-list
**Watch the 10Y Yield 4.85% level.** 
A break below this threshold would likely trigger a cascade of Fast-mode exits (flipping from + to -), forcing CTAs to buy back duration rapidly and accelerating a rally in the 10Y.
