## CTA Treasury Positioning Analysis: 2026-05-25

### Duration Crowding
* **Direction:** All tenors exhibit positive (+) positioning, confirming CTAs are collectively **short duration** (betting on rising yields).
* **Crowding:** The 5Y is the most crowded short-duration trade (+30.9 Fast / +17.3 Slow), followed closely by the 2Y and 10Y. This signals a strong, trend-driven conviction that yields will continue to climb across the curve.

### Yield Curve Context
* **Curve Shape:** With a steep 10Y-2Y (+49bps) and 30Y-2Y (+102bps) spread, the market is in a steepening regime.
* **Positioning Alignment:** CTAs are most aggressively short the belly (5Y), suggesting they are riding a "bear steepening" move where mid-to-short term rates are pushed higher relative to the long end.

### Fast vs. Slow Divergences
* **Trend Acceleration:** Fast mode scores (+24 to +30) are significantly higher than Slow mode (+13 to +17). 
* **Conviction:** High-conviction signals are concentrated in the Fast mode (34 vs 12). This indicates a recent, aggressive acceleration in the rising-yield trend, though the long-term trend is less entrenched.

### Exhaustion Signals
* **Overextension:** Fast mode positioning in the 5Y (+30.9) is approaching levels where trend-followers become vulnerable to "mean-reversion" shocks.
* **Long-End Hesitation:** The 30Y is the least short-duration tenor in Fast mode (+24.4), suggesting the trend is losing steam at the ultra-long end.

### 5-Day Watch-List
* **The 5Y Pivot:** Monitor for any yield stall or dip in the 5Y. Given the high Fast-mode crowding (+30.9), a break in the rising-yield trend could trigger a rapid short-covering rally (flip to long-duration).
