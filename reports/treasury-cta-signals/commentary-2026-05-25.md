## CTA Positioning Analysis: 2026-05-25

### 1. Duration Crowding
* **Concentration:** CTAs are uniformly long across the curve, with the heaviest concentration in the **5Y (+30.9 Fast / +17.3 Slow)** and **2Y**.
* **Signal:** This indicates a crowded "bull flatten" or "rally" trade. With positioning skewed heavily long, the risk of a sharp yield spike (long liquidation) increases if macro catalysts shift.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
* **10Y-2Y (+0.49bps) & 30Y-2Y (+1.02bps):** The curve is positively sloped. CTAs are long the belly (5Y) and front-end (2Y) more aggressively than the long-bond (30Y), suggesting they are betting on a normalization/steepening process while remaining net long duration.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
* **Conviction Gap:** Fast mode is significantly more aggressive (+24 to +30) than Slow mode (+13 to +17).
* **Implication:** The trend is accelerating in the short term, but long-term conviction remains moderate. This "fast-lead" suggests a momentum-driven rally rather than a fundamental structural shift.

### 4. Exhaustion Signals
* **High-Conviction Ratio:** Fast mode shows high conviction (34/118), while Slow mode is lagging (12/81). 
* **Risk:** The widening gap between Fast and Slow positioning often precedes a "mean-reversion" event. The trade is becoming "over-extended" relative to the 200-day trend.

### 5. Actionable Watch-list
* **Trigger:** A break above **4.65% on the 10Y**. 
* **Action:** If 10Y yields spike, expect a cascading liquidation of Fast-mode long positions, potentially accelerating a move toward 4.80% as CTAs flip to neutral/short.
