## CTA Treasury Positioning Analysis: 2026-06-24

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs are **uniformly short duration** across the curve. Crowding is most acute in the front end, specifically the **2Y (+31.2 Fast / +23.3 Slow)** and **5Y (+28.7 Fast / +24.7 Slow)**. This signals a high-conviction bet on rising short-term rates, likely driven by hawkish policy expectations or sticky inflation. The long end (30Y) is the least crowded, suggesting limited conviction in a term-premium blowout.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
With the 10Y-2Y spread at **+27bps** and 30Y-2Y at **+71bps**, the curve is positively sloped. CTA positioning is heavily skewed toward the front end, indicating they are playing a **bear-flattening** or a general yield-up move. The concentration in 2Y/5Y suggests the trend is being driven by the "policy" part of the curve rather than long-term growth/inflation expectations.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
We see a **bullish divergence for yields** (accelerating trend). Fast mode signals are more positive than slow mode across all tenors, particularly in the 2Y and 5Y. 
* **Implication:** Trend conviction is increasing. The "fast" money is aggressively piling into short duration, pulling the "slow" money along. This typically indicates a strong, momentum-driven move.
