## CTA Positioning Analysis: Treasury Markets (2026-07-23)

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs are **uniformly short duration** across the curve, with positioning heavily skewed toward the front end. 
* **Front-End Dominance:** The 2Y and 5Y tenors are the most crowded (+26.4 to +26.8), signaling a high-conviction bet on rising short-term yields.
* **Long-End Relative Lightness:** The 30Y is the least positioned (+12.6 to +15.1), suggesting CTAs are less convinced of a long-term inflation breakout compared to near-term policy hawkishness.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
The curve is positively sloped (10Y-2Y: +37bps). CTA positioning aligns with a **bear-steepening regime**. By aggressively shorting the 2Y/5Y while remaining lighter on the 30Y, CTAs are effectively betting on a shift in the short-end anchor, likely driven by expectations of "higher for longer" rates.
