## CTA Treasury Positioning Analysis: 2026-07-25

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs are universally **Short Duration (+)** across the curve. Crowding is most acute in the front end, specifically the **2Y (+28.7)** and **5Y (+27.9)**. This indicates a heavy consensus bet on rising short-term yields, likely driven by expectations of persistent inflation or a "higher-for-longer" policy regime.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
The curve is currently steep (10Y-2Y at +34bps; 30Y-2Y at +80bps). CTA positioning mirrors this steepening bias: they are significantly more aggressive in shorting the front end than the long end (30Y is the least short at +17.6). This suggests CTAs are positioning for a **bear steepener**, where short-term rates rise faster than long-term rates.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
There is strong **directional alignment** between Fast and Slow modes; both are positive across all tenors. However, conviction is skewed:
*   **Fast Mode:** Higher conviction (40 signals $\ge$ 60).
*   **Slow Mode:** Lower conviction (18 signals $\ge$ 60).
The lack of divergence suggests a high-conviction trend. The Fast Mode's dominance indicates that recent price action is accelerating the short-duration momentum.

### 4. Recent Exhaustion Signals
While the trend is intact, the extreme concentration in the 2Y and 5Y tenors (approaching +30) suggests the front-end trade is becoming **overcrowded**. When Fast Mode conviction significantly outpaces Slow Mode in a crowded trade, the risk of a "sharp mean-reversion" increases if a catalyst triggers a cover rally.
