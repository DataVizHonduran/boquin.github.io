## CTA Positioning Analysis: Treasury Markets (2026-07-08)

### 1. Duration Crowding
Positioning is skewed heavily toward the **front-end**, with the 2Y and 5Y tenors seeing the highest short-duration conviction (+24% to +27%). This signals a strong CTA bet on "higher for longer" or a delayed easing cycle. Conversely, the 30Y is virtually flat in Fast Mode (+0.0), suggesting a total collapse of the long-bond bear trend.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
The curve is positively sloped (10Y-2Y: +35bps; 30Y-2Y: +86bps). CTAs are effectively trading the **steepening bias**. By remaining short the 2Y/5Y while neutralizing the 30Y, CTAs are positioned for a "bear steepener" or a transition where front-end yields remain sticky while long-end yields stabilize.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
*   **Front-End Alignment:** Fast and Slow modes are in lockstep on the 2Y and 5Y (+24% to +27%), indicating high trend conviction and a "hard" floor for short-term yields.
*   **Long-End Divergence:** A critical split exists at the 30Y. Slow Mode remains marginally short (+9.8), but Fast Mode has flipped to neutral (+0.0). This suggests the long-term bear trend is decaying, and fast-money is already exiting duration shorts.

### 4. Exhaustion Signals
The 30Y is flashing a clear exhaustion signal. The June 24th "Long" signal (strength 53.5) combined with the Fast Mode flip to 0.0 indicates that the long-bond sell-off has peaked. With high-conviction signals dropping (only 18 in Slow Mode), the overall "Short Duration" regime is losing momentum.
