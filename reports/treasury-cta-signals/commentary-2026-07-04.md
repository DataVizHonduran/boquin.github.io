The curve is positively sloped (10Y-2Y: +31bps; 30Y-2Y: +80bps). CTA positioning aligns with this steepening regime; by being shorter the 2Y than the 30Y, CTAs are effectively riding the trend of rising short-term yields relative to long-term anchors.

*   **Implication:** The long-end trend is decaying. The fast mode has already flipped/neutralized, suggesting the "bear steepening" trade is losing momentum at the long end.

We are seeing clear signs of **duration short exhaustion**:
*   **30Y Pivot:** The June 24th signal (Long, strength 53.5) indicates fast-mode CTAs are beginning to cover shorts or enter longs in the 30Y.
*   **Conviction Drop:** High-conviction signals in the slow mode (18) are significantly lower than the fast mode (41), suggesting the broader trend is fragile and susceptible to a "short squeeze" if yields dip.
