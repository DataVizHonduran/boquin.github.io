## CTA Treasury Positioning Analysis: 2026-06-23

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs are **universally short duration** across the curve. Crowding is most acute in the front-to-belly tenors:
*   **2Y (+29.8) and 5Y (+28.6)** in Fast Mode indicate aggressive bets on rising short-term yields.
*   **30Y (+10.0)** is the least crowded, suggesting limited conviction in a long-end sell-off compared to the front end.
*   **Signal:** The market is heavily positioned for a "higher-for-longer" or hawkish surprise scenario.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
The curve is currently steepening (**10Y-2Y: +27bps**). CTA positioning reinforces a **bear-steepening** regime; by aggressively shorting the 2Y and 5Y, CTAs are betting that the front end will remain elevated or rise faster than the long end, potentially reflecting inflation persistence or a delayed pivot.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
There is strong **trend alignment** between modes (both are positive), which typically signals high conviction. However:
*   **Fast Mode** is significantly more aggressive in the 2Y/5Y than **Slow Mode**.
*   **Implication:** The trend is accelerating. When Fast Mode leads Slow Mode to this extent, the trend is "hot," but the risk of a tactical "snap-back" increases if the catalyst for rising yields fades.
