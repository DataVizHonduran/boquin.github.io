The curve is positively sloped (10Y-2Y: +42bps; 30Y-2Y: +92bps). CTA positioning is aligned with this steepening regime; by being more short the belly than the long-bond (30Y: +17.3), CTAs are effectively positioning for a **bear-steepening** environment where short-term rates remain elevated relative to the long end.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
*   **Alignment:** Both Fast and Slow modes are positive (+), confirming a synchronized bearish trend in duration.
*   **Conviction Gap:** Fast mode signals are significantly more aggressive (e.g., 5Y: +29.7 vs. Slow: +21.2). 
*   **Implication:** The trend is accelerating. The widening gap between fast and slow modes suggests momentum is driving the trade, increasing the risk of a "snap-back" if a catalyst triggers a rapid cover.

High-conviction signals are concentrated in the Fast mode (41 signals $\ge$ 60). While the trend is strong, the extreme concentration in the 2Y/5Y tenors suggests the trade is becoming **overcrowded**. We are seeing the early stages of a "momentum peak," where any surprise dovish data could trigger a violent short-covering rally (squeeze) in the belly.
