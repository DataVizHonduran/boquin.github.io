## CTA Positioning Analysis: Treasury Markets (2026-06-13)

### 1. Duration Crowding
CTAs are currently **net short duration** across the entire curve. Crowding is most acute in the **belly (5Y)** and **front-end (2Y)**, where positioning is highest (+30.9 and +29.8 in Fast Mode). This signals a strong conviction bet on "higher for longer" or a delayed easing cycle, with the most aggressive positioning concentrated in the shorter tenors.

### 2. Yield Curve Context
With a positive 10Y-2Y (+40bps) and 30Y-2Y (+90bps) slope, the curve is normalized/steepening. CTA positioning aligns with this regime; by being more short the 2Y/5Y than the 30Y, CTAs are effectively betting on a **bear-flattening or a neutral-steepening** environment where short-term rates remain elevated relative to long-term growth expectations.

### 3. Fast vs. Slow Divergences
There is **strong alignment** between Fast and Slow modes. Both are positive (+), indicating a synchronized trend. However, the Fast Mode is significantly more aggressive (e.g., 5Y: +30.9 vs. +23.4). This suggests that while the long-term trend is bullish for yields, the recent acceleration is driving a "momentum chase," increasing the risk of a sharp reversal if a catalyst triggers profit-taking.

### 4. Exhaustion Signals
High-conviction signals are elevated in Fast Mode (41) but thinning in Slow Mode (15). This divergence is a classic **exhaustion warning**. When fast-trend conviction spikes while slow-trend conviction remains modest, the trade becomes "over-extended." The heavy concentration in 2Y/5Y shorts makes these tenors highly susceptible to a "short squeeze" on any dovish macro surprise.

### 5. Actionable Watch-list
**Watch: 5Y Yield break below 4.10%.** 
A breach here would likely trigger a cascade of Fast Mode exits (long duration flips), accelerating a rally in the belly of the curve as CTAs liquidate their most crowded short positions.
