Conditions remain fragile. Housing starts in May 2026 were nearly flat. There is a noted "deflating housing bubble" which is stymying the wealth effect typically seen from a booming stock market. Mortgage markets show slight signs of life, but recovery is described as fragile.

6. Forward Guidance Evolution
Guidance has shifted from an aggressive cutting cycle (from the 5.0% peak) to a period of stagnation. The "dilemma" mentioned by Macklem suggests the BoC is caught between growth concerns (technical recession) and inflation persistence.
