Executive Summary

The Bank of Canada (BoC) maintained the policy rate at 2.25% on July 15, 2026, signaling a transition to a cautious, data-dependent hold. 5. Neutral Rate Estimate & Real Rate Stance

6. Forward Guidance Evolution
