Executive Summary

The Bank of Canada (BoC) is currently navigating a "dilemma" characterized by a weakening economy juxtaposed with a recent surge in inflation (May CPI at 3.2%). Governor Macklem has attempted to downplay this price action, stating there is "no evidence of generalized inflation" despite May hikes. However, the Summary of Deliberations from the June 10 meeting reveals a Governing Council grappling with these conflicting signals. While the BoC has reaffirmed its 2% inflation target following framework consultations, market sentiment is split between a "long hold" and further cuts. The CAD is under significant pressure, hitting 14-month lows, which adds a layer of complexity to the inflation outlook via imported costs.
