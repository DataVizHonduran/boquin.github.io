Accuracy by forecast horizon

Bias = actual − median forecast, averaged across all vintages at that horizon. Positive means the Fed underestimated; negative means it overestimated. MAE is the average size of the miss, regardless of direction.

Method. SEP medians are scraped from the Fed's own "accessible version" projection tables (federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomcprojtabl*.htm). The FOMC did not publish a formal median for unemployment/inflation until the September 2015 SEP — earlier vintages (back to 2007) gave only a central-tendency range, so this history starts there rather than in 2007. Actual unemployment = Q4 average of UNRATE; actual core PCE = Q4-over-Q4 % change in PCEPILFE, matching the Fed's own convention. Both series from FRED.

Gaps. The March 2022 SEP's accessible-table page is missing from the Fed's site (404) and is excluded. October 2025 unemployment uses a 2-of-3-month Q4 average due to a BLS reporting gap that month. 2026 and later target years are still projections — no actual yet.
