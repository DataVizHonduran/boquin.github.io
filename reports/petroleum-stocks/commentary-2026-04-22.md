### 1. Crude Oil Positioning
* **National View:** Total NUS stocks remain balanced at roughly the midpoint of the 5-year seasonal range (49.6%). The modest WoW build of 1.9 MMBbl is negligible and suggests a steady-state supply/demand equilibrium.
* **PADD 3 Focus:** The Gulf Coast remains the primary storage hub, sitting at 77.2% of its seasonal range. This relative abundance in PADD 3 acts as a ceiling for WTI upside, providing a buffer against immediate supply shocks.

### 2. Gasoline Positioning
* **Seasonal Tightness:** Stocks are significantly lean at 17.2% of the 5-year range. The 4.6 MMBbl draw indicates strong early-season demand as the market ramps up for the summer driving season.
* **Regional Alarm:** PADD 3 is currently in negative territory (-7.6%), signaling critical tightness at the primary refining hub.

### 3. Distillate Positioning
* **Extreme Scarcity:** Distillates are the most constrained segment of the complex, sitting at a precarious 2.4% of the seasonal range.
* **Demand Signal:** The 3.4 MMBbl draw, coupled with negative seasonal positioning in PADD 2 and PADD 5, suggests that diesel demand is outstripping production, likely driven by strong industrial activity or agricultural planting.

### 4. Cross-Product Divergences
* **Crude vs. Products:** There is a clear divergence between "loose" crude (neutral seasonal positioning) and "tight" refined products. 
* **Refining Margin Pressure:** The combination of high crude inventories in PADD 3 and critically low gasoline/distillate stocks suggests a high-incentive environment for refiners to maximize throughput, which should eventually pull down crude inventories.

### 5. Price Implications
* **WTI:** **Neutral.** The modest build and balanced seasonal positioning provide no immediate catalyst for a breakout.
* **Refined Products:** **Bullish.** Both gasoline and distillates are trading in a deficit relative to seasonal norms. Expect upward pressure on RBOB and ULSD futures, particularly as PADD 3 gasoline stocks dip below zero.

### 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
* **PADD 3 Gasoline Recovery:** Monitor if the Gulf Coast gasoline deficit widens or if refiners successfully replenish stocks. A further slide into negative territory will likely trigger a spike in refined product premiums.
