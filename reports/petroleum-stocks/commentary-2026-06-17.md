### 1. Crude Oil Positioning
* **NUS Total:** Crude stocks are essentially flat relative to the 5-year seasonal norm (-0.1%), suggesting a balanced global supply-demand equilibrium.
* **WoW Movement:** A healthy draw of 8.3 MMBbl indicates strong refinery throughput as the industry ramps up for peak summer demand.
* **PADD 3 Highlight:** The Gulf Coast remains the primary storage hub with 243.8 MMBbl, sitting 9.7% above its seasonal range. This suggests that while national stocks are neutral, the US Gulf Coast is slightly oversupplied, potentially capping immediate WTI upside.

### 2. Gasoline Positioning
* **Seasonal Tightness:** Gasoline stocks are 8.2% below the 5-year average, signaling a tight market entering the peak driving season.
* **Demand Signal:** The WoW draw of 0.9 MMBbl is modest, but the regional data is alarming. PADD 3 (Gulf Coast) is severely depleted at -56.3% of its seasonal range, and PADD 5 (West Coast) is -23.2%. This indicates aggressive consumption and potential regional supply bottlenecks.

### 3. Distillate Positioning
* **Seasonal Tightness:** Distillates remain structurally tight, sitting 8.9% below seasonal norms.
* **Demand Signal:** A marginal build of 1.0 MMBbl suggests a temporary lull in diesel demand or a slight increase in production. However, the overall seasonal deficit indicates that the market remains sensitive to any disruption in middle distillate supply.

### 4. Cross-Product Divergences
* **The PADD 3 Paradox:** There is a stark divergence in the Gulf Coast. While crude oil is slightly oversupplied (+9.7%), gasoline is in a deep deficit (-56.3%). This suggests that refineries are processing crude, but the finished product is being exported or consumed faster than it can be stored.
* **Gas vs. Diesel:** Gasoline continues to draw (demand-led), while distillates saw a slight build, indicating that summer travel demand is currently outstripping industrial diesel demand.

### 5. Price Implications
* **WTI Crude:** **Net Bullish.** The 8.3 MMBbl draw and tight refined product stocks provide a strong floor for crude prices.
* **Refined Products:** **Bullish.** Extreme tightness in PADD 3 and PADD 5 gasoline stocks is likely to support higher RBOB crack spreads and regional price premiums.

### 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
* **PADD 3 Gasoline Levels:** Monitor if the Gulf Coast gasoline deficit widens further. If stocks drop below the current -56.3% threshold, expect significant volatility in gasoline futures and potential regional shortages.
