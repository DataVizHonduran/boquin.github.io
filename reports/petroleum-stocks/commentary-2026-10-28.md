# EIA Weekly Petroleum Analysis: Week Ending 2026-10-23

## Executive Summary
The latest EIA snapshot reveals a stark divergence between crude/distillates and gasoline. While crude inventories are significantly below seasonal norms—driven by a hollowed-out Gulf Coast—gasoline is building, suggesting a softening in demand as the market transitions out of the driving season.

## 1. Crude Oil Positioning
*   **NUS Total:** A 1.9 MMBbl draw pushes total stocks further below the 5-year seasonal average (-14.7%), indicating tight fundamental support.
*   **PADD 3 Highlight:** The Gulf Coast is the primary driver of this tightness, sitting at **-70.3% of its seasonal range**. This suggests high refinery utilization or aggressive export flows, leaving very little buffer for supply disruptions in the US hub.

## 2. Gasoline Positioning
*   **Seasonal Surplus:** A 3.9 MMBbl build brings total stocks to 13.1% above the 5-year norm.
*   **Demand Signal:** The build is widespread (PADD 1, 3, 4, and 5 are all above range), signaling a clear drop in consumption. The only outlier is PADD 2 (-79.5%), likely due to localized regional logistics or specific blending transitions.

## 3. Distillate Positioning
*   **Seasonal Tightness:** A 1.5 MMBbl draw keeps stocks slightly below the 5-year average (-7.1%).
*   **Demand Signal:** The draw is concentrated in PADD 1, 2, and 5. This suggests early heating oil uptake or resilient diesel demand for late-autumn logistics, offsetting the surplus currently held in the Gulf Coast (PADD 3).

## 4. Cross-Product Divergences
We are seeing a **"Crude-Distillate vs. Gasoline" split**. Crude and distillates are trending toward a deficit, while gasoline is trending toward a surplus. This suggests that refinery yields are currently favoring middle distillates or that the gasoline market is oversupplied relative to current demand.

## 5. Price Implications
*   **WTI:** **Net Bullish.** The combination of a WoW draw and severe PADD 3 depletion provides a strong floor for crude prices.
*   **Refined Products:** **Mixed.** Bearish for RBOB (gasoline) due to builds; Neutral to Bullish for ULSD/Heating Oil due to seasonal draws.

## 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
**PADD 3 Crude Levels:** Monitor if the Gulf Coast continues to bleed inventories. If PADD 3 drops further while NUS total remains low, expect increased volatility and a potential spike in WTI premiums.
