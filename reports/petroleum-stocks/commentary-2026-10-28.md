# EIA Weekly Petroleum Analysis: Oct 28, 2026

## Executive Summary
The latest EIA data reveals a tightening crude and gasoline complex, contrasted by a modest build in distillates. The most striking signal is the severe depletion of Gulf Coast crude stocks, suggesting high refinery utilization or significant export outflows.

## 1. Crude Oil Positioning
*   **NUS Total:** A 3.1 MMBbl draw brings total stocks 10.6% below the 5-year seasonal norm, indicating a lean inventory environment heading into Q4.
*   **PADD 3 Highlight:** The Gulf Coast is the primary driver of this tightness, sitting at **-55.2% of its seasonal range**. This extreme deficit suggests a "just-in-time" inventory model at the primary refining hub, leaving the market vulnerable to any unplanned outages.

## 2. Gasoline Positioning
*   **Seasonal Status:** Gasoline stocks are notably tight, sitting 13.5% below the 5-year average.
*   **Demand Signal:** The 3.0 MMBbl WoW draw suggests stronger-than-expected late-October demand or a strategic shift in refinery yields. PADD 2 (Midwest) is particularly lean (-51.2%), signaling regional tightness.

## 3. Distillate Positioning
*   **Seasonal Status:** Stocks remain slightly below the 5-year norm (-3.5%), but the WoW build of 1.7 MMBbl indicates a temporary surplus.
*   **Demand Signal:** The build suggests that heating oil demand has not yet ramped up sufficiently to offset diesel production, providing a short-term cushion for the middle distillate complex.

## 4. Cross-Product Divergences
We are seeing a **clear divergence between Gasoline and Distillates**. While gasoline is drawing down and sitting well below seasonal norms (bullish), distillates are building (bearish/neutral). This suggests refineries may be optimizing for gasoline output or that diesel demand is lagging behind the seasonal transition to heating oil.

## 5. Price Implications
*   **WTI:** **Net Bullish.** The combination of a total NUS draw and the critical shortage in PADD 3 provides strong fundamental support for crude prices.
*   **Refined Products:** **Mixed.** Bullish for RBOB (Gasoline) due to seasonal tightness; Neutral to Bearish for ULSD/Heating Oil due to the weekly build.

## 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
**PADD 3 Crude Inventory Trend:** Monitor if the Gulf Coast continues to draw. If PADD 3 drops further below the -55% mark, expect increased volatility and a potential spike in WTI as the "buffer" disappears.
