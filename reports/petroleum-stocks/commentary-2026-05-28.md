### 1. Crude Oil Positioning
*   **Overall Trend:** A significant WoW draw of 7.9 MMBbl indicates strong refinery utilization or unexpected demand spikes.
*   **Seasonal Context:** Total NUS stocks are lean, sitting at only 23.6% of the 5-year seasonal range, suggesting a tight physical market heading into the summer.
*   **PADD 3 Highlight:** The Gulf Coast remains the primary anchor with 259.2 MMBbl. At 49.4% of its range, PADD 3 is far more balanced than the national average, acting as the critical buffer for US supply.

### 2. Gasoline Positioning
*   **Seasonal Tightness:** Gasoline stocks are in "critical" territory, sitting at -5.4% of the 5-year range (below the historical floor).
*   **Demand Signal:** The 1.5 MMBbl draw during the May ramp-up to peak driving season confirms aggressive consumption.
*   **Regional Crisis:** PADD 3 is severely undersupplied (-55.2% of range), suggesting that Gulf Coast refining output is struggling to keep pace with domestic demand and exports.

### 3. Distillate Positioning
*   **Seasonal Context:** Stocks are marginally below the 5-year range (-1.2%), indicating a tight but managed market.
*   **Demand Signal:** A nominal build of 0.4 MMBbl suggests a temporary equilibrium between diesel demand and production.
*   **Regional Note:** PADD 5 (West Coast) is notably tight (-25.3%), which may lead to localized price premiums for ULSD in the Pacific region.

### 4. Cross-Product Divergences
*   **Gasoline vs. Distillates:** There is a stark divergence in tightness. Gasoline is significantly below its historical seasonal floor, while distillates are hovering just at the edge of the range. 
*   **Refinery Optimization:** The combination of crude draws and gasoline deficits suggests refineries are likely maximizing gasoline yields (high-severity cracking) to capture summer premiums, potentially contributing to the slight distillate build.

### 5. Price Implications
*   **WTI Crude:** **Bullish.** Strong WoW draws and low seasonal positioning provide a firm floor for prices.
*   **Refined Products:** **Strongly Bullish for RBOB / Neutral-Bullish for ULSD.** The extreme deficit in PADD 3 gasoline is a primary catalyst for upward price pressure on the gasoline crack spread.

### 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
*   **PADD 3 Gasoline Inventories:** With stocks already below the 5-year range, any further draw in the Gulf Coast will likely trigger a sharp spike in RBOB futures.
