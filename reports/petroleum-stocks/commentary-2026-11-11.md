# EIA Weekly Petroleum Analysis: Week Ending 2026-10-30

## Executive Summary
The latest EIA data reveals a broad-based draw across all major categories, with distillates leading the decline. Crude oil remains structurally undersupplied relative to seasonal norms, particularly in the Gulf Coast hub.

| Product | MMBbl | WoW | Seasonal % | Signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 1. Crude Oil Positioning
*   **NUS Total:** Crude stocks are sitting 9.1% below the 5-year seasonal average, indicating a tight physical market heading into Q4.
*   **WoW Trend:** A modest draw of 0.9 MMBbl suggests steady refinery throughput.
*   **PADD 3 Highlight:** The Gulf Coast is the primary driver of the deficit, sitting **37.3% below seasonal norms**. This severe tightness in the primary import/export hub limits downside risk for WTI.

## 2. Gasoline Positioning
*   **Seasonal Status:** Gasoline remains in a surplus (+14.7% vs 5yr range), suggesting a comfortable cushion as the market transitions out of the summer driving season.
*   **Demand Signal:** The negligible WoW draw (-0.2 MMBbl) confirms a seasonal lull in demand, though PADD 2 (Midwest) is notably tight (-42.6%), likely due to regional blending or logistics.

## 3. Distillate Positioning
*   **Seasonal Status:** Distillates are the standout bullish signal, trading 14.5% below the 5-year average.
*   **Demand Signal:** A significant WoW draw of 3.6 MMBbl indicates strong early-season heating oil demand or robust diesel consumption for freight. PADD 2 (-35.3%) and PADD 5 (-20.2%) are showing acute regional shortages.

## 4. Cross-Product Divergences
*   **Gas vs. Distillates:** There is a clear divergence between "light" and "heavy" ends. Gasoline is oversupplied while distillates are tight. This creates a strong incentive for refiners to shift yields toward diesel/heating oil to capture higher margins (widening the diesel-gas crack spread).

## 5. Price Implications
*   **WTI:** **Bullish.** Low PADD 3 inventories provide a strong floor for crude prices.
*   **Refined Products:** **Mixed/Bullish.** Neutral for RBOB gasoline due to surplus; strongly bullish for ULSD/Heating Oil given the aggressive draw and seasonal deficit.

## 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
**PADD 3 Crude Recovery:** Watch for any significant build in Gulf Coast crude. If PADD 3 continues to bleed, expect WTI to test higher resistance levels regardless of global macro headwinds.
