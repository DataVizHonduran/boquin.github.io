# EIA Weekly Petroleum Analysis: June 11, 2026

## Executive Summary
The latest EIA snapshot reveals a tightening physical market across all three major categories. Crude inventories are flirting with the bottom of the seasonal range, while refined products—particularly gasoline—show significant regional deficits that should support product cracks.

### Inventory Snapshot
| Product | MMBbl | WoW | Seasonal % | Signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 1. Crude Oil Positioning
*   **NUS Total:** A healthy draw of 7.2 MMBbl pushes total stocks to the 8th percentile of the 5-year range, indicating a lean inventory environment heading into the peak summer season.
*   **PADD 3 Focus:** Gulf Coast stocks sit at 19.3% of the seasonal range. While higher than the national average, this provides the necessary buffer for export flows and refinery throughput.
*   **Regional Note:** PADD 5 (West Coast) is critically low at -11.5%, suggesting localized tightness and potential for regional price spikes.

## 2. Gasoline Positioning
*   **Seasonal Status:** Despite a nominal WoW build of 0.2 MMBbl, total stocks are 5.7% below the 5-year seasonal norm.
*   **Demand Signal:** The massive deficit in PADD 3 (-31.4%) suggests that Gulf Coast refining output is being consumed or exported immediately, leaving little for storage. This is a strong bullish signal for RBOB futures.

## 3. Distillate Positioning
*   **Seasonal Status:** Stocks are 8.1% below the 5-year range, reflecting a consistent draw.
*   **Demand Signal:** With heating demand dormant, this draw is driven purely by diesel/industrial consumption. The broad-based deficit across PADD 1, 2, and 3 suggests a synchronized tightening in middle distillate demand.

## 4. Cross-Product Divergences
*   **Gasoline vs. Distillates:** Both are seasonally tight, but gasoline shows extreme regional volatility (PADD 4 surplus vs. PADD 3 deficit), whereas distillates are uniformly lean.
*   **Crude vs. Products:** The simultaneous draw in crude and the seasonal deficit in products suggest a "balanced-to-tight" market where refinery runs are high but unable to fully replenish stocks.

## 5. Price Implications
*   **WTI:** **Bullish.** Low seasonal positioning (8%) and a strong WoW draw provide a firm floor for crude prices.
*   **Refined Products:** **Bullish.** The combination of sub-seasonal gasoline and distillate levels should support elevated crack spreads.

## 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
**PADD 3 Gasoline Stocks:** Monitor if the -31.4% deficit deepens. If Gulf Coast stocks continue to slide while national demand rises, expect a sharp spike in gasoline futures.
