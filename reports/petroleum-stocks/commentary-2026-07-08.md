## 1. Crude Oil Positioning
*   **NUS Total:** A modest build of 3.0 MMBbl puts total stocks at 411.4 MMBbl. While the WoW build is bearish, the overall position remains 6.3% below the 5-year seasonal average, providing a floor for prices.
*   **PADD 3 Highlight:** The Gulf Coast remains the primary pivot point at 237.2 MMBbl (+3.1% of range). The slight surplus here suggests that refining throughput may be lagging behind crude arrivals or exports are softening.

## 2. Gasoline Positioning
*   **Seasonal Tightness:** Gasoline is the primary bullish driver, sitting 21.6% below the 5-year seasonal range.
*   **Demand Signal:** The 1.9 MMBbl draw confirms strong summer consumption. The severe deficit in PADD 3 (-37.2%) indicates that the primary refining hub is struggling to keep pace with regional demand.

## 3. Distillate Positioning
*   **Demand Signal:** A significant 5.0 MMBbl draw suggests robust diesel demand for freight and industrial activity.
*   **Seasonal Status:** Total stocks are exactly at the 5-year median (0.0%). However, the draw indicates a shift toward tightness, particularly in PADD 3 and PADD 4.

## 4. Cross-Product Divergences
*   **Crude vs. Products:** We are seeing a "refined product squeeze" paired with "crude accumulation." This divergence suggests that the current market bottleneck is not crude availability, but rather refining capacity or operational outages (implied by the PADD 3 gasoline deficit).

## 5. Price Implications
*   **WTI:** **Neutral.** The WoW build offsets the seasonal deficit. Expect WTI to trade sideways unless a larger crude draw emerges.
*   **Refined Products:** **Bullish.** Gasoline cracks should remain elevated given the -21.6% seasonal positioning. Distillates are trending bullish following the 5.0 MMBbl draw.

## 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
**PADD 3 Gasoline Levels:** Monitor if the Gulf Coast gasoline deficit deepens. If PADD 3 stocks continue to slide while crude builds, it confirms a refining bottleneck that will drive gasoline premiums higher.
