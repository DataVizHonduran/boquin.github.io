# EIA Weekly Petroleum Analysis: July 8, 2026

## Executive Summary
The latest snapshot reveals a classic mid-summer divergence: gasoline inventories are under significant seasonal pressure, while distillate stocks are beginning to accumulate. Crude oil remains lean overall, though regional imbalances persist.

| Product | MMBbl | WoW | Seasonal % | Signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Crude Oil** | 408.4 | -3.8 | -5.7% | Bullish |
| **Gasoline** | 214.0 | -2.3 | -13.8% | Strongly Bullish |
| **Distillates** | 108.6 | +2.5 | +4.7% | Bearish |

---

## 1. Crude Oil Positioning
*   **NUS Total:** A 3.8 MMBbl draw keeps total stocks 5.7% below the 5-year seasonal average, suggesting a tight physical market heading into the peak driving season.
*   **PADD 3 Highlight:** The Gulf Coast remains the primary outlier, sitting **10.3% above** its seasonal range. This indicates a regional glut or a strategic build-up at export terminals despite the national draw.
*   **Regional Stress:** PADD 4 (-38.1%) and PADD 5 (-14.9%) are significantly lean, indicating high local consumption or supply chain constraints in the West.

## 2. Gasoline Positioning
*   **Seasonal Tightness:** Stocks are **13.8% below** the 5-year range, a clear signal of high summer demand.
*   **Demand Signal:** The WoW draw of 2.3 MMBbl confirms that consumption is outpacing refinery throughput.
*   **Regional Divergence:** PADD 3 is severely depleted (-37.8%), while PADD 1 and 5 are surprisingly flush, suggesting a need for internal product redistribution.

## 3. Distillate Positioning
*   **Seasonal Surplus:** A WoW build of 2.5 MMBbl has pushed stocks 4.7% above the seasonal norm.
*   **Demand Signal:** With heating demand dormant, the build suggests a softening in diesel/industrial consumption or a shift in refinery yields toward gasoline.
*   **Regional Note:** PADD 5 (+44.9%) and PADD 2 (+32.4%) are heavily oversupplied.

## 4. Cross-Product Divergences
We are seeing a **sharp "Gas-vs-Diesel" split**. Gasoline is in a deficit (seasonal draw/low stocks), while distillates are in a surplus (seasonal build/high stocks). This typically encourages refiners to maximize gasoline yields (high-severity cracking) to capture the summer premium.

## 5. Price Implications
*   **WTI:** **Bullish.** National crude draws and low overall stocks provide a strong floor.
*   **RBOB Gasoline:** **Strongly Bullish.** Significant seasonal deficits and continued WoW draws support higher prices.
*   **ULSD/Heating Oil:** **Bearish.** Inventory builds and a seasonal surplus will likely weigh on distillate cracks.

## 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
**PADD 3 Gasoline Recovery:** Monitor if the Gulf Coast gasoline deficit (-37.8%) begins to close. If PADD 3 continues to draw, expect a spike in regional spot prices and potential export curtailments.
