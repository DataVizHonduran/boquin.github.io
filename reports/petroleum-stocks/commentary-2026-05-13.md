### 1. Crude Oil Positioning
* **Overall Trend:** A modest WoW draw of 2.3 MMBbl keeps total NUS stocks in the lower third of the 5-year seasonal range (35.6%), suggesting a tight physical market heading into the summer.
* **PADD 3 Focus:** The Gulf Coast remains the primary buffer with stocks at 63.5% of the seasonal range. While the national trend is lean, PADD 3's relative surplus prevents an immediate price spike in WTI.
* **Regional Weakness:** PADD 2 (Midwest) is critically low at 12.8% of its range, indicating localized tightness.

### 2. Gasoline Positioning
* **Seasonal Tightness:** Stocks are effectively at the bottom of the 5-year range (0.2%). 
* **Demand Signal:** The 2.5 MMBbl draw confirms strong early-season demand. PADD 3 is in a deficit (-21.5% of range), signaling that the primary refining hub is struggling to keep pace with the summer transition.

### 3. Distillate Positioning
* **Seasonal Tightness:** Stocks have dipped below the 5-year historical floor (-3.6%).
* **Demand Signal:** The 1.3 MMBbl draw suggests resilient diesel demand. PADD 5 (West Coast) is particularly stressed at -34.9% of its seasonal range, likely reflecting regional supply disruptions or high industrial activity.

### 4. Cross-Product Divergences
* **Uniform Tightness:** Unusually, all three major categories are showing WoW draws. 
* **Refining Pressure:** The extreme tightness in Gasoline (0.2%) vs. the moderate surplus in PADD 3 Crude (63.5%) suggests that the bottleneck is not feedstock availability, but rather refining throughput or operational outages.

### 5. Price Implications
* **WTI:** **Bullish.** Low national crude stocks and strong product draws provide a firm floor.
* **Refined Products:** **Strongly Bullish.** Gasoline and Distillates are both trading at or below seasonal norms. Expect crack spreads to widen as the market prices in the scarcity of finished fuels.

### 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
* **PADD 3 Gasoline:** Monitor if the Gulf Coast deficit deepens. If PADD 3 gasoline stocks continue to slide while crude remains high, expect a sharp spike in RBOB futures.
