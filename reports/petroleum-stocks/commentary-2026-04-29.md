# EIA Weekly Petroleum Analysis: April 29, 2026

## Executive Summary
The latest EIA snapshot reveals a broad-based draw across all major product categories. While crude inventories remain moderately positioned, refined product stocks—particularly gasoline and distillates—are flirting with critical seasonal lows, signaling a tight transition into the summer driving season.

| Product | MMBbl | WoW | Seasonal % | Signal |

## 1. Crude Oil Positioning
*   **NUS Total:** A healthy 6.2 MMBbl draw brings total stocks to 459.5 MMBbl. Positioning at 39.8% of the 5-year range suggests a lean but not critical inventory level.
*   **PADD 3 Highlight:** The Gulf Coast remains the primary storage hub but is heavily weighted at 72.6% of its seasonal range. This creates a divergence where national stocks are lean, but the primary export/refining hub remains well-supplied.

## 2. Gasoline Positioning
*   **Seasonal Tightness:** Total stocks are nearly exhausted relative to historical norms (3.0% of 5yr range). 
*   **Demand Signal:** The 6.1 MMBbl WoW draw indicates strong pre-Memorial Day demand. PADD 3 is in a severe deficit (-44.0%), suggesting heavy outflows from the Gulf Coast to meet regional or international demand.

## 3. Distillate Positioning
*   **Seasonal Tightness:** Distillates have dipped into negative territory (-3.5% of 5yr range), indicating stocks are below the lowest levels seen in the last five years for this period.
*   **Demand Signal:** The 4.5 MMBbl draw suggests robust diesel consumption and a lack of heating oil replenishment, with PADD 5 (West Coast) showing extreme tightness (-40.1%).

## 4. Cross-Product Divergences
*   **Crude vs. Products:** We are seeing a "refined product squeeze." While crude oil is comfortably within its seasonal range (39.8%), both gasoline and distillates are at the absolute floor of their historical ranges. This suggests refinery utilization is struggling to keep pace with consumption.

## 5. Price Implications
*   **WTI:** **Net Bullish.** Broad draws across the board and lean national crude stocks provide a firm floor for prices.
*   **Refined Products:** **Strongly Bullish.** The combination of negative seasonal positioning in distillates and near-zero margins in gasoline suggests significant upside for crack spreads.

## 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
**PADD 3 Gasoline Recovery:** Monitor if the Gulf Coast gasoline deficit (-44%) deepens or recovers. A further slide here would likely trigger a spike in RBOB futures as the market prices in a physical shortage.
