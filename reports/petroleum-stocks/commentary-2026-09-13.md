### Crude — near the floor, PADD-mixed
NUS crude drew modestly (-0.4 MMBbl) to 424.1, sitting just 8.9% up the 5-yr band — close to the seasonal low but not breaching it. The PADD split is the story: PADD 3 (Gulf Coast), the most-watched region, is mid-range at 51.6% and comfortable. PADD 2 (Midwest, -4.1%) and PADD 5 (West Coast, -6.2%) are both running *below* their 5-yr low bands — genuinely tight, not just seasonally low. PADD 4 (Rockies) is elevated at 59.8%. Net: national crude looks tight-ish, but it's a regional story (Midwest/West Coast squeeze) more than a coastal supply crunch.

### Gasoline — tight nationally despite the build
NUS gasoline built +1.3 MMBbl to 206.9, but that's still -32.3% versus the 5-yr seasonal band — well below the historical floor for this time of year. Every PADD but 5 (27.7%) is soft-to-tight (PADD 1 at -5.0%, PADD 3 at 3.4%). A build that still leaves stocks that far under normal is a demand/run-rate signal, not a supply-glut one.

### Distillate — winter-fuel watch starting early
Distillate built +2.1 MMBbl to 106.3 (-8.6% of range), driven by builds across most PADDs, but PADD 1 (East Coast, heating-oil territory) is the outlier at -17.4% — meaningfully below its 5-yr low band heading into the shoulder season before winter demand ramps.

### Cross-product divergence
Crude is roughly seasonal (PADD 3 comfortable); both refined products are structurally under their 5-yr bands even with builds this week. That combination — ample-ish crude, tight products — usually reflects strong refinery throughput/exports pulling barrels out of product stocks faster than crude stocks are being drawn. Crack spreads should stay firm as long as this gap persists.

### Price read
- **WTI**: Neutral. Modest crude draw, PADD 3 comfortable — no fresh crude-side catalyst.
- **RBOB / gasoline crack**: Bullish. -32% of range is a structurally tight print; upside risk to crack spreads.
- **ULSD / distillate crack**: Bullish, especially PADD 1. Early-season East Coast tightness ahead of heating demand is the standout risk.

### Watchlist for next Wednesday
PADD 1 distillate (-17.4% of range) — if it doesn't rebuild through September, East Coast heating-oil premiums could start pricing in a supply scare well ahead of the traditional November ramp.
