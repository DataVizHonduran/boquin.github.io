# EIA Weekly Petroleum Analysis: Week Ending May 15, 2026

## Executive Summary
The latest EIA data reveals a bullish tilt in crude and gasoline, offset by a neutral-to-soft distillate profile. The primary narrative is the aggressive draw in crude and the critical tightness in Gulf Coast gasoline stocks as the market enters the peak summer driving season.

## 1. Crude Oil Positioning
*   **NUS Total:** A significant WoW draw of 7.9 MMBbl pushes total stocks to the lower quartile (23.6%) of the 5-year seasonal range.
*   **PADD 3 Highlight:** Gulf Coast stocks remain the primary anchor at 259.2 MMBbl (49.4% of range). While not critically low, the overall NUS draw suggests strong refinery runs or unexpected export demand.

## 2. Gasoline Positioning
*   **Seasonal Tightness:** Total stocks are now below the 5-year seasonal norm (-5.4%).
*   **Demand Signal:** The WoW draw of 1.5 MMBbl indicates strong early-season consumption.
*   **Critical Alert:** PADD 3 gasoline is severely depleted at -55.2% of its seasonal range, signaling a high risk of regional price spikes and potential supply bottlenecks.

## 3. Distillate Positioning
*   **Seasonal Status:** Stocks are essentially flat relative to the 5-year norm (-1.2%).
*   **Demand Signal:** A marginal WoW build of 0.4 MMBbl suggests that diesel demand is currently balanced with production, lacking the bullish momentum seen in the gasoline sector.

## 4. Cross-Product Divergences
*   **Gasoline vs. Distillates:** There is a clear divergence in the refined product complex. Gasoline is trending toward a deficit (especially in PADD 3), while distillates remain stable. This suggests a "summer-heavy" demand profile where leisure travel is outperforming industrial/commercial diesel activity.

## 5. Price Implications
*   **WTI:** **Bullish.** The combination of a large crude draw and low seasonal positioning provides strong fundamental support for crude prices.
*   **Refined Products:** **Bullish (Gasoline) / Neutral (Diesel).** RBOB futures should see upward pressure due to the PADD 3 deficit. ULSD is likely to trade sideways.

## 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
*   **PADD 3 Gasoline Recovery:** Monitor if the Gulf Coast gasoline deficit continues to widen or if imports/production begin to refill the gap. Any further draw here will likely trigger a sharp spike in RBOB cracks.
