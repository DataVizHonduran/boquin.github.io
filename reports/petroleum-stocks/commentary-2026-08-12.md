# EIA Weekly Petroleum Analysis: Aug 12, 2026

## Executive Summary
The latest data reveals a stark divergence between raw crude accumulation and refined product depletion. While crude inventories are building, gasoline is significantly below seasonal norms, suggesting strong late-summer demand despite a crude glut at the Gulf Coast.

| Product | MMBbl | WoW | Seasonal % | Signal |

## 1. Crude Oil Positioning
*   **NUS Total:** A significant build of **17.4 MMBbl** pushes total stocks to 424.4 MMBbl. While the 5-year seasonal position is nearly flat (0.8%), the WoW momentum is bearish.
*   **PADD 3 Highlight:** The Gulf Coast is the primary driver of the build, sitting **22.3% above its seasonal range**. This suggests either a slowdown in exports or a temporary bottleneck in refining throughput.

## 2. Gasoline Positioning
*   **Seasonal Tightness:** Gasoline is heavily undersupplied, sitting **22.2% below the 5-year average**. 
*   **Demand Signal:** The WoW draw of 1.0 MMBbl, combined with extreme tightness in PADD 2 (-50.8%), indicates robust late-season driving demand and potential refinery constraints in the Midwest.

## 3. Distillate Positioning
*   **Seasonal Status:** Stocks are slightly below norm (-6.6%). 
*   **Demand Signal:** The flat WoW change (-0.0) suggests a balance between diesel consumption and production. However, regional tightness in PADD 4 (-28.2%) and PADD 5 (-15.7%) indicates localized diesel pressure on the West Coast.

## 4. Cross-Product Divergences
*   **Crude vs. Products:** We are seeing a "bottleneck" profile. Crude is piling up (especially in PADD 3), while gasoline is severely depleted. This implies that the issue is not a lack of raw material, but rather a lack of refining capacity or operational outages preventing crude from being converted into gasoline.

## 5. Price Implications
*   **WTI:** **Bearish.** The substantial 17.4 MMBbl build and PADD 3 surplus will likely weigh on the front-month contract.
*   **Refined Products:** **Bullish.** The deep seasonal deficit in gasoline provides a strong floor for RBOB prices.
*   **Net Outlook:** Bearish for upstream, Bullish for downstream (crack spreads likely to widen).

## 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
**PADD 3 Crude Levels:** Monitor if the Gulf Coast surplus begins to clear. If PADD 3 stocks continue to rise while gasoline remains tight, expect a sharp increase in refinery utilization or a surge in crude exports to balance the market.
