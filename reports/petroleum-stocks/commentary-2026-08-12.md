# EIA Weekly Petroleum Analysis: Aug 12, 2026

## Executive Summary
The latest snapshot reveals a market characterized by significant seasonal deficits in crude and gasoline, offset by a modest build in raw crude stocks. While the headline crude number is a build, the underlying structural deficit remains the primary driver.

## 1. Crude Oil Positioning
*   **NUS Total:** Despite a WoW build of 2.5 MMBbl, total stocks remain **21.3% below the 5-year seasonal average**, indicating a persistent deficit.
*   **PADD 3 Highlight:** The Gulf Coast remains lean at **-14.1% of its seasonal range**. Given PADD 3's role as the primary export and refining hub, this tightness limits the US's ability to absorb supply shocks without impacting exports.

## 2. Gasoline Positioning
*   **Seasonal Tightness:** Gasoline is the most stressed product, sitting **21.5% below seasonal norms**.
*   **Demand Signal:** The WoW draw of 1.6 MMBbl suggests late-summer driving demand is still robust. The extreme deficit in PADD 2 (-49.8%) indicates localized scarcity in the Midwest.

## 3. Distillate Positioning
*   **Seasonal Status:** Distillates are nearly flat relative to the 5-year range (-3.1%), though the WoW draw of 3.5 MMBbl is notable.
*   **Demand Signal:** The draw is likely driven by diesel consumption rather than heating oil. The massive deficit in PADD 4 (-122.2%) suggests severe regional imbalances in the Rocky Mountains.

## 4. Cross-Product Divergences
*   **Crude vs. Products:** We see a "divergence of flows"—crude is building slightly while refined products are drawing. This suggests a potential temporary slowdown in refinery utilization or a shift in yield patterns, despite strong end-user demand for gas and diesel.

## 5. Price Implications
*   **WTI:** **Net Bullish.** The WoW build is a "noise" event; the -21.3% seasonal deficit provides a strong floor for prices.
*   **Refined Products:** **Bullish.** Gasoline is significantly undersupplied, which should support RBOB crack spreads. Distillates are neutral-to-bullish based on the WoW draw.

## 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
*   **Refinery Utilization Rates:** I will be monitoring if the crude build persists. If crude continues to build while gasoline draws, it signals a refinery outage or maintenance cycle that could spike gasoline prices further.
