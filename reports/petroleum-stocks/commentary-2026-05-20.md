### 1. Crude Oil Positioning
* **NUS Total:** A significant draw of 7.9 MMBbl pushes total stocks deeper into the lower quartile (23.6% of 5yr range), signaling strong refinery runs heading into the summer peak.
* **PADD 3 Highlight:** Gulf Coast stocks remain the primary anchor at 259.2 MMBbl (49.4% of range). While the national trend is lean, the Gulf Coast maintains a healthy buffer, preventing immediate panic in WTI pricing.

### 2. Gasoline Positioning
* **Seasonal Tightness:** Stocks are now below the 5-year seasonal norm (-5.4%). 
* **Demand Signal:** The WoW draw of 1.5 MMBbl, coupled with a severe deficit in PADD 3 (-55.2% of range), indicates that the "summer driving season" ramp-up is consuming inventories faster than refineries can replenish them.

### 3. Distillate Positioning
* **Seasonal Tightness:** Stocks are marginally below the 5-year norm (-1.2%).
* **Demand Signal:** A negligible build of 0.4 MMBbl suggests a balanced market. While heating oil demand has vanished, diesel consumption remains robust, keeping inventories lean but stable.

### 4. Cross-Product Divergences
* **Gasoline vs. Distillates:** We are seeing a clear divergence in urgency. Gasoline is entering a "deficit phase" (especially in the Gulf Coast), while distillates are in a "maintenance phase." Refiners are likely maximizing gasoline yields at the expense of diesel to capture higher summer crack spreads.

### 5. Price Implications
* **WTI:** **Bullish.** The combination of a large crude draw and tightening gasoline stocks provides strong fundamental support for crude prices.
* **Refined Products:** **Strongly Bullish for Gasoline / Neutral for Diesel.** Expect RBOB premiums to widen as PADD 3 inventories remain critically low.

### 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
* **PADD 3 Gasoline Recovery:** Monitor if the Gulf Coast gasoline deficit continues to widen. If stocks drop further below the -55% mark, expect a sharp spike in gasoline futures and potential regional supply disruptions.
