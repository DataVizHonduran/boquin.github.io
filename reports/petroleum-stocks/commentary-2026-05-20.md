# EIA Weekly Petroleum Analysis: May 20, 2026

## Executive Summary
The latest EIA data reveals a tightening crude market coupled with significant regional gasoline deficits, suggesting strong seasonal demand heading into the summer driving peak.

## 1. Crude Oil Positioning
*   **NUS Total:** A healthy draw of 7.9 MMBbl brings total stocks to 445 MMBbl. Positioning remains lean at 23.6% of the 5-year seasonal range.
*   **PADD 3 Highlight:** Gulf Coast stocks sit at 49.4% of the seasonal range. While the national trend is lean, PADD 3 remains the primary buffer, preventing a more aggressive price spike in WTI.

## 2. Gasoline Positioning
*   **Seasonal Tightness:** Total stocks are below the 5-year average (-5.4%). 
*   **Demand Signal:** The WoW draw of 1.5 MMBbl confirms the onset of summer demand. 
*   **Regional Crisis:** PADD 3 is severely undersupplied at -55.2% of its seasonal range, indicating a critical lack of inventory at the primary refining hub.

## 3. Distillate Positioning
*   **Seasonal Status:** Stocks are essentially flat relative to the 5-year norm (-1.2%).
*   **Demand Signal:** A marginal build of 0.4 MMBbl suggests that diesel demand is currently balanced with production, with no immediate signs of the "diesel squeeze" often seen in late Q2.

## 4. Cross-Product Divergences
*   **Gasoline vs. Distillates:** There is a clear divergence in urgency. Gasoline is trending toward a deficit (especially in PADD 3), while distillates remain stable. This suggests refiners are likely maximizing gasoline yields (shifting the "cut") to capture summer margins.

## 5. Price Implications
*   **WTI:** **Bullish.** The combination of a significant crude draw and lean seasonal positioning supports a higher price floor.
*   **Refined Products:** **Bullish (Gasoline) / Neutral (Diesel).** The severe deficit in Gulf Coast gasoline is a strong catalyst for RBOB futures.

## 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
*   **PADD 3 Gasoline Recovery:** Monitor if the Gulf Coast gasoline deficit continues to widen. If it drops further below -55%, expect significant volatility in refined product cracks.
