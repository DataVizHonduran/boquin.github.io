# EIA Weekly Petroleum Analysis: Week Ending 2026-05-22

## Executive Summary
The latest EIA snapshot reveals a broad-based draw across all major product categories. While crude remains lean, the primary narrative is the significant seasonal tightness in refined products, particularly gasoline in the Gulf Coast.

| Product | MMBbl | WoW | Seasonal % | Signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 1. Crude Oil Positioning
*   **NUS Total:** Stocks are lean, sitting at 22.5% of the 5-year seasonal range. The 3.3 MMBbl draw suggests strong refinery runs or tightening imports.
*   **PADD 3 Highlight:** The Gulf Coast remains the primary liquidity hub at 259.2 MMBbl. At 46.9% of its seasonal range, PADD 3 is significantly better supplied than the East and West coasts, acting as the primary buffer against regional shocks.

## 2. Gasoline Positioning
*   **Seasonal Tightness:** Gasoline is in a deficit relative to historical norms (-11.5%). 
*   **Demand Signal:** The WoW draw of 2.6 MMBbl coincides with the ramp-up to the summer driving season. The critical signal is **PADD 3**, which is sitting at -50.1% of its seasonal range, indicating severe regional tightness and potential for price spikes in the Gulf.

## 3. Distillate Positioning
*   **Seasonal Tightness:** Distillates are slightly below the 5-year average (-5.5%).
*   **Demand Signal:** A 2.1 MMBbl draw suggests resilient diesel demand for freight and agriculture, offsetting the typical seasonal decline in heating oil consumption.

## 4. Cross-Product Divergences
*   **Crude vs. Products:** We are seeing a "synchronized draw." Usually, high refinery utilization builds products while drawing crude; however, the simultaneous draw in both crude and refined products suggests that **end-user demand is currently outstripping both production and refining capacity.**

## 5. Price Implications
*   **WTI:** **Bullish.** Low seasonal crude stocks combined with product draws support a higher price floor.
*   **Refined Products:** **Strongly Bullish.** The extreme deficit in PADD 3 gasoline is a major catalyst for RBOB futures.
*   **Overall:** Net Bullish.

## 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
**PADD 3 Gasoline Recovery:** Monitor if the Gulf Coast gasoline deficit continues to widen. If stocks drop further below the -50% seasonal mark, expect immediate volatility in gasoline crack spreads.
