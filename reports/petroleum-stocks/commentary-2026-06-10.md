# EIA Weekly Petroleum Analysis: June 10, 2026

## Executive Summary
The latest EIA data reveals a stark contrast between crude oil depletion and refined product accumulation. While crude stocks are trending toward the bottom of the seasonal range, gasoline and distillates remain critically tight, signaling strong underlying demand despite modest weekly builds.

## 1. Crude Oil Positioning
*   **NUS Total:** A healthy 8.0 MMBbl draw pushes total stocks to the 16.1% mark of the 5-year seasonal range, indicating a lean inventory environment heading into the summer.
*   **PADD 3 Highlight:** The Gulf Coast remains the primary buffer at 36.2% of its seasonal range. This relative surplus in PADD 3 prevents a full-scale price spike despite tightness in other regions.

## 2. Gasoline Positioning
*   **Seasonal Status:** Despite a 3.4 MMBbl build, gasoline stocks are sitting at -2.7% of the 5-year range.
*   **Demand Signal:** The negative seasonal positioning suggests that the "summer driving season" ramp-up is outstripping refinery output. PADD 3 is particularly stressed (-22.8%), suggesting heavy export demand or internal consumption gaps.

## 3. Distillate Positioning
*   **Seasonal Status:** Stocks are marginally below the 5-year norm (-1.6%).
*   **Demand Signal:** A small 1.5 MMBbl build is negligible. The lack of significant inventory accumulation suggests diesel demand remains resilient, likely supported by agricultural activity and freight.

## 4. Cross-Product Divergences
*   **Crude vs. Products:** We are seeing a "squeeze" pattern. Crude is being drawn down (bullish) while refined products are struggling to stay above seasonal norms (bullish). 
*   **Regional Divergence:** PADD 3 is the outlier—holding the most crude (surplus) but the least gasoline (deficit), indicating high refinery throughput but insufficient finished product storage.

## 5. Price Implications
*   **WTI:** **Bullish.** The combination of a significant WoW draw and low seasonal positioning provides a strong floor for crude prices.
*   **Refined Products:** **Bullish.** Gasoline and Distillates are both trading below seasonal norms. Any unplanned refinery outage in PADD 3 would likely trigger a sharp price spike.

## 6. Watchlist for Next Wednesday
**PADD 3 Gasoline Stocks:** Monitor if the Gulf Coast gasoline deficit continues to widen. If PADD 3 drops further below the 5-year range, expect gasoline crack spreads to widen significantly.
