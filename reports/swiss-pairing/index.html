Rafael's Swiss Tournament
National K-12 Grade Championships · Orlando · Dec 11–13 · 2nd Grade section

Lose a few, then the wins come

In a Swiss tournament nobody gets knocked out. Every round, you play a kid who has the same number of points as you. So the games keep getting closer to just right.

Everyone plays 7 games

Lose a game and you still play the next round. Nobody goes home early.

A win is 1 point

A draw is ½ point for each kid. After each round, every kid adds up their points.

3

Same points play each other

Kids who keep losing meet kids who keep losing. Kids who keep winning meet each other. Soon everyone plays someone at their level.

The ratings are real: all 225 kids on the 2nd Grade entry list as of Nov 19, 2026, with Rafael at 581. The games haven't been played yet, so who wins each one is decided by chance, weighted by rating, the way chess ratings predict. Opponent names are made up. Unrated kids count as 400. The field will grow until the event closes registration. Draws come from the ratings too: they are likelier when two kids are close in rating, and when both are stronger (about 4 games in 100 for two 600s, 13 in 100 for two 1800s).
