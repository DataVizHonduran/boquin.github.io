Banxico Watcher: September 2026

Executive Summary

The Junta de Gobierno maintained the overnight interbank funding rate at 6.50% on September 24, 2026. The decision was unanimous, marking a significant shift from the 3-2 split seen in March 2026. While the rate remained unchanged, the board notably dropped its previous signal of a "prolonged pause," suggesting a more flexible approach to future adjustments. Market analysis indicates a growing decoupling between Banxico and the US Federal Reserve, which has introduced increased volatility to the Mexican Peso (MXN). The board remains cautious due to mixed inflation signals, though the unanimous hold suggests a temporary consensus on the current restrictive level.
