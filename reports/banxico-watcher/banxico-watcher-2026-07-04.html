Executive Summary

The Junta de Gobierno maintained the overnight interbank funding rate at 6.50% in a unanimous decision on June 25, 2026. This marks a significant shift from the 3-2 split seen in March, suggesting a temporary consensus for a "prolonged pause." The Board is currently prioritizing the consolidation of inflation convergence over further easing.
