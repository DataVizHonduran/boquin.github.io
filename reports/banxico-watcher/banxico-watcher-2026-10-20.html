Executive Summary

The last 30 days were dominated by the September 24 monetary policy decision, where the Junta de Gobierno unanimously voted to hold the overnight interbank funding rate at 6.50%. While the decision was unanimous, the accompanying communication signaled a pivotal shift: Banxico has dropped its previous guidance of a "prolonged pause," opening the door for future easing. However, this shift occurs amidst mixed inflation signals and increasing volatility in the MXN, as the central bank appears to be decoupling from the US Federal Reserve's trajectory. Recent commentary from Deputy Governor Omar Mejía reinforces a technical focus on the outlook, while market participants remain divided on the timing of the next move.
