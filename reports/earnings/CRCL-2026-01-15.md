## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **Triple beat on EPS** ($0.64 vs. $0.18 consensus) driven by 108% YoY USDC circulation growth to $73.7B, representing 29% stablecoin market share
- **On-chain transaction volume surged 680%** to $9.6T in Q3, demonstrating accelerating institutional and payment adoption despite crypto market volatility
- **Revenue concentration risk remains acute:** 96% of revenue ($711M) derived from reserve income tied to interest rates; Fed rate cuts pose material headwind to 2026 earnings
- **Stock declined 7.3%** on earnings day, extending to ~40% drawdown from highs on margin concerns; currently trading at $82.81, down ~70% from IPO peak of $299

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Multiple at risk given 96% revenue dependence on interest rates facing a Fed easing cycle. Stock down 70% from IPO high reflects market repricing of rate sensitivity. However, non-interest revenue acceleration (+2,800% YoY) and conditional bank charter approval could provide diversification optionality. Valuation appears stretched at ~27x 2026E earnings given execution risk on revenue diversification. Hold-rated consensus (9 Hold, 10 Buy, 4 Sell) with $139-145 price targets implies limited near-term upside.
