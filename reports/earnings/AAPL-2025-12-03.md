## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **Services carry the narrative:** Services segment delivered record $28.8B revenue (+15% YoY), beating consensus by $630M and offsetting iPhone weakness. Subscription base exceeded 1 billion paid accounts, demonstrating recurring revenue momentum.

* **iPhone 17 launch underwhelms:** iPhone revenue of $49.0B missed Street estimates by $1.2B (-2.3%) despite encompassing initial iPhone 17 sales. Supply constraints and muted demand in China offset strength in other geographies. Management attributed some weakness to launch timing dynamics, but margin of miss signals cautious consumer adoption.

* **Tariff headwinds crystallizing:** CFO Kevan Parekh disclosed $1.1B in tariff-related costs in Q4, with Q1 FY2026 impact estimated at $1.4B. Management signaled these costs will compress gross margins by ~100 bps near-term, though partially offset by pricing actions and supply chain adjustments.

* **Stock reaction muted:** Shares traded down ~2% in after-hours following the print, reflecting iPhone miss and tariff cost disclosures despite robust December quarter guidance. Market pricing in margin compression concerns.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**Next Quarter (Q1 FY2026 - December Quarter):**
* **Revenue Guide:** +10% to +12% YoY growth, implying $131-134B (vs. consensus ~$130B prior). Represents acceleration from Q4's +8%.
* **iPhone Specific:** Management guided to "double-digit" iPhone revenue growth YoY, suggesting $60B+ (vs. $54.5B in Q1 FY2025). Confidence driven by iPhone 17 full-quarter impact and holiday demand.
* **Gross Margin:** 47-48%, down ~50 bps QoQ at midpoint due to $1.4B tariff headwind. Implies margin compression of ~105 bps from tariffs, partially offset by favorable mix (Services mix-up, new product pricing).

**Full Year FY2026:**
* No explicit FY guidance provided (consistent with Apple policy).
* Street extrapolating 8-10% revenue growth for FY2026 to ~$450B based on Q1 guide and management commentary.

**Macro & Strategic Commentary:**
* **Tariffs:** Parekh emphasized tariff situation "remains fluid" but company has "multiple levers" including pricing, procurement adjustments, and supply chain reconfiguration. Indicated willingness to absorb some cost vs. pass-through entirely.
* **China:** Management acknowledged "challenging environment" in Greater China but expressed confidence that iPhone 17 innovation and Services momentum will stabilize region in coming quarters.
* **AI Positioning:** Tim Cook highlighted Apple Intelligence features as "still early innings," suggesting multi-year upgrade cycle tailwind. No quantification of AI revenue impact yet.

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**iPhone ($49.0B, +6% YoY):** Missed estimates by $1.2B. Management attributed to supply constraints early in quarter and China softness. iPhone 17 sales described as "very strong start" but insufficient to offset China and timing headwinds. ASP (average selling price) estimated flat YoY despite iPhone 17 Pro mix, suggesting competitive pricing pressure.

**Services ($28.8B, +15% YoY):** All-time record, beat by $630M. Drivers included App Store growth (+12% est.), iCloud expansion (+18% est.), and accelerating advertising revenue. Paid subscriptions surpassed 1 billion for first time. Operating margin estimated at 73-75%, maintaining best-in-class profitability.

**Mac ($8.7B, +13% YoY):** Beat expectations by $140M. Strength driven by M3 chip refresh cycles in MacBook Pro and MacBook Air, as well as enterprise PC replacement tailwinds. Back-to-school and institutional demand robust.

**iPad ($7.0B, flat YoY):** In-line with consensus. Lack of growth reflects product cycle lull (iPad Pro/Air refreshed in Q3, no major launches in Q4). Management indicated new models in pipeline for 2026 to reinvigorate category.

**Wearables, Home & Accessories ($9.0B, flat YoY):** In-line. Apple Watch sales steady, AirPods constrained by supply. Vision Pro contribution remains immaterial (~$200-300M est.). Category viewed as mature, low-single-digit grower near-term.

**Geographic Mix:**
* Americas: $41.7B (+9% YoY) - strength in U.S. across all products
* Europe: $24.9B (+11% YoY) - robust iPhone and Services
* Greater China: ~$15.0B (-2% YoY) - iPhone declines, regulatory/macro headwinds
* Japan: $5.9B (+8% YoY) - stable
* Rest of Asia Pacific: $15.0B (+12% YoY) - India standout (+18% est.), offset China weakness

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Thesis remains constructive but margin-of-safety compressed. Services momentum and installed base growth (2.2B active devices) support long-term recurring revenue transformation, now contributing 28% of total revenue (vs. 20% in 2020). However, iPhone hardware cycle faces growing headwinds: China macro/regulatory risks, ASP plateau, and tariff margin compression create near-term EPS drag of 3-5% vs. prior Street models.

**Valuation Impact:** Multiple compression likely near-term. Stock trades at 31x NTM EPS vs. 5-year average of 28x, implying ~10% downside to fair value ($225-230) absent EPS revisions. December quarter guide strong (+11% revenue at midpoint) but tariff disclosures and China uncertainty warrant de-rating from peak multiples. Maintain price target of $240 (29x FY2026 EPS of $8.25, reflecting modest premium to historical average given Services quality).

**Positioning Recommendation:** Neutral-to-Underweight near-term. Await Q1 FY2026 print (late January 2026) for confirmation that iPhone 17 cycle accelerates and tariff impacts stabilize at guided levels. Long-term holders should maintain core positions given Services durability and capital return commitment, but avoid adding at current 31x multiple. Preferred entry $220-225 range (27-28x), offering 8-10% upside to $240 target with improved risk-reward.
