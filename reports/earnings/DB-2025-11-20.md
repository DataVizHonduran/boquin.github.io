## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

* **Record Profitability, Structural Improvement Validated:** Deutsche Bank delivered record 9M25 profit before tax of €7.7B (+64% YoY), with Q3 contributing €2.4B (+8% YoY). Post-tax RoTE hit 10.9% for 9M25 and 10.7% for Q3 alone, exceeding the bank's FY25 target of >10%. CFO von Moltke's assertion that "structural profitability has risen" is no longer aspirational—it's evidenced in the numbers.

* **Investment Bank Drives Beat; Trading Desk Outperforms:** IB revenue surged +18% YoY to €2.9B in Q3, with Fixed Income trading jumping +19% to €2.48B (beating consensus by +10.7%). This was the primary driver of the overall +21% EPS beat. Origination & Advisory also contributed, though specific O&A revenue not disclosed. IB's 9M25 PBT of €3.3B (+18% YoY) and RoTE of 12.5% signal sustainable momentum beyond volatility-driven gains.

* **Muted Stock Reaction Despite Strong Beat:** Shares dipped modestly in after-hours trading despite the robust results, likely reflecting profit-taking after YTD gains and investor caution about Q4 trading revenue sustainability. However, the announcement of a second €250M buyback provided partial support.

* **Capital Distribution Accelerates:** CET1 ratio of 14.5% (+70bps YoY) enabled the second €250M buyback tranche, bringing FY25 total capital returned to >€900M. With 450bps of CET1 buffer above regulatory minimum, further capital returns likely in FY26.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK (Crucial)

* **FY25 Targets Reaffirmed, No Explicit FY26 Guide:** Management confirmed the bank is on track to meet FY25 targets (RoTE >10%, Cost/Income <65%), both of which have already been achieved in 9M25 (RoTE: 10.9%, C/I: 63%). No explicit Q4 or FY26 numerical guidance provided, consistent with European banking norms.

* **Next Strategic Cycle Coming:** CEO Sewing indicated the bank will unveil its next multi-year strategic plan, building on the "global house bank" strategy. Emphasis on "structural profitability improvement" suggests continuity rather than strategic pivot. Investors will scrutinize FY26-28 RoTE targets and capital return commitments.

* **Macro Commentary Neutral:** No explicit macro headwinds cited for Q4 or FY26. Management noted provision for credit losses declined -7% YoY to €1.3B in 9M25, suggesting benign credit environment persists. FX cited as modest revenue drag. Enterprise client activity remains robust across Corporate Bank and IB.

* **Cost Trajectory:** Noninterest expenses rose +9.2% YoY to €5.2B in Q3, driven by technology investments and compliance. Adjusted costs (excluding transformation charges and litigation) remained flat YoY at €15.2B for 9M25, indicating operating leverage is emerging as revenues outpace cost growth.

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

* **Investment Bank (€2.9B revenue, +18% YoY):**
  * **Fixed Income & Currencies (FIC):** €2.48B (+19% YoY) — Beat consensus by +10.7%. Strength in Macro products, Credit Trading, and FIC Financing. Market share gains evident in volatile rates environment.
  * **Origination & Advisory (O&A):** Revenue not disclosed separately, but management noted "significantly higher year on year and sequentially," driven by Equity Origination.
  * **9M25 Metrics:** PBT €3.3B (+18% YoY), RoTE 12.5%, Cost/Income 55%.

* **Private Bank (€2.4B revenue, +4% YoY):**
  * **Net Interest Income:** €1.6B (+9% YoY) — Benefited from deposit repricing and loan growth.
  * **Commission & Fee Income:** €725M (flat YoY) — Offset by lower asset management fees due to market mix shift.
  * **9M25 Metrics:** PBT €1.8B (+71% YoY), RoTE 10.5%, Cost/Income 70%. Assets under management rose €66B YTD driven by net inflows.

* **Corporate Bank (Revenue ~€2.0B est., flat YoY):**
  * **Corporate Treasury Services:** Steady performance; 9M25 revenue €3.2B (+1% YoY).
  * **Business Banking:** Softness continued; 9M25 revenue €945M (-8% YoY).
  * **9M25 Metrics:** PBT €2.0B (+16% YoY), RoTE 16.0%, Cost/Income 62%. Q3 RoTE: 16.2%.

* **Asset Management (€734M revenue, +11.2% YoY):**
  * Performance and transaction fees drove the increase.
  * **9M25 Metrics:** PBT €666M (+48% YoY), RoTE 25.4%, Cost/Income 61%. Highest RoTE across all segments.

**Key Takeaway:** Investment Bank was the standout, with FIC trading delivering outsized gains. Private Bank showed resilience despite fee pressure. Corporate Bank stable but uninspiring. Asset Management quietly delivering exceptional returns.

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

* **Capital Ratios:** CET1: 14.5% (+70bps YoY, +30bps QoQ). Leverage ratio: 4.6%. CET1 buffer above regulatory minimum (~10%): 450bps, implying ~€4-5B excess capital available for deployment.

* **Liquidity:** LCR: 140% (well above 100% regulatory minimum). NSFR not disclosed but historically >120%. Customer deposits: €663B.

* **Debt Levels:** Specific total debt not disclosed in Q3 release, but bank maintains investment-grade ratings (BBB/Baa2) with stable outlook. Net leverage remains modest relative to equity base.

* **Share Buybacks:**
  * Completed first tranche: €675M (early 2025)
  * Announced second tranche: €250M (Q3 2025)
  * Total FY25 capital returned: >€900M
  * Remaining authorization under second tranche: €250M (the tranche itself is new authorization)

* **Dividend:** €0.68 per share paid for FY24 (May 2025). FY25 dividend expected to be announced March 2026, subject to AGM approval in May 2026. Consensus estimates ~€0.75-0.80/share for FY25 based on improved profitability.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis intact; structural profitability proven, but valuation re-rating contingent on trading revenue sustainability.** The 21% EPS beat and 10.9% RoTE achievement validate DB's turnaround, but the market's muted reaction underscores skepticism about Q4 and FY26 earnings power if FIC trading normalizes. At 0.5x tangible book value (TBV €30.17/share, stock ~€15-16), DB trades at a 50-60% discount to US peers (2-3x TBV) and a 20-30% discount to European peers (0.7-0.8x TBV). The key catalyst for multiple expansion is demonstrating RoTE >10% is sustainable beyond 2025. With €900M+ returned in FY25 and excess capital buffer of 450bps, capital return could reach €1-1.2B in FY26 if profitability holds, implying 6-8% shareholder yield at current price. Risks: (1) Trading revenue mean reversion in Q4/FY26, (2) German economic slowdown impacting Corporate Bank, (3) litigation tail risk. Catalysts: (1) FY26-28 strategic plan with elevated RoTE targets, (2) increased buyback/dividend authorization, (3) sustained IB momentum. **Maintain Overweight; price target €18-20 (0.6-0.65x TBV), implying 20-30% upside.**
