## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **Record quarterly profit** of NT$505.7B exceeded consensus of NT$475.2B by 6%, driven by better-than-expected gross margins (62.3% vs. 59-61% guidance) and robust AI chip demand
- **Advanced nodes dominate:** 3nm/5nm/7nm processes collectively accounted for 77% of wafer revenue, with 3nm alone contributing 28% (up from 23% in Q3)
- **HPC remains the growth engine:** High-performance computing segment comprised 55% of revenue; smartphone recovered slightly to 32% from 30% in Q3
- **Stock reaction:** TSM shares were trading near $331 pre-earnings; options market priced in ~4.9% move; actual post-earnings reaction pending (earnings released today)

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

### Q1 2026 Guidance
| Metric | Guidance | Implied YoY |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| Revenue | $34.6B - $35.8B | +35-40% |
| Gross Margin | 63% - 65% | +370-570bps |

### Full Year 2026 Outlook
- **Revenue growth:** ~30% in USD terms (Bernstein expects 23%)
- **Capex:** $52B - $56B (vs. $40.9B in 2025), representing 27-37% increase
- **Capex allocation:** 70-80% toward advanced technologies (N2, A16)
- **Long-term targets:** 56%+ gross margin through cycle; high-20s% ROE

### Management Commentary
- CEO C.C. Wei affirmed "AI megatrend remains in play" with positive demand signals from largest customers (Apple, NVIDIA)
- CFO Wendell Huang emphasized cost improvement efforts and favorable FX contributing to margin outperformance
- N2P technology introduction on track; A16 scheduled for H2 2026
