# TFC Q3 2025 Review: EPS Beats by 5%, Fee Income Surges; Stock Rallies +3.7% on 2027 ROTCE Target

---

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **Beat driven by fee income surge, margin stabilization**: EPS of $1.04 beat consensus $0.99 by 5% as noninterest income rose sharply (wealth management, investment banking strength), while NIM compressed only -1 bp QoQ to 3.01%—a major deceleration in margin headwinds.
* **Loan growth accelerates (+2.5% QoQ), but credit quality deteriorates modestly**: Average loans rose $7.9B (+2.5% QoQ) led by CRE, C&I, and auto; however, NPL ratio spiked +9 bps QoQ to 0.48% (driven by C&I portfolio), and net charge-offs remained elevated at 0.48%.
* **Stock jumped +3.7% on aggressive 2027 targets**: Management unveiled 15% ROTCE target for 2027 (vs. 13.6% in Q3 2025), guided to $3-4B annual buybacks starting 2026 (vs. $2B in 2025), and lowered CET1 target to 10% by 2027—signaling confidence in earnings trajectory and capital return acceleration.

* **Net Interest Income**: ~+2% QoQ (implies ~$3.75B)
* **Revenue**: +1-2% QoQ (implies ~$5.3-5.35B)
* **Expenses**: Stable QoQ
* **Share Repurchases**: $750M in Q4 2025

**Full Year 2025 Outlook** (Maintained):
* **Adjusted Revenue Growth**: +1.5% to +2.5% vs. 2024
* **Adjusted Expense Growth**: ~+1% vs. 2024
* **Share Repurchases**: $2B for full year 2025

**2026 Expectations** (Accelerating):
* **Revenue Growth**: Expected to more than double vs. 2025 growth rate
* **Operating Leverage**: ~100 bps improvement
* **EPS Growth**: +6-8%
* **Share Repurchases**: $2.5B in 2026 (25% increase vs. 2025)

**2027 Targets**:
* **ROTCE**: 15% (vs. 13.6% in Q3 2025)
* **CET1 Ratio Target**: 10% by end of 2027 (vs. 11.0% in Q3 2025; implies ~100 bps of excess capital for buybacks)
* **Share Repurchases**: $3-4B annually starting 2026 (50-100% increase vs. 2025)

**Management Commentary on Strategy**:
* **Tailwinds**: Fee income momentum (wealth management, investment banking); NIM stabilization (-1 bp QoQ vs. -10+ bps in prior quarters); loan growth accelerating (+2.5% QoQ); aggressive capital return authorized ($3-4B annually post-2026)
* **Headwinds**: Deposit attrition (-1.0% QoQ to $396.6B avg. deposits); rising NPLs (+9 bps QoQ to 0.48%); elevated NCO ratio (0.48%); efficiency ratio rose to 58% (vs. est. 56.8%)

---

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**Net Interest Income & Margin**:
* NII: $3.68B (+1.2% QoQ, +2.2% YoY) – beat internal expectations despite deposit declines
* NIM: 3.01% taxable-equivalent (-1 bp QoQ) – **major stabilization** (slowest compression in 8+ quarters)
* Loan yield compression decelerating; deposit beta tailwinds emerging

**Loan Portfolio** ($323.7B, +1.6% QoQ):
* Average loans held for investment: $320.5B (+2.5% QoQ, +$7.9B)
* **CRE**: Strong growth (specific $ not disclosed)
* **C&I**: Solid growth, but NPL spike noted in this segment
* **Consumer**: Other consumer loans grew; indirect auto portfolio expanded
* **Residential Real Estate**: Modest activity

**Deposit Base** ($396.6B avg., -1.0% QoQ):
* Average deposits: -$3.9B QoQ (-1.0%) – driven by lower short-term client deposits
* Mix shift: Time deposits +7.4% QoQ; interest checking -6.0% QoQ
* Noninterest-bearing deposit migration continues

**Fee Income** (Strong Performer):
* Noninterest income surged, driven by:
  * **Wealth Management**: Strong client activity, AUM growth
  * **Investment Banking**: Advisory/underwriting fees accelerated
  * **Trust Income**: Stable

**Credit Quality** (Modest Deterioration):
* **Nonperforming Loans**: +$313M QoQ; NPL ratio 0.48% (+9 bps QoQ)
* **Net Charge-Offs**: $385M; NCO ratio 0.48% (elevated but stable)
* **Provision for Credit Losses**: $436M
* **Allowance for Loan Losses (ALLL)**: $4.99B, or 1.54% of loans held for investment
