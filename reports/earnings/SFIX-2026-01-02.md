## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **Revenue accelerates; ninth consecutive quarter of beat vs. outlook.** SFIX posted $342.1M in Q1 revenue (+7.3% YoY), surpassing guidance and consensus ($336M), driven by 9.6% AOV growth (ninth straight quarter) and stabilizing client metrics. Active clients declined 5.2% YoY to 2.3M but registered sequential growth in men's business for the first time in recent quarters. Management highlighted market share gains as revenue growth outpaced Circana's 1% estimate for U.S. apparel market.

* **Margin pressure from mix shift and logistics overshadows profitability gains.** Gross margin contracted 180 bps YoY to 43.6% due to category mix shift toward lower-margin activewear/footwear and elevated transportation costs. Despite this, adjusted EBITDA of $13.4M (3.9% margin) exceeded outlook, reflecting operational leverage. Contribution margin held above 30% for seventh consecutive quarter at 32.5%.

* **FY26 guidance raised on revenue; EBITDA outlook improved.** Management increased FY26 revenue guidance to $1.32-1.35B (implying +4.2% to +6.5% growth) and adjusted EBITDA to $38-48M. Q2 guide of $335-340M revenue (+7.3% to +8.9% YoY) signals sustained momentum, though management cited macro headwinds as risk to second-half performance. Company reaffirmed expectation for full-year FCF positive.

* **Stock reaction muted; turnaround narrative intact but valuation constrained.** Shares traded flat to modestly positive following the release as revenue beat and raised guidance offset margin compression concerns. CEO highlighted AI-driven visualization tools and family account penetration (92% of female clients shopping for spouses/partners) as growth drivers. Sixth consecutive quarter of improvement in YoY active client decline signals stabilization.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

### Q2 Fiscal 2026 Guidance
* **Revenue:** $335-340M (+7.3% to +8.9% YoY); midpoint $337.5M
* **Adjusted EBITDA:** $10-13M; margin ~3.2% at midpoint

### FY 2026 Guidance (Raised)
* **Revenue:** $1.32-1.35B (+4.2% to +6.5% growth); **raised** from prior guidance
* **Adjusted EBITDA:** $38-48M; **raised** from prior outlook
* **Gross Margin:** 43-44% (Q1 at 43.6%, within range)
* **Advertising Expense:** 9-10% of revenue
* **Free Cash Flow:** Positive for full year

### Management Commentary on Macro
* **Consumer resilience across income segments:** Management noted client demand remains resilient across income cohorts, with no material deterioration despite macro uncertainty. However, cited "macro headwinds" as consideration for conservative second-half revenue guidance.
* **Market share gains accelerating:** Q1 revenue growth of 7.3% "meaningfully outpaced Circana's estimated 1% growth for the broader U.S. apparel, accessories, and footwear market," signaling share capture from traditional retail.
* **Category expansion unlocks $1B incremental TAM:** Management highlighted expanded relevance in activewear/athleisure, footwear, and accessories as potential to unlock approximately $1 billion of incremental revenue opportunity.
* **AI-driven engagement:** Vision generative AI visualization tool (lets shoppers see themselves in outfits) "resonating with clients" and driving higher conversion rates.

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

### Client & Engagement Metrics
* **Active Clients:** 2.3M (down 5.2% YoY but sequential growth in men's; sixth consecutive quarter of improvement in YoY decline rate)
* **Revenue per Active Client (RPAC):** $559 (+5.3% YoY); seventh consecutive quarter of growth, driven by 9.6% AOV expansion
* **Dormant Clients:** Lowest count in five years, signaling improved re-engagement and retention

### Category Performance (Women's Business)
* **Seasonal Categories:** Sweaters and coats up 19% YoY; sneakers up 63%; wide-leg denim up 217%
* **Family Accounts:** 92% of female clients shopping for spouses/partners, exceeding expectations and driving incremental household penetration

### Category Performance (Men's Business)
* **Revenue Growth:** Second consecutive quarter of double-digit revenue growth
* **Seasonal Categories:** Combined growth of 57% YoY
* **Sequential Active Client Growth:** First time in recent quarters, signaling stabilization after prolonged decline

### Strategic Category Expansion
* **Activewear/Athleisure, Footwear, Accessories:** Management identified these categories as unlocking ~$1B incremental revenue opportunity, though mix shift toward lower-margin products pressured gross margin in Q1

* **Dividends:** **No dividend program**; company retains all capital for growth investments and working capital
* **Free Cash Flow:** $5.6M in Q1 from continuing operations; full-year FCF positive guidance reaffirmed
* **Working Capital:** Improved inventory management and client re-engagement lowering working capital drag

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Turnaround inflecting but margin structure remains challenged.** SFIX delivered third consecutive quarter of 7%+ revenue growth, beating consensus and raising FY26 guidance, while stabilizing active client trends (sixth consecutive quarter of YoY improvement) validate the strategic pivot toward AI personalization and category expansion. However, gross margin compression (180 bps YoY to 43.6%) from lower-margin activewear/footwear mix and elevated logistics costs signals structural headwinds to profitability scaling. At 3.9% adjusted EBITDA margin, the company remains far from historical mid-teens peak profitability. Guidance raise reflects management confidence, but conservative second-half outlook given macro uncertainty limits re-rating potential. **Hold; accumulate on weakness if Q2 demonstrates margin stabilization and active client sequential growth sustains.**
