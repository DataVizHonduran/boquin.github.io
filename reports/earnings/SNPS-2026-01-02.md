## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **Revenue miss drives 2.3% after-hours selloff despite EPS beat.** SNPS reported $2.255B in Q4 revenue, missing consensus by $37M (1.6%), though non-GAAP EPS of $2.90 beat by $0.07 (2.5%). Revenue shortfall attributed to Design IP segment weakness (down 21.4% YoY to $407M) and China headwinds (down 18% for full FY25, excluding ANSYS). Design Automation revenue surged 65.2% YoY to $1.85B, driven by $668M ANSYS contribution in Q4.

* **ANSYS integration progressing; margin expansion roadmap intact.** Full-year FY25 revenue reached record $7.05B (+15% YoY), including $757M ANSYS contribution. Non-GAAP operating margin of 37.3% (+320 bps YoY guidance for FY26 to 40.5%) reflects accelerating cost synergies despite $225M restructuring charges (10% workforce reduction planned). Free cash flow of $1.35B exceeded expectations on accelerated collections; management guided FY26 FCF to $1.9B.

* **FY26 guidance implies 36% growth but China/IP risks temper outlook.** Management projected FY26 revenue of $9.56-9.66B (+36% at midpoint), with ANSYS contributing $2.9B (double-digit growth). However, guidance assumes no China recovery and "muted" Design IP growth (transitional year). Management cited "multitrillion-dollar AI infrastructure build-out" as tailwind for semiconductor demand, offsetting weakness in industrial and automotive end markets.

* **Stock sold off on revenue miss; debt reduction and margin expansion key to re-rating.** Shares declined 2.3% after hours as revenue miss overshadowed EPS beat. Management announced $2.55B term loan prepayment in H1 FY26 (following $850M Q4 and $900M November repayments), reducing debt from $13.5B. Nvidia's recent $2B investment validates AI-driven design automation thesis, but near-term overhang from China export restrictions and IP segment weakness limits multiple expansion.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

### Q1 Fiscal 2026 Guidance
* **Revenue:** $2.365-2.415B; midpoint $2.39B (+39% YoY at midpoint)
* **Non-GAAP EPS:** $3.52-3.58; midpoint $3.55

### FY 2026 Guidance
* **Revenue:** $9.56-9.66B; midpoint $9.61B (+36% YoY)
* **ANSYS Revenue Contribution:** $2.9B at midpoint (double-digit growth vs. FY25 $757M run-rate)
* **Non-GAAP Operating Margin:** 40.5% at midpoint (+320 bps vs. FY25 37.3%)
* **Non-GAAP EPS:** $14.32-14.40; midpoint $14.36 (+11% vs. FY25 $12.91)
* **Free Cash Flow:** ~$1.9B (+41% vs. FY25 $1.35B)
* **Operating Cash Flow:** ~$2.2B
* **Restructuring Charges:** ~$225M related to 10% workforce reduction

### Management Commentary on Macro
* **AI infrastructure tailwind:** "Multitrillion-dollar AI infrastructure build-out" driving robust semiconductor demand; Design Automation well-positioned as customers accelerate chip complexity and time-to-market requirements.
* **Geographic bifurcation:** China declined 18% in FY25 (down 22% excluding ANSYS); management assumes "no material environment improvement" in FY26. Mobile and automotive strengthening; industrial remains subdued.
* **IP business transitional year:** Management expects "muted growth" in Design IP for FY26 due to product cycle dynamics and export restrictions, though "mid-teens" long-term growth target reaffirmed based on backlog strength.
* **ANSYS integration on track:** Divestitures of Optical Solutions and PowerArtist completed in October. Separate go-to-market strategies maintained (integrated for semiconductor customers; separate for broader ANSYS base). First joint solutions expected in FY26.

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

### Design Automation (81.9% of Q4 Revenue)
* **Q4 Revenue:** $1.85B (+65.2% YoY), benefiting from $668M ANSYS contribution
* **Full-Year FY25 Revenue:** $5.3B (+26% YoY); excluding ANSYS, grew ~8%
* **Adjusted Operating Margin:** ~42% in FY25 (41.5% in Q4)
* **Key Drivers:** AI-driven workload acceleration; advanced node (3nm, 2nm) design wins; ANSYS simulation/analysis tools expanding TAM
* **Customer Highlights:** Nvidia's $2B investment validates strategic positioning in AI chip design

### Design IP (18.1% of Q4 Revenue)
* **Q4 Revenue:** $407.2M (down 21.4% YoY vs. $517.8M in Q4 FY24)
* **Full-Year FY25 Revenue:** $1.75B (down 8% YoY)
* **Adjusted Operating Margin:** 24% in FY25 (13.8% in Q4, down from prior-year levels)
* **Headwinds:** Export restrictions in China impacting licensing; product title maturity in certain categories
* **Outlook:** Management expects "muted growth" in FY26 as transitional year; long-term "mid-teens" growth target intact based on backlog and customer engagement

### Geographic Performance
* **China:** Down 18% in FY25 (excluding ANSYS, down 22%); no recovery assumed in FY26 guidance
* **Rest of World:** Robust demand in North America and Europe; mobile/automotive strengthening offsetting industrial weakness

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Margin expansion thesis intact but China/IP headwinds create near-term overhang.** SNPS delivered on ANSYS integration execution with 320 bps operating margin expansion guided for FY26 (37.3% to 40.5%) and accelerating FCF ($1.35B to $1.9B), while Design Automation growth (+65% YoY in Q4) validates AI-driven semiconductor design tailwinds. However, revenue miss (1.6% vs. consensus), China deterioration (down 18% FY25), and "muted" FY26 IP growth outlook temper re-rating potential despite 36% topline growth guide. Debt reduction roadmap ($2.55B prepayment in H1 FY26) and Nvidia's $2B validation strengthen balance sheet and strategic positioning. **Hold; accumulate on weakness if Q1 FY26 demonstrates stabilizing IP trends and China bottoming.**
