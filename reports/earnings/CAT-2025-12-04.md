## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **First positive revenue growth in seven quarters** driven by 10% YoY sales increase to $17.6B, beating estimates by $850M on higher volumes across all three segments (Construction +7%, Resource +2%, Energy & Transportation +17%).
* **EPS beat (+10% vs. consensus) masked margin compression** as tariff headwinds totaling $600M in Q3 (140 bps impact) drove operating margin down 220 bps YoY to 17.3%; CFO flagged Q4 tariff hit will be highest yet.
* **Record $39.8B backlog (+$2.4B QoQ) and raised FY2025 guidance** signal thesis intact despite macro headwinds; **stock surged +12%** intraday to $585 on strong demand visibility, particularly in Energy & Transportation (+17% YoY) driven by data center power generation (+33% YoY).

---

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**Full Year 2025** (Raised):
* **Sales and revenues**: Now expected to show modest growth vs. 2024 (up from prior guidance)
* **Services revenue**: Expected flat vs. 2024
* **Tariff impact**: Full-year net impact expected between $1.6B and $1.75B

**Q4 2025 Outlook**:
* **Sales**: Strong growth anticipated vs. Q4 2024, with higher volumes expected across all three segments
* **Adjusted operating margin**: Expected higher YoY despite elevated tariff headwinds (CFO noted Q4 will see highest quarterly tariff impact)

**CFO Commentary on Macro**:
* **Tailwinds**: Record backlog ($39.8B), sales to users +12% YoY (led by Energy & Transportation +25%), data center reciprocating engine demand +33% YoY
* **Headwinds**: Tariffs remain primary pressure (140 bps drag in Q3, worsening in Q4); unfavorable price realization; elevated SG&A and R&D expenses

---

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**Energy & Transportation** (35% of sales) – **Best performer**:
* Sales: $8.4B (+17% YoY, +$1.2B)
* Segment profit: $1.68B (+17% YoY, +$245M)
* Power Generation +31% YoY (data center demand surge)
* Oil & Gas +20% YoY
* Industrial and Transportation each +5% YoY

**Construction Industries** (38% of sales):
* Sales: $6.76B (+7% YoY, +$415M)
* Volume drove +$568M; favorable FX (euro) +$69M; offset by unfavorable price realization -$262M
* Segment profit pressured by margin compression

**Resource Industries** (18% of sales) – **Laggard**:
* Sales: $3.1B (+2% YoY)
* Segment profit: $499M (-19% YoY, -$120M)
* Unfavorable manufacturing costs primarily reflected tariff impact
