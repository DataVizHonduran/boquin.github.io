## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

- **27th Straight Quarter Above 30% Growth**: Q3 revenue hit $7.41B (+39% YoY), driven by 34% GMV acceleration in Brazil and 49% fintech growth. Mercado Pago MAUs reached 72M (+29% YoY) with credit portfolio surging 83% to $11.0B, cementing MELI's fintech leadership in LatAm.

- **EPS Miss Driven by FX and Tax, Not Operations**: While EPS of $8.32 missed consensus by 11.8%, the shortfall stemmed from Argentine peso devaluation losses and higher tax rates—not operational weakness. Revenue beat by 2.2%, and operating income grew 30% YoY to $724M despite margin compression from strategic investments.

- **Brazil Momentum Accelerating**: Lowering free shipping thresholds catalyzed Brazil GMV growth to 34% YoY (FX-neutral), with items sold up 42% YoY. Brazil now represents 54% of revenue ($4.01B), validating management's aggressive logistics investments. Mexico GMV also accelerated to 34% YoY.

- **Stock Reaction Muted**: Shares declined modestly in after-hours trading, reflecting the EPS miss and 70 bps operating margin contraction (9.8% vs. 10.5% prior year) as MELI prioritizes market share over near-term profitability in key markets.

- **Headwind**: Rising tax rates impacting net income; operating margin compressed 70 bps YoY to 9.8% due to strategic investments prioritizing growth over profitability

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis intact; EPS miss is noise, not signal.** MELI's Q3 results validate the secular growth story: 27th consecutive quarter above 30% revenue growth, fintech revenue accelerating to +49% YoY, and Brazil/Mexico GMV re-accelerating to 34%. The 11.8% EPS miss reflects transitory FX losses and higher taxes, not operational deterioration—operating income still grew 30% YoY. Near-term margin compression (9.8% vs. 10.5% prior year) is the cost of cementing market leadership through aggressive free shipping and logistics investments. With $9.2B cash, $11B credit portfolio (+83% YoY), and 72M fintech MAUs, MELI remains the dominant LatAm e-commerce and fintech platform. Multiple likely stable given revenue beat and sustained growth trajectory; any post-earnings weakness represents a tactical entry point ahead of Q4 seasonality. Key risk: sustained Argentine peso volatility and margin dilution from prolonged investment phase.
