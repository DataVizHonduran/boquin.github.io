## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

- **Revenue miss overshadowed earnings beat:** Despite adjusted EPS surpassing estimates by 5.7%, flat revenue and a 16% decline in linear TV network revenue drove an 8% stock selloff on release day. Investors remain focused on top-line trajectory.
- **Streaming transformation complete:** DTC swung from a $4B operating loss three years ago to $1.3B operating profit in FY2025—a $5.3B swing. Q4 DTC operating income grew 39% YoY to $352M.
- **Parks delivered record results again:** Experiences segment posted record annual operating income of $10B (+8% YoY) and record Q4 operating income of $1.9B (+13%).
- **Aggressive capital return signals confidence:** Disney doubled its FY26 buyback target to $7B and raised the dividend 50% to $1.50/share, returning to shareholder-friendly mode after years of deleveraging.

Management reiterated expectations for **double-digit adjusted EPS growth in FY27** as well, supported by:
- Two new cruise ships launching

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

### Entertainment ($10.2B revenue, -6% YoY)

| Sub-Segment | Q4 Revenue | YoY Change | Q4 Operating Income |
|-------------|------------|------------|---------------------|
| **Linear Networks** | — | -16% | Declined |
| **Direct-to-Consumer** | +8% | +8% | $352M (+39%) |
| **Content Sales/Licensing** | — | -$368M vs. PY | Tough comp vs. Inside Out 2/Deadpool |

- **DTC:** Added 12.4M net subs across Disney+/Hulu. Disney+ core reached 132M subs. Full-year DTC operating income of $1.3B.
- **Linear:** Continued structural decline. Ad revenue partially offset by affiliate fee increases.
- **Theatrical:** Weak Q4 comps vs. blockbuster Q4 FY24 (Inside Out 2, Deadpool & Wolverine). Full calendar year 2025 still exceeded $4B global box office for fourth consecutive year.

### Experiences ($8.77B revenue, +6% YoY)

| Sub-Segment | Q4 Revenue | YoY Change | Q4 Operating Income |
|-------------|------------|------------|---------------------|
| **Domestic Parks** | $5.86B | +6% | — |
| **International Parks** | $1.74B | +10% | $375M (+25%) |
| **Consumer Products** | — | — | — |

- **Record Operating Income:** $10.0B for full year (+$723M YoY), $1.9B in Q4 (+$219M)
- **International outperformance:** International parks operating income grew 25% in Q4
- **Consumer Products:** Stitch merchandise exceeded $4B in FY25 retail sales

### Sports/ESPN ($911M operating income, -$18M YoY)

- Domestic ESPN operating income declined 3% vs. prior year
- Higher marketing and programming costs partially offset by advertising and affiliate revenue growth
- ESPN standalone streaming app launch on track; critical for future segment trajectory

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Mixed quarter but thesis strengthening for patient investors.** The revenue miss and linear TV headwinds drove the selloff, but the fundamental story is improving: streaming is now profitable and scaling, parks are generating record cash flow, and management is returning capital aggressively. At ~18x forward P/E with double-digit EPS growth guidance for FY26 and FY27, valuation is reasonable if DTC continues its profitability trajectory. Key risks remain linear TV secular decline and consumer discretionary sensitivity in parks. Multiple expansion likely requires sustained revenue acceleration, not just cost discipline.
