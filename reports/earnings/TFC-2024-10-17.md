## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

* **Beat driven by investment banking resurgence and expense discipline.** Truist posted adjusted EPS of $0.97, beating consensus of $0.89 by 8.99%, driven by 79% YoY surge in investment banking revenue and 2.4% sequential revenue growth. Net interest income rose 2.2% QoQ to $3.66B with NIM expanding 10 bps to 3.12%.
* **Loan and deposit contraction reflects weak commercial demand.** Average loans declined $3B (1%) QoQ as commercial client demand and line utilization remained muted. Deposits also declined, though the bank maintained disciplined pricing. Management cited cautious CRE posture with office portfolio down $232M and HVCRE loans declining 10.2% to $1.18B.
* **Capital deployment accelerates; CET1 stable at 11.6%.** Truist returned $1.2B to shareholders through dividends and $500M in buybacks in Q3, with plans for an additional $500M buyback in Q4. Dividend and total payout ratios stood at 52% and 90%, respectively. CET1 ratio held at 11.6%.
* **Stock reaction:** Shares rose modestly on the beat, with investors rewarding the investment banking strength and disciplined capital return despite tepid loan growth.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

### Q4 2024 Guidance
* **Net Interest Income:** Expected to decline 1.5%, factoring in two anticipated federal rate cuts. Lower commercial loan balances and margin pressure anticipated.
* **Net Interest Margin:** Management projected margin compression in Q4 due to temporary beta lag from rate cuts, with stabilization expected in Q1 2025 and improvement as further cuts materialize.
* **Expenses:** Targeted to rise 4% QoQ due to strategic investments.

### Full Year 2024 Guidance (Revised)
* **Revenue:** Slight decline expected for FY 2024
* **Adjusted Expenses:** Projected to decrease compared to 2023
* **Net Charge-Offs:** Expected closer to 60 basis points

### Management Commentary on Headwinds/Tailwinds

**Headwinds:**
* **Weak commercial loan demand:** Average loans down $3B (1%) QoQ; commercial client demand and line utilization remain subdued.
* **Rate cut pressure on NIM:** Two Fed rate cuts anticipated in Q4 with temporary beta lag compressing margins before stabilization in Q1 2025.
* **CRE caution persists:** Management maintaining "somewhat cautious" stance on commercial real estate despite seeing selective opportunities. Office portfolio down $232M QoQ with reserves increased to 10.4%; HVCRE loans declined 10.2% to $1.18B.
* **Deposit repricing lag:** Q4 margin compression expected from deposit pricing adjustments.

**Tailwinds:**
* **Investment banking momentum:** Revenue up 79% YoY and 43% YTD, reflecting robust capital markets activity and M&A advisory strength.
* **NIM expansion trajectory:** Net interest margin improved 10 bps QoQ to 3.12% (+20 bps YoY), with management expecting stabilization in Q1 2025 and improvement with further rate cuts.
* **Fee income growth:** Noninterest income rose 3.1% QoQ, driven by investment banking and trading gains.
* **Expense discipline:** Adjusted expenses declining YoY despite strategic investments; positive operating leverage maintained.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis intact; investment banking tailwind offsets loan growth headwinds.** Truist delivered a solid Q3 beat driven by 79% YoY surge in investment banking revenue and disciplined expense management, more than offsetting tepid loan growth (-1% QoQ) and deposit declines. The 10 bps NIM expansion to 3.12% signals stabilization after years of compression, though Q4 will see temporary pressure from Fed rate cuts before reaccelerating in 2025. CRE exposure remains well-managed with office and HVCRE portfolios declining and reserves building. At 11.6% CET1 and 90% total payout ratio, capital deployment is robust, with $1B in buybacks across Q3-Q4 2024. Multiple likely holds at 1.1-1.2x tangible book as investors weigh investment banking momentum against commercial loan stagnation. Buy rating for investors seeking regional bank exposure with capital markets upside; revisit if loan demand inflects positively in 2025.
