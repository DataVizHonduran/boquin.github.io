# HEADLINE: PWR Q3 2025 Review: Revenue Beats on Power Infrastructure Demand; Record Backlog Drives 2026 Thesis

## THE DASHBOARD

| Metric | Actual | YoY % | Vs. Consensus |
|--------|--------|-------|---------------|
| Revenue | $7.63B | +17.5% | Beat by $220M (+3.0%) |
| Adjusted EPS | $3.33 | +22.4% | Beat by $0.09 (+2.8%) |
| GAAP EPS | $2.24 | -17.6% | Miss (vs. $3.24) |

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **Primary driver of beat**: Accelerating demand in electric power infrastructure drove 17.9% YoY growth in Electric segment (81% of revenues) to $6.17B; segment margin expanded 40bps to 11.4%. Underground & Infrastructure segment (19% of revenues) delivered $1.46B, up 15.9% YoY with margin expansion to 8.4% from 7.5%.

- **Record backlog**: $39.2B backlog (+15.4% YoY) and $21.0B remaining performance obligations underpin multi-year visibility. Management highlighted momentum from data center power generation projects and utility grid hardening.

- **Thesis reinforcement**: Company raised FY2025 revenue guide to $27.8B-$28.2B (implying Q4 revenue of $6.6B-$7.0B) and FCF guide to $1.5B midpoint. Management expects double-digit EPS growth in 2026 with record backlog providing revenue visibility.

- **After-hours reaction**: Stock traded higher post-announcement on raised guidance and backlog strength, offsetting GAAP EPS miss attributable to non-cash acquisition-related intangible amortization.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**FY2025 (Raised):**
- Revenue: $27.8B to $28.2B (prior: $27.2B to $28.0B; consensus: $27.9B)
- Net cash from operations: $1.85B to $2.25B
- Free cash flow: $1.30B to $1.70B (prior midpoint: ~$1.4B)
- Adjusted diluted EPS: $10.33 to $10.83 (no change)

**FY2026 Outlook:**
- Management expects another year of double-digit adjusted EPS growth
- Record backlog of $39.2B provides substantial revenue visibility into 2026 and beyond
- No specific revenue or margin targets disclosed

**Macro commentary:**
- CFO highlighted robust demand environment driven by AI-related data center power needs, utility grid modernization, and renewable energy integration
- Transmission infrastructure build-out accelerating as utilities address capacity constraints
- Regulatory tailwinds from federal infrastructure spending and state-level grid hardening mandates
- No material headwinds cited; supply chain constraints have normalized

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**Electric Infrastructure Solutions (80.9% of revenue):**
- Revenue: $6.17B (+17.9% YoY)
- Operating margin: 11.4% (up 40bps from 11.0% prior year)
- Key driver: Large-scale transmission projects, data center power generation platform, and renewable energy integration

**Underground Utility & Infrastructure Solutions (19.1% of revenue):**
- Revenue: $1.46B (+15.9% YoY)
- Operating margin: 8.4% (up 90bps from 7.5% prior year)
- Key driver: Natural gas infrastructure expansion and communications infrastructure deployment

**Notable developments:**
- Announced new "total solutions" power generation platform including major NiSource project to deliver ~3 gigawatts of capacity
- Record backlog reflects accelerating Electric segment demand; company well-positioned to capitalize on AI/data center power infrastructure wave

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

**Cash & liquidity:**
- Cash position: $610M (as of Q3 2025)
- Total debt/equity: 71.6%
- Generated $726.3M in free cash flow year-to-date through Q3
- Issued $1.5B of notes during 2025 to recapitalize balance sheet

**Share buybacks:**
- Repurchased 538,559 shares for $134.6M in Q3
- $365.1M remaining under current authorization
- Buyback activity demonstrates management confidence in growth trajectory

**Dividends:**
- Board declared quarterly dividend of $0.11/share ($0.44 annualized)
- Represents ~10% increase from prior quarterly dividend paid in October 2025
- Modest payout ratio reflects capital allocation priority toward growth investments and M&A

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Thesis intact; growth re-accelerating. Record backlog, margin expansion, and raised guidance validate PWR's positioning at the intersection of three secular tailwinds: (1) AI-driven data center power demand, (2) utility grid modernization, and (3) renewable energy integration. FY2026 double-digit EPS growth guidance with $39B backlog visibility supports premium multiple. GAAP EPS miss is non-operational (intangible amortization); focus remains on adjusted metrics. FCF inflection underway with 2H2025 run-rate tracking to $1.5B+ annually. Valuation appears reasonable at current levels given multi-year earnings CAGR and infrastructure super-cycle exposure.
