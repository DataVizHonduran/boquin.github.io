## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

- **Historic Milestone:** Premium cabin revenue ($5.70B) exceeded main cabin revenue ($5.62B) for the first time in Delta's 100-year history, signaling structural shift toward high-yield passengers.
- **Revenue Miss Driven by Shutdown:** December quarter revenue growth was impacted by ~2 points from the 43-day U.S. government shutdown in Oct-Nov 2025, resulting in a ~$200M revenue hit concentrated in Domestic.
- **Conservative 2026 Guide Spooked Investors:** Full-year 2026 EPS guidance midpoint of $7.00 fell short of Street consensus at $7.32, triggering the sell-off despite record 2025 results.
- **Stock Reaction:** Shares dropped **5%+** in pre-market trading on January 13, closing at $68.50 (-3.6%) despite the EPS beat.
