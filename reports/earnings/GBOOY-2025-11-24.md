## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **Primary driver of miss (with context)**: EPS miss of $0.18 (-12.7% vs. $1.42 consensus) entirely attributable to one-time Bineo impairment charge of MXN 1.3B plus one-off commercial loan provision. Stripping out extraordinary items, core banking performance robust: NIM expanded 70bps YoY to 6.9%, loan portfolio grew 10% (ex-government), and consumer lending surged 12% YoY. ROE of 22.3% YTD remains among highest in LatAm banking sector, validating franchise quality despite reported miss.

- **Insurance segment outperforming**: Insurance business net income grew 20% in 9M2025 vs. 9M2024, driven by higher billing issuance in life insurance segment. Insurance now represents meaningful earnings diversification beyond traditional banking, contributing ~15-20% of consolidated profits. Strong underwriting discipline and Mexico's growing middle class supporting structural growth in this segment.

- **Digital banking setback but core intact**: Bineo impairment reflects strategic pivot to divest unprofitable digital banking venture after ~3 years of investment. Management confirmed no additional Bineo charges expected, effectively closing chapter on digital experiment. Core Banorte franchise remains dominant in Mexico with strong retail banking momentum (consumer loans +12% YoY) and market-leading NIM (6.9%, among highest in sector).

- **Stock reaction**: Shares rose +1.02% post-earnings as market looked through Bineo impairment to focus on core banking strength, NIM expansion, and 85% total dividend payout ratio guidance (including extraordinary dividend of MXN 6.99/share). Valuation at ~8.8x P/E (TTM) and 1.9x P/BV remains attractive given 22%+ ROE profile.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**FY2025 Guidance (Adjusted for Bineo):**
- Net Income: Revised downward to reflect MXN 1.3B Bineo impairment; management noted all other operational metrics on track or improving
- Loan Growth: 10% YoY (ex-government); original guidance implied high-single-digit growth
- Cost of Risk: Maintained at 1.8-2.0% (Q3 tracking in-range despite one-off commercial provision)
- NIM: 9M2025 NIM of 6.2% (+7bps vs. 9M2024); Q3 exit rate 6.9% suggests positive momentum
- ROE: YTD 22.3%; full-year expected near this level post-Bineo charge

**FY2026 Outlook (Preliminary):**
- Management expects **double-digit loan growth** in 2026, implying acceleration from 2025's 10% pace
- Consumer portfolio positioned as primary growth driver given Mexico's consumption recovery and Banorte's market-leading retail franchise
- Cost of risk expected to remain in 1.8-2.0% range absent macro shocks

**Macro Commentary from Management:**
- **Mexico Economic Outlook**: Banorte's economic analysis team maintains FY2025 GDP forecast at 0.5%, driven by resilient domestic consumption recovery. For 2026, management anticipates GDP rebound to 1.8%, supported by multiple catalysts: (1) Mexico's FIFA World Cup hosting expected to contribute 40-50bps to growth via tourism and consumption; (2) improved agricultural output from favorable 2025 weather conditions; (3) government infrastructure reactivation equivalent to 1.4% of GDP. Management characterizes 2026 growth dynamics as "pretty balanced" across sectors.

- **Monetary Policy Path**: Banxico expected to deliver **two additional 25bps rate cuts in 2025**, bringing policy rate to 7.0% by year-end (from current 7.5%). For 2026, management forecasts two further cuts with rate reaching 6.5% in Q1 2026 and remaining stable thereafter. Peso forecast at 18.80 per USD by year-end 2025. Inflation trajectory supportive of continued easing cycle.

- **Interest Rate Sensitivity**: NIM expanded to 6.9% in Q3 despite monetary easing cycle, reflecting asset repricing lag and deposit cost discipline. Management noted modest NIM compression expected in 2026 (~20-30bps) as rate cuts flow through, but double-digit loan volume growth should offset margin pressure on absolute net interest income. Deposit betas remain favorable with retail funding base sticky.

- **Asset Quality**: NPL ratios stable across portfolio; one-off commercial loan provision in Q3 was idiosyncratic (single borrower event) rather than systemic. Credit quality benefits from Mexico's low unemployment and real wage recovery. Consumer delinquencies normalizing but well-controlled. Cost of risk guidance maintained at 1.8-2.0% for 2025-2026.

- **USMCA Review & Trade**: Management acknowledged USMCA review process begins formally July 2026 but expects "constructive dialogue" between Mexico and U.S. Nearshoring tailwinds intact with FDI inflows supporting commercial banking deposit growth. Banorte's exposure to multinational corporates benefiting from Mexico's competitive trade position.

- **Competitive Dynamics**: Competitive environment remains rational with no irrational pricing observed despite digital bank entrants. Banorte's omnichannel model (1,300+ branches plus digital platform) proving resilient versus digital-only challengers post-Bineo exit. Market share gains in consumer lending (+12% YoY) validate franchise strength.

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

Grupo Financiero Banorte operates through three primary segments: Banking, Insurance & Pensions, and Other Financial Services.

**Banking Segment [~75-80% of consolidated net income]:**
- Net Income Growth: +2% YoY (9M2025 vs. 9M2024), impacted by Bineo impairment
- Loan Growth: +10% YoY (ex-government loans); consumer +12%, commercial +8%, mortgage stable
- NIM: 6.9% in Q3 2025 (+70bps YoY from ~6.2%); 9M2025 NIM 6.2% (+7bps vs. 9M2024)
- Consumer Lending Surge: 12% YoY growth driven by credit card, personal loans, and auto financing; Banorte's retail footprint (1,300+ branches) and digital channels capturing market share
- Commercial Banking: Benefiting from nearshoring FDI flows; multinational deposit gathering supporting low-cost funding
- Key Metrics: Loan-to-deposit ratio healthy at ~90%; deposit growth tracking loan growth

**Insurance & Pensions Segment [~15-20% of consolidated net income]:**
- Net Income Growth: +20% YoY (9M2025 vs. 9M2024), strongest segment performance
- Life Insurance: Primary growth driver with higher billing issuance; Mexico's expanding middle class and pension system supporting structural demand
- Property & Casualty: Profitable with disciplined underwriting; low claims environment in Q3
- Combined Ratio: Not disclosed but implied strong underwriting profitability given 20% earnings growth
- Pension Fund Management (Afore): Stable AUM growth; fee-based revenue provides earnings diversification

**Other Financial Services [~5-10% of consolidated net income]:**
- Includes asset management, brokerage, and leasing operations
- Performance steady; no material call-outs in Q3 beyond Bineo impairment
- Bineo (Digital Bank): Divested/impaired in Q3 with MXN 1.3B charge; management confirmed no future charges expected

**Extraordinary Items:**
- **Bineo Impairment**: MXN 1.3B (~$65M USD) write-down on digital banking venture after strategic review concluded standalone profitability unlikely; digital capabilities folded into core Banorte platform
- **Commercial Loan Provision**: One-off NPL case in commercial portfolio (idiosyncratic borrower); ~MXN 500M provision taken

**Key Segment Takeaway**: Insurance +20% growth is standout performer, validating diversification strategy beyond banking. Core banking resilient with 10% loan growth and NIM expansion to 6.9% (among LatAm leaders), offsetting Bineo impairment drag. Consumer lending +12% YoY driven by retail franchise strength; commercial banking benefiting from nearshoring tailwinds.

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

**Capital Position:**
- Tier 1 Capital Ratio: Strong regulatory capital base (Mexican banks typically >13-14%)
- ROE: 22.3% YTD (among highest in LatAm banking); normalized ROE post-Bineo ~23-24%
- Organic Capital Generation: Robust internal capital generation enabling elevated dividend payouts

**Dividends (Key Highlight):**
- **Extraordinary Dividend Announced**: MXN 6.99/share (~$0.35 USD) proposed for approval at Q4 shareholders meeting
- Represents ~35% of 2024 net income as one-time extraordinary payout
- **Total Payout Ratio FY2025**: 85% (ordinary + extraordinary dividends combined)
- Ordinary Dividend: Semi-annual payments; historical yield 5-6%
- Dividend Yield (Current): ~7.2% based on recent stock price (includes extraordinary dividend)
- Management justified elevated payout by strong organic capital generation and excess capital post-Bineo exit

**Share Buybacks:**
- No active buyback program disclosed in Q3 2025
- Mexican banks historically prefer dividends over buybacks due to shareholder base preferences

**Balance Sheet Quality:**
- Total Assets: ~MXN 2.5T (~$125B USD equivalent)
- Loan-to-Deposit Ratio: ~90%, indicating balanced deposit-funded model
- NPL Ratio: Stable at ~2.0-2.2% (estimated); within historical range
- Provisioning Coverage: Strong with reserves covering 1.5-2.0x NPLs
- Liquidity: Ample liquidity ratios above regulatory minimums; Mexico's stable macro supports funding access

**Capital Deployment Priorities (in order):**
1. Organic loan growth (10%+ annually in consumer, commercial, mortgage)
2. Dividends (targeting 60-70% ordinary payout ratios; opportunistic extraordinary dividends when capital exceeds growth needs)
3. Technology/digital investments (post-Bineo, focus on integrating digital into core platform rather than standalone ventures)
4. Selective M&A (insurance, wealth management, payments; management indicated no major M&A pipeline currently)

**Liquidity & Funding:**
- Deposit Base: Diversified across retail (60%), corporate (30%), government (10%)
- Nearshoring driving multinational deposit inflows at attractive rates
- Funding costs declining as Banxico cuts rates; deposit betas favorable

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Thesis intact; Bineo impairment is one-time noise, not signal. Core banking franchise firing on all cylinders: 10% loan growth (consumer +12%), 6.9% NIM expansion (+70bps YoY), and 22.3% ROE validate Banorte's position as Mexico's best-run bank. Insurance +20% growth demonstrates successful diversification beyond traditional banking. At 8.8x P/E and 1.9x P/BV, GBOOY trades at 20-30% discount to LatAm bank peers (Itaú, Bradesco at 2.0-2.5x P/BV) despite comparable/superior ROE—spread reflects Mexico political risk premium and Bineo overhang, creating asymmetric entry point. Extraordinary dividend of MXN 6.99/share (85% total payout ratio) signals management confidence in sustainable earnings power post-digital banking exit. Catalysts: (1) 2026 double-digit loan growth guidance suggests earnings inflection; (2) nearshoring tailwinds in commercial banking accelerating; (3) Banxico rate cuts supporting NII growth via loan volume. Risks: (1) Mexico political uncertainty into 2026 elections could pressure Peso and loan demand; (2) NIM compression in 2026 if rate cuts exceed 100bps; (3) consumer credit quality deterioration if unemployment rises (low probability). Maintain Buy with $60 PT (10x P/E, 2.2x P/BV), implying +25% upside plus 7.2% yield. Key monitorables: Q4 NIM sustaining >6.5% and consumer loan growth >10%.
