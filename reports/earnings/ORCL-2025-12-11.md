# HEADLINE: ORCL Q2 FY26 Review: Cloud Backlog Surges but Revenue Miss Triggers Selloff

**Report Date:** December 10, 2025 (Fiscal Q2 ended November 30, 2025)

---

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

* **Revenue Miss Overshadows Record Backlog:** Despite posting a 438% YoY surge in Remaining Performance Obligations to $523B (driven by Meta, NVIDIA, and other hyperscaler contracts), Oracle missed revenue estimates by $150M, triggering an 11% after-hours decline to $198.88 from $223.01 close.

* **Cloud Infrastructure Accelerates, Applications Lag:** OCI revenue growth accelerated to +68% YoY ($4.1B), with GPU-related revenue +177% and capacity expanding 50% QoQ. However, Cloud Applications grew only +11% ($3.9B), reflecting enterprise budget constraints.

* **CapEx Surge Rattles Street:** Oracle raised full-year CapEx guidance to $50B from $35B (vs. $21.2B in FY25), resulting in negative $10B free cash flow in Q2. Management emphasized commitment to investment-grade debt rating but heavy AI infrastructure spending clouded profitability outlook.

* **EPS Beat Distorted by One-Time Gain:** Headline non-GAAP EPS of $2.26 included $2.7B pre-tax Ampere gain; excluding this, underlying operating performance was less impressive relative to elevated Street expectations.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

### Q3 FY26 Guidance:
* **Revenue Growth:** 19-21% YoY (16-18% constant currency)
* **Cloud Revenue Growth:** 37-41% constant currency; 40-44% USD
* **Adj. EPS:** $1.70-$1.74 (+12-14% YoY)

### Full-Year FY26:
* **Total Revenue:** $67B (unchanged)
* **FY27 Incremental Revenue:** +$4B expected from RPO conversion
* **CapEx:** $50B (up from $35B prior guidance)

**CFO Commentary on Macro:**
* Management highlighted "flexible funding" via customer-financed hardware and vendor leasing to synchronize payments with receipts and preserve investment-grade rating.
* Near-term RPO (12-month recognition) up 40% YoY, signaling robust FY26 visibility.
* CEO McGork noted infrastructure utilization velocity: customers "typically spend that capacity up in the order of two to three days."

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis Intact but Execution Risk Rising.** Oracle's $523B backlog validates its position as a Tier-1 AI infrastructure provider, with hyperscaler wins (Meta, NVIDIA, OpenAI partnership) confirming demand. However, the revenue miss, margin compression from capital-intensive OCI buildouts, and $50B CapEx guidance signal near-term profitability headwinds. Street will watch Q3 closely for RPO-to-revenue conversion rates and gross margin trajectory on AI contracts (estimated 30-40%). Valuation multiple compression likely given FCF burn and debt concerns, though long-term thesis remains supported by structural cloud migration and AI training/inference demand.

**Key Risks:** (1) Revenue recognition lag from backlog, (2) Margin pressure from GPU infrastructure contracts, (3) Debt serviceability if RPO conversion disappoints, (4) Competition from AWS, Azure, and GCP in enterprise cloud.

**Key Catalysts:** (1) Q3 revenue acceleration from $523B backlog, (2) Improved FCF as CapEx intensity normalizes, (3) Multi-cloud partnership traction (Azure, AWS), (4) GenAI app layer monetization via Oracle Database + AI integration.
