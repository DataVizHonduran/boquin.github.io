## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

- **Record quarterly net income:** Grupo Aval posted COP 521 billion net income in Q3 2025, marking the highest quarterly figure in three years with robust 25.3% YoY growth. Year-to-date net income reached COP 1.4 trillion, an 88% surge versus 2024.

- **Revenue miss overshadows EPS beat:** Despite topping consensus EPS estimates by $0.01 ($0.11 vs. $0.10), revenue of $1.15B fell short of the $1.23B analyst forecast by 6.5%, driving a -2.1% after-hours stock decline following the November 13 release.

- **Asset quality inflection drives profitability:** The 90-day past-due loan ratio compressed 90 bps YoY to 3.4% (from 4.3%), while NIM expanded 20 bps to 3.1%. Net interest income grew 12% YoY to COP 1.87 trillion, benefiting from a lower 6.8% average cost of funds.

- **Digital payments innovation:** The September launch of Gou Payments—Grupo Aval's instant payment platform—processed 10 million transactions worth COP 2 trillion in its first month, capturing 45%+ merchant key market share with 8 million individual keys registered.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

### 2025 Full-Year Guidance (Reaffirmed)
- **Cost of Risk:** 1.9%
- **ROE Target:** 10.5%

### 2026 Projections
- **Cost of Risk:** 2.0% (reflecting end of recovery cycle)
- **ROE Target:** 12.0%–12.5% (200-250 bps expansion)

### Management Commentary on Macro Headwinds
- **Fiscal deficit concerns:** Colombia's projected 7.5% of GDP fiscal deficit in 2025 creates elevated sovereign risk and constrains credit growth.
- **Inflation persistence:** Inflation expected to close 2025 at 5.3%, above Banco de la República's 3% target (+1% tolerance band), pressuring NIM outlook.
- **FX headwind:** Colombian peso appreciation negatively impacted gross loan growth by 110 bps YoY, translating to flat nominal loan growth versus 4.6% underlying volume expansion.

**CFO Tone:** Cautiously optimistic. Management emphasized that asset quality improvements are "sustainable" and cost of risk guidance of 2% for 2026 signals confidence in stabilized credit metrics. However, persistent fiscal uncertainty and inflation stickiness remain "structural drags" on ROE expansion beyond 12.5%.

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

- **Gross Loans:** COP 203 trillion (+4.6% YoY, +2.1% QoQ), representing 59% of total assets
  - **Consumer loans:** Strongest quarterly growth in 10 quarters at +1.5% QoQ, signaling retail credit demand recovery
  - **Peso-denominated deposits from individuals:** +22% YoY, with savings/checking accounts +11% and term deposits +31%

- **Fixed Income Investments:** COP 58 trillion (+24% YoY, +9.3% QoQ), reflecting balance sheet repositioning into Colombian sovereign bonds amid rate volatility

- **Net Interest Income (including trading):** COP 1.87 trillion (+12% YoY)

- **Digital banking initiatives:** Gou Payments rollout positioned across all four banks to capture P2P and merchant payment flows

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis Intact; Multiple Compression Risk Contained**

Grupo Aval's Q3 2025 results reinforce the asset quality recovery narrative, with NPL ratio compression to 3.4% and 25%+ net income growth validating the improving credit cycle thesis. The record COP 521B quarterly net income and 88% YTD earnings surge position ROE for 10.5% in 2025, tracking toward the 12%-12.5% 2026 target.

**However, the -6.5% revenue miss ($1.15B vs. $1.23B consensus) exposes topline pressure from:**
1. **FX translation drag:** Peso appreciation eroded 110 bps of reported loan growth
2. **NIM ceiling:** Despite 20 bps YoY expansion to 3.1%, structural inflation (5.3% close expected in 2025) and fiscal deficit (7.5% of GDP) constrain rate repricing power

**Valuation Implication:** The -2.1% after-hours decline reflects profit-taking on the revenue miss, not fundamental deterioration. At 9.7% ROE with a glide path to 12%+, AVAL trades at a discount to LATAM banking peers (Colombia's Bancolombia at ~11% ROE, Brazil's Itaú at ~18% ROE).

**Multiple compression likely pauses** given:
- **Cost of risk normalization:** 2% guidance for 2026 anchors credit loss expectations
- **Digital payments optionality:** Gou Payments' 45% merchant key market share in month one signals fee income upside not yet in numbers
- **ROE inflection:** 200-250 bps ROE expansion to 12%+ in 2026 should support 1.0x P/B re-rating from current 0.8x P/B (estimated)

**Actionable View:** Accumulate on revenue-miss weakness. The earnings beat (+10% vs. consensus EPS) with asset quality tailwinds ($90 bps NPL compression) and digital franchise buildout (Gou Payments) justify holding through fiscal/macro volatility. Target: 12% ROE achievement in 2026 triggers 10-15% upside from current $4.00 ADR levels (est. fair value $4.40-$4.60 on 1.0x P/B).
