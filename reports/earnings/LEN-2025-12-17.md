## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **Mixed Quarter:** Revenue beat by 2.6% driven by 4% YoY delivery growth, but adjusted EPS of $2.03 missed consensus by $0.18 due to severe margin compression. Gross margins contracted 510 bps to 17.0% as incentives remained elevated at ~14%.

* **Volume Over Price:** New orders surged 18% YoY to 20,018 homes, signaling strategic pivot toward affordability. However, average sales price held flat at $386K while homebuilding revenue declined 7% YoY, reflecting compressed profitability.

* **Stock Reaction:** Shares fell 4.2% in after-hours to $112.60 as light Q1 2026 guidance (17K-18K deliveries vs. 20,189 expected) overshadowed order strength.

* **Profitability Concerns:** Net margin compressed to 9.1% in Q4 from prior-year levels. Management acknowledged "affordability challenges and weak consumer confidence" despite modestly lower rates.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**Q1 FY2026 (Feb-Apr):**
- Deliveries: 17,000-18,000 homes (vs. consensus 20,189; **15.9% miss at midpoint**)
- Average sales price: $365K-$375K (vs. consensus $378K)
- Gross margin: 15-16% (further 100-200 bps compression from Q4)
- SG&A: ~9.5% of revenues

**Full Year FY2026:**
- Expected deliveries: ~85,000 homes (3% growth vs. FY2025's 82,583)

**Management Commentary:**
CFO highlighted continued pressure from "affordability challenges" despite "interest rates moved slightly lower." Maintained ~14% incentive levels. No indication of near-term margin recovery; pricing power remains constrained. Guidance implies sequential margin erosion through Q1.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Thesis under pressure. While order growth (+18% YoY) validates affordability-focused strategy, **margin compression trajectory is unsustainable** absent Fed rate cuts or pricing discipline recovery. Q1 guide implies 15-16% gross margins (200+ bps below FY2025 average of 17.7%), suggesting profitability trough ahead. Strong balance sheet (15.7% net leverage) provides runway, but **multiple compression likely** as Street reprices 2026 EPS estimates downward. Current setup favors waiting for margin stabilization signal before re-entry; near-term catalyst unclear absent material mortgage rate decline.
