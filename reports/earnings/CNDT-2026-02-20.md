## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **Dual Miss on Top and Bottom Line:** Conduent missed both revenue ($770M vs. ~$793M consensus, -2.9%) and adjusted EPS (-$0.09 vs. -$0.06 consensus, a -63.6% negative earnings surprise per Zacks). The quarter continues a multi-year pattern of revenue erosion, with adjusted revenue down 4.2% YoY for the full year (ex-divestitures). Contract runoffs and volume declines in the Commercial segment are the primary structural drag.

- **Primary Beat Driver — Margins:** The only material outperformance was on cost. Adjusted EBITDA of $50M in Q4 (+56% YoY) and a margin of 6.5% (+250 bps) reflects meaningful progress from cost optimization and efficiency programs. Full-year adj. EBITDA of $164M (+32% YoY) at a 5.4% margin (+150 bps) shows the profit recovery trajectory is intact even as revenue contracts.

- **New CEO, No Guidance:** Incoming CEO Harshita Agadi (appointed <30 days prior to earnings) declined to issue formal 2026 guidance, citing the need for a proper strategic review. She disclosed that an estimated 15–20% of the business faces AI disruption risk — concentrated in Commercial — which she characterized as a "moving target" that could increase. This ambiguity overhung the print.

- **After-Hours/Day-Of Stock Reaction:** CNDT rallied **+12.6%** on the day of earnings (Feb 12, 2026) to ~$1.61, suggesting the market interpreted the EBITDA improvement and new management tone as a more credible turnaround signal than the top-line miss implied. The stock had been deeply depressed ahead of the print.
