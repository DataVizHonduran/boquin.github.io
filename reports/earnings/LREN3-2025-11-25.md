## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

- **Realize CFI Emerges as Margin Defender**: Financial services arm delivered R$ 79.8M result (+37% YoY), accounting for 13% of total EBITDA and marking its eighth consecutive quarter of growth. Credit portfolio quality remains healthy, cementing CFI as a strategic pillar for customer loyalty and margin protection in a challenging retail environment.

- **LTM Net Income Surges 49%, but Q3 Modest**: While trailing-twelve-month net income reached R$ 1.4B (+49% YoY) with EPS up 59%, Q3 standalone net income grew only 9.4% to R$ 279.4M. The disconnect reflects tough YoY comps and climate headwinds that weighed on seasonal merchandise sell-through in September (described as "the worst month of the quarter").

- **Gross Margin Expansion Despite Sales Slowdown**: Retail gross margin expanded 40 bps to 55.1%, and apparel gross margin reached 56.2% (+50 bps), demonstrating pricing power and mix improvement despite modest 4.2% retail sales growth. Digital channel maintained 17% sales penetration.

- **Stock Reaction & Market Context**: Shares reacted positively to the earnings beat (+0.03% vs. consensus), with management highlighting sustained market share gains and reaffirming 30-37 store opening guidance for FY2025 (18 opened YTD).

- Store openings: 30-37 new stores (18 opened YTD, on track)

**Management Commentary on Outlook:**

- CEO Fabio Faccio highlighted confidence in business trajectory despite Q3 climate challenges, noting "distinct climate dynamic compared to 2024"
- CFO Daniel dos Santos affirmed strength of free cash flow (R$ 473M in Q3) and balance sheet, supporting continued capital returns

**Macro Headwinds/Tailwinds:**
- **Headwind**: Atypical weather patterns in Q3 (particularly September) pressured seasonal inventory turnover, with industry feedback indicating sector-wide challenges
- **Headwind**: Brazilian consumer sentiment remains mixed; discretionary spending cautious
- **Tailwind**: Realize CFI credit business continues to drive engagement, with healthy portfolio risk profile supporting recurring revenue growth
- **Tailwind**: Digital penetration at 17% of sales provides omnichannel leverage; store expansion into new markets on track

- Dividends: Company distributed R$ 1.4B YTD through interest on equity and buybacks

**Capital Deployment Strategy:**
- Management prioritizing shareholder returns through aggressive buyback execution and interest on equity payments
- Organic growth investments: 30-37 store openings in FY2025; digital infrastructure; Realize CFI credit portfolio expansion
- Balance sheet flexibility: Net cash position enables continued capital returns without compromising growth investments

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis intact; CFI diversification offsets retail volatility.** LREN3's Q3 results validate the strategic shift toward financial services, with Realize CFI delivering 37% YoY growth and now representing 13% of EBITDA—providing a countercyclical earnings stream as retail sales moderate (+4.2% YoY). The 49% LTM net income surge reflects operating leverage from prior-quarter momentum, though Q3 standalone growth of 9.4% signals normalization. Gross margin expansion (+40 bps retail, +50 bps apparel) demonstrates pricing power despite climate-driven volume headwinds. With R$ 1.3B net cash, R$ 473M quarterly FCF, and 85% of R$ 1B buyback completed, capital allocation remains shareholder-friendly. Multiple likely stable given earnings beat and CFI growth trajectory; key risk is sustained weather volatility pressuring seasonal merchandise cycles and potential Brazilian consumer spending deceleration. Store expansion (18 YTD toward 30-37 target) positions LREN3 for market share gains, but near-term margin upside limited by operating deleverage (-20 bps EBITDA margin). Hold rating appropriate; CFI growth and buyback provide downside support.
