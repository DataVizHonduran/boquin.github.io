## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

* **Revenue acceleration masked by weak execution**: Q3 revenue of $30.5M represents highest quarterly revenue in company history and 100% sequential growth, but fell 23% short of Street estimates, driven by project timing delays rather than fundamental demand issues.
* **Margin inflection narrative intact despite elevated losses**: Net loss of $641M dominated by $572M non-cash mark-to-market adjustments; management reaffirmed path to positive contribution margin in Q4 2025 and positive gross margin exit by Q1 2026, a critical inflection point for the thesis.
* **Pipeline momentum accelerating**: Commercial opportunity pipeline expanded to $22.6B (up 21% QoQ, 59% YoY) with $644M backlog and recent wins including 228 MWh from Frontier Power and 750 MWh MN8 Energy supply agreement.
* **Market reaction constructive**: Stock rallied 6.2% in premarket trading to $15.78 and closed up 8.4%, suggesting investors look through near-term execution misses to focus on scaling trajectory and margin pathway.
