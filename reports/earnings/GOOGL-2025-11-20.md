## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

* **Historic Quarter, AI Monetization Accelerating:** Alphabet crossed $100 billion in quarterly revenue for the first time, driven by robust Search growth (+15% YoY to $56.6B) and Cloud momentum (+34% YoY). AI Overviews and AI Mode in Search are driving query growth acceleration, validating the monetization thesis.

* **Cloud Inflection Materializing:** Google Cloud delivered $15.2B (+34% YoY), beating consensus by +2.7%. More critically, backlog surged 46% sequentially and 82% YoY to $155B, signaling sustained enterprise AI demand. Management noted Cloud signed more billion-dollar deals in 9M25 than the prior two years combined.

* **Beat Across the Board, Stock Up 5%:** Revenue beat by $2.5B (+2.5%), EPS beat by $0.77 (+33%). Market reacted positively (+5% after-hours) despite elevated CapEx guidance, indicating investors are rewarding AI-driven growth over near-term margin compression concerns.

* **CapEx Surge Continues:** Company raised FY25 CapEx guidance to $91-93B (from $85B prior), reflecting infrastructure buildout for AI workloads. Management acknowledged tight demand-supply dynamics persisting through Q4 and into 2026.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK (Crucial)

* **Q4 2025 & FY26 Guidance:** No explicit revenue or EPS guidance provided. Management reiterated expectation to remain in a "tight demand-supply environment in Q4 and 2026," implying continued infrastructure constraints and elevated CapEx.

* **CapEx Guidance Raised:** FY25 CapEx now expected at $91-93B, up from $85B. This represents 89-91% of FY25 revenue, underscoring aggressive AI investment posture.

* **Macro Headwinds/Tailwinds:** CFO cited sustained enterprise AI adoption as primary tailwind. No explicit macro headwind commentary, though FX remains a modest drag. Management emphasized AI is driving "broad-based growth" across Search, YouTube, and Cloud.

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

* **Google Services ($86.3B, +10% YoY):**
  * Google Search & Other: $56.6B (+15% YoY) — Beat consensus of $55B. AI Overviews now rolled out globally; query growth rate increased in Q3.
  * YouTube Advertising: $10.3B (+15% YoY) — Beat consensus of $10.0B. Shorts monetization and Connected TV growth cited as drivers.
  * Google Network: $7.5B (-3% YoY) — Continued secular decline in programmatic display.

* **Google Cloud ($15.2B, +34% YoY):**
  * Revenue beat consensus by $400M. Operating income not disclosed separately, but segment profitability improving.
  * Backlog: $155B (+82% YoY, +46% QoQ). Management highlighted enterprise AI workload wins (Gen AI solutions, Vertex AI, Gemini Enterprise).
  * Customer Momentum: More $1B+ deals signed in 9M25 than prior two years combined.

* **Other Bets ($392M, +29% YoY):**
  * Modest revenue; operating loss not disclosed. Waymo and Verily remain primary focus areas.

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

* **Cash Position:** Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities: $97.5B (down from $111B in Q2). Operating cash flow: $31.5B in Q3.

* **Debt Levels:** Total debt: $13.2B (modest leverage; net cash position remains strong).

* **Share Buybacks:** Repurchased $15.3B of Class A and Class C shares in Q3. $70.8B remaining under current authorization (approved in April 2025).

* **Dividend:** $0.20 per share quarterly dividend maintained (initiated in April 2024). ~$2.5B cash outflow per quarter.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis intact; AI monetization inflecting, Cloud at scale.** The $100B quarterly milestone is not symbolic—it reflects real AI-driven demand across Search, YouTube, and Cloud. The 33% EPS beat and 46% sequential backlog growth in Cloud signal Alphabet is capturing enterprise AI spend at scale. While CapEx at $91-93B (90% of revenue) pressures near-term margins, the market is rewarding growth, as evidenced by the +5% after-hours rally. Multiple expansion likely if Cloud maintains 30%+ growth and Search query acceleration sustains. Valuation remains attractive at ~20x NTM P/E vs. 30%+ earnings growth. Key risk: CapEx discipline and ROI realization on AI infrastructure buildout. Maintain Overweight.
