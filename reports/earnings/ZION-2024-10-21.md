# HEADLINE: ZION Q3 2024 Review: Solid Beat on NII Expansion; CRE Headwinds Intensify

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

* **Beat driven by NIM expansion and loan yield improvement.** Net interest income rose 6% YoY to $620M as earning asset yields outpaced funding cost increases. NIM improved 10 bps to 3.03% from 2.93% prior year, while operating costs rose a modest 1%.
* **CRE stress materializing in multifamily.** Classified loans surged 66% QoQ, with $442M of the $829M increase concentrated in multifamily CRE due to missed leasing targets, rent concessions, elevated costs, and higher rates. NPAs rose $103M to $306M (62 bps of loans).
* **Capital build continues; ROTCE stable at 17.4%.** CET1 ratio improved to 10.7% from 10.2% YoY. Tangible book value per share jumped to $33.12 from $25.75, driven by retained earnings and reduced AOCI losses. Return on average assets expanded to 0.95% from 0.80%.
* **Stock reaction:** Shares rose 3.2% on the beat, though multifamily concerns weighed on sentiment.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

* **No explicit Q4 or FY guidance provided.**
* **Management commentary on headwinds/tailwinds:**
  * **Multifamily CRE:** Continued pressure expected from lower-than-projected leasing velocity, rent concessions, and elevated operating costs. Allowance coverage for multifamily increased to 2.4% vs. 2.2% for overall CRE portfolio.
  * **Interest rate environment:** Net interest margin benefiting from stabilizing/declining funding costs, though deposit mix normalization ongoing.
  * **Loan growth:** Muted demand environment; loans contracting at 3% annualized linked-quarter rate (noted in subsequent quarters).
  * **Capital deployment:** Common shares outstanding decreased 0.4M from Q3 2023 due to Q1 2024 buybacks. Dividend held flat at $0.41/share. February 2024 authorization for up to $35M in buybacks (no expiration).

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

### Net Interest Income
* **Net Interest Income:** $620M, up 6% YoY and 4% QoQ
* **Net Interest Margin:** 3.03%, up from 2.93% YoY but well below Q4 2022 peak of $720M in NII

### Customer-Related Noninterest Income
* **Total:** Increased 3% YoY (+$4M)
* **Capital markets fees:** +$10M YoY, driven by increased swap fees, loan syndication fees, and expanded real estate capital markets activity

### Loan Portfolio Composition (Sept 30, 2024)
* **CRE Portfolio:** $13.5B (23% of total loans)
  * Multifamily experiencing acute stress (specific % not disclosed for Q3 2024)
  * Office: ~15% of CRE portfolio, or 3% of total loans
  * Portfolio characterized as granular with growth managed over a decade
* **Total Loans:** $49.3B (implied from CRE being 23%)

### Credit Quality
* **Classified Loans:** +66% QoQ (+$829M total)
  * Multifamily CRE drove $442M of increase
* **Non-Performing Assets:** $306M (62 bps of loans), up $103M QoQ
* **Allowance for Credit Losses:**
  * CRE portfolio: 2.2%
  * Multifamily CRE: 2.4% (higher due to stress)
* **Net Charge-offs:** Normalized at 4 bps annualized (subsequent quarter data: $6M normalized, $56M total including $50M charge-off and $10M reserve from two related companies with irregularities)

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

### Balance Sheet Strength
* **Total Assets:** ~$87B (Sept 30, 2024)
* **CET1 Capital:** $7.2B, up 6% YoY from $6.8B
* **Tangible Book Value/Share:** $33.12, up from $25.75 YoY (primarily due to increased retained earnings and reduced AOCI losses)
* **AOCI:** Loss of $2.3B at Sept 30, 2024 (driven by mark-to-market losses on AFS fixed-rate securities from rate moves)

### Capital Ratios (Sept 30, 2024)
* **CET1 Ratio:** 10.7% vs. 10.2% prior year
* **Tier 1 Leverage Ratio:** 8.6% vs. 8.3% prior year
* **Tier 1 Risk-Based Capital Ratio:** 11.4% vs. 10.9% prior year

### Return Metrics
* **Return on Average Assets (ROAA):** 0.95% vs. 0.80% prior year
* **Return on Average Tangible Common Equity (ROTCE):** 17.4% vs. 17.3% prior year

### Shareholder Returns
* **Share Buybacks:** Common shares outstanding decreased 0.4M from Q3 2023, primarily from Q1 2024 repurchases. February 2024 authorization for up to $35M (no expiration).
* **Dividend:** $0.41/share (unchanged from Q3 2023)
* **Total Capital Returned:** Modest given focus on balance sheet fortification amid CRE stress

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis intact but CRE risk repricing underway.** ZION delivered a solid operational beat driven by NIM expansion and disciplined expense control, but the 66% QoQ surge in classified loans—concentrated in multifamily—signals mounting CRE stress that could weigh on earnings via elevated provision expense in coming quarters. Capital ratios remain robust (10.7% CET1), providing cushion, but multiple likely ranges 0.9-1.0x tangible book near-term as investors discount CRE headwinds. Solid Western/Sunbelt franchise with improving NIM trajectory, but elevated NCO risk warrants cautious positioning. Hold rating; revisit if multifamily stabilizes or management provides credible reserve build guidance.
