# ISRG | Q2 2026 Recap

### 1. The Numbers
- **Revenue:** $2.89B (+18.5% YoY) | Beat by ~$80M vs. consensus $2.81B
- **Non-GAAP EPS:** $2.80 (+28% YoY) | Beat by $0.32 vs. consensus $2.48 — includes $0.08 IEEPA tariff refund tailwind
- **Margins:** Gross: 67.8% (+150bps YoY) | Operating (GAAP): 33.6% (+310bps YoY) — tariff refunds ($35.9M in COGS) aided gross margin; ongoing mix shift toward leasing and high-margin services structural driver

### 2. Segment Performance
- **Instruments & Accessories:** $1.73B (+17.7% YoY) | ~15% da Vinci procedure growth + 36% Ion procedure growth driving consumable pull-through
- **Systems:** $685M (+19.2% YoY) | 468 da Vinci placements (vs. 395 prior year); 246 da Vinci 5 units (vs. 180); higher lease installed base and ASP expansion
- **Services:** $472M (+20.8% YoY) | Installed base compounding — da Vinci base +12% to 11,710 systems; Ion base +21% to 1,096 systems

### 3. Management Narrative & Guidance
- **The "Why":** Procedure volume (+16% combined) driving consumable leverage; da Vinci 5 mix (246 of 468 placements) supporting higher ASPs and lease revenue
- **The "Why":** One-time IEEPA tariff refund ($35.9M pre-tax, $28M after-tax, $0.08/share) boosted both gross margin and EPS — non-recurring
- **Guidance Update:** Reiterated FY2026 da Vinci procedure growth at 13.5%–15.5%, trending toward midpoint — implied deceleration acknowledged; non-GAAP gross margin 68–69% (including ~1% tariff drag); non-GAAP opex growth 11–13%
- **Capital Allocation:** Repurchased 0.9M shares for $380M in Q2; ended quarter with $8.63B in cash, cash equivalents, and investments (+$650M QoQ)

### 4. Analyst Focus (Q&A)
- Stock fell ~11% after hours to ~$356 despite the headline beat — Q&A centered on procedure growth trending to the midpoint of guidance rather than the high end, with specific concern about U.S. hospital capital spending caution and whether 15% da Vinci growth is a ceiling or a floor given macro headwinds.
