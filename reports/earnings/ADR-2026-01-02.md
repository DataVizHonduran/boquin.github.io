## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **Revenue headwinds offset by dramatic profitability inflection.** ADR reported H1 FY2026 revenue of R6.39B (down 5.5% YoY, or 3.2% on constant currency), pressured by rand strength against AUD, softer volumes in certain customer sectors, and deliberate exit from lower-margin activities. Despite topline contraction, profit before tax surged 150.3% to R60.8M and EPS jumped 87.9% to 53.0c, driven by stable gross margins (9.8%, +20 bps), operating leverage from prior restructuring, and absence of R25.6M once-off transformation costs incurred in prior year.

* **Dividend nearly doubled; balance sheet pristine.** Board declared interim dividend of 24.78c (+85% vs. 13.40c prior year), signaling confidence in cash generation and profitability sustainability. Balance sheet remains ungeared (zero debt) with strong liquidity and no covenant pressure, providing strategic flexibility amid challenging South African macro environment.

* **Segment performance bifurcated: SA contingent staffing resilient, professional services lag.** South Africa contingent staffing and staffing solutions performed well, supported by resilient demand in logistics, manufacturing, and consumer sectors. However, Professional Services experienced softer activity due to muted hiring sentiment and high unemployment weighing on permanent placement. Australia contingent staffing delivered stable results with strong client retention, though protein-processing sector softened; Australian Professional Services showed early restructuring benefits from pivot toward higher-margin tech/consulting.

* **Stock rallied 27.5% YTD; market validated turnaround narrative.** Shares climbed from ZAR 4.94 (Jan 2025) to ZAR 6.30 (Dec 2025), with trading volume spiking to 977,930 shares on October 31 (day after results release). Market appears to have rewarded management's disciplined cost control, margin expansion, and strategic portfolio pruning despite topline headwinds.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

### Management Commentary (No Formal Guidance Provided)

Adcorp did not issue formal FY2026 guidance but provided operational commentary:

**South Africa Outlook:**
* **Contingent Staffing:** Resilient demand expected to persist in logistics, manufacturing, and consumer-facing sectors, supported by ongoing client relationships
* **Professional Services:** Muted hiring sentiment and high unemployment continue to weigh on permanent placement; recent structural changes and efficiency initiatives beginning to improve performance and client delivery
* **Macro Headwinds:** Persistent high unemployment, trade tariff impact on automotive clients, and delays in new contract awards remain challenges

**Australia Outlook:**
* **Contingent Staffing:** Stable outlook underpinned by strong client relationships and contract renewals, though protein-processing sector softness persists
* **Professional Services:** Early benefits from restructuring and diversification into higher-margin technology and consulting work expected to continue

**Strategic Focus:**
* Continue disciplined cost management and operating efficiency gains from restructuring
* Maintain stable gross margins despite revenue mix shifts
* Exit lower-margin activities to improve portfolio quality
* Leverage ungeared balance sheet for opportunistic growth or shareholder returns

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Turnaround inflecting as restructuring benefits materialize despite macro headwinds.** ADR delivered on profitability transformation with 150% PBT growth and 88% EPS expansion, demonstrating operating leverage from prior-year restructuring (R25.6M transformation costs) and disciplined cost management. Gross margin expansion (+20 bps to 9.8%) despite 5.5% revenue contraction validates portfolio pruning strategy (exit of lower-margin activities). However, topline remains challenged by South African macro (high unemployment, muted hiring sentiment) and selective sectoral softness (Australian protein-processing, SA automotive tariffs). Ungeared balance sheet and 85% dividend increase signal management confidence, while 27.5% YTD stock rally suggests market validation of turnaround narrative. **Hold; monitor H2 FY2026 for revenue stabilization and sustained margin gains as key catalysts for re-rating.**
