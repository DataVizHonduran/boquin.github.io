## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **Earnings miss driven by expense inflation, not demand weakness**: Despite robust 18% YoY gross profit growth and Cash App accelerating to 24% YoY, operating expenses exceeded Street expectations, causing 14% EPS miss. Revenue light at $6.11B (+2.3% YoY) vs. $6.33B consensus reflects Bitcoin revenue volatility, not core payments weakness.

* **Cash App re-accelerating as primary growth engine**: Cash App delivered $1.62B gross profit (+24% YoY), accounting for 61% of total, with monthly actives at 58M and primary banking actives reaching 8.3M (+18% YoY). Gross profit per user expanded 25% YoY to $94, signaling improving unit economics.

* **Stock reaction: -13% after-hours selloff despite guidance raise**: Market punished XYZ for cost discipline concerns, dropping shares 13% in extended trading. Street focused on operating expense growth outpacing gross profit expansion rather than improved FY25 guidance (GP raised to $10.24B, +15% YoY).

* **Management raised FY25 guidance materially**: Q4 2025 gross profit guide of $2.755B implies 19%+ YoY growth with 20% adjusted operating margin target, up from 18% in Q3. Full-year gross profit now expected at $10.243B (+15% YoY), adjusted operating income at $560M for Q4 alone.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**Q4 2025 Guidance (Raised):**
* Gross Profit: $2.755B (19%+ YoY growth)
* Adjusted Operating Income: $560M (20% margin)
* Implies strong sequential acceleration from Q3's 18% gross profit growth and 18% margin

**Full Year 2025 (Raised):**
* Gross Profit: $10.243B (+15% YoY vs. 2024)
* Previous guidance implied slower growth trajectory; raise driven by Cash App momentum

**Macro Commentary:**
* Management cited no material macro headwinds; growth driven by product execution and AI integration
* Cash App user acquisition and engagement accelerating organically
* Square GPV growth stable at 12% YoY despite consumer spending concerns

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**Cash App (61% of Gross Profit - Accelerating):**
* Gross Profit: $1.62B (+24% YoY) - primary growth driver
* Monthly Actives: 58M (continued YoY acceleration into September and October)
* Primary Banking Actives: 8.3M (+18% YoY)
* Gross Profit per Monthly Active: $94 (+25% YoY) - strong unit economics
* **Key Insight:** Cash App is re-accelerating after several quarters of deceleration, validating product-market fit expansion beyond payments into banking

**Square (39% of Gross Profit - Steady):**
* Gross Profit: $1.018B (+9% YoY) - solid but decelerating
* Gross Payment Volume (GPV): +12% YoY - stable seller demand
* Transaction Revenues: $1.82B (+10.7% YoY)
* **Key Insight:** Square showing resilience in challenged SMB environment; 9% GP growth acceptable given macro backdrop

**Dividend:**

**Capital Allocation Signal:** Management's $5B buyback expansion post-earnings miss indicates conviction that operating expense issues are temporary and growth trajectory intact. Buyback at distressed post-earnings levels (~13% selloff) creates accretive opportunity.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Multiple compression justified in near-term; thesis intact medium-term.** The 14% EPS miss driven by operating expense inflation (not revenue deterioration) warrants derating, but 24% Cash App gross profit growth and raised FY25 guidance support $75-80 price target range (15-17x 2026E gross profit). **Street overreacted to cost discipline concerns**—Q4 guide implies operating leverage inflection with 20% adjusted operating margin (vs. 18% in Q3). Buyers emerge if XYZ demonstrates Q4 expense discipline; thesis breaks if Cash App deceleration resumes or Square GP growth falls below mid-single digits. **Hold rating** until Q4 execution de-risks cost structure; patient capital rewarded if management delivers 20% margin target.
