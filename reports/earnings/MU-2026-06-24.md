# MU | Q3 FY2026 Recap

### 1. The Numbers
- **Revenue:** $41.46B (+345.7% YoY) | Beat by $5.77B vs. $35.69B consensus
- **Adj. EPS:** $25.11 (+1214.7% YoY) | Beat by $4.62 vs. $20.49 consensus
- **Margins:** Gross: 84.6% GAAP / 84.9% Non-GAAP | Operating: 80.4% GAAP / 81.2% Non-GAAP (vs. 23.3% / 26.8% a year ago) — pricing power and volume leverage on tight DRAM/NAND supply

### 2. Segment Performance
- Cloud Memory BU: $13.77B (+306.7% YoY) | HBM ramp, cloud/AI server demand
- Core Data Center BU: $11.52B (+653.3% YoY) | AI server build-out, enterprise SSD attach
- Mobile and Client BU: $11.52B (+253.9% YoY) | Pricing recovery across smartphone/PC
- Automotive and Embedded BU: $4.63B (+311.1% YoY) | Recovering auto/industrial demand

### 3. Management Narrative & Guidance
- **The "Why":** Record pricing power and volume leverage across DRAM and NAND as AI-driven memory demand outstrips supply — gross margin expanded from 37.7% to 84.6% YoY.
- **The "Why":** Execution of "transformational" multi-year Strategic Customer Agreements (~$100B in contracts, ~$22B in cash deposits/letters of credit) locking in durability and predictability of future revenue.
- **Guidance Update:** FQ4-26 guide of $50.0B ± $1.0B revenue and $31.00 ± $1.00 non-GAAP EPS — both well above the $43.24B / pre-print Street estimates, implying sequential growth continues.
- **Capital Allocation:** Quarterly dividend declared at $0.15/share, payable July 21, 2026 (record date July 6, 2026); $7.1B in net capex this quarter as Micron continues record investment in capacity/technology; $650M in buybacks under the repurchase program YTD.

### 4. Analyst Focus (Q&A)
- Analysts pressed on the durability of gross margins and supply-demand tightness beyond FY2026 — Micron declined to guide past Q4 but said it expects the memory market to stay tight through 2027, while also digging into the cash/LOC commitments backing the new Strategic Customer Agreements.
