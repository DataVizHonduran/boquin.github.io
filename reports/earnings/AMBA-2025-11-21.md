## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **Revenue acceleration driven by HAI portfolio pivot.** Q2 revenue of $95.5M crushed estimates, up 50% YoY and 11% QoQ, exceeding high-end guidance. HAI (High-performance AI) now represents the majority of revenue and is tracking to ~80% of FY2026 sales, validating the company's strategic shift from legacy CV2x to CV5/CV7 platforms.

* **Margin compression is the cost of the transition.** Non-GAAP gross margin contracted 280bps YoY to 60.5% due to unfavorable product mix (higher weighting of lower-margin CV5 ramps vs. mature CV2 products). Management expects this to persist through FY2026 as HAI scales, but the volume-driven operating leverage story remains intact.

* **Stock surged 19% after-hours.** Market focused on the guidance raise (FY2026 revenue now $379M midpoint vs. $348M prior, implying 31-35% growth) and record IoT strength. The print validates that edge AI demand is real and diversified beyond security cameras into robotics, drones, and portable video.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis remains intact; margin compression is temporary, not structural.** The 50% YoY revenue growth and guidance raise validate that Ambarella is successfully transitioning from a legacy security camera chipmaker to a diversified edge AI platform play. The HAI product cycle (CV5/CV7) is driving both volume and ASP expansion, and the IoT TAM is broadening beyond cameras into higher-value robotics and portable video applications. Automotive remains a wildcard—CV7 production revenue is a positive signal, but the segment is still <25% of sales and facing macro headwinds.

**The margin story is the risk.** Non-GAAP gross margin contracted 280bps YoY to 60.5%, and management guided Q3 to 60.0-61.5%, implying no near-term relief. The culprit is product mix: CV5 ramps carry lower margins than mature CV2 products, and automotive (historically lower-margin) is stabilizing. If HAI margins don't improve as CV5 scales, the multiple will compress despite revenue growth.

**Valuation implication:** At $95.5M Q2 revenue and $379M FY2026 guide, AMBA is trading at ~6-7x forward sales (assuming $60-70 stock price post-earnings pop). For a 31-35% revenue grower with edge AI exposure, that's reasonable but not cheap—especially with 60% gross margins (vs. 65%+ for peers like NVDA in edge AI). **Multiple expansion requires either: (1) margin inflection in FY2027 as CV5 matures, or (2) accelerating automotive design wins that validate the CV7 platform.** Near-term, the stock is fairly valued; accumulate on pullbacks if margins stabilize or automotive inflects.
