## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

- **Steady Revenue Growth Above Inflation:** FUNO delivered 5.1% YoY revenue growth to MXN 7,525M in Q3 2025, driven by inflation indexation and positive leasing spreads across all segments. NOI reached MXN 5,581M with an 82.2% margin—demonstrating operational efficiency in a stable demand environment.
- **AFFO Acceleration Signals Operating Leverage:** AFFO per CBFI surged 7.1% YoY, outpacing revenue growth, reflecting disciplined cost management and margin expansion. FUNO distributed MXN 0.6050 per CBFI (94.7% of AFFO)—maintaining high payout while retaining capital for growth.
- **Occupancy Plateau at 95%:** Portfolio occupancy held at 95.0% for the second consecutive quarter (615 properties), with industrial at 97.4%, retail at 93.6%, and office at 83.0% (+80 bps QoQ). Office segment continues recovery trajectory, while industrial and retail remain elevated.
- **Balance Sheet in Transition:** Total debt stands at MXN 148B. Management advancing deleveraging strategy through proposed Fibra Next joint venture, targeting lower LTV and net debt-to-EBITDA to maintain BBB-/Baa3 stable credit ratings. NEXT Properties announced debt exchange offer for six series of FUNO's senior unsecured notes (November 2025).

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**Industrial:** 97.4% occupancy (Q3 2025)
- Strongest segment; occupancy near full capacity
- Leasing spreads robust (Q4 2024 data: +1,670 bps MXP contracts, +1,440 bps USD contracts)
- Nearshoring tailwinds supporting demand

**Retail:** 93.6% occupancy (Q3 2025)
- Stable performance; occupancy near pre-pandemic highs
- Q4 2024 revenue growth: +10% YoY; leasing spreads +830 bps (MXP), +470 bps (USD)
- Consumer spending resilient despite macro headwinds

**Office:** 83.0% occupancy (Q3 2025, up 80 bps QoQ)
- **Recovery Accelerating:** Occupancy surpassed pre-pandemic levels, reaching highest since COVID-19
- Q4 2024 metrics: 7.1% rent growth YoY; leasing spreads +290 bps (MXP), +530 bps (USD)
- Return-to-office trend supporting incremental leasing activity

**Portfolio Composition:**
- 615 properties totaling 11.1M sq meters of GLA
- Presence across all Mexican states—geographic diversification mitigates regional risk

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

High-yield income play with occupancy ceiling concerns. FUNO delivered solid Q3 2025 operationally—5.1% revenue growth, 7.1% AFFO/CBFI expansion, and 95% occupancy maintained—but upside constrained by portfolio maturity. Office recovery (83% occupancy, +80 bps QoQ) provides incremental positive, though industrial/retail segments already optimized near peak occupancy. Key catalyst: Fibra Next JV execution, which could meaningfully improve leverage metrics (net debt-to-EBITDA) and de-risk refinancing profile. Debt exchange offer (November 2025) signals proactive liability management. At ~11% dividend yield and 94.7% AFFO payout, valuation attractive for income investors, but multiple expansion limited absent significant occupancy gains or accretive M&A. Maintain exposure for yield; monitor Fibra Next closing and 2026 refinancing calendar.
