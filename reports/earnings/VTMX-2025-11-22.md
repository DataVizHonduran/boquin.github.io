## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

- **Beat driven by operating leverage**: Adjusted EBITDA margin hit 85.3% (vs. FY guidance of 84.5%), demonstrating exceptional cost discipline as portfolio scale effects materialize; NOI margin sustained at 94.4%
- **Nearshoring momentum accelerating**: 1.7M sf leasing activity (600K sf new leases in electronics, e-commerce, automotive) validates Mexico industrial demand thesis; 1.3M sf delivered in quarter with $91M development pipeline ongoing
- **Balance sheet transformation**: Closed $500M senior unsecured notes at 5.50% with BBB- rating (S&P/Fitch), using proceeds to prepay $176.6M secured debt post-quarter, advancing toward fully unsecured capital structure
- **Stock reaction**: Shares reached new 52-week high post-earnings; Barclays raised price target to $40 (from $38) with Overweight rating, citing structural nearshoring tailwinds

---

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

- **FY 2025 EBITDA margin raised to 84.5%** (from prior guidance), reflecting continued operational efficiency gains
- **No explicit revenue or FFO guidance provided**: Management focuses on operational KPIs (leasing, development pipeline, margins) rather than quantitative forecasts
- **Macro tailwinds emphasized**: CFO highlighted U.S.-Mexico nearshoring trend as "structural, multi-year opportunity" with supply chains relocating from China; northern Mexico markets (Monterrey, Tijuana) seeing strongest demand
- **Development pipeline visibility**: 1.3M sf under construction with $91M investment; post-quarter acquired 330 acres in Monterrey-Apodaca Airport Corridor for future development
- **Capital allocation priorities**: Management reaffirmed focus on (1) development to meet nearshoring demand, (2) balance sheet de-leveraging toward unsecured structure, (3) dividend growth (7.5% increase authorized for 2025)

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

### Geographic Portfolio (43.0M sf GLA, 235 properties across 16 Mexican states):

- **Regional Breakdown**: Portfolio concentrated in key nearshoring corridors (specific revenue by region not disclosed)
  - **Northern Mexico (Monterrey, Tijuana)**: Strongest leasing demand driven by automotive and electronics manufacturing
  - **Bajío Region (Guadalajara, Querétaro)**: E-commerce and logistics growth; started construction on one new building in Guadalajara in Q3
  - **Central Mexico**: Continued steady demand

### Leasing Activity (Q3 2025):

- **New Leases**: 600K sf with new and existing tenants
  - **Electronics sector**: Benefiting from semiconductor/tech supply chain shifts
  - **E-commerce sector**: Logistics/distribution center demand
  - **Automotive sector**: OEM and Tier 1 supplier expansion
- **Renewals**: 1.1M sf with weighted average lease life of ~6 years (demonstrates tenant retention and portfolio quality)
- **Total Q3 Leasing**: 1.7M sf

### Development Activity:

- **Deliveries**: 1.3M sf completed and delivered in Q3 2025
- **Under Construction**: 1.3M sf ongoing ($91M estimated investment)
- **Land Bank Expansion**:
  - Q3: Acquired 128.4 acres (Guadalajara) and 20.2 acres (Monterrey)
  - Post-Q3 (October 2025): Acquired 330 acres in Monterrey-Apodaca Airport Highway Corridor (high-demand location near airport/logistics hub)

*Note: Vesta does not disclose revenue or NOI breakdown by geographic segment in public earnings releases*

---

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

- **Debt Position**: $930M total debt as of Q3 2025; Debt-to-Equity ratio of 0.50 (conservative for REIT sector)
- **Debt Refinancing (Major Event)**:
  - Closed $500M senior unsecured notes at **5.50%** due 2033 on September 30, 2025
  - Achieved **BBB- / Positive Outlook** credit rating from both S&P Global and Fitch (investment grade)
  - Used proceeds to prepay $150M Metlife II facility and $26.6M incremental facility (total $176.6M secured debt repaid in October 2025)
  - Strategic objective: Transition toward fully unsecured capital structure, reducing borrowing costs and increasing financial flexibility
- **Liquidity**: Current ratio of 2.97 and quick ratio of 2.97 indicate strong liquidity cushion
- **Dividends**:
  - Q3 2025: Paid $17.4M (MXN$0.3751 per ordinary share) on October 15, 2025
  - FY 2025 Authorization: $69.5M total dividend (+7.5% vs. prior year), demonstrating commitment to shareholder returns
- **Share Buyback**: Shareholders approved $150M buyback authorization for 2025 (execution details not disclosed in Q3 earnings)

---

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Thesis strengthened; VTMX executing on nearshoring secular tailwind with disciplined capital allocation. Investment-grade debt refinancing de-risks balance sheet while locking in attractive 5.50% long-term cost of capital. Margin expansion to 85.3% (above FY guide of 84.5%) demonstrates operating leverage as portfolio scales. Leasing momentum (1.7M sf) and 6-year average renewal terms validate demand durability. Valuation appears supported by 20%+ FFO/share growth, 94%+ occupancy, and strategic land acquisitions positioning Vesta for multi-year development pipeline. Multiple expansion likely continues as nearshoring theme gains institutional investor attention.
