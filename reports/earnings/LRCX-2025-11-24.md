# HEADLINE: LRCX Q1 FY2026 Review: Record Revenue and Margins on AI; China Headwind Emerges

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **Primary driver of beat**: Record foundry revenue (60% of systems revenue) driven by AI/HBM capacity expansion and advanced logic node transitions. Gross margin expansion of 340bps YoY to record 50.6% reflects favorable product mix (foundry-heavy) and operational leverage. Operating margin reached record 35.0%, up 660bps YoY.

- **China exposure elevated but declining**: China accounted for 43% of Q1 revenue, well above management's stated <30% target. New export restrictions announced in Q1 will impact $200M in Q2 revenue and $600M across CY2026, accelerating the geographic revenue rebalancing toward leading-edge customers.

- **Guidance reflects China headwind**: Q2 FY2026 revenue guide of $5.2B ±$300M ($4.9B-$5.5B range) reflects $200M China export restriction impact. Gross margin guided to 48.5% ±1%, down 210bps QoQ due to revenue mix shift from China constraints. Full-year WFE spending outlook remains healthy at ~$105B (vs. prior $100B view).

- **After-hours reaction**: Stock rallied 7.2% and hit all-time highs following earnings, driven by record margins, sustained AI demand visibility, and market focus on leading-edge foundry/logic strength offsetting near-term China headwinds.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**Q2 FY2026 (Dec Quarter) Guidance:**
- Revenue: $5.2B ±$300M (midpoint implies +0.4% YoY; -2.3% QoQ)
- Gross margin: 48.5% ±1% (down 210bps QoQ from record 50.6%)
- Operating margin: Not disclosed (implied compression from 35% due to GM decline)
- Adjusted EPS: Not explicitly disclosed; consensus $1.22
- Note: $200M revenue headwind from new China export restrictions

**Full Year FY2026 Outlook:**
- Management reiterated confidence in sustained AI-driven growth across foundry and advanced logic
- WFE spending estimate raised to ~$105B for CY2025 (from prior $100B view), predominantly driven by domestic China spending uptick
- China revenue expected to decline below 30% of total over time (from current 43%)
- CY2026 China impact: $600M total revenue headwind from export restrictions

**Macro commentary:**
- CFO emphasized robust secular demand driven by AI/HBM memory and advanced packaging proliferation at leading foundries (TSMC) and logic customers
- NAND recovery remains nascent (18% of systems revenue); DRAM stable at 16%
- Export control uncertainty persists as wildcard but management views leading-edge demand trajectory as resilient
- No incremental concerns on customer capex discipline; foundry/logic visibility extending into 2H CY2026

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**By Device Type (% of Systems Revenue):**
- **Foundry**: 60% (record high; driven by AI chip production at TSMC and others)
- **NAND (Non-volatile Memory)**: 18% (up from recent lows; early signs of recovery)
- **DRAM**: 16% (stable; HBM capacity expansion offsetting commodity weakness)
- **Logic & Other**: 6% (consistent; advanced node R&D)

**By Geography (% of Total Revenue):**
- **China**: 43% (elevated but declining; target <30%)
- **Taiwan**: 19% (TSMC-driven foundry strength)
- **Korea**: 15% (Samsung/SK Hynix memory)
- **Other**: 23%

**Customer Support Business Group (CSBG):**
- Revenue: $1.78B (+2.9% QoQ from $1.73B in Q4 FY2025)
- Represents ~34% of total revenue; provides stable, high-margin recurring stream
- Growth driven by expanding installed base and aging fab tool utilization

**Key segment takeaway**: Geographic and device mix heavily skewed toward leading-edge AI beneficiaries (Taiwan foundry, Korea HBM). China exposure is strategic headwind but declining; portfolio rebalancing toward higher-value customers should sustain margin structure.

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

**Cash & liquidity:**
- Cash and short-term investments: $6.7B (strong fortress balance sheet)
- Total debt: $4.5B
- Net cash position: $2.2B ($1.76 per share)
- Debt-to-equity ratio: 56.6% (down from 100.6% five years ago; deleveraging trend)
- Current ratio: 2.21x (ample liquidity)

**Share buybacks:**
- Repurchased ~$990M of stock in Q1 FY2026 (September quarter)
- Recent quarterly run-rate: Q4 FY2025: $1.3B; Q3 FY2025: $435M; Q2 FY2025: $698M
- Demonstrates aggressive capital return during AI upcycle
- Authorization remaining: Not disclosed but historically replenished

**Dividends:**
- Quarterly dividend: $0.26/share ($1.04 annualized)
- Dividend yield: 0.64% (modest given growth orientation)
- 11 consecutive years of dividend increases
- Total capital return (buybacks + dividends): ~$1.3B in Q1 (substantially exceeds net income)

**Free cash flow:**
- Trailing 12-month FCF: $5.55B
- Operating cash flow: $6.38B (LTM)
- Capex: $834M (LTM)
- FCF conversion remains robust; management prioritizing shareholder returns over balance sheet hoarding

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Thesis intact; AI secular tailwind drives multi-year earnings visibility. Record margins (50.6% gross, 35% operating) validate LRCX's strategic positioning in advanced etch/deposition for leading-edge AI/HBM manufacturing. China headwind ($600M CY2026 impact) is manageable noise given foundry/logic strength and geography rebalancing. Q2 guide appears conservative with $300M range absorbing uncertainty; underlying demand trajectory supports upside case. Valuation remains reasonable at current levels (~25x NTM P/E) given 15%+ long-term EPS CAGR potential, 50%+ incremental margins, and aggressive capital return ($5.5B+ annual FCF run-rate). Multiple expansion likely if China export restrictions stabilize and foundry capex cycle extends into 2026. Maintain conviction.
