# HEADLINE: IOT Q3 FY26 Review: First GAAP Profit, 29% Growth, but Shares Drift on Muted After-Hours Reaction

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

- **First-ever GAAP profitability**: GAAP EPS of $0.01 vs. consensus loss of -$0.03, marking inflection from revenue growth story to sustainable profit generator. Non-GAAP operating margin of 19% (up 900 bps YoY) exceeded FY26 full-year guide of 16%, signaling operating leverage acceleration.
- **Robust top-line momentum persists**: 29% revenue growth driven by record net new ARR ($105M, +24% YoY)—highest growth rate in seven quarters—and 20% of net new ACV from products launched in past year (AI Multicam, Asset Maintenance, Connected Workflows).
- **Large enterprise traction deepening**: Added quarterly-record 219 customers with $100K+ ARR (now 2,990 total, +35% YoY) and tied record with 17 new $1M+ ARR customers (164 total). Notable wins: State of New York, global oilfield services provider, top-five North American school bus operator.
- **Stock reaction muted despite beat**: Shares initially jumped ~4.3% after-hours on December 5, 2025, but pared gains to close extended trading down ~0.8%, suggesting investors had already priced in strong execution and are now focused on deceleration in FY27 revenue guide (Q4 implies 22% YoY growth vs. 29% in Q3).

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**Q4 FY26 Guidance** (vs. Prior Consensus):
- Revenue: $421M–$423M (midpoint $422M, +22% YoY in constant currency vs. 29% in Q3)—implies sequential deceleration
- Non-GAAP Operating Margin: 16% (down 300 bps QoQ from Q3's 19%)
- Non-GAAP EPS: $0.12–$0.13

**Full Year FY26 Guidance** (Raised):
- Revenue: $1.595B–$1.597B (midpoint $1.596B, +28% YoY)—raised from prior $1.589B–$1.593B
- Non-GAAP Operating Margin: 16% (maintained)
- Non-GAAP EPS: $0.50–$0.51 (raised from $0.46–$0.48)

**CFO Commentary**: Management highlighted macro headwinds from elongated enterprise sales cycles but emphasized secular tailwinds from AI-driven safety/compliance demand and multiproduct bundle adoption. Noted Q4 guide reflects "conservative posture" given typical seasonality and tougher comps.

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

Samsara does not report segment revenue by product line, but disclosed **product adoption metrics**:

- **New Products (launched past year)** contributed **20% of net new ACV** in Q3, led by:
  - AI Multicam (video-based safety)
  - Asset Maintenance
  - Asset Tags
  - Connected Training
  - Connected Workflows
- **Video-Based Safety & Telematics** remain core revenue drivers (specific growth rates not disclosed)
- **Public Sector & Construction** verticals cited as "robust demand" areas, evidenced by State of New York win
- **International** expansion cited as growth vector, though no specific revenue contribution disclosed

**Assessment**: Lack of segment-level disclosure limits visibility into product mix shifts. Investor focus on ARR cohort metrics (e.g., $100K+ customers growing 35% YoY) as proxy for enterprise upsell velocity.

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

- **Cash & Equivalents**: Not disclosed in earnings release (refer to 10-Q filing for precise figure)
- **Total Debt**: $0 (debt-free for past 5 years)
- **Total Shareholder Equity**: $1.2B
- **Free Cash Flow Margin**: 13% in Q3 (up from prior quarters)
- **Share Buyback**: No program announced or authorized
- **Dividend**: None

**Assessment**: Conservative balance sheet with zero debt provides ample dry powder for M&A or strategic investments. Absence of buyback program despite GAAP profitability suggests management prioritizes organic R&D spend to sustain 20%+ growth rates.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis intact; margin inflection de-risks valuation, but growth deceleration caps multiple expansion.** Q3 marks strategic milestone (first GAAP profit, 19% non-GAAP operating margin) that validates long-term unit economics, yet Q4 guide implies growth stepping down to low-20s% range. At ~$40/share (post-earnings), IOT trades at ~9x NTM revenue (est. FY27 revenue ~$2B)—premium to SaaS peers but justified by 25%+ growth, expanding margins, and large TAM. **Rating: Market Perform.** Upside hinges on re-acceleration in net new ARR growth and segment disclosure to alleviate concerns over product mix commoditization.
