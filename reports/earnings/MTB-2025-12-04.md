# MTB Q3 2025 Review: EPS Beats by 10%, NIM Expands; Stock Dips on Deposit Concerns

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **Robust beat driven by margin expansion and expense discipline**: NIM widened 28 bps YoY to 3.68% on favorable asset/liability repricing, while efficiency ratio improved 150 bps YoY to 53.6%; net income rose 11% YoY to $792M.
* **Credit quality improved, but deposit attrition persists**: Nonaccrual loans declined to 1.10% (from 1.16% in Q2), but average deposits fell $700M QoQ to $162.7B, driven by $1.1B decline in noninterest-bearing deposits—a headwind to NII outlook.
* **Stock dipped -2.4% post-earnings despite beat**: Market focused on tepid deposit growth and conservative NII guidance (Q4: $1.8B, implying flat sequential); aggressive capital return continued with $409M in buybacks and 11% dividend hike to $1.50.

---

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**Q4 2025 Guidance** (Reaffirmed):
* **Net Interest Income**: ~$1.8B (implies sequential flatness from Q3)
* **Net Interest Margin**: ~3.70% (+2 bps sequential)
* **Fee Income**: $670-690M
* **GAAP Expenses**: $1,350-1,370M
* **Net Charge-Offs**: 40-50 bps
* **Loan Balances**: $137-138B
* **Deposits**: $163-164B

**Full Year 2025**:
* **Net Interest Income**: Low end of $7.0-7.15B range (slightly below midpoint)
* **Rate Cut Assumptions**: Two additional Fed cuts in Q4 2025

**Management Commentary on Macro**:
* **Tailwinds**: Economy remains in "relatively good shape"; favorable asset repricing continues to benefit NIM; stress capital buffer decreased to 2.7% (from 3.8%) on October 1, improving capital flexibility.
* **Headwinds**: Noninterest-bearing deposit migration persists; loan/deposit growth expected to track GDP (~2-3%); CFO noted conservative underwriting to avoid credit deterioration ("don't want to put loans on the books that aren't going to be there in the next year or two").

---

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**Net Interest Income** (Core revenue driver):
* Taxable-equivalent NII: Increased $34M YoY on 28 bps spread widening
* NIM: 3.68% (+28 bps YoY, +6 bps QoQ)
* Interest-bearing deposit cost: 2.36% (-2 bps QoQ, favorable trend)

**Loan Portfolio** ($136.5B avg. loans, +$1.1B QoQ):
* Commercial & Industrial: +1% QoQ
* Commercial Real Estate: +3% QoQ
* Consumer Loans: +3% QoQ (strongest growth)
* Residential Real Estate: -4% QoQ (continued runoff)

**Deposits** ($162.7B avg. deposits, -$700M QoQ):
* Noninterest-bearing deposits: -$1.1B QoQ (ongoing migration)
* Interest-bearing deposits: +$397M QoQ (partial offset)
* Deposit beta compression continues, but volume pressure persists

**Fee Income**:
* Trust income: Expected to meet ~$185M consensus for Q3
* Noninterest income trends stable

**Credit Quality** (Improving):
* Nonaccrual loans: 1.10% of total loans (-6 bps QoQ)
* Criticized loans: $7.8B (-$584M QoQ)
* Provision for credit losses: $125M (flat QoQ)

---

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

**Capital Position**:
* CET1 ratio: 10.99% (unchanged QoQ; well above 4.50% minimum + 2.7% SCB)
* Stress Capital Buffer: Decreased to 2.7% (from 3.8%) on October 1, 2025

**Capital Deployment** ($2.1B YTD through Q3):
* **Share repurchases**: $409M in Q3 2025; $2.1B YTD (vs. $198M in 9M 2024)
* **Dividend**: Increased 11% to $1.50/quarter (31% payout ratio; 29% of operating FCF)

**Balance Sheet Strength**:
* Cash and equivalents: $18.7B (robust core liquidity)
* Total deposits: $162.7B (-$700M QoQ average)

---

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Solid execution on margin and efficiency, but deposit attrition and conservative NII guidance temper upside—**thesis intact, but multiple expansion unlikely near-term absent deposit stabilization**.
