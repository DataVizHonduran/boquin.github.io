## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

- **Volume decline the persistent headwind:** Volume/mix contracted 3.5% YoY, with weakness across North America and International Developed Markets. Consumer trade-down and promotional sensitivity continue to pressure branded categories.
- **EPS beat masks deteriorating fundamentals:** Adjusted EPS of $0.61 beat consensus by 5.5%, but this was achieved through cost discipline rather than top-line strength. Operating income guidance was slashed further.
- **Guidance cut for second time in 2025:** Full-year Organic Net Sales guidance lowered to -3.0% to -3.5% (from -1.5% to -3.5%); Adjusted EPS range narrowed to $2.50–$2.57 (from $2.51–$2.67).
- **Stock traded down 1.7%** immediately following results as investors digested the profit forecast reduction despite the EPS beat.

### CFO Commentary (Andre Maciel)
- Profit revision driven by: (1) lower U.S. consumption expectations, (2) slower Taste Elevation recovery, (3) incremental inflation in Meat and Coffee, (4) Q3 supply chain disruptions
- Increasing promotional investments by ~$300M in U.S., with $80M incremental marketing spend
- Promotional ROIs "are low" but focused on building long-term brand strength through household penetration

## CORPORATE ACTIONS

### Spin-Off Announcement (September 2, 2025)
- Board approved plan to separate into **two independent, publicly traded companies** via tax-free spin-off
- **Target completion:** Second half of 2026
- **Global Taste Elevation Co.:** Heinz, Philadelphia, Kraft Mac & Cheese (3 billion-dollar brands)
- **Remaining entity:** Focused on North American retail grocery

Management believes separation will "allow each business to better focus resources, improve execution, reduce complexity, and drive further efficiencies."

---

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis under pressure; execution story not gaining traction.** The second guidance cut of 2025 underscores the structural challenges facing legacy packaged food: persistent volume declines, elevated promotional spending with low ROIs, and limited pricing power as consumers trade down. The spin-off provides optionality but won't close until 2H 2026. At ~10x forward P/E with a 5.3% dividend yield, KHC offers value characteristics, but multiple expansion is unlikely until volume trends stabilize or spin-off unlocks value. Key catalyst is Q4 holiday consumption data.
