# FLG (NYCB) Q4 2024 Review: Loss Narrows 32% vs. Estimates; Stock Surges +15% on Turnaround Progress

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **Loss narrowed significantly, beating expectations by wide margin**: Adjusted net loss of $0.34/share beat consensus of -$0.50 by 32%, driven by $600M expense reduction initiative and stabilizing deposit base (+3% retail/private bank deposits QoQ).
* **Balance sheet restructuring accelerates despite elevated NPAs**: Total assets declined to $100.2B (-12% YoY) as multi-family loans fell $3.2B (-9% YoY) and CRE loans dropped $1.8B (-17% YoY); NPA ratio spiked to $2.6B (from $442M in Q4 2023) reflecting aggressive problem loan recognition.
* **Stock surged +15% on turnaround optimism**: Market rallied on better-than-expected loss, 280 bps YoY CET1 improvement to 11.9%, and management's Q4 2025 profitability guidance—investors pricing in successful pivot from legacy CRE exposure.

---

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**2025 Guidance** (Initial):
* **Net Interest Income**: $1,700-1,750M
* **Efficiency Ratio**: 85-90%
* **Expense Reduction Target**: $600M savings in 2025
* **Profitability Timeline**: Return to profitability by Q4 2025

**2026-2027 Targets**:
* **Net Interest Income (2027)**: $2,400-2,450M (+40% vs. 2025)
* **Efficiency Ratio (2027)**: 65-70% (vs. 85-90% in 2025)
* **ROATCE (2027)**: 8-9%

**Management Commentary on Strategy**:
* **Turnaround Pillars**: (1) Reduce CRE exposure via payoffs/sales; (2) Grow C&I and residential mortgage portfolios; (3) $600M expense reduction; (4) Stabilize deposit base (retail/private bank focus)
* **Tailwinds**: CET1 ratio improved 280 bps to 11.9%; retail deposits +3% QoQ to $35.9B; private bank deposits +3% QoQ to $18.2B; sold noncore mortgage warehouse and servicing businesses
* **Headwinds**: NIM compressed to 1.73% (-6 bps QoQ, -38% YoY); deposits declined -8.6% QoQ to $75.9B; net charge-offs rose 20% YoY to $222M; NPA ratio at elevated $2.6B

---

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**Loan Portfolio** ($68.3B, -4% QoQ):
* **Multi-Family Loans**: Declined $3.2B (-9% YoY) as strategic CRE runoff continues
* **Commercial Real Estate**: Fell $1.8B (-17% YoY) reflecting aggressive portfolio reduction
* **C&I Loans**: Modest decline; targeted for growth in 2025+
* **Residential Mortgage**: Targeted for growth as CRE exposure declines

**Deposit Base** ($75.9B, -8.6% QoQ):
* **Retail Deposits**: $35.9B (+3% QoQ) – bright spot in funding profile
* **Private Banking Deposits**: $18.2B (+3% QoQ) – stable, relationship-driven base
* **Wholesale Borrowings**: Declined $5.9B, now 13% of total assets (improving funding mix)
* **Brokered CDs**: Decreased $2.5B (reducing reliance on expensive funding)

**Credit Quality** (Deteriorating, but expected):
* **Non-Performing Assets**: $2.6B (vs. $442M at Dec 31, 2023) – aggressive problem loan recognition
* **Allowance for Credit Losses**: 1.78% of total loans (vs. 1.87% in Q3, 1.23% at Dec 31, 2023)
* **Net Charge-Offs**: $222M (+20% YoY)
* **Provision for Credit Losses**: $108M (-80.4% YoY, reflecting proactive 2023-2024 reserve builds)

**Net Interest Income**:
* Q4 2024 NII: $461M (-10% QoQ, -38% YoY)
* NIM: 1.73% (-6 bps QoQ)

---

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

**Capital Position** (Significantly Strengthened):
* **CET1 Ratio**: 11.86% (vs. 9.05% at Dec 31, 2023; +281 bps improvement)
* **Total Risk-Based Capital Ratio**: 15.17% (vs. 11.77% in Q4 2023)
* **Stress Capital Buffer**: Strong capital cushion built through asset sales and equity raise

**Balance Sheet**:
* **Total Assets**: $100.2B at Dec 31, 2024 (-12% YoY)
* **Total Loans**: $69.2B
* **Total Deposits**: $75.9B (-8.6% QoQ)
* **Total Stockholders' Equity**: $8.2B

**Capital Deployment**:
* No dividends or buybacks during turnaround phase (capital preservation mode)
* Focused on organic capital generation and balance sheet repair

**Asset Sales** (2024):
* Sold mortgage warehouse business
* Sold mortgage servicing business
* Proceeds reinvested in capital and liquidity

---

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Turnaround gaining traction; Q4 beat and +15% stock rally validate restructuring progress—**accumulate for 2026 profitability play**, but near-term volatility persists given elevated NPAs and NIM pressure.
