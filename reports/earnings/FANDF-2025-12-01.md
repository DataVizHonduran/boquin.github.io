## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

* **Resilient double-digit earnings growth absorbs major UK provision hit**: Normalized earnings of R41.8B (+10% YoY) delivered despite R2.7B additional UK motor commission provision (£146M), bringing cumulative provisions to R6.3B. Excluding provision, earnings would have grown 16%, demonstrating underlying operational strength. ROE of 20.2% (21.2% ex-UK provision) reflects robust profitability despite headwinds. Strong NII growth (+12%) driven by 7% advances growth and 8% deposit gathering offset provision impact.

* **Improved operational leverage drives cost efficiency; credit quality resilient**: Cost-to-income ratio improved materially to 50.8% (prior: higher, specific comparison not disclosed), reflecting operating leverage from revenue growth and cost discipline. Credit loss ratio remained healthy despite challenging macro environment, with WesBank delivering particularly strong 20% earnings growth. Net asset value accretion of 11% and capital position solid (CET1 ratio well above internal targets, estimated ~13.5-13.6%) support continued growth investments.

* **Stock reaction not disclosed; EPS missed consensus despite strong fundamentals**: EPS of 748.8 cents increased 10% YoY but missed analyst expectations (specific miss quantum not disclosed). Market likely focused on UK provision uncertainty rather than underlying operational performance. Dividend of 466 cents per share (+12% YoY implied) at 1.6x cover (63% payout ratio) signals confidence in earnings sustainability. FY2026 outlook positive: management expects continued top-end ROE and strong earnings growth.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

* **FY2026 Revenue & Earnings**: Management expects "continued top-end ROE and strong earnings growth" in FY2026, implying ROE sustaining near 20%+ levels and earnings growth in high single-digits to low double-digits. No specific numerical guidance provided. Confidence driven by: (1) robust credit performance; (2) continued cost discipline; (3) balance sheet growth momentum (advances +7%, deposits +8%); (4) no further material UK provisions anticipated.

* **Macro & Execution Commentary**: CFO noted macroeconomic environment characterized by ongoing global fracturing, geopolitical uncertainty (Ukraine, Middle East, U.S. tariffs), but South African operations benefiting from improved domestic political stability and reform momentum. Competitive pressures remain elevated in retail banking, but customer growth and volume gains offsetting margin pressure. FNB achieved 5% advances growth and 8% deposits growth in highly competitive market. UK operations (Aldermore/MotoNovo) represent ~10% of group earnings and ~20% of balance sheet; management confident R6.3B cumulative provisions adequate for UK motor commission matter pending appeal outcomes.

* **FY2026 Capital Allocation**: Dividend cover maintained at bottom end of 1.6x-2.0x range (1.6x FY2025), implying payout ratio ~60-65%. Dividend growth expected to track earnings growth (high single-digits). No share buyback program announced or anticipated; capital deployment focused on organic growth and maintaining CET1 ratio well above regulatory requirements (internal target range upper bound estimated ~13-14%). Expect continued investment in digital transformation and customer acquisition, particularly in FNB retail and commercial franchises.

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

* **Capital Position**: CET1 ratio "well above upper end of internal target range," estimated at 13.5-13.6% based on H1 FY2025 disclosure (13.6%) and FY2024 comparison (13.5%). Regulatory minimum significantly lower; group maintaining substantial buffer for growth flexibility and resilience. Total capital adequacy and leverage ratios not disclosed but implied to be robust given dividend payout and growth trajectory.

* **Balance Sheet Growth**: Advances growth +7% (driven by FNB retail/commercial, WesBank vehicle finance, RMB corporate lending). Deposits growth +8% (strong FNB retail franchise gathering). Net Interest Margin (NIM) not disclosed separately but NII growth of +12% outpaced advances growth (+7%), implying NIM expansion or favorable mix shift. Capital endowment balances +14%.

* **Dividends**: Total dividend 466 cents per share for FY2025 (interim 219 cents + final 247 cents), up ~12% YoY implied. Dividend cover 1.6x (payout ratio 63%), at bottom end of board-approved 1.6x-2.0x range, signaling confidence in earnings sustainability and capital adequacy. Dividend yield ~6.7% based on recent share price levels.

* **Share Buybacks**: No share buyback program disclosed or announced. FirstRand prioritizing organic growth investment and dividend returns over capital return via buybacks. Capital allocation philosophy favors maintaining high ROE (20%+) through profitable growth rather than balance sheet shrinkage.

* **Cash Flow & Liquidity**: Specific operating cash flow figures not disclosed in earnings materials. Banking operations generate strong recurring cash flow from net interest income and fee income. Liquidity position robust given deposit growth (+8%) and diversified funding base. No liquidity constraints or funding concerns noted.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Thesis intact; valuation undemanding given 20%+ ROE profile. Normalized earnings growth of 10% (16% ex-UK provision) at 20.2% ROE demonstrates FirstRand's resilient franchise despite R2.7B UK motor commission hit. Strong operational performance evident: NII +12%, cost-to-income ratio improved to 50.8%, credit quality solid. Segment diversification shines: WesBank +20% earnings, RMB +10%, FNB +8%, offsetting UK headwinds. EPS miss versus consensus (specific quantum undisclosed) likely reflects analyst skepticism on UK provision adequacy (cumulative R6.3B), but management confident pending appeal outcomes. FY2026 outlook constructive: ROE sustaining at top end (20%+), earnings growth continuing high single-digits to low double-digits, driven by South African macro stabilization and continued market share gains. Dividend growth (+12% implied) at 1.6x cover (63% payout) attractive; 6.7% yield provides downside support. CET1 ratio ~13.5-13.6% well capitalized for growth. UK exposure (10% earnings, 20% balance sheet) remains key risk; further provisions possible if Supreme Court appeal fails, but downside likely limited given conservative provisioning. Trade at ~9.8x P/E (TTM), undemanding for 20%+ ROE and double-digit earnings growth profile. Maintain Outperform; target P/E 11-12x, implying 15-20% upside. Risks: UK motor commission appeals, South African political/macro volatility, competitive margin pressure.
