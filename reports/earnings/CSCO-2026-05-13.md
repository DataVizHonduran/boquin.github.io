### 3. Management Narrative & Guidance
- **The "Why" (beat):** Networking demand explosion from simultaneous hyperscaler AI builds + multi-year campus refresh cycle; total product orders +35% YoY (+19% ex-hyperscalers), campus networking orders >25% YoY, DC switching orders >40% YoY
- **The "Why" (AI raise):** $5.3B in AI infrastructure orders YTD → FY26 AI orders target raised to $9B (from $5B); FY26 AI revenue guidance raised to $4B (from $3B)
- **Guidance Update:** Raised FY26 revenue to $62.8–$63.0B (vs prior $61.6B street consensus); raised Non-GAAP EPS to $4.27–$4.29 (from $4.13–$4.17). Q4 FY26: Revenue $16.7–$16.9B | Non-GAAP EPS $1.16–$1.18. All guidance includes estimated tariff impact.
- **Restructuring:** New plan announced — up to $1B in charges to redirect investment toward silicon, optics, security, and AI. ~$450M in Q4 FY26; remainder in FY27.
- **Capital Allocation:** Returned $2.91B in Q3 — $1.66B dividends ($0.42/share; next payment July 22, 2026) + $1.25B buybacks (~16M shares at $80.28 avg). $9.6B remaining buyback authorization (no expiry). RPO $43.5B (+4% YoY); Deferred Revenue $28.6B (+2% YoY).
