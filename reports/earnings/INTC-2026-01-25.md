## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **Fifth consecutive beat, but guidance crushed the stock:** Intel delivered Q4 results above expectations on every metric—revenue, gross margin, and EPS all beat guidance. Yet shares plunged 17% (worst day since 2024) as Q1 2026 guidance came in well below Street expectations due to acute internal supply constraints.

- **Supply, not demand, is the bottleneck:** CFO David Zinsner emphasized that Intel depleted buffer inventory supporting H2 2025 demand, and wafer mix shifts toward servers won't flow through until late Q1. Supply is "most acute" in Q1 before improving in Q2 and beyond.

- **Data Center surging, Client Computing lagging:** DCAI revenue jumped 15% sequentially to $4.7B—the fastest sequential growth this decade—while CCG declined 4% to $8.2B as Intel prioritized server supply over PCs.

- **Foundry losses persist but 18A momentum builds:** Intel Foundry posted another $2.5B operating loss, but revenue grew 6% QoQ driven by Intel 18A ramp and external foundry wins. Custom ASICs now exceed $1B annualized run rate (+50% growth).

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

### Intel Products ($12.9B, +2% QoQ)

#### Client Computing Group - CCG ($8.2B, -4% QoQ)
- PC demand remains solid but Intel is intentionally constraining supply
- AI PC adoption accelerating with double-digit YoY growth
- Supply prioritization shifted toward higher-margin server products
- Expect more pronounced Q1 decline vs. DCAI

#### Data Center and AI - DCAI ($4.7B, +15% QoQ)
- Fastest sequential growth this decade
- Server CPU demand strong; Xeon momentum continues
- Custom ASICs surged >50% to annualized run rate >$1B
- Benefiting from internal supply prioritization

### Intel Foundry ($4.5B, +6% QoQ)
- Operating loss: $2.5B (improved from prior quarters)
- Intel 18A volumes ramping with premium pricing
- External foundry revenue growing
- EUV wafer mix increasing
- Q1 expected up double-digits QoQ

### All Other ($574M, -42% QoQ)
- Decline due to Altera deconsolidation (sold 51% stake in September 2025)
- Now primarily Mobileye and IMS Nanofabrication
- Mobileye facing industry headwinds but remains strategic

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**The Q4 beat doesn't change the structural concerns.** Intel delivered solid execution on a quarter where expectations were already low, but the weak Q1 guidance and continued Foundry losses (~$10B annually) highlight the long road ahead. The supply constraint narrative provides cover, but Jefferies' assessment remains apt: "no clear path forward given further share loss, no AI strategy and unclear fab/packaging opportunities." The stock's 17% single-day drop reflects skepticism that internal restructuring can offset competitive pressures from AMD, NVIDIA, and ARM-based designs. Key catalysts to watch: (1) Q2 supply recovery, (2) Intel 18A customer wins, (3) progress toward 40% gross margin target. Until Foundry reaches breakeven and DCAI can demonstrate sustainable share gains, INTC remains a "show me" story trading on hope rather than fundamentals.
