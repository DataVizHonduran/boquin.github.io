## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

* **Record revenue validates Edge AI transition momentum.** Q3 revenue of $108.5M (+31% YoY, +14% QoQ) marked a new quarterly high, driven by Edge AI products reaching 80% of sales mix—the sixth consecutive quarter of record Edge AI performance. CV5 shipments exceeded 1M units, with 5nm-based products (CV5/CV7) now representing >45% of total revenue, cementing the platform shift from legacy CV2x.

* **Profitability inflection with robust cash generation.** Non-GAAP EPS of $0.27 crushed consensus ($0.21) by 29%, while free cash flow surged to $31.4M (29% of revenue). Operating cash flow of $34.3M drove cash position to $295.3M (+$34M QoQ), demonstrating operating leverage as Edge AI volume scales despite 60.9% gross margins (down from 62%+ peak).

* **Q4 guidance disappoints; stock down 1.8% after-hours.** Despite raising FY2026 revenue to $390M midpoint (36-38% growth vs. prior 31-35%), Q4 guidance of $97-103M implies sequential decline at midpoint (-8% QoQ), with gross margin guided to 59.0-60.5% (midpoint down 120bps QoQ). Management cited "normal seasonality" and automotive softness, but investors focused on implied H2 deceleration.

* **IoT strength offsets automotive weakness; CV7 ramps.** IoT (>75% of revenue) grew mid-teens QoQ, led by robotics, drones, and body-worn cameras, while automotive grew mid-single digits QoQ as CV7 production revenue accelerated. However, Q4 guide suggests IoT sequential decline, raising questions about sustainability of non-security IoT demand.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis intact, but near-term momentum questions emerge.** Q3's record $108.5M revenue (+31% YoY) and robust FCF ($31.4M, 29% of revenue) validate Ambarella's Edge AI platform transition, with CV5/CV7 5nm products now >45% of sales and Edge AI at 80% mix. The EPS beat ($0.27 vs. $0.21 consensus) and cash generation ($295.3M liquidity, up $68.8M YoY) demonstrate operational leverage despite gross margin compression (60.9% vs. 62%+ peak).

**However, Q4 guidance raises red flags.** Revenue guide of $97-103M (midpoint $100M, -8% QoQ) implies sequential decline despite full-year raise to $390M (36-38% growth). Management cited "normal seasonality" in IoT security cameras and automotive softness, but the -8% QoQ midpoint suggests demand headwinds beyond seasonality. Gross margin guide of 59.0-60.5% (midpoint 59.75%, -115bps QoQ) indicates continued product mix pressure as lower-margin CV5 ramps displace mature CV2x.

**The automotive wildcard remains unproven.** While CV7 production revenue is ramping (ASP $high-teens to $40, 5nm ADAS-grade SoC), automotive is still "low-double-digit %" of revenue (~$12-15M in Q3) and faces "inventory digestion" at Tier 1s. For AMBA to sustain 30%+ growth beyond FY2026, automotive must inflect to >25% of sales—a multi-quarter (if not multi-year) journey given 18-24 month design win cycles.

**Valuation implication:** At $108.5M Q3 revenue and $390M FY2026 guide, AMBA is trading at ~7-8x forward sales (assuming $90-100 stock price post-earnings). For a 36-38% revenue grower with 80% Edge AI exposure, that's reasonable but not cheap—especially with gross margins compressing toward 59-60% (vs. 65%+ for edge AI peers). **The 1.8% after-hours decline reflects Q4 guide disappointment; stock likely range-bound until FY2027 outlook clarifies whether Q4 is seasonal trough or demand inflection.** Multiple expansion requires: (1) automotive revenue inflection (CV7 >$20M/quarter), (2) gross margin stabilization/recovery (back to 61-62%), or (3) FY2027 guidance implying sustained >30% growth. Near-term, accumulate on weakness if stock tests $70-75 support; avoid chase above $90 until Q4 results confirm demand sustainability.
