# HEADLINE: SQM Q3 2025 Review: Record Lithium Volumes Drive Beat; Margins Compress on Pricing Headwinds

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

* **Record lithium volumes offset pricing pressure**: SQM delivered highest-ever quarterly lithium sales (72,900 MT, +43% YoY) at average realized prices down 15% YoY, demonstrating operational leverage from low-cost Salar de Atacama production. Lithium revenue +21.4% to $603.7M despite ASP compression.

* **Beat driven by volume acceleration, not pricing power**: Adjusted EBITDA of $404M (+24% YoY) exceeded Bloomberg consensus by 0.5%, but EPS of $0.62 missed Street expectations of $0.65 by -$0.03. Thesis-changing metric: lithium demand trajectory reinforced by +25% YoY global demand growth, validating structural bull case.

* **Market rewarded operational execution**: Stock surged +5.8% in after-hours trading to $62.83, signaling investor confidence in volume-driven margin recovery despite near-term pricing volatility. Focus shifted from per-unit economics to market share capture in recovering lithium cycle.

* **Codelco JV clears final regulatory hurdle**: China antitrust approval secured for landmark joint venture, removing key execution risk and positioning SQM for stable long-term production access to Salar de Atacama through 2060.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**Full Year 2025 (Reaffirmed/Raised):**
* Chile lithium production: 230,000 MT (reaffirmed)
* Mount Holland (Australia) sales: 23,000-24,000 MT (raised from prior 20,000 MT)
* Chilean operations sales volume: +10% minimum vs. 2024 (reaffirmed)

**Capital Allocation Updated:**
* Total CapEx 2025-2027: $2.7B (revised down from prior guidance without impacting capacity targets)
* Iodine & Plant Nutrition CapEx: ~$800M over 2025-2027
* Mount Holland (Kwinana) refinery: Now at full capacity (50,000 MT LiOH annually, 50% attributable to SQM)

**Macro/Market Commentary:**
* **Demand acceleration**: Management projects global lithium demand >1.5M MT in 2025 (+25% YoY), driven by EV penetration in Europe (+30% YoY) and energy storage systems growth.
* **2026 demand forecast**: Exceeds 1.7M MT, implying sustained structural growth above prior baseline assumptions.
* **Pricing stabilization**: Management noted firmer lithium pricing environment in Q3 vs. Q2, suggesting bottoming process underway after 18-month downcycle.
* **No mention of**: Tariff exposure, China demand slowdown risks, or potash market recovery timeline.

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**Lithium & Derivatives (51% of revenue):**
* Revenue: $603.7M (+21.4% YoY)
* Volumes: 72,900 MT (+43% YoY) – **Record quarterly sales**
* Average selling price: -15% YoY (pricing headwinds persist but moderating)
* Key driver: Low-cost Atacama operations scaling efficiently; deliberately reduced potash output to maximize lithium extraction from shared brine resources.

**Iodine & Derivatives (21% of revenue):**
* Revenue: $244.6M (+4.7% YoY)
* Average price: ~$73/kg (stable)
* Gross margin: 57% (contributed >50% of total gross profit despite smaller revenue share)
* Key driver: X-ray contrast media demand resilient; prices firm across key markets.

**Specialty Plant Nutrition (22% of revenue):**
* Revenue: $259.8M (+4.3% YoY)
* Performance: Modest but sustainable volume and revenue growth
* Key driver: Stable demand fundamentals; no commentary on pricing trends.

**Potassium (3% of revenue):**
* Revenue: $33.8M (-50.4% YoY)
* Volumes: -62% YoY (strategic reduction)
* Gross profit contribution: <1% of consolidated total
* Key driver: **Deliberate strategic shift** – SQM prioritizing lithium over potash extraction from Salar de Atacama to optimize resource allocation and reduce total brine extraction.

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

**Balance Sheet Strength:**
* Total equity: $5.1B
* Total debt: $4.8B
* Debt-to-equity ratio: 93.9% (0.89x net leverage – moderate)
* Current ratio: 2.92x (strong liquidity position)
* Q3 cash flow: -$90.5M net change in cash (reflecting CapEx deployment)

**Capital Deployment:**
* CapEx revised to $2.7B for 2025-2027 (down from prior guidance, no capacity impact)
* Mount Holland ramp continues on schedule; Kwinana refinery now at full 50,000 MT LiOH capacity
* No share buyback activity disclosed for Q3 2025
* **Dividend**: No dividend currently paid (capital prioritized for growth investments)

**CapEx Allocation by Segment:**
* Lithium expansion (Chile + Australia): Majority of $2.7B envelope
* Iodine & Plant Nutrition: ~$800M over 2025-2027

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis intact; valuation re-rating likely on demand visibility.** Q3 results validate SQM's operational thesis: record volumes from lowest-cost asset base (Salar de Atacama) driving margin recovery despite ASP compression. The +5.8% after-hours move reflects market recognition that volume trajectory matters more than near-term pricing in current cycle phase. Key thesis-reinforcing data points: (1) lithium demand +25% YoY in 2025 with acceleration to >1.7M MT in 2026, (2) Codelco JV de-risked via China regulatory clearance, (3) cost structure resilience with iodine contributing >50% of gross profit at 57% margins.

**Risks to monitor**: (1) Lithium ASP trajectory if China EV demand disappoints or battery tech shifts accelerate, (2) execution risk on Mount Holland ramp to nameplate capacity, (3) Chilean political/regulatory stability around Salar de Atacama concessions. Multiple expansion warranted if Q4 confirms pricing stabilization and 2026 volume guidance holds; stock likely re-rates toward $70-75 on 2026E estimates assuming lithium prices stabilize in $11-13K/MT range.
