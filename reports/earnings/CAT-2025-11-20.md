# HEADLINE: CAT Q4 2024 Review: EPS Beats but Revenue Declines; 2025 Outlook Cautious

| Adjusted EPS | $5.14 | -1.7% | Beat by 3.4% ($4.97 est.) |
| Operating Margin (Adj.) | 18.3% | -20 bps | N/A |

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

- **Mixed Quarter:** CAT delivered an EPS beat (+3.4% vs. consensus) driven by margin discipline and cost management, but topline contracted 5% YoY on weak volumes across Construction Industries (-8%) and Resource Industries (-9%). Energy & Transportation held flat.
- **Margin Resilience:** Despite revenue pressure, adjusted operating margin compressed only 20 bps YoY to 18.3%, reflecting pricing discipline and operational efficiency. FY 2024 adjusted margin expanded 20 bps to 20.7%.
- **Capital Returns:** CAT returned $10.3B to shareholders in 2024 ($7.7B buybacks, $2.6B dividends)—enabled by robust $9.4B ME&T free cash flow (top of target range). Balance sheet remains fortress-like with $6.9B enterprise cash.
- **Guidance Tempered:** 2025 sales expected "slightly lower" YoY, with margins in "top half of target range." Services revenue expected to grow across all segments. Management flagged dealer inventory normalization and macro headwinds.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**FY 2025 Guidance:**
- Sales & Revenues: Slightly lower than 2024 ($64.8B)
- Adjusted Operating Margin: Top half of target range (below 20.7% FY 2024 level)
- ME&T Free Cash Flow: Top half of $5B-$10B range
- CapEx: ~$2.5B (vs. $2.0B in 2024)

**Key Commentary:**
- Services revenue expected to grow across all segments in 2025
- Dealer inventory normalization ongoing; ended 2024 above mid-cycle levels
- Management cautious on end-market demand, particularly Construction and Mining
- Pricing remains supportive, but volume headwinds persist

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**Construction Industries:** $6.0B revenue (-8% YoY)
- Weakest segment; volume declines driven by softer North America residential construction and dealer destocking
- Margin pressure from lower volumes

**Resource Industries:** $3.0B revenue (-9% YoY)
- Profit declined 22% to $466M on lower mining equipment demand
- Aftermarket services held relatively steady

**Energy & Transportation:** $7.65B revenue (flat YoY)
- **Outperformer:** Segment profit rose 3% to $1.48B despite flat sales
- Oil & Gas, Power Generation, and Rail offsetting Marine weakness
- Strong pricing and mix effects

**Financial Products:** ~$1.0B revenue (+4% YoY)
- Small but steady contributor

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

**Cash & Liquidity:**
- Enterprise Cash: $6.9B (Q4 2024)
- Additional $2B in marketable securities for yield optimization
- Balance sheet characterized as "strong" by management

**Shareholder Returns (FY 2024):**
- Share Buybacks: $7.7B executed
- Dividends: $2.6B paid ($1.41/share quarterly, raised 8% in June 2024)
- Total Capital Returned: $10.3B
- Remaining Repurchase Authorization: ~$21.8B (no expiration)

**Free Cash Flow:**
- FY 2024 ME&T FCF: $9.4B (near top of $5-10B target range)
- FY 2024 Enterprise Operating Cash Flow: $12.0B
- CapEx: $2.0B (2024); $2.5B guided for 2025

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Thesis intact but growth decelerating. CAT demonstrated margin resilience and FCF generation in a demand-constrained environment, returning over $10B to shareholders. However, FY 2025 guidance for "slightly lower" sales and margin compression signals multiple contraction risk. Energy & Transportation segment strength and services growth provide downside protection. Valuation likely range-bound until topline inflects positive. Maintain constructive view on FCF yield and capital return program, but near-term multiple expansion unlikely absent demand recovery.
