## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **Earnings blowout driven by Macau + digital turnaround.** MGM China delivered record Q4 and FY EBITDA, capturing 16.5% Macau market share (highest annual level in company history). BetMGM achieved a ~$470M EBITDA improvement for the full year, distributing $135M to MGM in Q4 alone.
- **Las Vegas Strip remains the weak spot.** Net revenues declined 3% YoY to $2.2B, with adjusted EBITDAR down 4% to $735M, partially attributable to a ~$65M EBITDA disruption from the MGM Grand room renovation completed late in Q4.
- **Massive EPS beat obscures mixed operating picture.** While the top line and bottom line both exceeded expectations, the revenue composition shift (away from high-margin Vegas, toward lower-margin digital) warrants monitoring.
- **Stock dipped ~1.75% post-earnings** to ~$36.27 in pre-market, suggesting the market viewed the beat as non-recurring or discounted the Vegas softness.

---

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

- **Las Vegas:** Management expects EBITDA recovery in H2 2026 as MGM Grand renovation tailwind materializes. Mid-single-digit group and convention revenue growth anticipated; group mix expected to approach ~20% in 2026.
- **BetMGM:** Targeting $300-$350M in adjusted EBITDA for 2026, representing continued rapid scaling.
- **MGM Digital:** Expects to halve 2025 losses in 2026 (Q4 2025 EBITDAR loss of -$7M vs. -$22M prior year).
- **MGM China:** Branding fee will double in 2026, delivering >$50M incremental cash flow to the parent.
- **Overall:** CFO Jonathan Halkyard indicated solid top-line growth anticipated in 2026 with EBITDAR improvement expected to recover approximately half the losses incurred in 2025.

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

- **Share Repurchases:** $516M in Q4 2025 (15M shares at ~$32.43 avg). Full-year 2025 repurchases exceeded $1.2B. Share count reduced by nearly half over the past five years.
- **Remaining Authorization:** $1.6B as of December 31, 2025, under the $2B program authorized in April 2025.
- **Dividend:** MGM does not pay a dividend.
- **Debt:** Total debt of ~$6.9B; debt-to-equity ratio of ~179%.
- **Liquidity:** Current ratio of 1.23; quick ratio of 1.19. Interest coverage ratio of 3.24x.
- **Osaka:** Capital allocation priorities include funding the entirety of the MGM Osaka commitment alongside maintenance CapEx, interest, and rent obligations.

---

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Thesis intact but bifurcated — Macau re-rating and BetMGM cash flow inflection provide durable earnings growth, but multiple expansion is capped until Las Vegas Strip demonstrates organic EBITDA recovery post-renovation in H2 2026. Aggressive buyback cadence ($1.6B remaining) provides a floor; watch for Vegas group/convention mix improvement as the leading indicator.
