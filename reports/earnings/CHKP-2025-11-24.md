**Note:** Q3 EPS includes one-time tax benefit of ~$1.47/share from tax settlement; normalized EPS ~$2.47.

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **Primary driver of beat (with asterisk)**: Massive EPS beat ($3.94 vs. $2.45 consensus) driven primarily by $1.47/share one-time tax settlement benefit; normalized EPS of ~$2.47 still beat by $0.02 (+0.8%). Revenue beat modest at +0.6%. Operational performance strong with calculated billings +20% YoY to $672M, signaling robust demand conversion in coming quarters. Subscription revenue +10.3% reflects secular shift to recurring model.

- **Emerging tech portfolio inflection**: Harmony SASE, Harmony Email, and External Risk Management each grew >40% in ARR organically, demonstrating product-market fit in next-gen security categories. This cohort now drives meaningful growth offset to legacy network security headwinds. Management highlighted successful Lakera AI acquisition (announced in quarter) to bolster AI security positioning.

- **Guidance implies acceleration**: FY2025 revenue guide raised $15M to $2.700B-$2.740B (midpoint $2.720B, +6.5% YoY); Q4 guide $724M-$764M (midpoint $744M, +6.1% YoY). Q4 guide implies modest sequential revenue growth despite typical seasonality, suggesting pipeline strength. Q4 EPS guide $2.70-$2.80 (midpoint $2.75) reflects normalization post-tax benefit.

- **After-hours reaction**: Stock surged 11% post-earnings, driven by calculated billings acceleration (+20% YoY) and emerging tech traction validating multi-year platform transformation story. Market looked through one-time tax benefit to focus on operational momentum.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**Q4 2025 Guidance:**
- Revenue: $724M to $764M (midpoint $744M; +6.1% YoY, +9.7% QoQ at midpoint)
- Non-GAAP EPS: $2.70 to $2.80 (midpoint $2.75)
- Consensus prior to earnings: Revenue ~$756M, EPS ~$2.94
- Guidance midpoint below prior consensus by 1.6% on revenue, 6.5% on EPS

**Full Year 2025 (Raised):**
- Revenue: $2.700B to $2.740B (raised $15M from prior $2.685B-$2.725B)
- Midpoint $2.720B implies +6.5% YoY growth
- Non-GAAP EPS: $11.22 to $11.32 (includes Q3 tax benefit; normalized ~$9.75-$9.85)
- Prior full-year consensus: Revenue $2.72B, EPS $9.90

**Key Guidance Commentary:**
- CFO Roei Golan noted strong pipeline conversion with calculated billings +20% YoY significantly outpacing revenue growth, indicating deferred revenue build and future revenue visibility
- Management expects continued double-digit billings growth driven by Infinity platform adoption and Harmony suite traction
- No explicit margin guidance provided; Q3 operating margin ~33% in line with historical range

**Macro Commentary:**
- CEO Nadav Zafrir highlighted accelerating enterprise security refresh cycles driven by hybrid work, cloud migration, and heightened threat landscape
- APAC region inflected to +17% YoY growth (vs. +8% in Q2), driven by large deals in financial services and government verticals
- Americas growth reaccelerated to +10% (Q3) from +7% (Q2); EMEA stable at +7%
- Management cited no macro headwinds but noted elongated deal cycles for $1M+ transactions requiring executive-level approvals
- Competitive environment remains rational; Check Point gaining share in SASE and email security vs. point solution vendors

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

Check Point does not report traditional business unit segments but discloses revenue by geography and emerging product metrics:

**By Geography (% of Total Revenue, Q3 2025):**
- **Americas**: 41% of revenue | +10% YoY (vs. +7% in Q2; reacceleration driven by enterprise demand)
- **EMEA**: 46% of revenue | +7% YoY (stable growth; large deal activity in Western Europe)
- **APAC**: 13% of revenue | +17% YoY (vs. +8% in Q2; inflection driven by Japan and Australia government wins)

**By Revenue Type:**
- **Subscription Revenue**: $305M | +10.3% YoY (45% of total revenue; recurring model shift ongoing)
- **Product & Services Revenue**: $373M (implied) | +4.5% YoY (55% of total; includes hardware, perpetual licenses, and professional services)

**Emerging Technology Products (ARR-based):**
- **Harmony SASE**: >40% ARR growth YoY (secure access service edge; competes with Zscaler, Netskope)
- **Harmony Email & Collaboration**: >40% ARR growth YoY (email/Teams security; competes with Proofpoint, Mimecast)
- **External Risk Management**: >40% ARR growth YoY (attack surface management; competes with CrowdStrike, Palo Alto)
- **Quantum Security Gateways**: Stable revenue; mature product but still 40%+ of systems revenue
- **CloudGuard**: Mid-single-digit growth; cloud-native security competing with Palo Alto Prisma, Wiz

**Key Segment Takeaway**: Emerging products (Harmony suite + ERM) now represent material portion of billings growth and validating Check Point's platform transformation beyond legacy firewall. APAC inflection (+17% YoY) notable after quarters of mid-single-digit growth; suggests geopolitical security spend acceleration. Subscription revenue mix (45%) still below peers (60-70% for pure SaaS vendors) but improving.

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

**Cash & liquidity:**
- Cash, marketable securities, and short-term deposits: $2.817B (as of Sept 30, 2025)
- Down from $2.873B year-ago due to $160M land purchase for new Tel Aviv campus (disclosed in Q3) and ongoing share buybacks
- Zero debt; fortress balance sheet with $2.8B net cash (~$21/share, or 13% of market cap)

**Share buybacks:**
- Repurchased 1.6M shares in Q3 2025 at total cost of $325M (avg price ~$203/share)
- Year-to-date through Q3: Aggressive buyback activity totaling ~$900M+ (exact YTD figure not disclosed but implied from quarterly run-rate)
- Historical buyback authorization: Check Point has evergreen buyback program; typically repurchases $1B+ annually
- Capital return remains primary use of cash given high FCF generation (~$1.5B+ annually)

**Dividends:**
- Check Point does NOT pay a dividend
- Company discontinued dividend 15+ years ago; strategy focused exclusively on buybacks
- Management philosophy: Return 100% of excess FCF via buybacks given share-based comp dilution offset and opportunistic valuation

**Cash flow:**
- Operating cash flow Q3 2025: $241M (includes one-time $66M tax payment related to settlement)
- Adjusted for one-time tax payment: Operating cash flow ~$307M, up 23% YoY
- Free cash flow (not explicitly disclosed): Typically ~95% of operating cash flow given minimal capex (~$20M/quarter for IT infrastructure)
- Implied Q3 FCF: ~$287M (post-tax adjustment), annualized run-rate ~$1.15B

**Balance Sheet Quality:**
- Deferred revenue: $1.887B (+8% YoY), reflects strong renewal rates and multi-year deal structures
- Remaining performance obligations (RPO): $2.4B (+9% YoY), visibility into future revenue recognition
- Current ratio: Strong liquidity with current assets significantly exceeding current liabilities
- No debt; enterprise value ≈ market cap less $2.8B net cash

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Thesis intact but valuation stretched post-11% rally. Strip out $1.47/share tax benefit and normalized Q3 EPS is $2.47 (in-line with expectations), making revenue beat (+0.6%) and billings growth (+20%) the real story. Calculated billings acceleration to +20% YoY (vs. +7% revenue growth) is critical leading indicator—implies 1-2 quarter lag before billings convert to revenue, supporting mid-to-high single-digit revenue growth trajectory into FY2026. Emerging tech products (Harmony, ERM) validating platform evolution thesis with >40% ARR growth, but still <30% of total revenue mix; need 2-3 more quarters of sustained execution to declare inflection. APAC +17% YoY growth noteworthy after quarters of stagnation; monitor if sustainable or lumpy deal timing. Q4 guidance conservative (midpoint +6% YoY) but billings momentum suggests upside. Valuation at ~28x NTM P/E (adjusting for net cash) is full but defendable if billings growth sustains in double-digits and emerging tech scales to 40%+ of mix by FY2027. Risk/reward balanced; maintain Hold with positive bias. Await Q4 to confirm billings not one-quarter anomaly.
