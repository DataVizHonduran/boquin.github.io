## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **Revenue miss drives 14% intraday selloff.** WRBY reported $221.7M in Q3 revenue, missing consensus by $2.6M (1.2%), driven by consumer volatility in September after a robust July-August period. Management cited pressure among younger, lower-income cohorts, while progressives and higher-income segments remained resilient.

* **Margin expansion story intact; profitability inflects.** Adjusted EBITDA surged ~50% YoY to $25.7M (11.6% margin, +260 bps), beating estimates by 2.8%. GAAP net income turned positive at $5.9M vs. ($4.0M) prior year. Non-GAAP EPS of $0.11 beat by 26%, reflecting operational leverage and inventory discipline (down 13% YTD).

* **Guidance cut compounds sell pressure.** FY25 revenue guide reduced to $871-874M (~13% growth) vs. prior ~14-15%, implying Q4 midpoint of $212.5M (+11.5% YoY). Adjusted EBITDA margin outlook raised to 11.3-11.6% (vs. prior ~11%), but revenue shortfall overshadowed profitability gains.

* **Stock down 14% to $17.40; now 39% below 52-week high.** Shares fell from $28.56 (September 2025 peak) as revenue miss and lowered guidance overshadowed 9th consecutive quarter of accelerating customer growth and AI partnership announcements with Google and Samsung.

---

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

### Q4 2025 Guidance
* **Revenue:** $211-214M (+11-12% YoY); midpoint $212.5M
* **Adjusted EBITDA:** $18-21M; margin ~9.2% at midpoint (+190 bps YoY)

### FY 2025 Guidance (Revised)
* **Revenue:** $871-874M (~13% growth); **lowered** from prior ~14-15% implied growth
* **Adjusted EBITDA:** $98-101M
* **Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** 11.3-11.6% (+180-210 bps expansion); **raised** from prior ~11%
* **New Store Openings:** 45 stores, including 5 Target shop-in-shops

### Management Commentary on Macro
* **Consumer bifurcation:** "July and August represented the strongest 2-month period of the year before trends moderated in September and have remained consistent." Younger, lower-income consumers showing sensitivity; older, higher-income and progressives customers resilient.
* **Inventory discipline:** 13% YTD inventory reduction minimizing working capital drag; operating cash flow up 11% YTD to $87.5M.
* **Strategic pruning:** Sunset of Home Try-On program and Scout private-label contacts to streamline operations and improve unit economics.

---

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

### Channel Mix (Retail: 73% of Revenue)
* **Retail Revenue:** +20.2% YoY (vs. +16.4% store count growth), driven by densification strategy and suburban expansion
* **E-Commerce Revenue:** +3.2% YoY, reflecting channel shift toward physical stores
* **Retail Productivity:** 103.8% vs. prior year; 313 stores at quarter-end (+15 net new in Q3)

### Product Category Performance
* **Glasses (Core):** +13% YoY, accelerating from ~10% in H1 2025
* **Contact Lenses:** +21% YoY; now 11.5% of revenue (strategic growth driver)
* **Eye Exams:** +41% YoY; now 6.5% of revenue; 275 stores (88% of fleet) equipped with exam capabilities
* **Progressives Penetration:** 22.3% (+30 bps), signaling mix shift toward higher-value, higher-margin products

### Geographic/Strategic Initiatives
* **Store Expansion:** Densification of suburban markets; Target shop-in-shop pilot (5 locations planned for FY25)
* **AI Partnerships:** Google and Samsung intelligent eyewear development announced; internal deployment of "Adviser" AI for virtual try-on and recommendations driving productivity gains

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

* **Cash Position:** $280.4M in cash and cash equivalents (strong liquidity)
* **Debt:** Total debt-to-equity ratio of 63.18%; minimal leverage
* **Share Buybacks:** **None announced or executed** in Q3 2025
* **Dividends:** **No dividend program**; company retains all capital for growth investments
* **Operating Cash Flow:** $87.5M YTD (+11% YoY), benefiting from inventory reduction and improving profitability
* **Working Capital:** Inventory down 13% YTD, minimizing cash drag and supporting operational efficiency

---

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis remains constructive but multiple compression likely near-term.** Revenue miss and guidance cut trigger de-rating despite robust margin expansion (+260 bps EBITDA margin) and 9th consecutive quarter of accelerating customer growth. Consumer bifurcation (weakness in younger/lower-income cohorts) creates uncertainty for FY26, though progressives penetration, retail densification, and AI partnerships offer medium-term upside. At $17.40 (-39% from highs), valuation reset reflects tempered growth expectations; re-rating contingent on Q4 revenue stabilization and FY26 guide above Street's likely conservative reset. **Hold near-term; accumulate on further weakness if Q4 confirms demand stabilization.**
