## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

- **AI Commerce Breakout via WhatsApp**: Magalu launched Lu's AI-powered WhatsApp commerce experience, achieving 10% higher conversion rates vs. the app and 90 NPS. The strategic pivot to conversational AI commerce positions MGLU3 to capitalize on Brazil's WhatsApp ubiquity, potentially unlocking a high-margin, low-CAC channel at scale.

- **Profitability Inflection Sustained**: Q3 marked the fifth consecutive quarter of profitability improvement, with adjusted net income of R$ 21M and total net income of R$ 85M (including non-recurring gains). EBITDA reached R$ 700M (7.9% margin) despite Brazil's challenging 15% Selic rate environment, validating management's operational efficiency gains.

- **Luizacred Returns to Profitability, Magalu Ads Explodes**: Fintech arm Luizacred posted R$ 68M net profit with historically low 8% delinquency (2.6% short-term), while Magalu Ads revenue surged 69% YoY. These high-margin verticals now represent 13% of business mix (up from negligible levels), providing earnings diversification beyond core retail.

- **Cash Generation Robust, Debt Declining**: Operating cash flow of R$ 535M in Q3 contributed to R$ 2.5B LTM free cash flow, covering all capex, Luizacred funding, and leasing expenses. Net cash position improved to R$ 1.6B (total cash R$ 7.6B vs. debt R$ 4.8B), with management prioritizing deleveraging over shareholder returns.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Profitability inflection validates turnaround; AI commerce and fintech diversification de-risk retail exposure.** MGLU3's Q3 results demonstrate sustained operational discipline, with five consecutive quarters of profitability improvement culminating in R$ 85M net income and R$ 700M EBITDA (7.9% margin). The strategic pivot to high-margin verticals is gaining traction: Luizacred returned to profitability (R$ 68M, 8% NPL), Magalu Ads surged 69%, and AI-powered WhatsApp commerce posted 10% higher conversion rates. While gross revenue growth of 1.4% reflects Brazil's challenging 15% Selic environment, same-store sales of +5.2% and gross margin expansion to 31.5% (multi-year high) validate pricing power. Cash generation remains robust (R$ 2.5B LTM FCF, R$ 1.6B net cash), with management prioritizing deleveraging (debt reduced to R$ 4.8B) over distributions. Multiple likely range-bound given modest top-line growth, but profitability inflection and high-margin adjacencies (Ads, Luizacred, AI commerce) provide structural re-rating catalyst over 12-18 months. Key risk: prolonged high interest rates (15% Selic) compressing consumer discretionary spend; upside dependent on AI commerce scaling and Luizacred portfolio expansion without NPL deterioration.
