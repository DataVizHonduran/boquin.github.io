## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

- **Beat driven by operational leverage**: Cost-to-income ratio compressed to 28%, while ARPAC crossed $13 threshold for first time, demonstrating improving unit economics at scale
- **Credit portfolio acceleration**: $30.4B total loan book expanded +42% YoY FXN with asset quality improving (NPL 15-90 day ratio down 20 bps QoQ to 4.2%), validating credit underwriting model
- **Stock reaction paradoxical**: Shares declined 3.71% in regular trading with further after-hours weakness despite material earnings beat and operational improvements
- **Funding cost advantage widening**: Cost of funding improved to 89% of interbank rates while deposits surged +34% YoY FXN to $38.8B, reinforcing structural moat

---

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

- **No explicit guidance provided**: Management does not issue traditional quarterly/annual guidance on revenue, EPS, or credit metrics
- **Analyst expectations for Q4**: Street consensus estimates $0.19 EPS (not company guidance)
- **Macro commentary**: Management highlighted continued expansion runway with Brazil penetration at 60% of adults, Mexico at 14%, Colombia at 10%, signaling multi-year growth opportunity
- **Credit outlook**: Company reiterated it does not provide near-term cost of risk guidance but noted leading NPL indicators remain favorable

---

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

### Geographic Penetration (Customer Base):

- **Brazil (Core Market)**: 110.1M customers, representing >60% of adult population; activity rate >85%; deposit base $30.4B
- **Mexico (High-Growth)**: 13.1M customers (~14% penetration); Mexico revenue nearly doubled YoY in Q1 2025 (Q3 segment revenue not separately disclosed)
- **Colombia (Emerging)**: 3.8M customers (10% penetration); early-stage market expansion

### Product Performance:

- **Net Interest Income**: $2.3B, +32% YoY FXN, driven by credit portfolio expansion (+42% YoY FXN to $30.4B) and deposit growth
- **Interest-Earning Portfolio**: $17.7B, +54% YoY FXN, outpacing loan growth as Nu expands treasury/investment operations
- **Gross Profit**: $1.8B, +32% YoY FXN; margin expansion to 43.5% (+130 bps QoQ) reflects operating leverage inflection

*Note: Nu does not disclose revenue breakdown by country in public earnings releases*

---

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

- **Cash Position**: $14.12B cash vs. $3.49B debt = **$10.63B net cash** ($2.20 per share); fortress balance sheet supports regulatory capital requirements and expansion
- **Share Buybacks**: None executed or authorized; shares outstanding increased 0.44% over past year (modest dilution from equity compensation)
- **Dividends**: No dividend policy; zero payout as company prioritizes reinvestment in growth markets
- **Deposits**: $38.8B (+34% YoY FXN), providing low-cost funding base at 89% of interbank rates

---

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Thesis intact; NU demonstrates rare combination of 40%+ growth with margin expansion and credit discipline. Market's negative reaction appears technical (profit-taking after YTD run) rather than fundamental. Valuation likely supported by sustained ROE of 31% and structural moat in Latin American digital banking, though lack of capital returns (buybacks/dividends) may cap multiple expansion. Watch Q4 for sustained ARPAC growth and Mexico revenue trajectory.
