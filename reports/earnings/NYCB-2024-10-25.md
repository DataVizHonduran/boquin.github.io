## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

* **Deeper-than-expected loss driven by CRE provisions and NIM collapse.** NYCB posted a GAAP net loss of $289M ($0.79/share), missing consensus by 72.5% as the adjusted loss of $0.69/share vastly exceeded the expected $0.40 loss. Provision for credit losses surged to $242M (4x prior year) as CRE stress intensified.
* **NIM compression accelerates; multifamily portfolio hemorrhaging.** Net interest margin plunged to 1.79% from 3.27% in Q2 2024, a catastrophic 148 bps contraction. Multifamily NPLs hit $1.5B (of $2.5B total NPAs), with $188M in multifamily net charge-offs YTD. Office portfolio recorded $388M in charge-offs YTD.
* **Profitability timeline pushed to 2026; 2025 guidance slashed.** Management now expects 2025 EPS of $(0.30) to $(0.35), abandoning prior breakeven/+$0.05 guidance. Profitability now targeted for 2026 at $0.75-$0.80/share (vs. prior $1.25-$1.30 guidance). Fourth consecutive quarterly loss underscores severity of CRE exposure ($36.9B multifamily, $3.1B office).
* **Stock reaction:** Shares declined on the deeper loss and profitability delay, though deposit growth (+$4B, +5% QoQ) and liquidity ($41B, 300% coverage to uninsured deposits) provided some stability.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

### 2024 Full Year Guidance (Updated)
* **Provision for Loan Losses:** $1.1B - $1.2B for FY 2024
* **Noninterest Income:** $570M - $620M
* **Operating Expenses:** $2.3B - $2.4B

### 2025 Outlook (Revised)
* **EPS:** $(0.30) to $(0.35) loss per share (prior guidance: breakeven to +$0.05 profit)
* **Profitability:** Now expected in **2026**, not 2025
* **Nonaccrual Loans:** Expected to remain elevated through 2026, impacting interest income

### 2026 Outlook (Revised)
* **EPS:** $0.75 to $0.80 (prior guidance: $1.25 to $1.30)

### Management Commentary on Headwinds/Tailwinds

**Headwinds:**
* **CRE stress intensifying:** Multifamily delinquencies spiked 990% YoY in rent-regulated NYC properties (~$18B exposure). Non-accrual loans increased $600M QoQ to $2.5B, with 68% still performing as agreed.
* **NIM pressure:** Margin contracted to 1.79% from 3.27% in Q2 (148 bps decline) as funding costs remain elevated and CRE portfolio transitions to nonaccrual status.
* **FDIC assessment costs:** Higher FDIC assessments expected through 2026 due to elevated criticized and classified loan levels.
* **Office portfolio stress:** $3.1B office exposure with $388M in net charge-offs YTD; $323M in reserves allocated.

**Tailwinds:**
* **Deposit growth accelerating:** Total deposits up $4B (5%) QoQ to $83B; retail deposits +$2.5B (8%), Private Bank deposits +$1.8B (11%). Wholesale borrowings down 31%.
* **Liquidity position robust:** $41B in liquidity representing ~300% coverage to uninsured deposits.
* **CRE de-risking in progress:** Commercial real estate exposure declined 3% QoQ as management pursues portfolio reduction to ~$30B over time from current $40B ($36.9B multifamily + $3.1B office).
* **Capital ratios stable:** Pro forma CET1 ratio of 11.4% (including MSR and third-party origination business sale impact); GAAP CET1 of 10.8%.

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

NYCB does not report traditional business segment revenue. Key portfolio compositions and performance:

### Loan Portfolio ($73.0B as of Sept 30, 2024)
* **Multifamily CRE:** $36.9B (45% of total loans)
  * ~$18B in NYC rent-regulated apartments (~50% of multifamily portfolio)
  * Non-accrual loans: $1.5B (of $2.5B total NPAs)
  * Net charge-offs YTD: $188M
  * Allowance coverage: $469M (2.4% reserves)
  * **Performance:** Acute stress; delinquencies up 990% YoY driven by rent regulation constraints, lower leasing velocity, rent concessions, and elevated operating costs

* **Office CRE:** $3.1B
  * Net charge-offs YTD: $388M
  * Allowance coverage: $323M
  * **Performance:** Severe impairment; portfolio under heavy pressure from WFH trends and valuation declines

* **Other Commercial & Industrial:** Balance of $73.0B portfolio

### Deposit Base ($83.0B as of Sept 30, 2024)
* **Retail Deposits:** Up $2.5B (8% QoQ growth)
* **Private Bank Deposits:** Up $1.8B (11% QoQ growth)
* **Total Growth:** $4.0B (5% QoQ)
* **Strategy:** Diversifying away from wholesale funding; wholesale borrowings reduced 31% QoQ

### Net Interest Income
* **Q3 2024 NII:** $510M (-42.2% YoY, -8% QoQ)
* **Net Interest Margin:** 1.79% (down from 3.27% in Q2 2024, down from 3.27% in Q3 2023)
* **Key Driver:** Elevated funding costs, shift of CRE loans to nonaccrual status (reducing interest income), compressed earning asset yields

### Provision for Credit Losses
* **Q3 2024:** $242M (up 4x YoY)
* **Allowance for Credit Losses:** 1.87% of loans (up from 1.78% in Q2 2024)
* **Total Reserves:** $1.2B ($469M multifamily, $323M office, remainder other)

### Credit Quality Metrics
* **Non-Accrual Loans:** $2.5B (up $600M QoQ); 68% performing as agreed
* **Net Charge-offs Q3 2024:** $240M net of recoveries
* **Classified Loans:** Elevated; driving higher FDIC assessments through 2026

* **Total Loans:** $73.0B (down 4.6% QoQ as CRE portfolio shrinks)
* **Total Deposits:** $83.0B (up 1.8% QoQ)
* **Liquidity:** $41B (~300% coverage to uninsured deposits)
* **Tangible Book Value/Share:** ~$17.69 (Q2 2024 data; Q3 2024 specific figure not disclosed in search results)

* **Pro Forma CET1 Ratio:** 11.4% (includes impact of MSR and third-party origination business sale)
* **Tier 1 Leverage Ratio:** Not disclosed
* **Total Risk-Based Capital Ratio:** Not disclosed

### Shareholder Returns
* **Dividend:** $0.05/share (slashed from $0.17/share in Q4 2023/Q1 2024)
  * Annual dividend cut from ~$0.68/share to $0.20/share represents 71% reduction
* **Share Buybacks:** None; company suspended repurchases amid restructuring
* **Equity Raise:** Completed $1B+ equity investment in Q1 2024 to shore up capital (Liberty Strategic Capital, Hudson Bay Capital, Reverence Capital led)

### Strategic Initiatives
* **CRE Portfolio Reduction:** Targeting ~$30B in CRE exposure over time (currently $40B); Q3 saw 3% QoQ decline
* **Wholesale Funding Reduction:** Wholesale borrowings down 31% QoQ as deposit franchise strengthens
* **Expense Rationalization:** Workforce reductions and cost controls ongoing; full-year operating expenses guided to $2.3B-$2.4B
* **Business Sale:** Completed sale of mortgage servicing rights (MSR) and third-party origination business in Q3; contributed to pro forma CET1 improvement

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis impaired; avoid until CRE resolution visibility emerges.** NYCB's Q3 loss underscores the severity of its NYC rent-regulated multifamily exposure ($18B) and office portfolio stress ($3.1B). With profitability now deferred to 2026 and 2025 expected to deliver $0.30-$0.35 loss/share, the bank faces a multi-year workout scenario. While deposit growth (+5% QoQ) and liquidity ($41B) mitigate terminal risk, and the pro forma 11.4% CET1 provides capital cushion, the NIM collapse to 1.79% (from 3.27% in Q2) and $2.5B in non-accrual loans signal prolonged earnings pressure. Multiple likely remains depressed at 0.6-0.8x tangible book until CRE credit costs stabilize and NIM recovery materializes. Sell rating; reassess in 2026 as portfolio repositioning completes.
