## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **Revenue momentum accelerating, profitability lagging:** GE Vernova beat revenue expectations by 8.9% ($10.0B vs. $9.18B consensus) driven by robust Power (+15% organic to $4.8B) and Electrification (+35% organic to $2.6B) growth, offsetting Wind headwinds (-8% to $2.6B). However, EPS of $1.64 missed consensus $1.78 by 7.9%, reflecting mix pressures and Wind segment losses of $61M (though dramatically improved from $317M loss in Q3 2024, representing an 81% reduction in losses).

* **Order inflection provides multi-year visibility:** Q3 orders of $14.6B (up 55% organically) underscore structural demand tailwinds. Power captured $7.8B orders (+50% organic), with Gas Power backlog expanding to 62 GW from 55 GW. Electrification orders surged 102% organically to $5.1B, driving equipment backlog to $26B. Wind orders stabilized at $1.8B (+4% organic), suggesting demand floor.

* **Guidance reaffirmed for 2025, raised for out-years:** Management maintained full-year 2025 outlook of $36-37B revenue (trending high-end) and 8-9% EBITDA margins, while raising FCF guidance to $3.5-4.0B from $3.0-3.5B. Critically, at December 9 Investor Update, company issued 2026 guidance of $41-42B revenue and 11-13% EBITDA margins, and raised 2028 targets to $52B revenue (from $45B) and 20% EBITDA margins (from 14%).

* **Stock reaction mixed:** Shares declined modestly post-Q3 earnings on EPS miss, but surged 5.9% after December 9 Investor Update following announcement of doubled dividend ($0.50/share quarterly, up from $0.25) and increased buyback authorization to $10B from $6B ($3.3B spent through December 3).

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**Power ($4.8B revenue, +15% YoY organic):**
Outperformance driven by equipment demand and services growth. Orders of $7.8B (+50% organic) reflect AI/data center gas turbine demand and grid reliability investments. Revenue up 14% reported. EBITDA margin expanded to 13.3% from 11.9% in prior year (+140 bps, +120 bps organically). Gas Power equipment backlog and slot reservations rose from 55 GW to 62 GW, providing multi-year equipment revenue visibility.

**Electrification ($2.6B revenue, +35% YoY organic):**
Strongest growth segment, with equipment orders more than doubling (+102% organic to $5.1B) driven by grid infrastructure investments for transmission/distribution, data centers, and industrial electrification. Revenue growth reflects accelerating backlog conversion. EBITDA margin exceeded 15% in Q3 (noted as 14-15% for segment overall). Equipment backlog reached ~$26B (up $6.5B YTD), providing 2-3 year revenue coverage.

**Wind ($2.6B revenue, -8% YoY):**
Continued headwinds but improving trajectory. Revenue decline reflects absence of Q3 2024 offshore wind project cancellation settlement and ongoing onshore wind pressures. Orders of $1.8B (+4% organic) stabilizing, driven by Onshore Wind services. EBITDA losses dramatically improved to $61M from $317M in prior year (-81% loss reduction), driven by cost restructuring and improved project mix. Company targeting breakeven trajectory.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Structural thesis reinforced; execution trajectory improving but requires monitoring. GE Vernova's positioning in secular growth themes (AI power infrastructure, grid modernization, industrial electrification) validated by exceptional order momentum (+55% organic) and expanding backlogs ($16B YTD growth). December 9 Investor Update's raised 2028 outlook ($52B revenue, 20% EBITDA margins) and aggressive capital return program (dividend doubled, $10B buyback authorization) signal management confidence in multi-year earnings power. However, near-term profitability delivery remains pressured by Wind segment losses (though rapidly improving) and mix headwinds, evidenced by Q3 EPS miss despite revenue beat. **Valuation Impact:** December 9 announcements mitigate prior Q3 EPS miss concerns—2026 guidance of 11-13% EBITDA margins (vs. 8-9% in 2025) demonstrates inflection trajectory, while 2028 margin target of 20% (vs. prior 14% target) materially increases long-term earnings power. Stock's 5.9% post-announcement gain reflects re-rating on improved out-year visibility and shareholder-friendly capital allocation.
