## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **AI-driven blowout quarter**: TSMC delivered explosive Q4 results with revenue up 39% YoY to NT$868.5B and net income surging 57% to NT$374.7B, crushing consensus on relentless AI accelerator demand. Gross margin expanded 250 bps YoY to 59%, while operating margin reached 49%—best-in-class profitability.

* **3nm/5nm dominance**: Advanced 3nm process contributed 26% of wafer revenue (up from 18% full year), while 5nm held at 34%. Advanced technologies (7nm and below) accounted for 74% of total wafer revenue—TSMC's technology leadership unassailable as HPC surged to 53% of revenue mix.

* **2025 guidance raises bar**: Management upgraded full-year 2025 revenue growth forecast to ~35% (up from 30%) and lifted capex range to $40-42B (from $38-42B), with 70% allocated to advanced nodes. Q1 2025 revenue guidance of $25.0-25.8B implies continued momentum.

* **Structural AI tailwind**: CEO emphasized TSMC is "entering a period of stronger growth as the multiyear structural AI megatrend continues to unfold." With NVIDIA, Apple, and hyperscalers locked into 3nm/5nm roadmaps, TSMC's moat deepens while competitors lag multiple generations behind.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**Q1 2025 Guidance:**
* Revenue: **$25.0-25.8B** (NT$ basis at midpoint ~NT$815B)
* Gross margin: **57-59%**
* Operating margin: **46.5-48.5%**

**FY 2025 Outlook:**
* Revenue growth: **~35%** (upgraded from 30% prior)
* Capex: **$40-42B** (narrowed to upper end from $38-42B)
* Capex allocation: **70% advanced process, 10-20% specialty, 10-20% packaging/testing**

**Management Commentary:**
* CEO: "We are entering a period of stronger growth as the multiyear structural AI megatrend continues to unfold"
* Management expects AI server revenue to grow at 50% CAGR from 2023-2028, with TSMC positioned as sole supplier for leading-edge AI accelerators
* Full-year 2025 revenue growth of 35% implies ~$121.5B revenue target—massive acceleration from $90B in 2024
* Technology roadmap on track: 2nm entering volume production in 2025, with N2P variant and 1.4nm (A14) in development

**Analyst Takeaway on Guidance:**
Guidance reflects unabated confidence in AI-driven semiconductor cycle with TSMC capturing disproportionate share. 35% revenue growth in 2025 would add ~$31B revenue—equivalent to Intel's entire foundry business. Capex raise to $40-42B (vs. $36B in 2024) signals management prioritizing capacity expansion over near-term margin optimization—correct strategic call given structural demand visibility. Q1 guidance implies modest QoQ sequential decline (typical seasonality) before reaccelerating through year.

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**By Technology Node** (Q4 2024):
* 3nm: **26% of wafer revenue** (up from 18% full year 2024)
* 5nm: **34% of wafer revenue** (consistent with full year)
* 7nm: **14% of wafer revenue**
* Advanced technologies (7nm and below): **74% of total wafer revenue**
* Performance: 3nm ramping aggressively on Apple A18/M4 and NVIDIA Blackwell demand; 5nm sustained by hyperscaler AI chips and smartphone processors

**By Platform** (Q4 2024):
* High Performance Computing (HPC): **53% of revenue** (+19% QoQ)
* Smartphone: **35% of revenue**
* IoT: **6% of revenue**
* Automotive: **4% of revenue**
* DCE (Digital Consumer Electronics): **2% of revenue**
* Performance: HPC dominance reflects AI accelerator surge (NVIDIA H100/H200/B200); smartphone stabilizing after 2023 inventory correction

**Segment-Level Analysis:**
The portfolio shift toward HPC is strategic gold—AI accelerators command 3-5x ASPs vs. smartphone processors and enjoy better supply/demand dynamics. 3nm revenue mix expanding to 26% in Q4 (from 18% full year) demonstrates successful node transitions, with further upside as 2nm enters production in 2025. Smartphone at 35% provides diversification but lower growth; automotive at 4% is nascent but structurally attractive long-term. TSMC's ability to command 74% revenue from advanced nodes (vs. competitors stuck at mature nodes) is the ultimate competitive moat.

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

**Cash Position:**
* Cash & equivalents: **NT$2.2T** (approximately $68B)
* Operating cash flow (Q4): **NT$620B** ($19.2B)
* Free cash flow (Q4): **NT$258B** ($8.0B) after capex of NT$362B ($11.2B)

**Balance Sheet:**
* Total debt: **NT$956.7B** (approximately $29.6B)
* Debt-to-equity: **19%** (fortress balance sheet)
* Total assets: **NT$7.35T** ($227B)
* Return on Equity (Q4): **36.2%** (world-class capital efficiency)

**Capital Expenditures:**
* Q4 2024 capex: **NT$362B** ($11.2B)
* Full year 2024 capex: **~$36B**
* 2025 guidance: **$40-42B** (raised from $38-42B)
* Allocation: 70% advanced process (3nm/2nm), 10-20% specialty, 10-20% advanced packaging

**Dividend:**
* Q4 2024 cash dividend: **NT$4.0 per share**
* Full year 2024 total dividend: **NT$13.0 per share** (yield ~2.2% at current prices)
* Policy: "Sustainable and steadily increasing cash dividend per share on both annual and quarterly basis"
* Payout ratio: Approximately 60-70% of net income

**Share Repurchases:**
* No buyback program disclosed—TSMC prioritizes dividends and capex over buybacks
* Management philosophy: "Return cash through dividends while investing aggressively in capacity"

**Balance Sheet Note:**
TSMC operates from position of absurd financial strength: NT$2.2T cash vs. NT$957B debt yields net cash position of NT$1.2T (~$37B). 19% debt-to-equity ratio is vanishingly low for capital-intensive semiconductor manufacturing. Combined with 36% ROE, TSMC generates returns that defy gravity—deploying $40B+ capex while maintaining fortress balance sheet and growing dividends. Unlike competitors burning cash to chase foundry dreams (Intel), TSMC prints money at leading edge.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Structural winner entering hypergrowth phase**. Q4 confirmed TSMC's unassailable position as AI infrastructure arms dealer, with 57% profit growth and 35% revenue growth guidance for 2025 reflecting multi-year runway. Technology leadership at 3nm/2nm (ramping 2025) leaves competitors 2-3 generations behind—Samsung struggles at 3nm yields, Intel's 18A uncertain, Chinese foundries stuck at mature nodes due to export controls.

**Valuation: 25x forward P/E appears reasonable** for business generating 36% ROE, 59% gross margins, and poised for 35% revenue growth in 2025. Bull case: If AI capex cycle extends into 2026-2027 (likely given hyperscaler commitments), TSMC could sustain 20%+ growth longer than expected. Bear case: Potential AI accelerator demand pause in 2026 if workload optimization reduces chip requirements—but even then, TSMC's cost position ensures profitability.

**Risks**: (1) Geopolitical—Taiwan Strait tensions pose binary risk to supply chain; (2) Customer concentration—NVIDIA, Apple, AMD represent ~60% of revenue; (3) Capex intensity—$40B annual capex creates execution risk if demand disappoints; (4) Technology transitions—2nm yield ramps could delay revenue recognition.

**Recommendation: Buy** on structural AI tailwind and technology moat. Price target $250 (30x 2026E EPS of ~$8.30) represents 30% upside. TSMC remains only foundry capable of manufacturing leading-edge AI accelerators at scale—irreplaceable position in semiconductor stack. Raise target to $280 if 2nm ramp accelerates or hyperscalers commit to next-gen AI clusters.
