## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

- **Revenue materially underperformed** driven by substantially lower WaferPak (wafer-level burn-in consumables) shipments, with contact revenue declining to $3.4M (35% of revenue) from $8.6M (64% of revenue) YoY—a direct result of timing delays at the lead silicon carbide (SiC) EV customer.

- **Margins compressed to multi-year lows** as non-GAAP gross margin contracted 1,550 bps YoY to 29.8%, reflecting unfavorable product mix (less high-margin WaferPak) and lower absorption on reduced volume.

- **Management reinstated H2 guidance** citing "improved visibility" from AI processor and data center semiconductor test demand, with $60-80M in projected H2 bookings—a transformative inflection if realized.

- **Stock declined ~6%** in after-hours trading on heavy volume (5.2x average), with Craig-Hallum subsequently cutting its price target to $21 from $24 (Hold maintained).

### Key CFO/Management Commentary:

- **SiC EV Headwind Easing:** Management noted the "worst is behind" for the lead silicon carbide customer, with orders expected to resume in H2.

### By Technology Platform:

- **FOX WLBI (Wafer-Level Burn-In):** Continued engagement expansion with AI processor customers; lead customer requesting additional capacity and transition to fully automated WaferPak aligner for 300mm wafers.

- **Sonoma PPBI (Packaged-Part Burn-In):** Received $5.5M+ in Q3-to-date orders for ultra-high-power systems testing AI CPUs, GPUs, and networking processors (up to 2000W per device). New fully-automated Sonoma platform introduced for high-volume production.
