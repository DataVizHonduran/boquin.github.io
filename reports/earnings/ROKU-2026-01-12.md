## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **First Profitable Quarter Since 2021**: ROKU posted operating income of $9.5M and net income of $24.8M, marking the inflection from chronic losses to sustainable profitability on platform monetization strength.

* **Platform Revenue Drives Beat**: Platform segment grew 17% YoY to $1.065B (88% of total revenue), fueled by robust video advertising demand and streaming services distribution. Platform gross profit reached $547.8M, offsetting a $22.9M devices loss.

* **Revenue Miss Weighs on Sentiment**: Total revenue of $1.21B came in 1.6% below Street consensus of $1.23B, primarily due to devices revenue declining 5% YoY to $146M. After-hours stock reaction was mixed, with initial negative pressure despite the profitability milestone.

* **Guidance Raises Bar**: Management lifted FY25 platform revenue guidance to $4.11B (from $4.075B) and adjusted EBITDA to $395M (from $375M). Q4 guidance implies record adjusted EBITDA of $145M and 15% platform revenue growth.

---

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**Q4 2025 Guidance:**
* Total net revenue: ~$1.35B (+12% YoY)
* Platform revenue: +15% YoY
* Platform gross margin: ~52%
* Adjusted EBITDA: $145M (highest quarterly level ever)

**Full Year 2025 Raised Outlook:**
* Platform revenue: $4.11B (raised from $4.075B)
* Adjusted EBITDA: $395M (raised from $375M)
* Adjusted EBITDA margin: ~8.4% (up 200 bps YoY)

**Management Commentary:**
* CFO emphasized continued platform monetization momentum, with the Ads Manager self-serve product attracting 90% new advertisers and representing a "multibillion dollar" TAM opportunity.
* Major home screen redesign planned for 2026, which testing shows increases both viewer engagement and monetization per user.
* Management expressed confidence in achieving positive operating income for full year 2026, with margin expansion continuing into next year.
* Macroeconomic backdrop described as stable, with no meaningful headwinds noted in ad spend or streaming services distribution.

---

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**Platform Segment (88% of Total Revenue):**
* Revenue: $1.065B (+17% YoY) — Core driver of growth
* Gross Profit: $547.8M
* Video advertising and streaming services distribution both accelerated
* The Roku Channel remained #2 app by engagement in U.S., with streaming hours up 80% YoY
* Ads Manager self-serve platform scaling rapidly with 90% new advertiser adoption

**Devices Segment (12% of Total Revenue):**
* Revenue: $146M (-5% YoY) — Continued strategic de-emphasis
* Gross Loss: $22.9M
* Roku deliberately de-prioritizing hardware to focus on higher-margin platform business
* Device sales act primarily as gateway to platform monetization

**Key Metrics:**
* Total Streaming Hours: 36.5B (+4.5B or +14% YoY)
* Active Accounts: Not disclosed (Roku discontinued quarterly household reporting starting Q1 2025)
* Trailing Twelve-Month Free Cash Flow: $440M+

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Platform momentum validates thesis; profitability inflection on track. Guidance raise and record Q4 EBITDA outlook signal durable margin expansion. Devices headwind isolated and strategic. Multiple re-rating likely if ad platform scale continues and home screen redesign delivers in 2026. Thesis intact; positive operating income trajectory reinforces long-term value creation despite near-term revenue mix pressures.
