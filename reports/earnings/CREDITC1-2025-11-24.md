# HEADLINE: BAP Q3 2025 Review: ROE Hits 19.6% on NIM Expansion; Revenue Miss Overshadowed

## THE DASHBOARD

| Metric | Actual | YoY % | Vs. Consensus |
|--------|--------|-------|---------------|
| Revenue | $1.53B | +8.5% | Miss by $90M (-5.6%) |
| EPS | $6.51 | +14.1% | Beat by $0.33 (+5.3%) |
| ROE | 19.6% | +330bps | Above target |
| Net Interest Margin | 5.53% | +53bps | Record high |
| Loan Growth (FX-neutral) | 7.0% | N/A | Above guidance |
| Cost of Risk | 1.7% | -30bps | Asset quality improving |

**Note:** CREDITC1 (Banco de Crédito del Perú) is the primary subsidiary of Credicorp Ltd. (NYSE: BAP).

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **Primary driver of beat**: EPS outperformance (+5.3% vs. consensus) driven by record net interest margin expansion to 5.53% (+53bps YoY) and cost of risk compression to 1.7% (-30bps YoY), offsetting revenue miss. ROE of 19.6% exceeded management's FY2025 guidance of ~17.5% and approached medium-term target of 19.5%, validating operating leverage thesis. Loan growth accelerated to 7% (FX-neutral) vs. FY guidance of 3.5%, reflecting market share gains in retail banking at BCP and Mibanco.

- **Revenue miss reflects timing, not deterioration**: $1.53B revenue (-5.6% vs. $1.62B consensus) attributable to lower-than-expected fee income timing and FX headwinds (Peru Sol depreciation vs. USD). However, core revenue drivers remain intact: risk-adjusted revenue showed sequential improvement with Yape (digital wallet) now contributing 6.6% of total risk-adjusted revenue. Fee-based and transactional income growth demonstrated platform strength beyond traditional lending.

- **Digital ecosystem inflection accelerating**: Yape surpassed 15.5M active users (+23% YoY), cementing position as Peru's dominant digital payments platform. Management highlighted Yape monetization roadmap gaining traction with contribution to risk-adjusted revenue expanding from ~4% in Q1 to 6.6% in Q3. Innovation portfolio (digital banking, insurtech, wealth management) collectively contributing meaningful earnings, validating multi-year investment thesis.

- **Stock reaction**: Shares rallied +1.43% post-earnings to $252.15, with market focusing on ROE expansion and asset quality improvement rather than revenue miss. Valuation at ~1.4x P/BV appears attractive given 19.6% ROE and improving trajectory toward 19.5% medium-term target.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**FY2025 Guidance (Reaffirmed/Implicit Raise):**
- ROE: ~17.5% (original guidance); Q3 actual 19.6% suggests substantial beat likely
- Loan Growth: ~3.5% in average daily balances (~6% in quarter-end balances)
- Q3 actual: 7% FX-neutral loan growth implies guidance conservative; management signaled acceleration continues
- Cost of Risk: No explicit guidance but Q3 1.7% vs. historical 2.0%+ suggests structural improvement

**Medium-Term Targets (2026+):**
- ROE: Sustainable 18% by 2026, with medium-term target of 19.5%
- Loan Growth: ~6.5% YoY (compound annual)
- Management maintains confidence in trajectory given Q3 19.6% ROE already approaching 2026+ targets

**Macro Commentary from Management:**
- **Peru Economic Outlook**: GDP growth expected ~3% in 2025 (consistent with Q3 commentary), supported by historically high terms of trade, controlled inflation (2.2%, lowest in 4 years), and real wage recovery. Peru's economic recovery sustained with GDP expanding >3% in 2024 after 2023 contraction.

- **Political Risk**: Management flagged potential volatility heading into Peru's electoral campaign in late 2025/2026, though no material impact anticipated on credit quality or loan demand in near term. Historical precedent shows Peruvian banks resilient through political cycles.

- **Asset Quality Tailwinds**: NPL ratios contracting across portfolio; cost of risk fell to 1.7% (-30bps YoY) driven by enhanced risk management, economic recovery, and shift toward higher-quality retail segments. Management expects cost of risk to remain below 2% through 2025.

- **Digital Monetization**: Yape ecosystem expansion continues with merchant adoption accelerating; management highlighted plans to scale lending, insurance cross-sell, and B2B payment solutions. Innovation portfolio contributing ~10-15% of consolidated earnings, up from negligible levels 2 years ago.

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

Credicorp operates through four primary business lines: Universal Banking (BCP), Microfinance (Mibanco), Insurance & Pensions (Grupo Pacifico), and Investment Management.

**Universal Banking - BCP (Banco de Crédito del Perú) [~70% of consolidated earnings]:**
- Loan Growth: +4.6% YoY (+7% FX-neutral); retail banking outpacing corporate
- NIM: Key driver of consolidated 5.53% record margin; BCP's retail/SME mix shift positive
- Fee Income: Growth in transactional banking and digital channels; Yape integration driving cross-sell
- Key Metric: BCP is Peru's largest bank with ~32% market share in loans

**Microfinance - Mibanco [~10-15% of consolidated earnings]:**
- ROE: 18.8% (vs. 19.6% consolidated)
- Strong profitability despite competitive microfinance landscape; asset quality stable
- Loan growth accelerating as Peru's informal economy recovers

**Insurance & Pensions - Grupo Pacifico [~15-20% of consolidated earnings]:**
- ROE: 20.9% (highest among segments)
- Net Income Growth: +23% YoY (strongest segment performance)
- Benefiting from economic recovery driving life insurance and pension contributions
- Property & casualty insurance profitable; low claims environment

**Investment Management [~5% of consolidated earnings]:**
- ROE: 17.4%
- Credicorp Capital and Prima AFP (pension fund manager) showing steady AUM growth
- Fee-based revenue model provides diversification from credit risk

**Digital Innovation Portfolio:**
- Yape: 15.5M active users (+23% YoY); contributing 6.6% of risk-adjusted revenue
- Yape monetization accelerating via merchant fees, lending (Yapita Prestamos), and insurance cross-sell
- Other digital ventures (Tenpo in Chile, digital banking initiatives) still in investment phase but gaining traction

**Key Segment Takeaway**: BCP Universal Banking remains core earnings driver but Grupo Pacifico Insurance emerging as high-ROE growth engine (+23% net income YoY). Yape's 6.6% revenue contribution is inflection point—validates $500M+ cumulative investment in digital ecosystem. Mibanco's 18.8% ROE demonstrates resilience despite competitive microfinance market.

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

**Cash & Liquidity:**
- Total Assets: ~$60B (consolidated; majority at BCP)
- Tier 1 Capital Ratio: Strong regulatory capital position; Peruvian banks typically >12%
- Liquidity ratios comfortably above regulatory minimums; no funding concerns

**Dividends:**
- Annual Dividend: $11.01/share (4.23% yield based on $260 reference price)
- Payout Frequency: Semi-annual
- Last Ex-Dividend Date: May 19, 2025
- **Capital Allocation Philosophy**: Management stated intent to pay "everything else" as dividends beyond capital needed for organic growth and selective M&A. Expects dividend payout ratios to increase from 2025 levels (though not necessarily to 75% level seen in 2024). Management signaled "increasing ordinary dividends" in coming years as ROE stabilizes near 19%+.

**Share Buybacks:**
- No active share repurchase program announced in Q3 2025
- Management prioritizes dividends and organic growth over buybacks
- Philosophy: Retain capital for loan growth (~6.5% annual target) and inorganic opportunities (M&A), though "nothing relevant" in M&A pipeline currently disclosed

**Debt & Leverage:**
- Credicorp (holding company) maintains minimal debt; leverage concentrated at BCP bank subsidiary level
- BCP's loan-to-deposit ratio healthy at ~85-90%, indicating deposit-funded balance sheet
- No near-term refinancing concerns; Peru's stable macro environment supports funding costs

**Capital Deployment Priorities (in order):**
1. Organic loan growth (BCP, Mibanco: 6-7% annual)
2. Digital ecosystem investments (Yape, innovation portfolio: $100M+ annually)
3. Dividends (increasing payout ratios targeting 60-70% over time)
4. Selective M&A (bolt-on fintech acquisitions, regional expansion if compelling)

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Thesis intact; ROE trajectory toward 19%+ validates premium valuation. Q3 19.6% ROE already exceeds FY2025 guidance (17.5%) and approaches medium-term target (19.5%), suggesting management sandbagging or structural profitability inflection. Revenue miss (-5.6% vs. consensus) is noise—attributable to FX/timing rather than competitive loss—while EPS beat (+5.3%) driven by sustainable margin expansion (NIM +53bps) and credit quality improvement (CoR -30bps) is signal. Yape's 6.6% revenue contribution is critical milestone: $500M+ cumulative investment now monetizing at scale with 15.5M users (>50% of Peru's banked population). At 1.4x P/BV and ~11x P/E (estimated), BAP trades at 30% discount to LatAm bank peers (Itaú, Bradesco at 2.0x+ P/BV) despite comparable/superior ROE profile—spread reflects Peru political risk premium but creates asymmetric upside if 2026 elections resolve without disruption. Risks: (1) Peru political volatility into 2026 elections could pressure Sol and loan demand; (2) Yape monetization execution uncertain—pathway from 15.5M users to material earnings contribution needs 2-3 more quarters of validation. Maintain Buy with $275 PT (~1.7x P/BV, 14x P/E), implying +9% upside plus 4.2% yield. Key monitorable: Q4 ROE sustaining >19% and Yape revenue contribution exceeding 8%.
