## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

* **One-Time Tax Hit Masked Strong Execution**: The $619M Brazilian tax expense (covering 2022-Q3'25 dispute) reduced Q3 operating margin by 520 bps to 28.2% vs. 31.5% guidance. Absent this charge, Netflix would have exceeded both operating income and margin forecasts, with normalized margin near 33-34%.

* **Revenue Acceleration Driven by Dual Engines**: 17.2% revenue growth represents the strongest top-line performance in recent quarters, fueled by (1) robust subscriber additions pushing global base to 301.6M (+8% YoY), and (2) ad revenue on track to double in 2025 with 94M monthly active users on ad-tier, up from 40M in May 2024.

* **Market Overreaction Creates Entry Point**: Stock plunged 12% post-earnings (6-10% in after-hours, extending to -12% next session) despite fundamentals remaining intact. The earnings miss was entirely attributable to the non-recurring Brazilian tax charge; underlying business momentum accelerating across all regions (APAC +21%, UCAN +17%, EMEA +18%).

* **FCF Guidance Raised, Capital Returns Sustained**: Management upgraded FY2025 FCF guidance from $8.0-8.5B to ~$9.0B. Netflix repurchased $1.9B of shares in Q3 (1.5M shares), leaving $10.1B remaining under authorization. No dividend; all excess capital deployed into buybacks.
