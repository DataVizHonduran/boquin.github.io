# FISRV Q3 2025 Review: Strategic Reset After Major Miss; Guidance Slashed, Stock Craters 32%

## THE DASHBOARD

| Metric | Actual | YoY % | Vs. Consensus |
|--------|--------|-------|---------------|
| **Revenue** | $4.92B | -6% | Miss by 8.2% ($5.36B est.) |
| **Adjusted EPS** | $2.04 | -11% | Miss by 23% ($2.65 est.) |
| **GAAP EPS** | $1.46 | +49% | N/A |
| **Adjusted Operating Margin** | 37.0% | -320 bps | N/A |
| **Organic Revenue Growth** | 1% | N/A | Well below expectations |

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **Dramatic miss on both lines**: Revenue and adjusted EPS materially undershot consensus, with adjusted EPS missing by 23% ($2.04 vs. $2.65 expected) and revenue falling 8.2% short of the $5.36B estimate at $4.92B.

* **Guidance annihilated**: FY25 adjusted EPS outlook cut to $8.50-$8.60 from prior $10.15-$10.30 range (17% reduction at midpoint); organic revenue growth slashed to 3.5-4% from 10% prior view—a stunning capitulation signaling deeper structural issues.

* **Market reaction severe**: Stock plunged 32% in premarket trading on October 29, 2025, reflecting investor shock at the magnitude of the miss and reset. This represents one of the largest single-day declines in company history.

* **Strategic overhaul announced**: Management launched "One Fiserv" action plan, citing performance "below expectations for all stakeholders" and promising refocus on client service, technology solutions, and operational efficiency—code for execution failures.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**FY 2025 Guidance (Revised):**
* Organic revenue growth: **3.5-4%** (prior: 10%)
* Adjusted EPS: **$8.50-$8.60** (prior: $10.15-$10.30)

**Management Commentary:**
* CEO acknowledged results "fell short" and emphasized need for "meaningful action" through One Fiserv initiative
* CFO transition: Paul Todd appointed CFO effective October 31, 2025, replacing prior leadership
* No specific Q4 guidance provided, but implied run-rate suggests continued pressure
* Macro commentary absent—issues appear execution-driven rather than demand-related

**Analyst Takeaway on Guidance:**
Unprecedented 17% downward revision to full-year EPS guidance mid-stream signals either: (1) prior guidance was recklessly optimistic, or (2) operational deterioration accelerated materially in Q3. Either scenario erodes credibility.

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**Merchant Solutions** (higher-growth segment):
* Organic revenue growth: **+5%** YoY
* GAAP operating margin: **37.2%**
* Relative outperformance suggests payments/merchant acquiring remains resilient
* No specific dollar revenue disclosed for segment

**Financial Solutions** (legacy banking tech):
* Organic revenue: **-3%** YoY (contraction)
* GAAP operating margin: **42.5%** (higher margin but shrinking)
* Decline signals continued secular pressure on core banking software; potential client losses or pricing compression

**Segment-Level Analysis:**
The bifurcation is clear: Merchant Solutions showing modest growth but not enough to offset Financial Solutions' decline. Combined with margin compression, this points to mix issues and potential underinvestment in Financial Solutions innovation. The -3% Financial Solutions contraction is concerning given this remains ~half of revenue base.

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

**Cash Position:**
* Free cash flow (9M 2025): **$2.88B**
* Operating cash flow (9M 2025): **$4.12B** (down from $4.41B prior year)
* Cash generation remains robust despite operational challenges

**Debt:**
* Specific debt levels not disclosed in earnings release
* Leverage remains elevated post-First Data acquisition

**Share Repurchases:**
* Board authorized buyback of **60 million shares** on February 20, 2025
* No expiration date on authorization
* Repurchase activity levels in Q3 not disclosed—likely paused given stock decline

**Dividend:**
* **No dividend policy**: Fiserv does not pay cash dividends (last payment: September 2017)
* No change expected given capital needs for technology investments

**Balance Sheet Note:**
Despite operational missteps, cash generation remains healthy at ~$2.9B free cash flow run-rate. However, absence of disclosed debt metrics in release suggests management deflecting attention from leverage.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis broken; await stabilization**. Multiple compression inevitable as Street digests 17% earnings power reduction and credibility reset. Prior valuation assumed durable double-digit growth and margin expansion—both narratives now discredited. Financial Solutions segment decline accelerating, Merchant Solutions growth insufficient to offset. One Fiserv initiative may drive medium-term efficiencies, but near-term quarters will remain volatile as new CFO assesses damage. **Avoid until Q4 demonstrates stabilization and FY26 guidance restores confidence**. Price target cuts of 20-30% likely across Street. Former compounder now a show-me story.
