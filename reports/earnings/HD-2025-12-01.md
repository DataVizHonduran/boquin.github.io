## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

* **Third consecutive earnings miss signals structural demand weakness**: Adjusted EPS of $3.74 missed consensus by $0.10 (-2.6%), marking third straight quarterly miss. Revenue beat (+$0.25B) driven by $900M contribution from GMS acquisition (closed September), masking 0.2% comp growth that missed expectations (+1.4%). Operating margin compressed 60 bps to 12.9% despite flat gross margin (33.4%), reflecting SG&A deleverage. Core demand deteriorating: comp transactions -1.6%, partially offset by ticket growth +1.8%.

* **Storm absence drives 80 bps comp headwind; guidance cut reflects demand capitulation**: Management cited lack of hurricane/storm activity as primary Q3 driver, creating 80 bps comp sales headwind versus prior-year period. Monthly comps decelerated sharply: +2.0% (August) → +0.5% (September) → -1.5% (October). FY25 EPS guidance slashed to -5% YoY (from -2%), now implying $14.48 (below $14.98 consensus). Comp sales guidance lowered to "slightly positive" from +1%, suggesting Q4 comps flat to slightly negative.

* **Stock down 6% on earnings; market losing patience with macro excuses**: Shares fell 6% on November 18 following results, reflecting investor frustration with third straight miss and deteriorating trajectory. Despite 11% online growth and Pro/DIY "in line," traffic declining (-1.6% transactions) and big-ticket demand (+2.3%) insufficient to offset macro headwinds (consumer uncertainty, housing market weakness). Share buybacks remain suspended post-GMS acquisition; focus on deleveraging.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

* **FY25 Total Sales**: ~+3% YoY (includes $2B from GMS acquisition). Implies $164.3B total sales versus $164.1B consensus, effectively in-line. Organic sales growth ~+1.7%, down from prior +4% guidance. Nine-month sales of $122.9B (+2.6%) implies Q4 sales ~$41.4B (+3.5%), aided by GMS contribution but organic growth decelerating.

* **FY25 Comp Sales**: "Slightly positive" (prior: +1%). Implies FY25 comps +0.2-0.3%, down from +0.7% nine-month actual. Suggests Q4 comps -0.5% to flat, assuming no storm activity rebound. Management noted similar 80 bps Q4 storm headwind expected.

* **FY25 Adjusted EPS**: -5% YoY to ~$14.48 (versus FY24 $15.24, adjusted for 53-week to 52-week comparison). Consensus $14.98 now 3.3% too high. Nine-month EPS of $11.36 implies Q4 EPS ~$3.12, down 8-10% YoY, reflecting margin pressure and muted top-line.

* **Macro & Execution Commentary**: CEO Decker attributed deceleration to: (1) absence of major hurricanes/storms (80 bps headwind Q3, similar Q4); (2) consumer uncertainty driven by macroeconomic concerns and election; (3) softer housing market limiting big project demand. Noted big-ticket transactions +2.3% suggests Pro demand resilient but DIY discretionary weakness. Digital sales +11% outpacing total, but not enough to offset traffic declines. CFO Tobolski highlighted operating margin compression driven by SG&A deleverage on flat gross margin. Capex at 2.5% of sales (~$4.1B FY25) consistent with prior guidance. Share repurchases remain paused until end-2026 to prioritize GMS integration and debt reduction.

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

* **Cash Position**: $1.68B cash and equivalents at November 2, 2025, up from $1.53B at October 27, 2024 (+9.8%) and consistent with $1.66B at February 2, 2025. Net debt position not disclosed separately, but liquidity adequate given strong operating cash flow generation.

* **Operating Cash Flow**: Nine-month operating cash flow $13.0B, down 14% YoY. Q3 OCF ~$4.3-4.5B implied. Cash flow deployed: $6.9B dividends (nine months), $2.6B capex, $5.5B GMS acquisition. Free cash flow nine months ~$10.4B ($13.0B OCF - $2.6B capex).

* **Capex**: Q3 capex $900M (~2.2% of Q3 sales). FY25 capex guidance ~2.5% of sales, implying ~$4.1B full-year. Capex focused on supply chain optimization, store refreshes, and digital/omnichannel investments.

* **Dividends**: Quarterly dividend $2.30/share ($9.20 annualized), yielding 2.56%. Represents 154th consecutive quarterly dividend. Nine-month dividend payments $6.9B, up from prior year, reflecting dividend growth commitment. Payout ratio sustainable given FCF generation.

* **Share Buybacks**: Repurchases remain **paused** following SRS (2024) and GMS (Q3 2025) acquisitions. Management guidance: buybacks to resume end-2026 after debt reduction and cash flow normalization. $15B authorization from August 2023 remains available. Zero repurchases Q3 2025; focus on deleveraging and integration execution.

* **Debt/Leverage**: Short-term debt $3.2B at November 2, 2025. Total debt and leverage ratios not disclosed in earnings materials, but management prioritizing debt reduction post-GMS acquisition (~$5.5B cash outlay).

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Thesis deteriorating; multiple compression warranted. Third consecutive EPS miss ($3.74 vs. $3.84) and guidance cut to -5% FY25 EPS (from -2%, now $14.48 vs. $14.98 consensus) reflect structural demand weakness beyond storm absence excuse. Comp sales +0.2% (missed +1.4% est.) and October inflection to -1.5% signal accelerating deceleration in Q4. Operating margin compression (12.9%, -60 bps) on flat gross margin (33.4%) exposes SG&A deleverage vulnerability. Transaction decline (-1.6%) offsets ticket growth (+1.8%), indicating traffic erosion. GMS acquisition ($900M Q3 contribution) obscures organic weakness; pro forma comps likely -50 bps. Stock down 6% post-earnings reflects market fatigue with macro excuses (storms, housing, uncertainty). Buybacks paused until end-2026 removes support; $9.20 dividend (2.56% yield) offers limited downside cushion. Housing market weakness and consumer uncertainty persist; no catalyst for reacceleration near-term. Downgrade to Underperform; await stabilization of comps and margin trajectory before revisiting. Valuation undemanding at 18x consensus FY26 EPS, but earnings risk remains given guidance cut track record.
