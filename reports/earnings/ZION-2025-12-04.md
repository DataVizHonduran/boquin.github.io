# ZION Q3 2025 Review: EPS Beats Slightly, Revenue Surges +8.7%; Stock Rises +2.5% Despite $60M Fraud Hit

---

## THE DASHBOARD

| Metric | Actual | YoY % | vs. Consensus |
|--------|--------|-------|---------------|
| **Revenue** | $861M | +8.7% | Beat by $23M (+2.7%) |
| **GAAP EPS** | $1.48 | +8.0% | Beat by $0.02 (+1.4%) |
| **Non-GAAP EPS** | $1.54 | N/A | Beat by $0.04 (+2.7%) |
| **Net Interest Margin** | 3.28% | +25 bps YoY | 7th consecutive quarter of expansion |
| **Net Interest Income** | $563M | +4% QoQ | Beat expectations |
| **NCO Ratio** | 0.37% | +35 bps YoY | Elevated due to fraud losses |

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

* **Strong revenue beat (+2.7%) driven by NIM expansion and fee income growth**: Revenue of $861M beat consensus $838M on 7th consecutive quarter of NIM expansion (+11 bps QoQ to 3.28%); pre-provision net revenue grew 14% YoY (18% adjusted), led by +8% customer-related noninterest income.
* **Fraud charge-offs ($60M) masked core credit quality strength**: EPS of $1.48 beat estimates despite $50M charge-off + $10M reserve against two related fraudulent commercial loans; excluding this, NCO ratio would have been ~0.01% (vs. reported 0.37%), and classified loans declined -10% QoQ to $2.4B (4.00% of total).
* **Stock rallied +2.5% after hours on deposit/loan growth momentum**: Average customer deposits grew +3.1% annualized QoQ while loans rose +2.1% annualized; management guided NIM toward 3.50% over time and held dividend at $0.45/share, though buybacks remain on hold pending capital ratio improvement (targeting peer levels in ~12 months).

---

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

**Q4 2025 / Near-Term Outlook**:
* **Net Interest Income**: Moderately increasing; continued sequential growth expected
* **Net Interest Margin**: Expected to continue improving toward long-term target of ~3.50%
* **Loan Growth**: Modest growth anticipated, with C&I loans as primary driver
* **Deposit Growth**: Average customer deposits growing at 3.1% annualized pace
* **Fee Income**: Broad-based noninterest income growth across capital markets, treasury management, and mortgage

**Full Year 2025 / 2026**:
* **NIM Target**: ~3.50% over time (vs. 3.28% in Q3 2025; implies +22 bps upside)
* **Loan Portfolio**: C&I loans to lead portfolio expansion over next 12 months
* **Capital Deployment**: Buybacks on hold pending CET1 ratio improvement to peer levels (expected in ~12 months)
* **Dividend**: Quarterly dividend maintained at $0.45/share

**Management Commentary on Strategy**:
* **Tailwinds**: 7th consecutive quarter of NIM expansion (+25 bps YoY); customer deposit growth accelerating (+3.1% annualized QoQ); average loans +3.6% YoY; fee income broad-based growth across multiple lines
* **Headwinds**: $60M fraud-related losses from two related commercial borrowers (no further exposure); elevated NCO ratio (0.37% vs. 0.02% in Q3 2024); capital levels below peers delaying buyback authorization
* **Credit Outlook**: CEO Harris Simmons: "We have no further exposure related to these borrowers or guarantors" (fraud-impacted loans); classified loans down -10% QoQ

---

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

**Net Interest Income & Margin** (Core Strength):
* **NII**: $563M (+4% QoQ) – driven by +3% increase in interest income on loans and -13% decrease in interest expense on borrowings
* **NIM**: 3.28% (+11 bps QoQ, +25 bps YoY) – 7th consecutive quarter of expansion
* **Asset Mix**: Favorable shift driving margin improvement

**Loan Portfolio** (Modest Growth):
* **Average Loans**: +2.1% annualized QoQ, +3.6% YoY
* **End-of-Period Loans**: Contracted -3% annualized QoQ (payoffs/runoff)
* **C&I Loans**: Expected to drive growth over next 12 months
* **Commercial Portfolio**: Two related fraudulent borrowers identified; $50M charge-off + $10M reserve established

**Deposit Base** (Accelerating Growth):
* **Average Customer Deposits**: +3.1% annualized QoQ
* **Deposits Excluding Brokered**: +7% annualized QoQ (strong organic growth)
* **Brokered Deposits**: Declining (improving funding mix)

**Fee Income** (Broad-Based Strength):
* **Customer-Related Noninterest Income**: +8% YoY (adjusted for CVA)
* **Capital Markets**: Increased contributions
* **Treasury Management**: Strong growth
* **Mortgage Activities**: Higher revenue

**Credit Quality** (Mixed):
* **Classified Loans**: $2.4B, or 4.00% of total loans (-10% QoQ from $2.7B/4.43% in Q2)
* **Nonperforming Loans**: Decreased from prior quarter (specific $ not disclosed)
* **Net Charge-Offs**: $56M; NCO ratio 0.37% (vs. 0.02% in Q3 2024)
* **Fraud Impact**: $50M charge-off + $10M reserve on two related commercial borrowers (no further exposure)
* **Core NCO Ratio** (excluding fraud): ~0.01% (implies strong underlying credit quality)

---

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION & BALANCE SHEET

**Capital Position**:
* **CET1 Ratio**: >11% (healthy, but below peer average)
* **Capital Outlook**: Expected to approach peer levels in ~12 months based on current earnings trajectory
* **Balance Sheet**: ~$89B total assets; net cash position

**Capital Deployment**:
* **Dividend**: Quarterly dividend maintained at $0.45/share
* **Share Repurchases**: $40M authorization approved for FY2025, but management not yet comfortable initiating buybacks; capital deployment on hold pending CET1 improvement
* **Total Payout Ratio**: Limited in near term due to capital build phase

**Balance Sheet Strength**:
* **Total Assets**: ~$89B
* **Liquidity**: Net cash position; strong balance sheet
* **Funding Mix**: Improving (brokered deposits declining, customer deposits growing +7% annualized ex-brokered)

---

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Strong core earnings (+14% PPNR) and margin expansion trajectory intact despite fraud hit; NIM path to 3.50% and deposit momentum support upside, but capital constraints delay buybacks—**accumulate below $48 for 2026 capital return acceleration**.
