## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

* **EPS miss driven by margin compression, not revenue weakness.** Net income declined 9.2% YoY to MXN 11.7B despite revenue growth of 4.9%, as gross margin contracted 20 bps to 24.8% due to inventory investments and a one-off accounting adjustment. EBITDA margin also compressed to 10.5% from 10.9%.

* **Mexico operations robust; Central America lagging.** Mexico delivered 5.6% revenue growth and 3.9% same-store sales, outpacing ANTAD by 280 bps, reinforcing market share gains. Central America grew revenue 2.5% with 0.6% comps—underwhelming but stabilizing.

* **Omnichannel strength offsets margin headwinds.** eCommerce GMV accelerated 20% in Mexico and 38% in Central America; Walmart Connect advertising platform grew 16% YoY; Bait connectivity reached 23.5M active users. Strategic investments in eCommerce and new revenue streams show traction but weigh on near-term profitability.

* **Stock reaction muted; consensus downgrades likely.** Shares rose 1.2% post-earnings despite the miss, suggesting expectations were already tempered. Scotiabank downgraded to Sector Perform (PT: MXN 62); Barclays cut PT to MXN 66 from MXN 70, signaling analyst concern over margin trajectory.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Multiple compression likely on guidance miss and margin reset.** Despite revenue in line with mid-single-digit expectations and continued market share gains, the 9.2% net income decline and EPS miss signal a margin reset is underway. Elevated capex (MXN 41.8B, +20% YoY) and high single-digit SG&A growth suggest profitability will remain under pressure through FY25. eCommerce acceleration (+20% in Mexico) and Walmart Connect growth (+16%) provide structural upside, but monetization remains nascent. Valuation likely to compress toward 16-17x forward P/E (from 18.2x current) as FY26 estimates reset lower. Thesis intact for long-term investors betting on omnichannel leadership in Mexico, but near-term headwinds warrant caution.
