## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

- **Consolidated revenue contraction:** Koç Holding posted TRY 688.9 billion in Q3 2025 revenue, declining 7.8% YoY from TRY 747.4 billion, reflecting Turkish lira volatility and weaker demand across consumer durables. Sequential revenue also dipped 0.9% QoQ from TRY 695.1 billion in Q2, signaling persistent macro headwinds.

- **Net income compression:** Q3 net income fell to TRY 7.65 billion from TRY 8.2 billion in Q2 (-6.7% QoQ), with EPS at TRY 0.30. The decline reflects margin pressure from lira depreciation, elevated input costs in Energy (Tüpraş), and weak international demand impacting Consumer Durables (Arçelik).

- **Automotive segment outperformance:** Tofaş delivered explosive 205% YoY revenue growth to TRY 87.2 billion in Q3, driven by robust domestic demand and export volume expansion. This contrasts sharply with Arçelik's -11% real revenue decline (TRY 124.4B) amid weak European demand and unfavorable product mix.

- **Energy segment resilience:** Tüpraş (refining) generated TRY 220.7 billion in Q3 revenue with TRY 12.3 billion in earnings, maintaining its position as Turkey's largest industrial company despite margin compression from crude oil price volatility and weaker refining spreads.

## GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK

### Management Commentary (November 6, 2025 Earnings Call)

**No explicit FY2025 or Q4 2025 guidance provided.** Koç Holding does not typically issue quarterly forward guidance, preferring to focus on annual investment targets and strategic priorities.

### 2025 Investment Outlook (Nine-Month Update)
- **9M 2025 Combined Investments:** USD $2.6 billion across Energy, Automotive, Consumer Durables, and Finance segments
- **5-Year Investment Total (2021-2025):** USD $15.7 billion, underscoring long-term growth commitment despite near-term volatility

### Macro Headwinds/Tailwinds Commentary (Implicit from Results & Subsidiary Disclosures)

**Headwinds:**
1. **Lira depreciation:** Turkish lira weakness (averaging ~34 TRY/USD in Q3 vs. ~20 TRY/USD in Q3 2024) pressures import-heavy segments (Consumer Durables, Automotive components) and creates FX translation drag on USD-denominated financials
2. **Inflation persistence:** Turkish inflation running ~50-60% YoY throughout 2025, compressing real purchasing power and consumer durables demand (Arçelik -11% real revenue)
3. **European demand weakness:** Koç's export-oriented businesses (Arçelik, Tofaş, Ford Otosan) face sluggish EU demand amid eurozone industrial recession
4. **Refining margin compression:** Tüpraş margins under pressure from volatile Brent crude spreads and elevated crude acquisition costs

**Tailwinds:**
1. **Domestic automotive demand:** Turkish passenger vehicle sales resilient, supporting Tofaş's 205% revenue surge and Ford Otosan's stable production volumes
2. **Energy infrastructure capex:** Turkish government focus on energy security supports Tüpraş refining utilization and Aygaz LPG distribution expansion
3. **Diversified conglomerate structure:** Finance (Yapı Kredi Bank) and Other segments (retail, IT) provide earnings stability offsetting cyclical industrial segments

**CFO/CEO Tone:** Management emphasizes "long-term value creation" and "strategic investments" despite near-term volatility. The $2.6B 9M investment pace signals confidence in Turkey's structural growth trajectory, though lack of explicit guidance reflects macro uncertainty.

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

### 1. **Energy** (Tüpraş - Refining; Aygaz - LPG Distribution; Opet - Fuel Retail)
- **Tüpraş Q3 2025 Revenue:** TRY 220.7 billion
- **Tüpraş Q3 2025 Earnings:** TRY 12.3 billion
- **Performance:** Tüpraş remains Turkey's largest industrial company (2024 full-year revenue: TRY 810B). Q3 results reflect stable refining volumes but compressed margins due to volatile crude-product spreads. Raised refining margin guidance post-Q3, signaling improved outlook for Q4.
- **Segment Outlook:** Neutral. Refining capacity utilization remains robust, but margin volatility persists. Aygaz LPG distribution benefits from residential heating demand entering winter months.

### 2. **Automotive** (Tofaş - Fiat/Stellantis JV; Ford Otosan - Ford JV)
- **Tofaş Q3 2025 Revenue:** TRY 87.2 billion (+205% YoY)
- **Tofaş Q3 2025 Net Profit:** TRY 1.2 billion
- **Ford Otosan Q3 2025 Revenue:** Not disclosed (FY2024: TRY 595B)
- **Performance:** **Outperformer.** Tofaş delivered exceptional 205% YoY growth driven by:
  1. Strong domestic passenger vehicle sales (Turkey market +15-20% YoY in 2025)
  2. Export volume recovery to EU markets (Fiat Egea/Tipo production)
  3. Favorable product mix (higher-margin SUV/crossover variants)
- **Segment Outlook:** Positive. Domestic demand resilience and export diversification (CEE markets) support 2026 volume growth, though European slowdown remains a risk.

### 3. **Consumer Durables** (Arçelik - White Goods/Electronics)
- **Arçelik Q3 2025 Revenue:** TRY 124.4 billion (-11% YoY in real terms)
- **Performance:** **Underperformer.** Revenue contraction driven by:
  1. Weak international demand (Europe accounts for ~40% of Arçelik sales; eurozone appliance demand -5% to -7% in 2025)
  2. Unfavorable product mix (lower-margin entry-level SKUs outpacing premium categories)
  3. Pricing pressure in hypercompetitive white goods market (Chinese competition in EU)
- **Segment Outlook:** Cautious. Arçelik's global footprint (30+ countries) provides diversification, but European demand recovery unlikely until H2 2026. Domestic Turkish market remains stable but insufficient to offset export weakness.

### 4. **Finance** (Yapı Kredi Bank)
- **Q3 2025 Performance:** Not disclosed in consolidated results (Yapı Kredi reports separately as UniCredit subsidiary)
- **9M 2025 Contribution:** Finance segment typically contributes 15-20% of Koç Holding's consolidated earnings
- **Segment Outlook:** Turkish banking sector benefits from high interest rates (CBRT policy rate at 50% in Q3 2025), supporting net interest margin expansion. Credit growth modest due to inflation and economic uncertainty.

### 5. **Other** (Migros Retail; Koç Bilgi IT Services; Setur Tourism)
- **9M 2025 Performance:** Diversified portfolio provides earnings stability
- **Migros (Retail):** Benefits from inflation-driven nominal sales growth, though real consumption is flat to down
- **Koç Bilgi (IT):** Enterprise digital transformation demand supports steady growth
- **Segment Outlook:** Neutral to positive. Defensive characteristics offset industrial segment cyclicality.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis Under Pressure; Multiple Contraction Reflects Turkish Macro Risk Premium**

Koç Holding's Q3 2025 results underscore the challenges of operating a diversified industrial conglomerate in Turkey's high-inflation, lira-volatile environment. The -7.8% YoY revenue decline and -6.7% QoQ net income contraction signal macro headwinds outweighing segment-level execution.

**However, the thesis is **not broken**:**
1. **Automotive resilience:** Tofaş's 205% growth demonstrates domestic demand strength and export recovery potential
2. **Energy stability:** Tüpraş maintains market dominance with improving refining margin guidance for Q4
3. **Valuation support:** 3.5% dividend yield + trading at 0.69x P/B (vs. Turkish industrials avg ~1.0x P/B) provides downside cushion

**Valuation Implication:**
- **Current ADR Price:** ~$19.80 (December 2025)
- **52-Week Range:** $17.10 - $29.90
- **Analyst Consensus (Istanbul-listed KCHOL):** "Strong Buy" with avg. target of TRY 284.39 (~$8.40 USD equivalent, implying downside due to ADR/local share premium dynamics)
- **Fair Value Estimate:** $22-$24 ADR range (1.0x P/B rerating + 10-15% earnings recovery in 2026)

**Trading Recommendation:** **Accumulate on weakness** for Turkish exposure with 12-18 month horizon. Near-term volatility (Q4 earnings, lira FX, inflation) creates entry points below $19. Catalyst: Lira stabilization + European demand recovery in H2 2026 would drive 20-25% upside to $24-$25 ADR price.

**Key Risks:**
- Lira crisis (CBRT policy error, geopolitical shock)
- European recession deepens (Arçelik export collapse)
- Refining margin compression continues (Tüpraş earnings miss)
- Elevated leverage (1.96x D/E) constrains financial flexibility

**Thesis intact for patient capital; multiple compression offers value entry point at 0.69x P/B.**
