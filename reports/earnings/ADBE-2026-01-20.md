## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (The "So What?")

- **AI adoption accelerating but monetization timeline remains the debate:** Generative credit consumption grew 3x QoQ, and AI-influenced ARR now exceeds one-third of total book of business, but incremental revenue contribution remains embedded in existing subscriptions rather than standalone pricing.

- **Beat driven by Digital Media strength:** Creative & Marketing Professionals subscription revenue of $4.25B (+11% YoY) and Business Professionals & Consumers at $1.72B (+15% YoY) both exceeded expectations, reflecting robust user acquisition and expanded customer value.

- **Guidance solid but didn't "wow":** FY26 revenue guide of $25.9-26.1B slightly above Street ($25.87B), but the stock declined 0.35% in regular trading and fell another 0.67% in after-hours to $342, reflecting elevated expectations embedded in the multiple.

- **Stock underperformance continues:** ADBE down 37.6% over the past year, underperforming both the broader tech sector (+25%) and software industry (+4.9%), as investors await clearer AI monetization catalysts.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

**Thesis intact but valuation re-rating requires clearer AI monetization proof points.** Adobe delivered solid execution with double-digit ARR growth and record cash returns, but the stock's 37% YTD decline reflects investor frustration with the pace of AI-driven revenue acceleration. The $2.6B net new ARR target for FY26 is constructive, but multiple expansion likely requires (1) standalone AI product pricing or (2) visible market share gains from Firefly. Current valuation appears to discount continued execution; upside catalyst remains AI monetization clarity.
