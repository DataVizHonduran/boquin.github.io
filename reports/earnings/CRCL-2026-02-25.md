## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **Decisive triple beat on EPS** ($0.43 vs. ~$0.16 consensus, +186%) driven by accelerating USDC on-chain transaction volume (+247% YoY to $11.9T in Q4) and RLDC margin expansion to 40% — the strongest quarterly margin since IPO, expanding +1,004 bps YoY as Coinbase's revenue-share growth lagged USDC's total circulating supply growth
- **FY2025 net loss of ($70M) is optical**: the loss is entirely attributable to $566M in IPO-triggered stock-based compensation vesting; underlying Adj. EBITDA of $582M (+104% YoY) reflects a structurally profitable business with significant operating leverage
- **Reserve yield compression is the defining structural risk**: average reserve return rate declined to 3.8% in Q4 from ~5.1% in Q4 2024 (-131 bps YoY), tracking Fed funds rate trajectory; with USDC now yielding ~3.8% on reserves, each additional 25 bps cut removes ~$70M in annualized reserve income at current circulation levels
- **Stock surged 19–20%** to an intraday high of $75.56 on earnings day — the largest single-day move since IPO — reversing ~40% of the prior drawdown; retail sentiment flipped from bearish to bullish; notable counterweight: unanimous insider selling ($62.3M across 39 transactions in the prior six months, zero purchases)

- CEO Jeremy Allaire emphasized Circle Payments Network (CPN) as the growth catalyst: 55 financial institutions enrolled, 74 in eligibility review, annualized volume at $5.7B (trailing 30 days as of Feb 20)

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Thesis inflecting positively: RLDC margin re-rating (+1,004 bps in Q4) validates the structural bull case that Circle Platform USDC growth can compress Coinbase revenue-share dilution over time; combined with +$0.27 EPS beat and 40x net income growth, the stock's 19–20% intraday surge is justified. However, with the reserve return rate at 3.8% and the Fed's next move likely a cut, the market will demand evidence that Other Revenue ($150-170M guided for 2026, ~6% of total) and CPN volume can offset reserve yield compression at scale before awarding a durable multiple re-rate. Morgan Stanley's Equal-Weight at $66 represents the bear case; the path to consensus Buy at ~$131–142 requires demonstrated revenue diversification and bank charter execution.
