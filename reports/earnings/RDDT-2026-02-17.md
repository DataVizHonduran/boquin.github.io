## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **Advertising was the primary driver of the beat.** Ad revenue grew 75% YoY to $690M, representing ~95% of total revenue, driven by broad-based strength across objectives, channels, verticals, and geographies. Reddit Max (AI-powered campaign platform) delivered 17% CPA reduction and 27% conversion volume increase in beta testing.
- **Profitability inflected meaningfully.** Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 45% in Q4 (vs. 38% full-year average), and net income hit $252M — up ~255% YoY — signaling Reddit is past the investment phase and scaling margins.
- **Full-year 2025 was a breakout year.** Revenue of $2.2B (+69% YoY), net income of $530M (24% margin), and gross margins of 91.2% (+70bps YoY) validated the post-IPO thesis.
- **After-hours reaction was volatile.** Stock initially rallied ~7.7% to $167.88 on the beat, but reversed sharply the next day, falling ~8% as investors weighed AI data licensing sustainability concerns and regulatory scrutiny. Stock is down >35% YTD in 2026.

## SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

| Segment | Q4 Revenue | YoY Growth | % of Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Advertising** | $690M | +75% | ~95% |
| **Other Revenue (Data/Content Licensing)** | $36M | +8% | ~5% |

- **Advertising (+75% YoY):** The standout performer. Growth was broad-based across verticals and geographies, with Reddit Max AI-powered campaigns showing strong early results. This is an acceleration from prior quarters.
- **Other Revenue (+8% YoY):** Includes content/data licensing deals (Google, OpenAI). Growth decelerated meaningfully, raising questions about the scalability and renewal cadence of AI licensing contracts. Investors flagged regulatory scrutiny as an overhang.

## ANALYST TAKEAWAY

Thesis intact: Reddit delivered a clean beat across revenue, EPS, and users, with advertising growth accelerating to 75% and margins inflecting to 45%. However, **the deceleration in data licensing growth (+8% vs. prior quarters) and the Q1 EBITDA margin step-down to ~36% suggest the easy re-rating is behind us.** At current levels (stock down >35% YTD), the risk/reward is improving, but multiple expansion requires sustained ad revenue acceleration and clarity on the durability of AI licensing revenue. The $1B buyback provides a floor but is modest relative to the valuation reset. **Watch Q1 for confirmation that 50%+ topline growth is sustainable without a licensing tailwind.**
