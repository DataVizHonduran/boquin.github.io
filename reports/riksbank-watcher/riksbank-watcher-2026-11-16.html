Executive Summary

The Riksbank has pivoted back to a tightening bias, marking a significant shift in policy direction. On November 4, 2026, the Executive Board raised the policy rate to 2.0%, the first hike since 2023, citing the need to parry emerging price risks. Governor Thedéen has explicitly linked this move to strong growth and inflation risks. While Deputy Governor Seim continues to emphasize the inflation target as a nominal anchor and Deputy Governor Jansson focuses on the risks of wage formation, First Deputy Governor Bunge advocates for a gradual approach. The overall tone has shifted from the previous easing cycle toward a "higher-for-longer" or restrictive stance to ensure CPIF returns sustainably to the 2% target.
