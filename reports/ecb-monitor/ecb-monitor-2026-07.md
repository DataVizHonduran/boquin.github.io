1. **[ECB] Beyond energy prices: the ripple effects of Gulf supply disruptions**
This research highlights that Gulf supply shocks trigger second-round effects far beyond immediate fuel costs, specifically through increased input costs for petrochemicals and logistics. For the Eurozone, this suggests a higher risk of "sticky" core inflation and a potential reversal of the recent disinflationary trend, complicating the ECB's path toward a neutral rate.

**Synthesis:** The current research indicates a heightened vulnerability to external energy shocks that permeate deeper into the supply chain than previously modeled. This implies that the ECB may need to maintain a more restrictive policy stance for longer to counteract non-energy inflationary ripples.
