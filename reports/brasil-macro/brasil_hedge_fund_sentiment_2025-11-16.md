## Executive Summary

Brazilian hedge funds have demonstrated mixed performance and positioning during August-November 2025, with most funds maintaining bullish bets on local assets that have outperformed benchmarks for the first time since 2022. The consensus view includes positioning for further interest rate rallies in Brazil, strategic USD shorts (betting on BRL strength), and tactical plays on the yield curve. However, significant caution emerged in September as managers like Ibiuna expressed bearish views on Brazil's ability to benefit from global tailwinds, citing persistent fiscal concerns. The industry continues to face structural headwinds with BRL 357-378 billion in net outflows over the past 12 months due to rising rates and market volatility. Key positioning themes: long Brazilian rates (betting on cuts), short USD/BRL, selective equity exposure, and heightened fiscal risk awareness.

**Key Consensus Views:**
- **Rates:** Positioned for further rate rallies (betting on cuts); some tactical curve steepening
- **Currency (BRL):** Mixed to bearish; USD shorts early in period, reversed as fiscal concerns intensified
- **Equities:** Selective exposure; outperformance in September from equity gains
- **Fiscal Policy:** Major concern across all funds; primary driver of risk premium and positioning caution

## Conclusion & Outlook

### Current Positioning Summary (as of Nov 2025)

**Consensus macro view:**
- **Rates:** Positioned for eventual cuts, but timeline extended due to fiscal concerns; Selic at 15% for "prolonged period"
- **Currency:** Early USD shorts largely closed or reduced; BRL outlook deteriorated with fiscal worries
- **Equities:** Selective exposure; tactical rather than strategic
- **Fiscal risk:** Elevated to primary concern; driving cautious stance across most funds

**Contrarian voices:**
- **Ibiuna:** Most explicitly bearish on Brazil's macro outlook
- **Legacy:** Defensive positioning vindicated in September volatility

### Key Risks to Current Positioning

**If fiscal situation deteriorates further:**
- Rate cut timeline could be pushed even further out (pressure on long duration positions)
- BRL could weaken significantly (USD shorts would be painful)
- Capital outflows could accelerate (already BRL 378bn over 12 months)
- Equity markets could underperform (limited downside protection if tactical positions)

**If fiscal reforms materialize:**
- Rate cuts could come faster than "prolonged pause" suggests (curve steepening pays off)
- BRL could rally sharply (rewards those who held USD shorts)
- Equity markets could outperform (funds with exposure benefit)
- Capital flows could reverse (potential performance tailwind)

### Industry Structural Challenges

**Persistent headwinds:**
- Massive outflows (BRL 378bn) creating liquidity pressure
- High Selic (15%) making fixed income alternatives attractive vs. hedge funds
- Second consecutive year of benchmark underperformance (2023-2024)
- Political uncertainty and fiscal credibility concerns

**Potential catalysts:**
- First time beating CDI benchmark since 2022 (2025 YTD through Sep) could stem outflows
- Consolidation (Verde-Vinci Compass) may create stronger competitive entities
- Global macro volatility could favor hedge fund skill vs passive strategies
- Rate cut cycle (when it arrives) could drive performance
