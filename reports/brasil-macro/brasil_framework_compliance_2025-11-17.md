# Brazil Fiscal Framework Compliance Report
**Last Updated:** 2025-11-17
**Coverage:** Latest Bimonthly Report (4th Bimester Sept-Oct 2025) + Historical Time Series Since 2023

## Executive Summary

Brazil is on track to meet its 2025 fiscal framework target with a projected primary deficit of R$ 30.2 billion (0.25% of GDP), just within the lower tolerance band of R$ 31 billion. While technically compliant, the framework's credibility remains under market scrutiny following target revisions in April 2024 and weak spending control measures. Gross debt continues its upward trajectory toward 80% of GDP, raising long-term sustainability concerns despite improved revenue performance.
