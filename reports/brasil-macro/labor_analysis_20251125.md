# 🇧🇷 BRAZIL LABOR MARKET ANALYSIS

============================================================
## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
============================================================

Brazil's unemployment rate stands at **6.6%** (Q4 2025), down from 7.5% a year ago, indicating a **tight labor market** with limited slack.

Employment grew 0.8% YoY. Real wages increased 3.1% YoY, signaling wage pressures that could contribute to inflation persistence.

**Labor Market Assessment: Tight**

---

## KEY INSIGHTS

• Employment growing 0.8% YoY
• Real wages growing at 3.1% YoY
• Market expects unemployment at 5.7% for 2025
• Unemployment trend: **Improving** - rate declining over past year

---

## MONETARY POLICY IMPLICATIONS

• Tight labor market suggests limited output gap, supporting restrictive monetary policy
• Strong real wage growth may signal inflation pressures ahead

**Policy Stance**: Tight labor market combined with strong wage growth argues against premature easing. COPOM likely to maintain restrictive policy until labor market cools.
