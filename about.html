About Me

I am a discretionary global macro investor leveraging a combination of systematic filters and fundamental judgment to identify asymmetric opportunities across currencies, local rates, and sovereign bonds.

Macro assets are prone to mispricing due to misunderstood business cycles, political noise, and episodic liquidity dislocations. My process seeks to exploit these inefficiencies by aligning valuation anomalies with business cycle inflections and global macro regime shifts. The key is knowing when a mispricing is investable and structuring it effectively.

My edge lies in blending systematic rigor with fundamental insight. Quantitative models alone miss context ("assets can be cheap for a reason"), while pure fundamental approaches often overestimate priced-in narratives ("great macro stories"). A programmatic approach to macro and price data provides a comprehensive and disciplined assessment that avoids anecdotal cherry picking.
